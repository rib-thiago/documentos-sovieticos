**Telegram of G. K. Ordzhonikidze to I. V. Stalin** 
December 8, 1920

Moscow - the Kremlin, Stalin.

Baku, 8/XII-20

Georgians do not give transit for Armenia 1 and the last days have become terribly insolent. In my opinion, the question [about] Georgia in general is in the near future, moreover, then it will require more strength and more sacrifices. Is it impossible to guess. Sergo.

RTSKHIDNI. F. 85. Op. 15. D. 64. L. 1. Autograph.

Notes:

1 . We are talking about the transit of food, goods and weapons for Armenia through the territory of Georgia.
