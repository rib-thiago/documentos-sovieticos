# On confiscation of church valuables

**Resolution of the Politburo of the Central Committee of the RCP (b) "on the commission for the removal of valuables from Moscow churches." From the minutes of the meeting of the Politburo No. 111, item 39 of March 13, 1922.**

No. 23-6ö, STRICTLY SECRET

By a survey of PB members dated 11 March 22

39. - Approval of the commission for the removal of valuables from Moscow churches .
