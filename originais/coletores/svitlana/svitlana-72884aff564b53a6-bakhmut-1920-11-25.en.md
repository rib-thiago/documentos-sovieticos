On the transfer of the Center of the Donetsk province from Lugansk to Bakhmut.

Archive: Published in No. 268 of the News of the All-Russian Central Executive Committee of Soviets of November 28, 1920.

Article No. 491.

Decree of the All-Russian Central Executive Committee of Soviets.

On the transfer of the Center of the Donetsk province from Lugansk to Bakhmut.

The All-Russian Central Executive Committee, in agreement with the All-Ukrainian Central Executive Committee and in repeal of the decision of the All-Russian Central Executive Committee of October 21, 1920, decides: To transfer the center of the Donetsk province from Lugansk to Bakhmut.

Signed:

For the Chairman of the All-Russian Central Executive Committee Yu. Lutovinov .

Secretary of the All-Russian Central Executive Committee A. Yenukidze .

November 25, 1920.

Published in No. 268 of the News of the All-Russian Central Executive Committee of Soviets of November 28, 1920.
