INTERROGATION RECORD

OF NIKOLAEV, L. V. — dated December 13, 1934.

QUESTION: Did you have accomplices among the Smolny staff in carrying out the terrorist attack on Comrade KIROV?

ANSWER: I had no accomplices. I used a number of Smolny employees to establish KIROV's movements. I have testified about this. I did not even need direct accomplices among the Smolny staff, since I obtained what I needed to know to carry out the terrorist attack through conversations with employees I knew; moreover, KOTOLYNOV gave me considerable assistance in establishing KIROV's movements.

QUESTION: Without accomplices among the Smolny staff, you could not possibly have been so precisely informed of Comrade KIROV's movements. The investigation calls on you to name those Smolny employees who were direct accomplices in the murder.

ANSWER: I again testify that I had no accomplices at Smolny; there was no need whatsoever for them.

QUESTION: Did you have accomplices among the employees of the Regional NKVD Directorate?

ANSWER: On this question I have already testified that I have no accomplices.

QUESTION: Did other members of the counterrevolutionary organization have connections among the NKVD Directorate staff, and were those connections used in carrying out the terrorist attack?

ANSWER: I know nothing about this; I personally had no connections, apart from my acquaintances YAROLYANTS and PONOMARENKO, about whom I have already testified. They had no connection whatsoever with the preparation of the terrorist attack.

QUESTION: Were you acquainted with BORISOV, the commissar of the Regional NKVD Directorate who guarded Comrade KIROV?

ANSWER: No, I do not know him.

QUESTION: From whom did you learn of the existence of a special entrance at Smolny ordinarily used by Comrades KIROV, CHUDOV, and KODATSKY?

ANSWER: In addition to the general entrance, there is a special entrance at Smolny used only by leading officials of the City-Regional Party Committee and the Regional Executive Committee. I learned of this entrance through the observations I made of KIROV's arrivals at and departures from Smolny.

Recorded correctly from my words. The record has been read aloud to me.

L. NIKOLAEV

Interrogated by:

DEPUTY PEOPLE'S COMMISSAR FOR INTERNAL AFFAIRS OF THE USSR — AGRANOV

HEAD OF THE EKO OF THE GUGB OF THE NKVD OF THE USSR — MIRONOV

ASSISTANT HEAD OF THE EKO OF THE GUGB OF THE NKVD OF THE USSR — DMITRIEV
