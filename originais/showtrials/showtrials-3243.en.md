INTERROGATION RECORD

conducted by the Head of the 1st Section, SPO, UGB — LULOV,

on 14 December 1934.

VINOGRADOV, Vasily Alekseevich.

SUPPLEMENTARY.

QUESTION: What is the attitude of the members of your counter-revolutionary Trotskyist-Zinovievist organisation towards the Party leadership?

ANSWER: During meetings and conversations the members of our counter-revolutionary organisation systematically cultivated hatred of the Party leadership, especially Comrade STALIN. We particularly stressed that STALIN dealt harshly with former oppositionists, that he had abolished inner-Party democracy and replaced it with the apparatus's undivided domination, and that old Bolshevik cadres had been removed from the Party leadership. We noted bitterly that STALIN was responsible for excluding ZINOVIEV and KAMENEV from the Party leadership, whom we described as LENIN's closest disciples, and for removing EVDOKIMOV, ZALUTSKY, BAKAEV and others from leadership. All our counter-revolutionary organisation's members systematically developed these propositions, including RUMYANTSEV, FADEEV and ANTONOV.

QUESTION: Which members of your organisation know and were connected with Leonid NIKOLAEV?

ANSWER: Among the members of our counter-revolutionary Trotskyist-Zinovievist organisation, those who know NIKOLAEV well and were connected with him are myself — VINOGRADOV — RUMYANTSEV, SHATSKY, TOLMAZOV, ANTONOV, FADEEV, KHANIK, NIKANOROV, NADEL, ZVEZDOV, SOKOLOV and KOTOLYNOV.

QUESTION: What can you testify concerning Comrade KIROV's murder by NIKOLAEV, a member of your counter-revolutionary organisation?

ANSWER: Political responsibility for NIKOLAEV's murder of Comrade KIROV rests with our organisation, under whose influence NIKOLAEV grew up and was educated in a spirit of hatred towards the VKP(b) leaders.

Correctly taken down from my words and read by me, in confirmation of which I sign. —

V. VINOGRADOV

Interrogated by — G. LULOV.
