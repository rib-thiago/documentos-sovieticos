INTERROGATION RECORD

conducted by the HEAD OF THE 4TH SECTION OF THE SPO — L. KOGAN

on December 19, 1934.

BASHKIROV, Aleksandr Fabianovich

QUESTION: At the previous interrogation you testified that the question of replacing the Party leadership was not directly raised within the organization. The investigation has information to the contrary. Clarify your testimony.

ANSWER: The entire struggle of the Zinovievist counterrevolutionary organization was essentially directed toward replacing the Party leadership. This was the fundamental political orientation of all its actions. The instruction was to replace STALIN's leadership with ZINOVIEV and KAMENEV. It was precisely for this purpose that the question of bringing ZINOVIEV and KAMENEV into the Politburo was raised and discussed, so that, having won the Party's trust, they would remove STALIN from the leadership. When the possibility of winning a majority of the Party to the Zinovievist organization's side fell away, the following ways of removing STALIN were advanced: first, the possibility of war breaking out. We considered that war would give us an opportunity to prove our devotion to the cause of communism. In this case we were convinced that STALIN would be compelled to call ZINOVIEV and KAMENEV into the leadership. This would mean STALIN's withdrawal from the leadership. Second, the possibility of a split within the Politburo, as a result of which STALIN would find himself outside the leadership.

Recorded correctly from my words, in confirmation of which I sign.

BASHKIROV.

INTERROGATED BY: L. KOGAN.
