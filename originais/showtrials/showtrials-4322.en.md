CONFRONTATION RECORD

between the accused Artem Moiseevich GERTIK and Ivan Stepanovich GORSHENIN

dated January 7, 1935.

The accused A. M. GERTIK and I. S. GORSHENIN, after confirming their mutual acquaintance, testified as follows:

QUESTION TO GORSHENIN: What do you know about A. M. GERTIK's membership in the underground Zinovievist organization?

ANSWER: I know very well that Artem Moiseevich GERTIK is a member of our organization and until recently, that is, until December 1934, was closely connected with members of the Moscow Zinovievist center, including personally with the head of our organization, G. E. ZINOVIEV.

QUESTION TO GERTIK: Do you confirm this testimony?

ANSWER: Yes, I confirm it; until the latest period (until December 1934) of the underground Zinovievist organization's existence, I was indeed an active member of it and closely connected with members of the Moscow center.

QUESTION TO GORSHENIN: What was GERTIK's role within the organization?

ANSWER: In the period before the Fifteenth Congress of the AUCP(b), Artem Moiseevich GERTIK was in charge of the organization's entire technical apparatus. After the Fifteenth Congress and until recently, GERTIK remained an active organization member; his apartment was a headquarters and was used for illegal assemblies and gatherings of organization members. These assemblies were attended not only by Muscovites but also by visitors from Leningrad and other cities.

Personally, when present at the organization's illegal assemblies, GERTIK always spoke negatively on the fundamental questions of the AUCP(b) Central Committee's international and domestic policy, speaking with hostility toward the Party leadership.

He gave a sharply negative assessment of the AUCP(b) Central Committee's decisions and made personal attacks on Comrades STALIN and KAGANOVICH.

After his first arrest in early 1933, he began speaking against the Party leadership with even greater bitterness.

QUESTION TO GERTIK: What can you testify concerning this?

ANSWER: I confirm that illegal assemblies and gatherings did indeed take place at my apartment, as at the apartments of other members of our organization. These gatherings did indeed discuss the AUCP(b) Central Committee's policy on the fundamental questions of international and domestic life. I also confirm that I personally had a negative attitude toward individual decisions of the AUCP(b) Central Committee and made personal attacks on Comrades STALIN and KAGANOVICH. After the death of my wife Dina GERTIK, gatherings at my apartment ceased and were transferred to GORSHENIN's apartment.

GERTIK                                                  GORSHENIN

CONFRONTATION CONDUCTED BY:

DEPUTY HEAD, SPO, GUGB OF THE USSR — LYUSHKOV

ASSISTANT HEAD, SPO, GUGB OF THE USSR — RUTKOVSKY

DEPUTY HEAD, SPO, UGB — STROMIN
