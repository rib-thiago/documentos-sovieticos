CONFRONTATION RECORD

between KOTOLYNOV, Ivan Ivanovich, and NIKOLAEV, Leonid Vasilyevich,

18 December 1934.

The accused KOTOLYNOV I. I. and NIKOLAEV L. V., summoned for a confrontation, after mutually confirming that they knew each other personally, testified:

QUESTION TO KOTOLYNOV: Do you confirm that you were a member of the Zinovievist-Trotskyist counterrevolutionary organization?

ANSWER: Yes, I confirm that I was a member of the Zinovievist-Trotskyist counterrevolutionary organization.

QUESTION TO NIKOLAEV: Did you belong to the Zinovievist-Trotskyist counterrevolutionary organization, and who recruited you?

ANSWER: Yes, I belonged to the Zinovievist-Trotskyist counterrevolutionary organization; I was recruited into the organization by KOTOLYNOV; this was in September 1934, in the building of the Polytechnic Institute, where KOTOLYNOV studied.

QUESTION TO KOTOLYNOV: Do you confirm that you recruited NIKOLAEV into the Zinovievist-Trotskyist counterrevolutionary organization?

ANSWER: No, I deny it.

QUESTION TO NIKOLAEV: Did KOTOLYNOV propose to you, on behalf of the counterrevolutionary organization, that you murder Comrade KIROV? Did you accept, and under what circumstances did this take place?

ANSWER: Yes, KOTOLYNOV proposed to me that I kill KIROV on behalf of the Zinovievist-Trotskyist counterrevolutionary organization; I accepted this proposal in September 1934. The proposal was made at the Polytechnic Institute, where I had gone to meet KOTOLYNOV.

QUESTION TO KOTOLYNOV: Do you confirm NIKOLAEV's testimony that he murdered KIROV on your instructions?

ANSWER: No, I deny it.

The answers were recorded from our words; each confirms his own testimony.

NIKOLAEV L. V.                               KOTOLYNOV I. I.

Confrontation conducted by:

HEAD OF THE EKO OF THE GUGB OF THE NKVD OF THE USSR — MIRONOV

DEPUTY HEAD OF THE SPO OF THE GUGB OF THE NKVD OF THE USSR — LYUSHKOV

ASSISTANT TO THE HEAD OF THE EKO OF THE GUGB OF THE NKVD OF THE USSR — DMITRIEV
