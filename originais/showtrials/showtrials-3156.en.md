RECORD

of the confrontation between the accused POPOV, Georgy Fedorovich, and SHATSKY, Nikolai Nikolaevich, conducted on 18/XII-1934. —

The accused POPOV G. F. and SHATSKY N. N., summoned for a confrontation, after acknowledging that they knew each other personally, testified:

QUESTION TO POPOV: Do you know of SHATSKY N. N.'s membership in the counterrevolutionary Zinovievist-Trotskyist organization?

POPOV'S ANSWER: Yes, I know that SHATSKY was a member of the counterrevolutionary Zinovievist-Trotskyist organization.

QUESTION TO SHATSKY: Do you confirm this testimony by POPOV?

SHATSKY'S ANSWER: I deny POPOV's testimony.

QUESTION TO POPOV: State what you know about SHATSKY N.'s terrorist intentions.

POPOV'S ANSWER: In February or March 1934, I met SHATSKY at a meeting of the association of former comrades-in-arms, which, as I testified earlier, was used by the counterrevolutionary Zinovievist-Trotskyist organization as a cover for counterrevolutionary work.

After the meeting, in a conversation with me, SHATSKY expressed sharply hostile sentiments toward the party leaders. SHATSKY said that “the party leadership oppresses the party,” that the Seventeenth Congress's decisions on organizational questions actually meant “the victory of the apparatus over the party,” and similar counterrevolutionary fabrications. SHATSKY's malicious attacks against the party leaders were terrorist in character.

QUESTION TO SHATSKY: Do you confirm POPOV G.'s testimony?

SHATSKY'S ANSWER: I confirm that in February or March 1934 I did indeed meet POPOV at a meeting of the association of former comrades-in-arms, with whom I did indeed have a conversation after the meeting. However, I deny the character of the conversation as presented by POPOV in his testimony.

POPOV.                                              SHATSKY.
