INTERROGATION RECORD

OF KOTOLYNOV Ivan Ivanovich, 14 December 1934.

QUESTION: What additional testimony can you give concerning members of the counterrevolutionary organization in Moscow?

ANSWER: Besides those named in my previous testimony, I know the following persons who worked actively in the Zinovievist organization at the time: BULAKH Viktor, ROZOVSKY Mulya and SHVARTS; they all maintained contact with KHARITONOV.

I also know the active Zinovievist Senya MINAEV, connected with the group of “23.” \[1\]

I have not seen any of these persons for a long time. In December 1933, when I was in Moscow, I tried unsuccessfully to meet MINAEV and BULAKH. I do not know whether TARASOV is connected with them; I presume they have contacts.

I. KOTOLYNOV.

INTERROGATED BY:

Head of the 6th Section of the SPO, GUGB — KORKIN.

Certified correct: illegible

RGASPI, fond 671, inventory 1, file 114, folio 226.

\[1\] A group of oppositionists who wrote a statement breaking with the opposition after the Fifteenth Congress of the All-Union Communist Party (Bolsheviks).
