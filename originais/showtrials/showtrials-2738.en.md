INTERROGATION RECORD

OF KOTOLYNOV, Ivan Ivanovich — 12/XII/1934.

QUESTION: Since when has the counter-revolutionary Zinovievist organisation of which you are a member existed?

ANSWER: After the Party defeated the Zinovievist-Trotskyist bloc, the Zinovievists continued to exist as an independent organisation.

All of us Zinovievists continued to maintain organisational ties, and regarded all ZINOVIEV's declarations renouncing his anti-Party views and struggle against the Party as a manoeuvring tactic.

Direct confirmation of this tactic is ZINOVIEV's written directive of 30/VI/1928, about which I have already testified, instructing us to deceive the Party. Personally, together with other members of the organisation, I concealed this letter from the Party and subsequently put it into practice. In particular, when returning to the Party in August 1928, RUMYANTSEV, I. TARASOV and I, while negotiating with Comrade YAROSLAVSKY, were in fact directed by KAMENEV, who, after receiving our information about the negotiations with Comrade YAROSLAVSKY, edited the final text of our application for admission to the Party.

QUESTION: Who leads the counter-revolutionary organisation?

ANSWER: The organisation is led by ZINOVIEV, KAMENEV and their associates EVDOKIMOV, BAKAEV, KHARITONOV and GERTIK.

QUESTION: Whom do you know in the Leningrad counter-revolutionary organisation?

ANSWER: V. RUMYANTSEV, S. MANDELSHTAM, A. TOLMAZOV, F. FADEEV and Ya. TSEITLIN. At the Industrial Institute: ANTONOV, ZVEZDOV and M. NADEL; I was connected with all of them. I was also connected with the Trotskyist SUROV, exiled, I believe, for counter-revolutionary activity. In addition I was connected with NATANSON until her exile, and with BOGOMOLNY.

I know LEVIN as an active Zinovievist; I assume participants in the group of “23” gather around him, in particular MYASNIKOV and ZELIKSON [1].

QUESTION: Who besides you maintained contact with the counter-revolutionary organisation's Moscow centre?

ANSWER: RUMYANTSEV also maintained contact with Moscow; he was always informed and told me the latest political news. In particular, at one meeting RUMYANTSEV told me of ZINOVIEV's removal from the editorial board of “Bolshevik” magazine. RUMYANTSEV expressed sympathy for ZINOVIEV and dissatisfaction with the Party leadership's attitude towards him.

I recall that last year, before the Party purge, RUMYANTSEV instructed me on the need to prepare for it, since, according to him, the purge would be used to settle accounts with the Zinovievists by fabricating cases against them.

QUESTION: What can you testify concerning the terrorist act against Comrade KIROV committed by L. NIKOLAEV, a member of your counter-revolutionary organisation?

ANSWER: Political and moral responsibility for NIKOLAEV's murder of Comrade KIROV rests with our organisation, under whose influence NIKOLAEV was brought up in an atmosphere of bitterness towards the VKP(b) leaders. —

I. KOTOLYNOV

Interrogated by:

DEPUTY HEAD OF THE SPO, GUGB, NKVD OF THE USSR — LYUSHKOV.

HEAD OF THE 6TH SECTION, SPO, GUGB, NKVD OF THE USSR — KORKIN.
