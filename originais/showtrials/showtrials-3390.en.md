Top secret.

TO THE SECRETARY OF THE CENTRAL COMMITTEE OF THE ALL-UNION COMMUNIST PARTY (BOLSHEVIKS),

Comrade STALIN.

On December 21 of this year, at the border in Negoreloye, we arrested Moisei Markovich KHARITONOV, who was expelled from the Party by the Fifteenth Congress of the All-Union Communist Party (Bolsheviks) for belonging to the Zinovievist opposition.

On December 22 KHARITONOV is being sent to Leningrad.

PEOPLE'S COMMISSAR

OF INTERNAL AFFAIRS OF THE UNION OF SOVIET SOCIALIST REPUBLICS: (YAGODA)

December 22, 1934.

No. 31169

RGASPI, fond 17, inventory 171, file 200, folio 51.
