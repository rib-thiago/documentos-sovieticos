TESTIMONY

Of the accused VINOGRADOV, Vasily Alexeyevich.

Dated January 5, 1935.

Question: Do you know KOZIN?

Answer: I know KOZIN from the Vyborg district. His name is Konstantin. In 1923 he worked as organizer of the Komsomol collective on the Irinovka Railway (Okhta). Later, as far as I recall, apparently in 1924, KOZIN was removed from full-time Komsomol work for some misconduct and transferred to the “Krasnaya Zarya” plant. I know nothing about KOZIN’s membership in the Zinovievist opposition. About three years ago I met KOZIN at some meeting of activists. From my conversation with him I learned that he works in criminal investigation.

Recorded correctly from my words. —

V. VINOGRADOV.

INTERROGATED BY LULOV.

Certified correct: Churbanova
