INTERROGATION RECORD

Conducted by operational officer of the First Section of the SPO — MAKAROV

December 24, 1934.

FADEEV, Fedor Fadeevich (supplementary).

QUESTION: The investigation possesses information indicating that the counterrevolutionary Zinovievist organization, of which you were a member, regarded the possibility of war as a favorable moment for achieving the organization's basic objective: replacing the leadership of the All-Union Communist Party (Bolsheviks).

ANSWER: Around August 1934, together with organization member ANTONOV, I was at Rumyantsev's apartment, where we were drawing up a list of former Komsomol members to invite to an evening celebrating the Komsomol's anniversary. During the conversation, the question of relations between Japan and the USSR and a possible war was touched upon. I do not remember the exact content of the conversation, but in any event RUMYANTSEV pointed out that if war broke out, the present leadership of the All-Union Communist Party (Bolsheviks) would be unable to cope with the tasks facing the country in the more complicated situation and would be compelled to bring former oppositionists into work and leadership.

FADEEV.

Interrogated by — MAKAROV.

CERTIFIED TRUE: Kuznetsova
