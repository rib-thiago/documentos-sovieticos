TO THE SECRET POLITICAL DEPARTMENT OF THE LENINGRAD DIRECTORATE OF THE NKVD

From the prisoner in Block 1, Cell 233 of the House of Preliminary Detention, ZVEZDOV, Vasily Ivanovich,

Statement

Acknowledging the full gravity of my guilt before the party and the proletarian state, I consider it necessary to declare that, having reflected upon my entire situation, I now resolutely condemn my participation in an organization implicated in the commission of the disgraceful terrorist act against Comrade KIROV, a member of the Politburo of the Central Committee of the All-Union Communist Party (Bolsheviks).

Sincerely repenting of everything, I ask that, when my case is considered, account be taken of the fact that I shall devote all my future life to serving the Communist Party and Soviet power. I shall consider myself the happiest of men if I can atone for my guilt by working in the most dangerous and difficult sectors of the country's construction and defense.

ZVEZDOV V.

13 December 1934.
