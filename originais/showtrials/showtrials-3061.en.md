COPY

INTERROGATION RECORD

OF LEVIN VLADIMIR SOLOMONOVICH, 15 DECEMBER 1934.

(SUPPLEMENTARY)

QUESTION: Tell us where and when you hid the Zinovievists' archive.

ANSWER: In early 1928 I and the late Olga YURGENS took the Zinovievists' archive to Razliv, to EMELYANOV's sons. Kondraty EMELYANOV received the archive from us and hid it in his house.

QUESTION: What documents did the archive contain?

ANSWER: A set of printed Zinovievist documents and correspondence of the Leningrad Zinovievist group with ZINOVIEV and the Moscow center.

QUESTION: Did anyone come for the archive?

ANSWER: In 1931 KUKLIN Aleksandr asked me about the fate of the Zinovievist archive. I told him where it was and that I did not know what had subsequently happened to it.

Correctly recorded from my words and read to me.

LEVIN

INTERROGATED BY:

HEAD OF THE 1ST SECTION OF THE SPO, GUGB — PETROVSKY

CERTIFIED CORRECT: M. Koroleva
