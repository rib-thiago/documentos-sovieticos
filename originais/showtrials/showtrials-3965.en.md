INTERROGATION RECORD

OF KOSTRITSKY \[1\], Ivan Stanislavovich (supplementary)

January 1, 1935.

QUESTION: The investigation has information that you were associated with the center of the Zinovievist organization in Leningrad. Do you confirm this? Who belonged to it?

ANSWER: Yes, I confirm it. The center of the Zinovievist organization in Leningrad took shape before the Fifteenth Congress. The center included DMITRIYEV Timofei, ROTSKAN, SHURYGIN and LEVIN Vladimir; the center's meetings which I attended took place at SHURYGIN's and LEVIN Vladimir's apartments. I personally participated in the center's work as representative of the Narva district.

QUESTION: In your testimony of December 21, you stated that in 1934 you met EVDOKIMOV in Peterhof. Did you have any other meetings with EVDOKIMOV?

ANSWER: Yes. In addition to my repeated meetings with EVDOKIMOV in Moscow during 1933–1934 and the meeting in Peterhof in 1934, I met EVDOKIMOV in Peterhof about three times in 1933–1934.

Written correctly from my words—KOSTRITSKY.

INTERROGATED BY:

HEAD OF THE 1ST SECTION OF THE SPO—LULOV.

Certified correct: Kazakova

RGASPI, F. 671, Op. 1, D. 126, fol. 191.
