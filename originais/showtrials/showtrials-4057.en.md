INTERROGATION RECORD

Of FADEEV, Fyodor Fadeevich,

dated January 4, 1935.

QUESTION: Of what do you admit your guilt?

ANSWER: 1) Through comradeship with the Zinovievists TOLMAZOV, A., ANTONOV, N., RUMYANTSEV, V., and POPOV, I, FADEEV, F., was drawn into the counterrevolutionary Zinovievist organization and have remained its member until the most recent period;

2) from conversations with TOLMAZOV, A., I know that TOLMAZOV, A., and SEREDOKHIN, while in Moscow, established contact with KUKLIN, A., a member of the Moscow center of the Zinovievist organization; in addition, they met SAFAROV;

3) that recently the links between the members of our organization have grown stronger and our meetings have become more frequent at the apartments of SEVEROV and sometimes ANTONOV and RUMYANTSEV, V.;

4) that I kept a firearm (a “Browning” system revolver No. 2) without permission from the appropriate authorities.

Recorded correctly from my words and read aloud to me.

FADEEV.

INTERROGATED BY:

HEAD OF THE 1ST SECTION OF THE SPO OF THE GUGB OF THE NKVD OF THE USSR — PETROVSKY

OFFICER OF THE SPO OF THE UGB — MAKAROV.

Certified correct: A. Svetlova

RGASPI, fond 671, inventory 1, file 126, folio 238.
