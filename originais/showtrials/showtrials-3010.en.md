INTERROGATION RECORD

OF RUMYANTSEV, Vladimir Vasilyevich

13 December 1934.

QUESTION: Do you know members of the counterrevolutionary organization in Moscow besides those you have named?

ANSWER: At the end of 1928, when I, TARASOV Ivan and KOTOLYNOV were restored to membership rights in the All-Union Communist Party (Bolsheviks), we met MAGYAR, a Comintern official, at his apartment. KOTOLYNOV and TARASOV told me that MAGYAR was a covert member of the counterrevolutionary Zinovievist organization, maintaining illegal contact with ZINOVIEV and passing on to him everything of interest concerning the Comintern. Our conversation with MAGYAR revolved around our return to the party, and MAGYAR was very cautious in speaking with us.

V. RUMYANTSEV.

INTERROGATED BY:

DEPUTY HEAD OF THE SPO, GUGB, NKVD OF THE USSR — LYUSHKOV.
