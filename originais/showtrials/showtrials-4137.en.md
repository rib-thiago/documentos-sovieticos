INTERROGATION RECORD

Of KOSTINA, Anna Porfiryevna,

dated January 6, 1934.

QUESTION: Tell what you know about SAKHOV?

ANSWER: I met SAKHOV at GORSHENIN’s in 1933, when he returned from a business trip to Eastern Siberia, where he had gone with Comrade YAROSLAVSKY’s commission. SAKHOV informed us — myself, KOSTINA, KOZHURO, BAKAEV, GORSHENIN, I do not remember who else was present — that the entire Soviet apparatus of the East Siberian Territory was infiltrated and disorganized, that because of bureaucracy the apparatus’s maladies had not been exposed. Afterward, when meeting BAKAEV and me at Gort’s shop, SAKHOV told us that he was being transferred to Arkhangelsk. Asked why he was being transferred, SAKHOV replied: “Evidently it is an awkward situation: there was an oppositionist on the commission (meaning himself), so they are sending me to Arkhangelsk.” I know that SAKHOV met EVDOKIMOV, KUKLIN, A. S., and NAUMOV. In 1932 I met SAKHOV at ZINOVIEV’s.

Recorded correctly from my words and read aloud to me.

A. KOSTINA.

INTERROGATED BY:

HEAD OF THE 1ST SECTION OF THE SPO OF THE GUGB OF THE NKVD OF THE USSR — PETROVSKY.

Certified correct: Kazakova
