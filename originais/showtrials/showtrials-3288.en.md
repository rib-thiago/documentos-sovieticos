TOP SECRET

TO THE SECRETARY OF THE VKP(b) CENTRAL COMMITTEE

Comrade STALIN.

I report that on December 16 searches were conducted at the apartments of the following, and they were arrested:

1\. ZINOVIEV, Grigory Evseevich.

Seized during the search: a large quantity of archival materials — the opposition bloc's platform, a card of TROTSKY and old correspondence with him.

2\. KAMENEV, Lev Borisovich.

Seized during the search: archival correspondence.

3\. SAFAROV, Georgy Ivanovich, born in 1891, a member of the VKP(b) since 1908; expelled from the VKP(b) by the Fifteenth Party Congress for factional activity, but reinstated in November 1928. At the end of 1927 he was exiled by the OGPU for three years. Deputy head of the Eastern Department of the Communist International's Executive Committee.

Seized during the search: a statement to the Plenum of the Communist International's Executive Committee, dated 1928, containing an accusation against the VKP(b) Central Committee; a manuscript entitled “Proletarian Dictatorship, the Party and Bureaucratism”, written approximately in 1928; and old archival correspondence.

G. E. ZINOVIEV and L. B. KAMENEV were sent to Leningrad on December 16. G. I. SAFAROV will be sent to Leningrad on December 17.

I enclose G. E. ZINOVIEV's statement and SAFAROV's letter addressed to you.

PEOPLE'S COMMISSAR

FOR INTERNAL AFFAIRS OF THE UNION OF SSRs: (YAGODA)

December 17, 1934.

No. ……..
