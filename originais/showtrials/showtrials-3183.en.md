RECORD

of the confrontation between the accused SHATSKY N. N. and NIKOLAEV L. V., dated 20 December 1934.

QUESTION TO SHATSKY: Did you meet NIKOLAEV Leonid in February–March 1934? If so, state where this meeting took place.

ANSWER: Yes, I met Leonid NIKOLAEV in February–March in the Istmol building on the Moika.

QUESTION TO NIKOLAEV: Do you confirm that you met SHATSKY in February–March of this year in the Istpart building on the Moika?

ANSWER: Yes, I confirm it.

QUESTION TO NIKOLAEV: What conversation did you have with SHATSKY during this meeting?

ANSWER: I spoke with SHATSKY about the need to establish contact with the old cadres of the Zinovievist opposition and the need to organize ourselves; SHATSKY agreed with this; SHATSKY displayed bitterness against the party leaders. At the same time SHATSKY expressed the wish to go to work in Vyborg District, where the old opposition cadres were concentrated.

SHATSKY came to Istmol in order to contact oppositionists working at Istmol, POPOV and others.

QUESTION TO SHATSKY: Do you confirm that you did indeed discuss this with NIKOLAEV?

ANSWER: No, I do not confirm it.

QUESTION TO SHATSKY: Did you have another meeting with NIKOLAEV? If so, when precisely and where?

ANSWER: Yes, there was a meeting, approximately in July–August, near the house where KIROV lived, on Krasnye Zori Street.

Question to NIKOLAEV: Do you confirm that you did indeed meet SHATSKY on Krasnye Zori Street, near Comrade KIROV's apartment?

ANSWER: Yes, I confirm all this; the meeting was near the apartment where Comrade KIROV lived.

QUESTION TO NIKOLAEV: Relate the contents of your conversation with SHATSKY during this meeting.

ANSWER: During this August meeting, SHATSKY told me that he belonged to the counterrevolutionary Zinovievist organization, that he had established contact with the organization, with RUMYANTSEV, KOTOLYNOV, SOKOLOV, and others, that the organization was preparing to carry out a terrorist act against KIROV and that, in connection with this, SHATSKY was keeping watch on KIROV.

QUESTION TO SHATSKY: Do you confirm this testimony by NIKOLAEV?

ANSWER: No, I deny it.

QUESTION TO SHATSKY: During the aforementioned conversation with NIKOLAEV, did you encounter Comrade CHUDOV, secretary of the regional committee of the AUCP(b), as he drove past you?

ANSWER: Yes, during this August meeting we encountered Comrade CHUDOV, secretary of the Leningrad regional committee, as he drove past us.

QUESTION TO NIKOLAEV: Do you confirm that, during your conversation with SHATSKY, you did indeed encounter Comrade CHUDOV, secretary of the regional committee of the AUCP(b), as he drove past you?

ANSWER: Yes, I confirm it.

QUESTION TO NIKOLAEV: What conversation did you have with SHATSKY in connection with encountering Comrade CHUDOV?

ANSWER: In connection with encountering CHUDOV, SHATSKY said that CHUDOV was not suitable as a target for a terrorist act.

QUESTION TO SHATSKY: Do you confirm this testimony by NIKOLAEV?

ANSWER: No, I do not confirm it.

QUESTION TO SHATSKY: During the August conversation, did you tell NIKOLAEV that you had repeatedly been removed from work for opposition activity?

ANSWER: Yes, I told NIKOLAEV that I had been removed from work, expressing the supposition that this was connected with my former opposition activity.

QUESTION TO NIKOLAEV: What can you testify on this question?

ANSWER: SHATSKY told me that he had been removed from work as a former oppositionist and that he was on the OGPU's register.

QUESTION TO NIKOLAEV: During the aforementioned meeting, did SHATSKY tell you that he was preparing to carry out a terrorist act against Comrade STALIN?

ANSWER: Yes, SHATSKY told me about this.

QUESTION TO SHATSKY: Do you confirm this testimony by NIKOLAEV?

ANSWER: No, I do not confirm it.

QUESTION TO NIKOLAEV: Did SHATSKY propose that you establish contact with KOTOLYNOV to conduct counterrevolutionary work?

ANSWER: Yes, SHATSKY proposed this to me.

QUESTION TO SHATSKY: Do you confirm this testimony by NIKOLAEV?

ANSWER: No, I do not confirm it.

QUESTION TO NIKOLAEV: On the October anniversary in 1934, did you encounter SHATSKY near Comrade KIROV's apartment?

ANSWER: Yes, on the October anniversary in 1934 I encountered SHATSKY near Comrade KIROV's apartment on Krasnye Zori Street; I did not approach him because I assumed that SHATSKY was keeping watch on Comrade KIROV in order to carry out a terrorist act; I therefore transferred my own surveillance of KIROV to the Smolny, having agreed on this with KOTOLYNOV. As is known, I committed KIROV's murder in the Smolny.

QUESTION TO SHATSKY: Do you confirm this testimony by NIKOLAEV?

ANSWER: Yes, on the very day of the October anniversary in 1934 I was indeed on Krasnye Zori Street; I do not confirm that I was there for the purpose of keeping watch on KIROV to carry out a terrorist act.

QUESTION TO NIKOLAEV: Did SHATSKY speak with you about the need to establish contact abroad? What proposal did you make to SHATSKY, and later to KOTOLYNOV, on this question, and was it accepted?

ANSWER: Yes, SHATSKY told me that he wanted to establish contact abroad through Moscow, using RUMYANTSEV's Comintern connections; I proposed to SHATSKY that we try to establish contact abroad through the consulates in Leningrad; SHATSKY accepted my proposal.

QUESTION TO SHATSKY: Do you confirm this testimony by NIKOLAEV?

ANSWER: No, I do not confirm it.

QUESTION FROM SHATSKY TO NIKOLAEV: Does NIKOLAEV remember who was sitting beside them at the table while the conversation between SHATSKY and NIKOLAEV took place, when they met in February–March 1934 in that same room?

NIKOLAEV'S answer: We were alone together at the table.

SHATSKY                            L. NIKOLAEV

Confrontation conducted by:

Head of the EKO of the GUGB of the NKVD — MIRONOV

Deputy head of the SPO of the GUGB of the NKVD — LYUSHKOV

Assistant to the head of the EKO of the GUGB of the NKVD — DMITRIEV

Operational officer of the SPO of the GUGB — FEDOROV
