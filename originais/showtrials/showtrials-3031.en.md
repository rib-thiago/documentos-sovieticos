INTERROGATION RECORD

OF KOTOLYNOV Ivan Ivanovich

14 December 1934.

In addition to my testimony about TARASOV Ivan Ivanovich, I state: I met TARASOV and became close friends with him while we worked together in the Komsomol Central Committee, from 1924 onward.

During the opposition period TARASOV was an active Zinovievist.

In summer 1928 TARASOV was in Leningrad (he generally came here frequently on official business), and together with him at RUMYANTSEV's apartment we read ZINOVIEV's letter giving instructions to deceive the party. TARASOV, like me, returned to the party with a double-dealing purpose. The three of us (RUMYANTSEV, TARASOV and I) wrote a statement requesting admission to the party; we all went to YAROSLAVSKY and then to KAMENEV, who guided us and edited the final text of the statement.

In December 1933 I was on a business trip in Moscow and stayed with TARASOV I. I. One evening TARASOV and I visited FAIVILOVICH's apartment, where, as I already testified on 12 December, we made malicious counterrevolutionary attacks on the party leaders, especially Comrade STALIN.

I have not seen TARASOV personally since December 1933, but I maintain correspondence with him. In summer 1934 we corresponded about spending our vacation together; in particular, he wrote that it would be good to get into a rest home somewhere on the Volga run by the Zinovievist Senya MINAEV.

KOTOLYNOV.

INTERROGATED BY:

Head of the 6th SECTION OF THE SPO, GUGB — KORKIN.
