INTERROGATION RECORD

OF TOLMAZOV, Andrei Ilyich

11 December 1934.

QUESTION: Your testimony of 10/XII this year establishes that former Zinovievists in Leningrad maintained systematic contact with one another until recently. Explain on what basis these contacts were preserved and continued until recently.

ANSWER: The group of former Zinovievists mentioned in my testimony of 10/XII this year consists of former Komsomol workers who worked together for many years. Personal relationships developed in that work and later grew stronger on the basis of joint anti-Party activity and opposition sentiments. My relationship with RUMYANTSEV developed into personal friendship. Thus, as secretary of the Vyborg District Soviet, RUMYANTSEV helped me arrange treatment for my wife's sister at a dispensary. This was in the summer of this year. RUMYANTSEV also helped me obtain a duplicate of the partisan identity card I had lost. ANTONOV is my cousin; FADEEV and I shared an apartment until 1929.

Correctly taken down from my words, in confirmation of which I sign.

A. TOLMAZOV

INTERROGATED BY — KOGAN
