CONFRONTATION RECORD

Between the accused TARASOV, I. I., and FAIVILOVICH, L. Ya.,

conducted on January 5, 1935.

TARASOV, I. I., and FAIVILOVICH, summoned for a confrontation, after confirming that they knew each other personally, testified:

QUESTION TO FAIVILOVICH: What do you know about TARASOV, I. I.’s membership in the Zinovievist organization?

FAIVILOVICH’S ANSWER: I know that TARASOV, I. I., was a member of the counterrevolutionary Zinovievist organization.

QUESTION TO TARASOV: Do you confirm this testimony by FAIVILOVICH?

TARASOV’S ANSWER: Yes, I confirm it. As I have already testified to the investigation, I was a member of the counterrevolutionary Zinovievist organization.

QUESTION TO TARASOV: Describe the circumstances of the conversation held in December 1933 at FAIVILOVICH’s apartment about changing the party leadership and bringing ZINOVIEV into the leadership.

TARASOV’S ANSWER: I do not remember the exact circumstances of the conversation, chiefly how it began, but all of us — KOTOLYNOV, FAIVILOVICH, and myself, TARASOV — talked about “who could replace Comrade STALIN.” FAIVILOVICH also spoke then about the need to bring ZINOVIEV into the party leadership, and shared these attitudes of his (about the need to bring ZINOVIEV into the leadership) with me during our other meetings as well.

QUESTION TO FAIVILOVICH: Do you confirm TARASOV’s testimony?

FAIVILOVICH’S ANSWER: Yes, I held the view that ZINOVIEV needed to be brought into the leadership, about which I testified to the investigation.

As far as I remember, in our conversations in December 1933 there was no talk of replacing Comrade STALIN, but only of ZINOVIEV’s not being brought into the leadership, and in connection with this sharp dissatisfaction with Comrade STALIN was expressed.

TARASOV

FAIVILOVICH
