RECORD

of the confrontation between the accused Alexander Fabianovich BASHKIROV and Sergei Mikhailovich GESSEN, conducted on January 7, 1935.

A. F. BASHKIROV and S. M. GESSEN, summoned to the confrontation, after mutually acknowledging their personal acquaintance, testified:

QUESTION TO S. M. GESSEN: What do you know about A. F. BASHKIROV's membership in the Zinovievist organization and his role in it?

ANSWER BY GESSEN: I met BASHKIROV in 1927, when he was an active Zinovievist and belonged to Vladimir LEVIN's closest circle. I know that subsequently he maintained ties with LEVIN, who was one of the Zinovievists' leaders in Leningrad.

QUESTION TO A. F. BASHKIROV: Do you confirm S. M. GESSEN's testimony?

ANSWER BY BASHKIROV: I confirm GESSEN's testimony, adding that in 1925–1926 I had little contact, since I lived in Moscow.

QUESTION TO S. M. GESSEN: Tell the investigation about your ties with BASHKIROV and the nature of the political information you gave BASHKIROV and other Zinovievists when visiting Leningrad.

ANSWER BY GESSEN: I met BASHKIROV at LEVIN's in 1931. Our joint conversation touched on political questions, treated in the Zinovievist group's usual spirit. I do not remember the details of the conversation.

QUESTION TO A. F. BASHKIROV: Do you confirm this testimony by S. M. GESSEN?

ANSWER BY BASHKIROV: Yes, I confirm it.

GESSEN                                                              BASHKIROV

Confrontation conducted by:

ASSISTANT HEAD, SPO, GUGB — RUTKOVSKY

HEAD OF THE 4th SECTION, SPO, UGB, LENINGRAD REGION — KOGAN

Certified correct: A. Svetlova
