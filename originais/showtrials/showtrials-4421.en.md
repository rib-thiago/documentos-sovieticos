STATEMENT TO THE INVESTIGATION IN THE CASE OF THE ZINOVIEVIST ORGANIZATION

From the accus\<ed\> BASHKIROV, Aleksandr Fabianovich.

In order that the investigation may have a complete picture of me personally, and fully understand the sincerity of all my testimony in the case of the Zinovievist organization, I wish to make an additional statement about myself personally.

In 1919 I changed my surname. My father's surname was MAGIDS and until 1919 I bore the same surname. But in 1919 in Smolensk I changed my surname (officially), at the TsIK of Litbel (Lithuanian-Belorussian), to BASHKIROV. This was done by the entire group of Vilna Komsomol members who were initially in the detachment mobilized by the VK (Vilna Co\<mmittee\>) of the Komsomol. Thus, for example, APSHTEIN changed his surname to KUBANSKY, GETSILS to SOKOLOV, and LIVEN to LINOV. At first I did not conceal my father's surname, but later, when during the first party re-registration I concealed my social origin (I had not concealed it when joining the Komsomol), stating that my father was a master leatherworker, I began concealing my father's surname.

My father was in fact not a master leatherworker but a merchant, and owned a house in Vilna. I concealed this from the party and have continued concealing it until now, failing to find within myself the courage to state it honestly and directly.

Now, when reviewing the entire path I have traveled, I consider it my duty to inform the party and the proletarian court of this crime of mine against the party as well.

Aleksandr BASHKIROV.

January 11, 1935.

Statement received by: Head of the 4th Section of the SPO — KOGAN L.

Certified correct: Khvatov
