TO THE SECRET POLITICAL DEPARTMENT OF THE NKVD OF LENINGRAD REGION

From KHANIK L. O., prisoner in the House of Preliminary Detention, Building II, cell No. 220 —

Statement

I ask that my sincere acknowledgment and repentance for all the grave guilt that lies upon me be added to my case, as one implicated in the counterrevolutionary organization of the Zinovievist-Trotskyist remnants, which have clearly slid into fascism.

I sincerely ask the court, the NKVD investigation and, through them, the leading organs of the party to give me the opportunity to make amends for my crimes through tireless work.

I will devote my entire future life (I am 32 years old, more than half of which I spent in revolutionary work and in defending the dictatorship of the proletariat, arms in hand, during 1918–1921 as a volunteer in workers' detachments and cadet units of the Workers' and Peasants' Red Army) and all my ardor in work solely and exclusively to the cause of any sector of the great construction of socialism, led by the invincible party of LENIN–STALIN, and thereby atone, to at least some degree, for my guilt before the party, the working class, and the government of the USSR.

L. KHANIK.

21.XII-34.

Certified true: Potrokhova

RGASPI, fond 671, inventory 1, file 121, folio 153.
