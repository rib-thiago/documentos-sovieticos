INTERROGATION RECORD

OF EVDOKIMOV Grigory Eremeevich, January 14, 1935.

I ask the investigation to add the following to my previous testimony:

Having read a newspaper item a few days ago about REINGOLD's expulsion from the Party, I recalled a meeting of mine with REINGOLD in the summer of 1932, when one of us — he or I — gave the other a lift home after leaving the Kremlin dining room.

Before this I had not seen REINGOLD for a fairly long time, and during chance encounters I did not discuss political subjects with him and believed that he had broken his personal ties with ZINOVIEV.

At this meeting REINGOLD told me that he had recently visited ZINOVIEV at his dacha and would go there again on the next day off, saying that everything in the country was going to hell — things had been driven to the point that the Party leadership now did not know what to do. This statement immediately established his counterrevolutionary mood for me. REINGOLD and I agreed that I would also be at ZINOVIEV's dacha when he visited, but I did not happen to meet REINGOLD at ZINOVIEV's because I did not go to ZINOVIEV's that time and soon went on vacation. After my return from vacation the liquidation of RYUTIN's group was already taking place, and during my subsequent encounters with REINGOLD, which were chance encounters in the Kremlin dining room, the question of his ties with ZINOVIEV was not touched upon.

G. EVDOKIMOV.

INTERROGATED BY:

ASSISTANT HEAD OF THE SPO OF THE GUGB OF THE NKVD OF THE USSR — RUTKOVSKY.

Certified correct: A. Svetlova

RGASPI, F. 671, Op. 1, D. 136, fol. 6.
