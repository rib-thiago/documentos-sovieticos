INTERROGATION RECORD

OF BURMISTROV, Pavel Petrovich,

January 2, 1935.

QUESTION: What additional testimony can you give about your meetings with former participants in the Zinovievist-Trotskyist bloc?

ANSWER: After the Fifteenth Congress of the VKP(b), while often visiting DRYAZGOV, who lived at the “Astoria” hotel at that time, I became acquainted with METELKIN, whom I had known before. METELKIN is a Trotskyist. He put me in touch with the Trotskyists LUKIN Vasily and SOBOL, whom I had known previously.

I was subjected to persuasion by this group of Trotskyists. They began conversations with me about anti-party currents and the position of underground opposition groups, without, however, initially bringing me fully into the picture.

They gave me a characterization of the Zinovievist group, assessing it in the most negative terms. They assessed the Trotskyist group very positively, regarding its positions as the most correct.

In these conversations with me, LUKIN V. was the most active; he sharply criticized the line of the CC of the VKP(b), stating that the best Bolsheviks were being expelled, not allowed into the leadership, and so on.

This entire group spoke sharply of Comrade STALIN.

The group received illegal literature from sources unknown to me. At METELKIN's apartment in the “Astoria,” they gave me a typewritten copy of Tren's platform to read.

I worked with this Trotskyist group for four months.

The testimony has been read to me and recorded correctly from my words—

BURMISTROV.

INTERROGATED BY:

OPERATIONS OFFICER OF THE SPO OF THE GUGB: (FYODOROV)

Certified correct: Kazakova
