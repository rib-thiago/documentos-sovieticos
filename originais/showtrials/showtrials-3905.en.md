INTERROGATION RECORD

Of ANISHEV, Anatoly Isayevich

December 27, 1934.

ANISHEV Anatoly Isayevich, born in 1899, native of Gorky; father a physician; Jewish, USSR citizen; resides in Leningrad, Lesnoy Avenue, house 37, block II, apartment 47. Head of a detachment of the Tajik Comprehensive Agricultural Expedition of the Academy of Agricultural Sciences. Son: Yury ANISHEV, aged 16, in Leningrad, and son Dmitry BELYAEV in Moscow; mother, sister, and brother in Moscow. Secondary education, non-Party, formerly a member of the All-Union Communist Party (Bolsheviks) since 1919; expelled from the Party by the Fifteenth Congress for factional work. Reinstated by TsKK-UZ in August 1928. 2) During the 1933 purge, expelled from the Party “for connection with his Trotskyist wife.” Arrested under Kolchak in 1918. Removed from the military register. From December 1918 to June 1919, served in Kolchak's army as a praporshchik by mobilization. Simultaneously maintained contact with the underground Party organization (according to his statement).

QUESTION: The investigation has information about the existence in Leningrad of a counterrevolutionary Zinovievist organization and that you are a member of this organization. Do you confirm this?

ANSWER: Yes, I confirm it. The counterrevolutionary Zinovievist organization of which I was a member took shape on the basis of the practical work that we former oppositionists carried out from the moment of our reinstatement in the Party, toward consolidating Zinovievist cadres with the aim of preserving ourselves within the Party as an independent force. Among members of the organization personally known to me, I can name RUMYANTSEV Vladimir, KOTOLYNOV Ivan, LEVIN Vladimir, MANDELSHTAM Sergey, TSEITLIN Yakov, and BASHKIROV. I was personally connected with these persons on matters of the counterrevolutionary organization. The organization also included those arrested last year: ANTONEVICH, ALEKSANDROV, and NATANSON, with whom I was personally connected.

QUESTION: What do you know about the Moscow center of the counterrevolutionary Zinovievist counterrevolutionary organization?

ANSWER: I know that the center of the counterrevolutionary Zinovievist organization has been preserved in Moscow since the time of the Fifteenth Congress. Among members of the center personally known to me, I can name EVDOKIMOV, BAKAEV, KUKLIN, and GERTIK; I was personally connected with these persons for a number of years on matters of the counterrevolutionary organization until October 1933. I also know that these members of the center are organizationally and politically connected with ZINOVIEV and KAMENEV. I was also personally connected with SAFAROV. Whether SAFAROV belonged to this center, I do not know. I know SAFAROV's anti-Party counterrevolutionary sentiments.

QUESTION: Which participants in the Leningrad counterrevolutionary Zinovievist organization maintained contact with the Moscow center?

ANSWER: I have already testified about my connection with a member of the Moscow center, which I maintained for a number of years. I know from KOTOLYNOV himself that in the autumn of 1933 he was in Moscow and met with a member of the center. While in Moscow, he also met with GERTSBERG and PLOZDEEVA. The latter are closely associated with members of the Moscow center. It seems to me that RUMYANTSEV also maintained contact with the Moscow center. Individual leading persons of the Leningrad organization, in particular LEVIN, BASHKIROV, and myself, made contact with GERTIK, who visited Leningrad in 1932 or 1933. Mariya NATANSON, until her arrest in early 1933, also maintained contact with members of the Moscow center, BAKAEV, EVDOKIMOV, and KUKLIN. She also maintained contact with SAFAROV. I shall give additional, more detailed testimony about the activity of the Leningrad organization and the Moscow center.

Taken down correctly from my words and read by me — ANISHEV.

INTERROGATED BY: Lulov

Certified true: Shugayev
