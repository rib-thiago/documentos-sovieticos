INTERROGATION RECORD

OF FEDOROV, Grigory Fedorovich, January 10, 1935.

QUESTION: Which members of the Zinovievist organization in Moscow do you know?

ANSWER: Apart from the Zinovievist organization's Moscow center, which existed until the most recent times and whose members until their arrest were ZINOVIEV, KAMENEV, EVDOKIMOV, BAKAEV, SHAROV, KUKLIN, GERTIK, KHARITONOV, and myself — FEDOROV — I also knew the following members of our organization in Moscow: GORSHENIN and NAUMOV, extremely active members of our organization closely connected with the center; GORSHENIN also remained closely connected with the leaders of our organization — ZINOVIEV — until recently. In addition, BRAVO, PERIMOV, KOSTINA, KOZHURO, and SERGEEV Aleksandr belonged to the Zinovievist organization in Moscow.

QUESTION: Which members of the organization outside the capital do you know?

ANSWER: I knew of the existence of the Zinovievist organization in Leningrad, which included LEVIN Vladimir, RUMYANTSEV, MUSHTAKOV, ALEKSANDROV, and SOLOVYOV. In addition, extremely active members of our organization outside the capital were GESSEN in Smolensk, RAVICH Olga in Voronezh, and ELKOVICH in the Urals.

Recorded faithfully from my words, read by me —

FEDOROV

INTERROGATED BY:

HEAD OF SECTION 3 OF THE EKO OF THE GUGB OF THE NKVD OF THE USSR — CHERTOK

Certified true: Khvatov
