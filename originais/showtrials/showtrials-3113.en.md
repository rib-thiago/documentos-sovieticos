RECORD

of the supplementary interrogation of LEVIN, Vladimir Solomonovich.

December 16, 1934.

QUESTION: Tell us what you know about the work of the counterrevolutionary Zinovievist organization in 1933–1934.

ANSWER: At the beginning of 1933, in connection with the Party purge, ZINOVIEV's exile, GERTIK's arrest, and talk of my impending arrest, I had to curtail the counterrevolutionary organization's work somewhat; at that time I restricted my contacts with MYASNIKOV, BASHKIROV, and KORSHUNOV. I could maintain contact with RUMYANTSEV through MYASNIKOV. In 1934 BASHKIROV occasionally visited my apartment, and I met MYASNIKOV at the Leningrad Soviet. In July or August 1934 a meeting took place at my apartment, attended by ZILBERMAN Aleksandr, DMITRIEV Timofey, BASHKIROV, and KORSHUNOV. At this meeting we discussed ZINOVIEV's statement to the Party Central Committee. All those present considered that ZINOVIEV had to submit the statement in order to return to the Party, since with his return we would be able to continue the struggle to bring ZINOVIEV and KAMENEV into the Party leadership.

QUESTION: The investigation has information that the leading core of the counterrevolutionary Zinovievist organization discussed replacing the leadership of the All-Union Communist Party (Bolsheviks)?

ANSWER: We discussed replacing the leadership of the All-Union Communist Party (Bolsheviks) on numerous occasions. Throughout the period from 1927 to 1934, the question was posed as the need to return to the old Leninist Politburo without TROTSKY, to create a Politburo ranging from STALIN to BUKHARIN and RYKOV, and, more recently, to bring ZINOVIEV and KAMENEV into the Politburo. At various times, I, MYASNIKOV, RUMYANTSEV, BASHKIROV, SOSITSKY, MANDELSHTAM Sergey, and KORSHUNOV took part in discussing this question. At the meetings, I and the persons named concluded that we had to wage an active struggle to bring ZINOVIEV and KAMENEV into the Party leadership.

QUESTION: For what purpose did you preserve the discovered archive of the counterrevolutionary Zinovievist organization, typed by you, at EMELYANOV Kondraty's home at Razliv station?

ANSWER: I preserved the archive in order to use it in the struggle against the Party.

Recorded correctly from my words; read aloud to me. —

LEVIN.
