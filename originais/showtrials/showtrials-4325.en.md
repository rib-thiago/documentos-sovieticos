CONFRONTATION RECORD

between the accused Alexander Vladimirovich GERTSBERG and Alexander Sergeevich KUKLIN,

conducted on January 7, 1935.

A. V. GERTSBERG and A. S. KUKLIN, summoned to the confrontation, after mutually acknowledging their personal acquaintance, testified:

Question to the accused A. V. GERTSBERG: What do you know about Alexander Sergeevich KUKLIN's membership in the Zinovievist organization and his role in it?

Answer by GERTSBERG: During the open struggle against the Party, KUKLIN belonged to the Zinovievist organization's leadership. He retained ties with this organization's leadership, thereby belonging to the center of the Zinovievist organization that existed until recently.

Question to A. S. KUKLIN: Do you confirm A. V. GERTSBERG's testimony?

Answer by KUKLIN: Yes, I confirm A. V. GERTSBERG's testimony.

Question to A. V. GERTSBERG: Tell the investigation about your meeting and conversation with A. S. KUKLIN in 1934 during the Seventeenth Party Congress.

Answer by GERTSBERG: During the Seventeenth Party Congress I met A. S. KUKLIN, who, in reply to my question about the Congress, referred to it as “Stalinists' chatter,” making a hostile attack on the Party leadership.

Question to A. S. KUKLIN: Do you confirm this testimony by GERTSBERG?

Answer by KUKLIN: I told GERTSBERG: “They are singing the Party leadership's praises”; I did not speak about the rest.

KUKLIN.                                                                  GERTSBERG.

Confrontation conducted by:

ASSISTANT HEAD, SPO, GUGB — RUTKOVSKY

HEAD OF THE 4th SECTION, SPO, UGB — KOGAN
