INTERROGATION RECORD

Of SHIRYAEV Dmitry Nikitich, on the 27th \[1\] of 1934.

QUESTION: At the interrogation on December 16 of this year, you named SAKHOV as a member of the Zinovievist organization known to you. What can you testify about him?

ANSWER: I have known SAKHOV as a member of the counterrevolutionary Zinovievist organization for the last two to two and a half years. When visiting EVDOKIMOV (I have already testified that I frequently went to Moscow on official business and stayed with EVDOKIMOV), I constantly met SAKHOV at his apartment, both alone and together with GESSEN, BAKAEV, KUKLIN, and other Zinovievists. In late 1932 or early 1933, I was present during a conversation between SAKHOV and EVDOKIMOV at the latter's apartment, in which SAKHOV informed EVDOKIMOV that rumors were circulating in the Procuracy, where he worked, that he was to be transferred to the provinces as a person suspected of connections with the Zinovievists, particularly EVDOKIMOV. Both were greatly concerned about the proposed transfer and made arrangements to the effect that it had to be avoided at any cost. SAKHOV is so close to EVDOKIMOV and GESSEN that they constantly visit one another and are entirely frank with each other.

The record has been taken down from my words and read by me.

SHIRYAEV.

Interrogated by: authorized officer of the SPO of the GUGB of the USSR NKVD — ZAIKO.

Certified true: Signature

Certified true: Polunina
