Testimony on the substance of the case:

QUESTION: Which participants in the so-called Zinovievist opposition do you know?

ANSWER: I do not directly know any former oppositionist Zinovievists, since I personally did not belong to the opposition. From October 1925 to November 1926 I was on active military service in the Belorussian Military District and was not in Leningrad during that period.

QUESTION: With which participants in the opposition did you come into contact through work after returning to Leningrad?

ANSWER: In 1932, through working together in the Proletstud of the Machine-Building Union, I met the former oppositionist Zinovievist PANFILOV Mikhail, who was then chairman of the Proletstud bureau. I learned that PANFILOV was an oppositionist from his personal statement at the cell bureau.

In April 1933 I met through work the former oppositionist SOKOLOV Georgy, who worked in the bureau of the “trade-union thousand.”

QUESTION: Do you know the former oppositionist ZVEZDOV?

ANSWER: Yes, I have known ZVEZDOV since 1933. We became acquainted at the plenum of the student section, but I learned that ZVEZDOV was an oppositionist after his arrest.

QUESTION: Which of the persons you named do you know on a home-visiting basis and have met in a private setting?

ANSWER: In the summer of 1934, together with my friend LOBANOV, I visited PANFILOV's apartment to listen to the gramophone. After staying there about two hours, we left. PANFILOV has never been in my home. I am not on a home-visiting basis with ZVEZDOV and SOKOLOV and have not met them in a private setting.

QUESTION: The investigation knows that during your visit to PANFILOV you had conversations with him of an anti-Party character. Do you confirm this?

ANSWER: I deny it, since I never had anti-Party conversations with PANFILOV.

QUESTION: The investigation knows that in your contacts with ZVEZDOV and SOKOLOV you also had conversations with them of an anti-Party character. Do you confirm this?

ANSWER: I deny it, since I spoke with ZVEZDOV and SOKOLOV only on work-related subjects.

I have read the record; it was correctly recorded from my words.

K. APANASEVICH.

INTERROGATED BY —

ASSISTANT HEAD OF THE 1ST SECTION OF THE OO OF THE GUGB — BRICCI.
