INTERROGATION RECORD

OF KOTOLYNOV [1], Ivan Ivanovich

12 December 1934.

QUESTION: Do you acknowledge that you returned to the Party for a double-dealing purpose, on ZINOVIEV's directive which you read and discussed at V. RUMYANTSEV's apartment in July 1928?

ANSWER: Yes, I acknowledge it.

QUESTION: What do you know of the existing counter-revolutionary Zinovievist organisation and its activity?

ANSWER: I know that the establishment of systematic contacts among former participants in the Zinovievist-Trotskyist bloc in Leningrad actually led to the creation of a counter-revolutionary organisation. In particular, I was connected with Vladimir RUMYANTSEV, whom I periodically met to exchange information. I know that contact with Moscow was maintained. In December 1933 I personally met I. TARASOV and FAIVILOVICH at their apartments in Moscow. On one evening during my stay in Moscow, the three of us gathered at FAIVILOVICH's (I. TARASOV, myself — KOTOLYNOV — and FAIVILOVICH). First we exchanged opinions about how each of us had passed the purge, and then the conversation turned to the Party leadership. In the course of the conversation all of us made extremely malicious counter-revolutionary attacks on Comrade STALIN. After this, I do not remember how the transition came about, we discussed who could replace Comrade STALIN. It was mentioned that, although KAGANOVICH was not a bad organiser, he did not enjoy the Party's confidence and could not be admitted to its leadership.

In connection with this discussion of replacing Comrade STALIN, FAIVILOVICH named ZINOVIEV, saying that he — ZINOVIEV — could become the Party's leader. In addition, the possibility of using BAKAEV, EVDOKIMOV and KHARITONOV in leading Party work was raised.

On returning from Moscow, still in December, I called at V. RUMYANTSEV's apartment, told him I had seen I. TARASOV and FAIVILOVICH, and informed him of the content of my conversation with them in Moscow. TOLMAZOV was present during this conversation with RUMYANTSEV.

QUESTION: Whom do you know among the persons connected with V. RUMYANTSEV through the counter-revolutionary organisation?

ANSWER: I know the following persons who were constantly connected with RUMYANTSEV on matters of the counter-revolutionary organisation: Andrei Ilyich TOLMAZOV, Sergei MANDELSHTAM and Nikolai ANTONOV. Fedor FADEEV seems to visit him too.

KOTOLYNOV

Interrogated by:

DEPUTY HEAD OF THE SPO, GUGB — LYUSHKOV

HEAD OF THE 6TH SECTION, SPO, GUGB — KORKIN
