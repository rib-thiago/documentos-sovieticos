INTERROGATION RECORD

OF RUMYANTSEV, Vladimir Vasilievich

dated 9 December 1934.

QUESTION: Which former participants in the counter-revolutionary Zinovievist-Trotskyist bloc did you meet during your stay at the Kislovodsk resort in 1934?

ANSWER: In late September or early October 1934, while taking a holiday in Kislovodsk, I happened to meet ROTSKAN, a former Zinovievist, in the Pyatachok area. Some time later, Andrei Andreevich MUSHTAKOV arrived from Leningrad with his wife at the Leningrad Soviet sanatorium where I was staying. I had not previously known that MUSHTAKOV was coming.

QUESTION: What conversations did you have with A. A. MUSHTAKOV and ROTSKAN during your meetings in Kislovodsk?

ANSWER: I met MUSHTAKOV very often, since we lived in the same sanatorium. MUSHTAKOV frequently drank, which led to occasional incidents involving him. MUSHTAKOV and I discussed material we had read in the newspapers. Our conversations were consistently sound from a Party standpoint, and I observed no unhealthy sentiments in Party terms on MUSHTAKOV's part. When I met ROTSKAN, he told me that Zinoviev and Kamenev were in Kislovodsk at the Turgenev dacha and suggested that I visit them. ROTSKAN took my address and promised to call on me; at the same time he gave me his address (the sanatorium's), I think the “Tenth Anniversary of October” sanatorium.

QUESTION: Did your meeting with Zinoviev and Kamenev take place?

ANSWER: No, since I did not go to their dacha.

QUESTION: What, in your opinion, might have prompted the suggestion conveyed by ROTSKAN to visit Zinoviev and Kamenev at their dacha, and do you consider the suggestion accidental?

ANSWER: I believe the invitation was not accidental and was probably prompted by Zinoviev and Kamenev's desire to sound out the mood of people from Leningrad.

QUESTION: Did you meet ROTSKAN after this conversation?

ANSWER: No, I did not meet him again, either in Kislovodsk or in Leningrad.

The record has been correctly taken down from my words and read by me.

V. RUMYANTSEV

INTERROGATED BY:

DEPUTY HEAD OF THE SPO, GUGB — LYUSHKOV

HEAD OF THE 1ST SECTION, SPO, GUGB — PETROVSKY
