Top secret

TO THE SECRETARY OF THE CENTRAL COMMITTEE OF THE ALL-UNION COMMUNIST PARTY (BOLSHEVIKS) —

Comrade STALIN

On the night of December 8–9, searches of apartments were conducted and the following persons were arrested:

1\. GERTIK Artyom Moiseyevich, born in 1879, a Party member since 1902 (with interruptions from October 1927 to June 1928 and from February 1933 to August 1934). Head of a sector in a department of the Scientific and Technical Publishing House.

The search seized: counterrevolutionary materials from the Zinovievists' earlier struggle against the Party; anti-Party theses in GERTIK's handwriting dating from 1927, and retyped stenographic transcripts of speeches by ZINOVIEV and EVDOKIMOV from 1926.

2\. FEDOROV Grigory Fedorovich, born in 1891. A Party member since 1907. Expelled from the Party by the Fifteenth Congress and reinstated in 1928. Director of the NKTP Cartographic Trust.

The search seized: letters from ZINOVIEV to FEDOROV dating from 1928 concerning the sending of information materials to ZINOVIEV in Kaluga, and a stenographic transcript of speeches by Zinovievists before Leningrad metalworkers in 1926.

3\. EVDOKIMOV Grigory Yeremeyevich, born in 1884. A Party member since 1903, with an interruption from December 1927 to July 1928. Head of the Soyuzmoloko association of the People's Commissariat of the Food Industry.

The search seized: the opposition platform of 1927, the anti-Party document “On Socialist Accumulation,” anti-Party notes, and addresses of Zinovievists and Trotskyists. In addition, Trotskyist documents — a platform and leaflets from 1927 — were found in EVDOKIMOV's desk; according to EVDOKIMOV's statement, these belong to his son-in-law TARASOV Pyotr Nikitich, an active Leningrad Trotskyist now working in the Kuzbass and expelled from the Party a second time for double-dealing.

4\. PERIMOV Aleksey Viktorovich, a Party member since 1917, expelled in 1927 and reinstated in 1928. Head of construction of a glass-container factory in Central Asia.

Nothing was found in the search.

5\. BAKAEV Ivan Petrovich, a Party member since 1906, expelled by the Fifteenth Party Congress in 1927 and reinstated in 1928.

The search found and seized: the opposition platform, a leaflet of the united opposition bloc from 1927, VChK circulars and directives from 1919, stenographic transcripts of Politburo meetings from 1924–1925, and other materials.

6\. ZALUTSKY Pyotr Antonovich, born in 1887, a Party member since 1913, expelled by the Fifteenth Party Congress in 1927 and reinstated in 1928. Manager of the Soyuzpromstroymashina Trust.

Anti-Party materials from 1926 were found in the search.

GESSEN Sergey was arrested in Smolensk and brought to Moscow.

An order was sent by telegraph to Orenburg to search the home of the exiled Trotskyist PANKRATOV Vasily Fedorovich, arrest him, and send him urgently to Moscow.
