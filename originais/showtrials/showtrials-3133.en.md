INTERROGATION RECORD

OF KOTOLYNOV, Ivan Ivanovich,

dated December 17, 1934.

QUESTION: Did RUMYANTSEV provide you with material assistance, and what kind exactly?

ANSWER: Yes, in 1932 and at the beginning of 1933 I received from V. RUMYANTSEV on several occasions a card for the district committee's dining hall, each time valid for one or two months.

I received no other material assistance from RUMYANTSEV V.

KOTOLYNOV.

INTERROGATED BY:

HEAD OF SECTION 6 OF THE SPO — KORKIN.

CERTIFIED TRUE: illegible
