INTERROGATION RECORD

Of FEDOROV Grigory Fedorovich

December 30, 1934.

(Supplementary).

Question: In your previous testimony, you stated that your organization maintained contact with members of the same organization in other cities. Relate all the facts known to you about the methods used to maintain these contacts.

Answer: Before the Fifteenth Congress, the Zinovievist-Trotskyist organization had a system of organizational contacts with a number of local organizations. This entire system was preserved and continued to function after the Fifteenth Congress as well. In doing so, account was taken of the instructions given at a meeting of the center of the Zinovievist organization after the congress, with the aim of concealing these contacts. As before, maintaining contact with the local organizations was entrusted to NAUMOV, GERTIK, and KHARITONOV.

I know that contact with the Leningrad organization was maintained directly by NAUMOV. He was connected with the leading members of the Leningrad organization, LEVIN Vladimir and RUMYANTSEV Vladimir. KHARITONOV and GERTIK were connected with the other local organizations. I do not now remember precisely how I learned of NAUMOV's connection with the leading members of the Leningrad organization, LEVIN and RUMYANTSEV, but I can only point out that this was known to many members of our organization.

Besides NAUMOV, who was connected with Leningrad, KHARITONOV and GERTIK were connected with the Urals (ELKOVICH), Voronezh (GESSEN and Olga RAVICH), Vyatka (BAKAEV and OLKHOVSKY), Saratov (ZALUTSKY), Ulyanovsk (EVDOKIMOV and SHAROV), and Kharkov (myself, FEDOROV).

Contact was also maintained with a number of other places, but I do not now remember those places.

The central places to which representatives of the local organizations came were the apartments of GERTIK and NAUMOV. After ZINOVIEV and KAMENEV arrived in Moscow from Kaluga, their apartments were also brought into use for receiving members of the organization arriving from the localities.

As I have already testified, contacts with those arriving from the localities were maintained chiefly by NAUMOV, KHARITONOV, and GERTIK. If the information brought by someone from a locality was interesting and important from the standpoint of our organization, or if an active member of the organization arrived, he was directed to ZINOVIEV or KAMENEV through the aforementioned NAUMOV, GERTIK, and KHARITONOV.

Taken down correctly from my words and read by me. —

FEDOROV.

INTERROGATED BY: HEAD OF THE 3RD SECTION OF THE SPECIAL DEPARTMENT OF THE GUGB OF THE USSR NKVD, CHERTOK.
