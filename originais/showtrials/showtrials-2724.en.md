INTERROGATION RECORD

conducted by LYUSHKOV, Deputy Head of the SPO, GUGB, NKVD of the USSR, and

FEDOROV, operational officer of the SPO, GUGB, NKVD.

10 December 1934.

OF YUSKIN, Ignaty Grigorievich.

QUESTION: The investigation has information that, in response to NIKOLAEV's statement about preparing a terrorist act against Comrade KIROV, you answered him: “Why KIROV! It is STALIN [who must be killed].”

ANSWER: Yes, I did indeed answer NIKOLAEV: “Why KIROV? It is STALIN [who must be killed].”

I have read the testimony; it has been correctly taken down from my words.

YUSKIN.

Interrogated by:

Deputy Head of the SPO, GUGB, NKVD of the USSR, LYUSHKOV

Operational officer of the SPO, GUGB, NKVD, FEDOROV.

Certified true: illegible

Triangular seal of the Commandant's Office of the NKVD Directorate for Leningrad Region.
