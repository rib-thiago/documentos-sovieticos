INTERROGATION RECORD

OF FYODOROV, Grigory Fyodorovich, January 2, 1935.

(supplementary)

QUESTION: The investigation has established that until recently you kept the archive of the Zinovievist organization. Do you confirm this?

ANSWER: Until approximately the beginning of 1934, a number of counterrevolutionary documents of the Zinovievist-Trotskyist organization dating from the period before the Fifteenth Congress remained at my old apartment (Moscow, Staro-Pimenovsky Lane, building 7-b, apartment 16), where I lived until 1931. All these documents were in a folder approximately twenty centimeters thick. The folder was wrapped in newspaper and tied with string. Having collected these documents from the old apartment around the beginning of 1934, I intended to destroy them, but upon entering a dining room I met KUKLIN Aleksandr Sergeyevich there and told him that I had just collected the organization's old documents from the apartment and intended to destroy them. KUKLIN asked me not to destroy these documents but to leave them with him, pointing out that “these documents will be useful for history.”

As far as I now remember, among these documents were the opposition platform and a number of other documents published by the Zinovievist-Trotskyist organization before the Fifteenth Congress. Several documents were in two or three copies. All these documents belonged to me personally and had remained with me since their publication, that is, since before the Fifteenth Congress. After handing these documents to KUKLIN, neither I nor he spoke to the other about them again.

QUESTION: The investigation has established that what you handed to KUKLIN was not your personal archive, as you testify, but an archive belonging to ZINOVIEV. Moreover, you gave the archive to KUKLIN not at his request, but asked him to take it for temporary safekeeping. Do you confirm this?

ANSWER: No, I do not. I repeat that the documents I handed over belonged to me. I gave the documents to KUKLIN not for temporary safekeeping but for permanent use, in accordance with his request.

Recorded correctly from my words; read by me.

FYODOROV.

INTERROGATED BY:

SECTION HEAD OF THE EKO OF THE GUGB OF THE NKVD OF THE USSR—CHERTOK.

Certified correct: A. Svetlova
