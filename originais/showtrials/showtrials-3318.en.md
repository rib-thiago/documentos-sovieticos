RECORD

of a confrontation between the accused POPOV, Georgy Fedorovich, and SHATSKY, Nikolai Nikolaevich, conducted on December 18, 1934. —

Summoned for the confrontation, the accused G. F. POPOV and N. N. SHATSKY, after acknowledging personal acquaintance with one another, testified:

QUESTION TO POPOV: Do you know of N. N. SHATSKY's membership in the counterrevolutionary Zinovievist-Trotskyist organization?

POPOV'S ANSWER: Yes, I know that SHATSKY was a member of the counterrevolutionary Zinovievist-Trotskyist organization.

QUESTION TO SHATSKY: Do you confirm this testimony by POPOV?

SHATSKY'S ANSWER: I deny POPOV's testimony.

QUESTION TO POPOV: Describe what you know about N. SHATSKY's terrorist intentions.

POPOV'S ANSWER: In February or March 1934 I met SHATSKY at a meeting of the association of former comrades-in-arms which, as I previously testified, was used by the counterrevolutionary Zinovievist-Trotskyist organization as cover for counterrevolutionary work.

After the meeting, in conversation with me, SHATSKY expressed sharply hostile sentiments toward the Party leaders. SHATSKY said that “the Party leadership oppresses the Party”, that the Seventeenth Congress decisions on organizational questions actually meant “the apparatus's victory over the Party”, and similar counterrevolutionary fabrications. SHATSKY's malicious attacks on the Party leaders were terrorist in character.

QUESTION TO SHATSKY: Do you confirm G. POPOV's testimony?

SHATSKY'S ANSWER: I confirm that in February or March 1934 I did indeed meet POPOV at a meeting of the association of former comrades-in-arms, and that I did have a conversation with him after the meeting. I deny, however, the character of the conversation as described by POPOV in his testimony.

POPOV.                                             SHATSKY.

INTERROGATED BY:

HEAD OF THE EKO OF THE GUGB OF THE USSR NKVD — MIRONOV.

DEPUTY HEAD OF THE SPO OF THE GUGB — LYUSHKOV.

HEAD OF THE 6TH SECTION OF THE SPO OF THE GUGB — KORKIN.
