INTERROGATION RECORD

OF KUKLIN Aleksandr Sergeevich (supplementary)

dated January 14, 1935.

I ask the investigation to supplement my previous testimony with the following facts:

I acknowledge that after the Fifteenth Party Congress, expulsion from the Party, and subsequent reinstatement, participants in the former Zinovievist center began to group around ZINOVIEV and KAMENEV: EVDOKIMOV, BAKAEV, myself, KUKLIN, FEDOROV, SHAROV, KHARITONOV, GESSEN, and others.

Throughout these years we gathered in varying combinations at ZINOVIEV's and KAMENEV's apartments and, after their exile, at GORSHENIN's, and discussed questions of the Central Committee's policy, criticizing this policy in the spirit of the directives of the Zinovievist-Trotskyist bloc's platform of 1925-1927.

Of our principal positions I remember the following:

in the construction of industrial enterprises, we believed that they took a long time to build and cost the country too much. We said that capital invested in construction was immobilized because of a shortage of building materials, that consequently there was no effect, and that all this lay as a heavy burden on the population.

We all also believed that self-criticism produced no results, since criticism was possible only from above downward, and if anyone dared criticize from below upward, he was removed from his job or transferred somewhere.

We also said that the Party leadership selected for responsible posts people with no opinions of their own, who subsequently proved to be crooks and adventurers, while tried comrades were pushed into the background.

We members of the center in turn conveyed ZINOVIEV's and KAMENEV's directives for criticizing the Central Committee's policy to our associates; I, for example, during meetings with GERTSBERG and, as far as I remember, in Leningrad with RUMYANTSEV Vladimir and others.

KUKLIN.

INTERROGATED BY: RUTKOVSKY

Certified correct: Kazakova
