INTERROGATION RECORD

OF NIKOLAEV L. V., dated 5/XII-34.

Question: Under what circumstances did you meet the woman employed at the Mint, and how did you intend to make use of that acquaintance?

Answer: This was in November 1934. While preparing to carry out a terrorist attack against KIROV, I was watching his movements and, I do not remember the exact date, I was on the boulevard near the Peter and Paul Fortress, having chosen that place as an observation post. An unfamiliar woman walked past me. She noticed that I was staring at her and approached me. I made her acquaintance and we arranged another meeting; that meeting took place two days later, and I talked with her about various family and everyday matters. Our next meeting was to take place the following day, but did not happen; someone interfered with us. This woman turned out to be an employee of the Mint at the Peter and Paul Fortress. I wanted to establish contact with her and subsequently use her help in observing KIROV’s movements. Description of the woman: medium height, stout, wearing a short fur coat, black shoes and galoshes, about 40 years old. I never saw this woman again. –

L. NIKOLAEV.

INTERROGATED BY:

DEPUTY PEOPLE’S COMMISSAR FOR INTERNAL AFFAIRS – AGRANOV

ASSISTANT CHIEF OF THE EKO GUGB – DMITRIEV
