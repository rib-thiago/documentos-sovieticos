INTERROGATION RECORD

OF REM, Mikhail Simonovich

January 2, 1935.

QUESTION: In your testimony of December 22, you named YAKOVLEV Moisei as a member of the counterrevolutionary Zinovievist organization. Describe his activity in the organization in detail.

ANSWER: Personally, I had little contact with M. YAKOVLEV and therefore do not know his activity in detail. As I have already testified, in August or September 1928, V. LEVIN held a meeting at his apartment where duties were allocated among the members of the counterrevolutionary organization. YAKOVLEV was not at this meeting, but V. LEVIN named him as the organization's representative for the Vasileostrovsky district.

QUESTION: For what purpose did you and SHURYGIN go to YAKOVLEV's apartment in the summer of 1928?

ANSWER: I did not visit YAKOVLEV's apartment in 1928 and did not meet him personally in those years. I happened to meet YAKOVLEV at the theater in 1934; he asked why I did not visit him, and the conversation broke off there.

QUESTION: What do you know about YAKOVLEV's trip to Kaluga to see ZINOVIEV?

ANSWER: I know nothing and have heard nothing. Until July 1928, I was in Voronezh. From a letter by ZINOVIEV to O. RAVICH, I know that ROTSKAN visited ZINOVIEV in Kaluga; RAVICH told me this.

QUESTION: Do you know LEONTYEV and DORONIN, and are they members of the counterrevolutionary Zinovievist organization?

ANSWER: I know LEONTYEV, Aleksei Leontyevich, and DORONIN Aleksandr well. Before the Fifteenth Congress, they actively spoke against the party, but after the congress, it seemed to me that both sincerely adopted the party line. I am not aware of their having conducted any work against the party after the congress. I told neither LEONTYEV nor DORONIN about my work, particularly my participation in illegal meetings. I recall that DORONIN A., at my request, brought to me the worker SEMYONOV, whom I was supposed to contact on V. LEVIN's instructions. I did not tell DORONIN that I was to meet SEMYONOV on an assignment received at the meeting.

LEONTYEV lives in Leningrad, and DORONIN Aleksandr in the Valdai district of L\<eningrad\> Reg\<ion\>; his family lives in Leningrad.

REM.

INTERROGATED BY—KORKIN.

Certified correct: Kazakova
