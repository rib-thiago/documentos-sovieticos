INTERROGATION RECORD

OF SOROKIN, Vsevolod Alekseyevich (supplementary).

January 2, 1935.

QUESTION: The investigation has information that in conversations with certain persons you expressed a wish to go abroad illegally. What can you testify on this question?

ANSWER: I consider it my duty to state to the investigation that I may have expressed a wish to go abroad, where I could make more successful use of my abilities. As far as I remember, I expressed this idea in Moscow to the mother of the Zinovievist KHANIK, having had a little to drink. I attach no serious significance to this, since beforehand I had spoken at the CC of the VLKSM about my future work, because the work I was doing at that time did not satisfy me and, moreover, I wished to study to acquire qualifications.

Apart from KHANIK's mother, I did not express to any of my acquaintances a wish to go abroad.

QUESTION: What do you know about KHANIK's connections in the city of Kronstadt?

ANSWER: I know that KHANIK was closely connected with Boris YAROV, who worked as deputy \<secretary\> of the VKP(b) District Committee in Kronstadt. In conversation with me, KHANIK described YAROV as one of his own and a good worker.

Recorded correctly and read by me, which I confirm by signing.

V. SOROKIN.

INTERROGATED BY:

OPERATIONS OFFICER OF THE 1ST SECTION OF THE SPO OF THE UNKVD OF LENINGRAD REGION—MAKAROV.

CERTIFIED CORRECT: Kazakova
