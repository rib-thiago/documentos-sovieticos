STENOGRAPHIC TRANSCRIPT

INTERROGATION RECORD

OF NIKOLAEV Leonid Vasilyevich

14 December 1934.

QUESTION: In the interrogation record of 13 December of this year you testified that the persons you named in that record belonged to the counterrevolutionary Zinovievist organization; explain how you learned that these persons actually belonged to the counterrevolutionary organization.

ANSWER: KOTOLYNOV, SHATSKY, YUSKIN and SOKOLOV personally conversed with me about the counterrevolutionary organization's activities, the direction of its work, their role in the organization and individual participants; of these, SHATSKY, SOKOLOV and KOTOLYNOV recruited me into the organization.

As for those listed in my interrogation record — RUMYANTSEV, ZVEZDOV, ANTONOV, KHANIK Lev, TOLMAZOV, ALEKSANDROV, POPOV G. and TSARKOV — I learned of their participation in the organization from those named in the answer to the first question: KOTOLYNOV, SHATSKY, SOKOLOV and YUSKIN.

RUMYANTSEV — I never spoke with him about the counterrevolutionary organization's work; I knew of his participation in the organization, and a leading participation at that, mainly from SHATSKY's words. RUMYANTSEVAV, formerly secretary of the Komsomol Regional Committee in Leningrad, is an authoritative and influential member of the organization. \[1\] Although RUMYANTSEV belonged to the terrorist group headed by SHATSKY, the latter mainly used only his connections and position. RUMYANTSEV worked as secretary of the Vyborg District Soviet. This post enabled him to provide material assistance to individual organization members and former oppositionists.

I know from SHATSKY's words that he was to involve RUMYANTSEV, who has connections among former oppositionists in Moscow, in organizing and carrying out the terrorist act against STALIN.

ZVEZDOV Vasily — I know of his membership in the counterrevolutionary organization from SOKOLOV's words; SOKOLOV studied with him at the Industrial Institute for a time; he is a former oppositionist. ZVEZDOV carried out organizational work together with KOTOLYNOV, who headed the entire group of organization members studying at the Institute.

ANTONOV — I know of his membership in the counterrevolutionary organization from the same SOKOLOV's words. ANTONOV studied at the same Institute and conducts counterrevolutionary work in KOTOLYNOV's group. I never had occasion to discuss counterrevolutionary work with ANTONOV.

KHANIK Lev, TOLMAZOV, ALEKSANDROV, POPOV G. and TSARKOV — KOTOLYNOV told me of their membership in the counterrevolutionary organization.

Correctly dictated from my words; I have read the record.

(L. NIKOLAEV).

DEPUTY PEOPLE'S COMMISSAR OF INTERNAL AFFAIRS OF THE USSR — AGRANOV

HEAD OF THE EKO, GUGB, NKVD OF THE USSR — MIRONOV
