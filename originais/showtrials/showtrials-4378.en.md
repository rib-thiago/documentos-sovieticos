INTERROGATION RECORD

OF SHAROV Yakov Vasilyevich

dated January 10, 1935.

QUESTION: What additional information can you give the investigation about the Zinovievist organization's activity?

ANSWER: In the summer of 1933 I learned from one of the employees of the People's Commissariat of Light Industry (I do not now remember exactly whom) about events among the textile workers of the Ivanovo region. I informed EVDOKIMOV of this in detail. We both concluded that these events confirmed our views about the continuing deterioration in the working masses' material and living conditions as a result of the inept and incorrect policy of the Party leadership.

In August or September 1932 I was invited to BAKAEV's apartment, where members of our organization were present: BAKAEV, KOSTINA, GORSHENIN, KOZHURO, EVDOKIMOV, GERTIK, and myself.

This gathering discussed how to behave at Party meetings devoted to examining RYUTIN's case and ZINOVIEV's expulsion from the Party. It was decided not to speak in ZINOVIEV's defense, since his action could not be justified and, moreover, such statements would only complicate ZINOVIEV's own position.

In my previous testimony of January 7, 1935, I informed the investigation that a center of the Zinovievist organization had existed in Moscow until recently and that I belonged to it until my arrest.

In my previous testimony I did not indicate that GORSHENIN and GERTIK belonged to our organization's Moscow center. I add this now.

Recorded faithfully from my words, read by me.

Ya. SHAROV.

INTERROGATED BY:

HEAD OF SECTION 3 OF THE EKO OF THE GUGB OF THE NKVD OF THE USSR — CHERTOK. —
