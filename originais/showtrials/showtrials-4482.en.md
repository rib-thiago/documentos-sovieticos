INTERROGATION RECORD

OF PERIMOV Alexei Viktorovich

January 14, 1935.

(supplementary)

I ask the investigation to supplement my previous testimony with the following facts:

In the autumn of 1932, while visiting FAIVILOVICH, I told him that, on the question of ZINOVIEV's and KAMENEV's expulsion from the Party, the Zinovievists had decided to speak at Party meetings in defense of the Central Committee's decision. This was already after the campaign on this question. FAIVILOVICH reacted approvingly.

PERIMOV.

INTERROGATED BY — RUTKOVSKY

Certified correct: A. Svetlova

RGASPI, F. 671, Op. 1, D. 136, fol. 27.
