INTERROGATION RECORD

OF NIKOLAEV L. V., dated 5/XII-1934.

Question: Did you see Vanya KOTOLYNOV  \[1\] before the October celebrations of 1934?

Answer: Yes, I met him.

Question: What conversations did you have with him?

Answer: I did not talk with him because, knowing KOTOLYNOV’s views, I believed he would try to persuade me to carry out a terrorist attack against KIROV immediately; I, however, had decided to postpone it until after the October celebrations, so I avoided speaking to him.

Question: Did you involve KOTOLYNOV in preparing and carrying out the terrorist attack against KIROV?

Answer: No.

Question: Why did you not do so, since you knew of his terrorist inclinations?

Answer: I did not involve KOTOLYNOV because, out of my own convictions, I wanted to be the sole perpetrator of the terrorist attack against KIROV; secondly, I believed that KOTOLYNOV would not agree to KIROV’s murder but would demand that we aim higher, that is, carry out a terrorist attack against STALIN, to which I would not have agreed. –

L. NIKOLAEV.

INTERROGATED BY:

DEPUTY HEAD COMM.  \[2\]  INTERNAL AFFAIRS AGRANOV

ASSISTANT CHIEF OF THE EKO GUGB – DMITRIEV

Certified true:

RGASPI, fond 671, inventory 1, file 114, folio 16.

\[1\] Here and throughout the text, erroneously: “Katalynov”.

\[2\] So in the text. “Deputy People’s Commissar” is meant.
