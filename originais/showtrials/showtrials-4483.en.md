INTERROGATION RECORD

OF PERIMOV, Alexei Viktorovich,

January 14, 1935.

(supplementary)

I ask the investigation to supplement my previous testimony with the following fact:

In 1926, on the eve of the publication of SHLYAPNIKOV's and MEDVEDEV's letter renouncing their views, I met SHLYAPNIKOV at GERTIK's apartment. Besides myself, several Zinovievists whose surnames I do not now remember were at GERTIK's.

During the general conversation, while recalling events at the time of the Fourteenth Party Congress, SHLYAPNIKOV addressed the Zinovievists seated beside him with a phrase to the following effect: “Oh, you lot, you had rifles, you had cannon — and you couldn't manage to do anything.” This phrase went unnoticed in the general conversation, but I was deeply struck by it and remembered it.

PERIMOV.

INTERROGATED BY: RUTKOVSKY
