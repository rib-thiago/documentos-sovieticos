INTERROGATION RECORD

OF TOLMAZOV, Andrei Ilyich

13 December 1934.

QUESTION: In your testimony of 12 December of this year you acknowledged that the organization bears responsibility for the terrorist act against Comrade KIROV committed by organization member NIKOLAEV Leonid. Specify how terrorist sentiments arose in your organization.

ANSWER: Terrorist sentiments in the organization were based on hatred of the present party leadership and especially of STALIN personally. It is difficult for me now to remember and tell the investigation specifically who and where, but repeated conversations took place among organization members in which the most malicious epithets were attached to STALIN's name. We regarded the fact that STALIN headed the party leadership as accidental, explaining it by his ability to conceal the country's actual situation from the party through the apparatus. We spoke of other party leaders (KAGANOVICH, KIROV and others) as people who had not belonged to the Politburo under LENIN, whereas ZINOVIEV, KAMENEV and TROTSKY had been members. The organization's attitude toward KAGANOVICH and KIROV therefore also became hostile. In this atmosphere awareness developed and thoughts arose of the need to remove STALIN, KAGANOVICH and KIROV by force. Such things were not spoken of directly aloud, but this was implied, and that was exactly how we understood one another.

QUESTION: Which organization members formulated views on terror most clearly and spoke about it most directly?

ANSWER: RUMYANTSEV spoke about it most clearly. He spoke of it to me personally. Of all the organization members known to me, RUMYANTSEV was precisely the clearest advocate of terrorist methods of action.

The direct outcome of these sentiments was NIKOLAEV's shot at KIROV.

Correctly recorded from my words, in confirmation of which I sign —

A. TOLMAZOV.

INTERROGATED BY:

HEAD OF THE 4TH SECTION OF THE SPO — KOGAN.
