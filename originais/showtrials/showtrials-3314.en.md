INTERROGATION RECORD

OF POTASHNIKOV, GRIGORY PETROVICH, DATED DECEMBER 18, 1934

Continuation

QUESTION: In the record of December 15 of this year, you say that you know a group of former Komsomol workers from the Vyborg district who are members of the counterrevolutionary Zinovievist organization. State whom you know as a member of the counterrevolutionary organization.

ANSWER: Among the members of the counterrevolutionary Zinovievist organization I know Vladimir RUMYANTSEV, Nikolai TSARKOV, Ivan KOTOLYNOV, Fedor FADEEV, Nikolai ANTONOV, Mikhail MURAVYOV, Andrei TOLMAZOV, Mikhail NADEL and SEVEROV.

QUESTION: With which members of the organization were you connected?

ANSWER: I was connected with and met Vladimir RUMYANTSEV and all the above-mentioned members of the organization.

QUESTION: What do you know about the leadership of the Leningrad organization? From whom did you learn that a leading Center existed in Leningrad, and whom do you know as a leader of the organization?

ANSWER: I maintained direct contact with Vladimir RUMYANTSEV. I was also connected with Fedor FADEEV, Mikhail MURAVYOV and Nikolai ANTONOV. I do not know any other people in the leadership of the Leningrad Center.

The record has been written from my words and read by me, in confirmation of which I sign.

POTASHNIKOV

INTERROGATED BY: M. KOGAN

Certified correct: M. Koroleva
