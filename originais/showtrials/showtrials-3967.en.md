INTERROGATION RECORD

(supplementary)

OF ANTIPINA Aleksandra Aleksandrovna—

January 1, 1935.

QUESTION: Describe the circumstances in which you concealed the weapon.

ANSWER: On December 7, 1934, my husband, MANDELSHTAM S.O., informed me that V. RUMYANTSEV had been arrested. I kept, without a permit, a “Parabellum” pistol given to me in 1925 by ANTONOV, who was then working in the GPU. Assuming that MANDELSHTAM's home would also be searched, I decided to hide this pistol to avoid trouble. On December 9, I asked my sister—SOROKINA N.A.—to hide the pistol. SOROKINA took the pistol, together with a package of my correspondence with MANDELSHTAM, to her daughter KURVITS's apartment and hid them there.

QUESTION: What do you know about MANDELSHTAM's connections with former oppositionists?

ANSWER: I know of a number of MANDELSHTAM's connections among former oppositionists. He was friends with RUMYANTSEV. In the summer of 1934, RUMYANTSEV came to our home. During the October celebrations of 1934, MANDELSHTAM visited RUMYANTSEV. In 1932, TARASOV Pyotr, who had come from KUZNETSK, called on MANDELSHTAM. MANDELSHTAM maintained contact with SHURYGIN. During the latter's illness, MANDELSHTAM attended to his affairs. He took part in his funeral. BOGOMOLNY visited me and MANDELSHTAM. I know that MANDELSHTAM maintained relations with MYASNIKOV. In 1930, in Moscow, YELKOVICH called on me. I do not remember the content of my conversation with him. He was on his way to Leningrad. At activists' meetings in 1934, I met TOLMAZOV several times.

ANTIPINA.

INTERROGATED BY: HEAD OF THE 1ST SECTION OF THE SPO—LULOV.

CERTIFIED CORRECT: Kazakova
