INTERROGATION RECORD

of ZALUTSKY, Pyotr Antonovich

January 19, 1935.

QUESTION: How did your ties with the Zinovievist underground develop subsequently, after 1929?

ANSWER: As I testified in the previous interrogation record, I belonged to the Zinovievist underground in 1928 and 1929.

Beginning in 1930, I no longer meet ZINOVIEV and KAMENEV, apart from occasional fleeting encounters in the street, when we had no political conversations.

I withdraw from the Zinovievists. Throughout the subsequent period I retained vestiges of Zinovievism within me, which gave individual Zinovievists whom I met grounds to regard me as someone politically close to the Zinovievists.

For this reason, participants in the Zinovievist underground did not conceal their views from me.

BAKAEV, Ivan Petrovich — meeting him up to the last period, I established that he was a firm supporter of ZINOVIEV. BAKAEV and his wife KOSTINA maintained ties with quite a wide circle of Zinovievists; GORSHENIN and his wife KOZHURO, M. NATANSON, ANISHEV, NATANSON's husband, and others visited their apartment.

BAKAEV told me: “The achievements of socialist construction cost the country dearly; all this could have been accomplished without such costs.”

KOSTINA — from her behavior in connection with ZINOVIEV's expulsion from the AUCP(b) in 1932, it was clear to me that she was a Zinovievist.

From meetings with G. E. EVDOKIMOV I concluded that he was very close to ZINOVIEV. When ZINOVIEV was expelled from the party in 1932, EVDOKIMOV interceded a great deal for ZINOVIEV: he telephoned various persons; on precisely what questions I cannot remember.

EVDOKIMOV told me that he never broke ties with friends — meaning his relations with ZINOVIEV.

Sergei GESSEN — I last saw him in Sochi. It was either in 1931 or in 1932. He considered himself a Zinovievist.

Dina GERTIK — Artem GERTIK's wife; I met her in 1931 or 1932 at a sanatorium in Sochi. She held actively oppositional sentiments and considered herself a loyal Zinovievist.

Recorded correctly from my words; I have read the record.

ZALUTSKY.

INTERROGATED BY:

ASSISTANT HEAD OF THE EKO, GUGB, NKVD OF THE USSR — DMITRIEV.

Certified true: A. Svetlova
