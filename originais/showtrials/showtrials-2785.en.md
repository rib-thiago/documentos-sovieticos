RECORD

OF THE SUPPLEMENTARY INTERROGATION OF ANTONOV Nikolai Semyonovich

Dated December 12, 1934.

QUESTION: In the record of December 12 of this year, you testify that the bloc between the Zinovievists and the Trotskyists has recently been restored. Tell us which Trotskyists belong to the Zinovievist-Trotskyist counterrevolutionary organization.

ANSWER: I know that the following Trotskyists are members of the Zinovievist-Trotskyist organization: SUROV Pavel, exiled from Leningrad for counterrevolutionary activity in 1933, SHATSKY Nikolai, and TSARKOV Nikolai. The latter belonged to the Leningrad center of the Zinovievist-Trotskyist counterrevolutionary organization and is connected with RUMYANTSEV Vladimir.

QUESTION: Do you know through whom contact was maintained between the Leningrad center of the Zinovievist-Trotskyist counterrevolutionary organization and the Moscow center of the Zinovievist-Trotskyist counterrevolutionary organization?

ANSWER: With the Moscow center our counterrevolutionary organization \[1\], ZINOVIEV and BAKAEV, contact was maintained through KOTOLYNOV Ivan and RUMYANTSEV Vladimir. —

Recorded correctly from my words; read aloud to me. —

ANTONOV.

Interrogated by:

Head of Section 1 of the SPO of the GUGB PETROVSKY.

Certified true:

RGASPI, fond 671, inventory 1, file 114, leaf 165.

\[1\] Thus in the text.
