INTERROGATION RECORD

Of Anatoly Isaevich ANISHEV (supplementary)

dated January 7, 1935.

QUESTION: In your testimony of December 27, 1934, you stated that you personally maintained ties with members of the Zinovievist organization's center EVDOKIMOV, BAKAEV, KUKLIN, and GERTIK.

Specify how these ties were concretely expressed.

ANSWER: I have already testified that during 1932 and 1933 I repeatedly met members of this organization's center, EVDOKIMOV, BAKAEV, KUKLIN, and GERTIK, on business concerning the counterrevolutionary Zinovievist organization. In 1932–33 I met EVDOKIMOV as a member of the organization's center three times: in 1932 at his apartment together with NATANSON; the same year at BAKAEV's apartment; and in 1933 in Moscow on Lubyanka. During this last meeting, EVDOKIMOV and I discussed the situation created by NATANSON's arrest. We both characterized this arrest as a continuation of the Party leadership's course of reprisals against former oppositionists. In this conversation EVDOKIMOV and I assessed NATANSON's arrest as a collapse in one sector of our organization.

During 1932–33 I met BAKAEV, a member of the Zinovievist organization's center, approximately four or five times. These meetings most often took place at BAKAEV's apartment near the Pokrovsky Gates. At different times during those years I met center members A. GERTIK, EVDOKIMOV, and KUKLIN and organization members ELKOVICH, GESSEN, GORSHENIN, and KOSTINA at BAKAEV's apartment. I also met ZALUTSKY at BAKAEV's apartment. BAKAEV's apartment was a kind of headquarters for the organization.

QUESTION: Tell us about the meetings of organization members at BAKAEV's apartment. Who was present and what questions were discussed?

ANSWER: In 1933 I participated in a gathering of Zinovievist organization members at BAKAEV's apartment near the Pokrovsky Gates. The organization members present were BAKAEV, KUKLIN, ELKOVICH, myself, KOSTINA, and GESSEN. Of the organization members present at this gathering, I worked permanently in Leningrad, and ELKOVICH in Sverdlovsk. Of the questions discussed, I remember the following:

1\. Questions of collectivization: I provided information on the decline of agricultural production in connection with collectivization; I cited facts mainly concerning Ukraine, which I had drawn from materials of the Academy of Agricultural Sciences. Generalizing these facts, those present established the failure of the collectivization policy as a result of the Central Committee's incorrect leadership; 2. questions of industrialization arose at this gathering in connection with the program to provide the country with 8 million tons of pig iron. This was assessed as another piece of demagogy by the leadership, as an obviously unfulfillable program. In general, the attitude of the counterrevolutionary Zinovievist organization's members toward the pace of industrialization, expressed by those present both at this gathering and during the other meetings and conversations, amounted to the following: “Sit and wait for the Party leadership to break its neck over impossible rates, and use this in the struggle for leadership.” I do not remember exactly whether it was at this gathering or in a conversation with BAKAEV involving GORSHENIN and KOSTINA that questions connected with repression against some organization members were discussed (the arrest of GERTIK and NATANSON, KOSTINA's expulsion from the Party). These repressions were perceived as a blow against the organization as a whole, as a result of insufficiently clandestine behavior by NATANSON and GERTIK. KOSTINA's statement was assessed as correct in substance but wrong tactically, that is, as a step running counter to the (organization's) tactic of concealed double-dealing, as a step endangering the organization's existence as a whole.

QUESTION: What do you know about KUKLIN and GESSEN?

ANSWER: KUKLIN, as I have already testified, was until recently a member of the counterrevolutionary Zinovievist organization's center. I know GESSEN as an organization member. Both participated with me in the gathering of organization members at BAKAEV's and in discussing the questions set out above, taking the same anti-Party, counterrevolutionary position on these questions.

Recorded correctly from my words and read by me —

ANISHEV.
