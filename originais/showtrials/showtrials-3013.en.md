RECORD

OF THE SUPPLEMENTARY INTERROGATION OF THE ACCUSED KHANIK L. O.

13 December 1934.

KHANIK Lev Osipovich, born 1902, native of the hamlet of Tarkhovka, Gaisin District, Podolia Province; Kronstadt, 6/41 Velishchinskogo Street, apartment 3. Kronstadt District Committee of the All-Union Communist Party (Bolsheviks), instructor for industry and transport. Assistant metalworker (Komsomol official). Education Workers' Union; property status — none; before 1929 — the same; before 1917 — the same; social status: worker; service in the tsarist army — none. In the Red Army — volunteer in 1918, three months in a workers' sapper detachment; from 1919 to 1920, four months in Soviet infantry commanders' courses; in 1921, head of a detachment's personnel during the Kronstadt revolt; Red partisan, card No. 114; worker's son; belonged to the VLKSM from 1918 to 1927; nationality — Ukrainian; citizen of the USSR; member of the All-Union Communist Party (Bolsheviks) since 1920, party card No. 0039782; education — above secondary level, two years at a higher technical institute; junior commanding personnel; never tried in court; health — neurasthenia; married, wife ALEKSANDROVA Margarita Grigoryevna, son, daughter, father, mother.

QUESTION: The investigation of your case has information concerning your membership in a counterrevolutionary organization of former Zinovievists. What can you testify about this?

ANSWER: I plead guilty to having indeed belonged to the Leningrad counterrevolutionary organization, formed on the basis of the former Trotskyist-Zinovievist bloc. Our organization took definite shape in 1933 at a suggestion from Moscow, where at the same time an All-Union center of the organization was established on ZINOVIEV's initiative.

The organizing and leading role in creating the organization in Leningrad was played by RUMYANTSEV Vladimir and LEVIN Vladimir, who restored old opposition connections and called on them to take part in the continued struggle against the present party line and party leadership. In the very first period of the organization's existence in Leningrad, almost all former participants in the Vyborg Zinovievist group, the so-called youth, joined it. The need to continue an organized struggle was put before all of us, and many of us accepted this proposal.

QUESTION: Who exactly belonged to the Moscow and Leningrad centers of your organization?

ANSWER: The Moscow center included ZINOVIEV, KAMENEV, EVDOKIMOV, BAKAEV, KUKLIN, GERTIK and SHAROV; the Leningrad center included RUMYANTSEV Vladimir, LEVIN Vladimir, MANDELSHTAM Sergei, KOTOLYNOV Ivan, MYASNIKOV Nikolai and SOSITSKY Lev; I presume also BOGRACHEV, a (deputy) member of the Presidium of the Leningrad Soviet. I know all this from RUMYANTSEV's words.

QUESTION: What role did you personally play in the organization?

ANSWER: I headed a separate group of the organization which existed at the Leningrad Regional Istomol, within the “Combat Fraternity” of old Komsomol members who had participated in the civil war.

QUESTION: Who belonged to your group and what tasks did you set yourself?

ANSWER: The group included the following: POPOV (Egor) Georgy, VASILYEVA Raisa, TOLMAZOV Andrei, ZHESTYANNIKOV Izolit, SOROKIN Vsevolod, LUKIN Vasily, ALEKSANDROV Aleksandr, BOROVICHKOV Ivan, BURMISTROV Pavel and FADEEV Fyodor. I set myself the task of using the “Fraternity” as a legal opportunity to gather and unite around our organization forces that could be directed against the existing party policy. Besides cautiously recruiting new sympathizers, our group published a number of works on the history of the youth movement, intended to blur the anti-party and counterrevolutionary essence of the Zinovievist opposition in the Komsomol and the party. At the same time, the literature we published was intended to secure certain positions in publishing organizations (VASILYEVA Raisa, BURMISTROV, DRYAZGOV, LUKIN, SOROKIN).

QUESTION: What do you know about the organizational structure of the organization as a whole?

ANSWER: For reasons of secrecy, only members of the center possessed information about the organization's structure. However, I know from RUMYANTSEV that our organization had a number of units in places where young people were concentrated; for example, units existed at the Industrial Institute, the Student Section of the Trade Unions, and Istomol. I do not know where else such units existed. All units were connected with the Leningrad center through one of its members; I personally, under this same organizational principle, was connected with RUMYANTSEV.

QUESTION: How do you formulate the organization's ideological and political directives?

ANSWER: The organization's ideological and political directives stemmed from the platform of the Trotskyist-Zinovievist bloc: undermining the authority of the existing party leadership and replacing it with our organization's leaders, ZINOVIEV, KAMENEV and others, who favored changing the party line now being pursued.

QUESTION: The investigation has information that your organization's leaders cultivated feelings of bitterness and hatred toward the leaders of the All-Union Communist Party (Bolsheviks) among its members. What can you testify about this?

ANSWER: This circumstance corresponds to reality. Our organization's leaders constantly stated that all evil came from the present party leadership, Comrade STALIN and Comrades MOLOTOV, KAGANOVICH and KIROV. Accordingly, our organization's leaders instilled in us, especially the so-called youth, the view that difficulties in the country would continue (achievements were not taken into account) as long as the present leadership headed the party. It was from these counterrevolutionary directives and convictions that the terrorist act committed against Comrade KIROV by our organization's member NIKOLAEV Leonid became possible.

QUESTION: What did you know about preparations for this terrorist act?

ANSWER: I personally knew nothing about it; that is, NIKOLAEV was connected not with me but with KOTOLYNOV.

QUESTION: How do you know that NIKOLAEV was connected specifically with KOTOLYNOV?

ANSWER: I also know this from RUMYANTSEV Vladimir. NIKOLAEV has been connected with KOTOLYNOV for many years, since 1922, when they worked together in the Vyborg District Committee of the VLKSM.

The record was correctly written from my words and read by me.

KHANIK.

INTERROGATED BY:

DEPUTY HEAD OF THE SPO, UGB — STROMIN.
