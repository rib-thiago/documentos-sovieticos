RECORD

Of a confrontation between SAFAROV, G. I., and GERTSBERG, A. V.,

dated January 5, 1935.

SAFAROV, G. I., and GERTSBERG, A. V., summoned for a confrontation, after confirming that they knew one another personally, testified:

QUESTION TO SAFAROV: What do you know about GERTSBERG, A. V.’s membership in the Zinovievist organization?

ANSWER: I know that GERTSBERG is an active Zinovievist and was connected with GERTIK. Some of the people who were heading to GERTIK ended up with GERTSBERG. GERTSBERG is a member of the Zinovievist organization.

QUESTION TO GERTSBERG: Do you confirm SAFAROV’s testimony?

ANSWER: I confirm that I am a member of the Zinovievist organization and that I was connected with GERTIK. I deny that people heading to GERTIK ended up with me.

QUESTION TO SAFAROV: What do you know about GERTSBERG’s contacts with German anti-party groups?

ANSWER: I know that while GERTSBERG was in Germany he maintained contacts there with the anti-party group in the Communist Party of Germany which formed the entourage of Ruth Fischer and MASLOW, and also with the Wedding group. I introduced him to this group. GERTSBERG maintained contacts with LURIE (a former Bundist who worked for a time in the Agitation and Propaganda Department of the Communist Party of Germany’s Central Committee). In the summer of 1932 GERTSBERG asked me whether one of the German Trotskyists who had come from the USSR and the Communist International had called on me (I do not remember the surname). GERTSBERG said that he had met this Trotskyist and that the latter had told him about other members of the group. GERTSBERG maintained contacts with these groups after returning to the USSR as well.

QUESTION TO GERTSBERG: Do you confirm SAFAROV’s testimony?

ANSWER: I confirm that in 1927 I was connected with the Wedding group: BADING, RIESE, and others. In addition, I was connected with Ruth FISCHER, MASLOW, and LURIE, and that the connection rested on shared anti-party views and joint struggle against the Comintern leadership. Afterward I had individual meetings with them on the same basis until 1929.

In 1932 BADING, a former participant in the Wedding Trotskyist group who was in Moscow, did indeed come to my workplace. He came asking me to provide data on the machine-tool trade connected with the foreign import operations of Stankoimport, where I had professional contacts. I advised him to make inquiries on this question through the Comintern. I deny that I had a conversation with BADING at this meeting on political subjects at all, as well as that he informed me about the affairs of the Communist Party of Germany.

GERTSBERG.

ADDITION BY SAFAROV, G. I.: In the summer of 1932 GERTSBERG told me that BADING had informed him about the situation in the Communist Party of Germany, the critical situation in the party, and the inadequacy of its leadership.

SAFAROV.

THE CONFRONTATION WAS CONDUCTED BY:

DEPUTY HEAD OF THE SPO OF THE GUGB OF THE NKVD OF THE USSR — LYUSHKOV.

HEAD OF SECTION I OF THE SPO OF THE GUGB OF THE NKVD OF THE USSR — PETROVSKY.

HEAD OF THE 4TH SECTION OF THE SPO OF THE UGB OF THE LENINGRAD REGION — KOGAN, L.
