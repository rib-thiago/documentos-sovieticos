INTERROGATION RECORD

Of FADEEV, Fyodor Fadeevich, dated January 5, 1935.

Question: Tell the investigation what you know about KLADOVIKOV?

Answer: I know that in 1925 KLADOVIKOV worked at the Vyborg District Komsomol Committee. I do not remember what work KLADOVIKOV did at that time.

Question: Say with which participants in the so-called Zinovievist opposition KLADOVIKOV was connected?

Answer: I do not know whether KLADOVIKOV sided with the Zinovievists or was connected with any of them.

Recorded correctly from my words and read aloud to me. —

FADEEV.

INTERROGATED BY:

HEAD OF THE 1ST SECTION OF THE SPO OF THE GUGB OF THE NKVD OF THE USSR — PETROVSKY

Certified correct: Churbanova
