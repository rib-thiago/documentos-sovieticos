INTERROGATION RECORD

OF SAKHOV, Boris Naumovich —

January 12, 1935.

QUESTION: REPNIKOV's testimony has established that he visited your apartment in 1932 and that you informed him of the negative sentiments among the peasantry and shortcomings in the work of the procurator's office. Do you confirm this?

ANSWER: At present I cannot recall REPNIKOV's visit to my apartment in 1932 or the content of the conversation. It is quite possible that I told REPNIKOV all this, since through my work in the procurator's office I was always aware of many instances of excesses.

SAKHOV.

INTERROGATED BY: KORKIN.

Certified correct: Kazakova

RGASPI, F. 671, Op. 1, D. 134, fol. 120.
