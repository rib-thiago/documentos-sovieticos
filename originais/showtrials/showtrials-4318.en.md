INTERROGATION RECORD

Of ABRAMSON, Eskusiel Bentsianovich,

dated January 7, 1935.

QUESTION: Do you know SAKHOV?

ANSWER: I know SAKHOV through working together in the Vasilyevsky Ostrov district before the 14th Congress. We both took an active part in the Zinovievist organization. I met SAKHOV again in the summer of 1932 at Smolny during the Control Commission’s examination of my case, in which I was accused of illegally occupying an apartment belonging to an institution. The press reported on this case, in particular accusing the prosecutor’s office of glossing it over. Initially the party authority decided to remove me from my work as head of the City Housing Department and prohibit me from holding responsible posts for two years. The question was then reviewed, and it was decided to limit the penalty to removing me from work with a reprimand. SAKHOV participated in examining my case as a representative of the prosecutor’s office. I fully allow that SAKHOV may, out of sympathy for me as a former oppositionist, have exerted influence toward mitigating the decision in my case.

Recorded correctly from my words: — ABRAMSON.

INTERROGATED BY:

HEAD OF THE 1ST SECTION OF THE SPO OF THE UGB — LULOV.

Certified correct: Kazakova
