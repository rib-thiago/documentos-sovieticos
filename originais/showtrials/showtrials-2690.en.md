INTERROGATION RECORD

OF I. G. YUSKIN, dated 7 December 1934.

Conducted by Lyushkov, Deputy Head of the SPO, GUGB, and FEDOROV, operational officer of the SPO, GUGB.

YUSKIN, Ignaty Grigorievich, born in 1898, son of a peasant, village of Tretye Pargolovo, Leningrad Region, Russian, citizen of the RSFSR. Resides in Leningrad, 13/8 Lesnoy Prospekt, apartment 52. Student at the Leningrad Industrial Academy (second year). Mother: Maria Grigorievna, 28 Griboedov Canal, apartment 4; sisters: Alexandra in Semipalatinsk; Maria, Anastasia and Daria in Leningrad, 24 Griboedov Canal; wife: Anna Yakovlevna, Leningrad, 13/8 Lesnoy. His mother had two dachas in Pargolovo; now she has one. Secondary education, VKP(b) member since 1925. Member of the Party committee at the “Russky Diesel” factory for 5 years, secretary of a shop cell; now a member of the Party committee at the Industrial Academy and newspaper editor.

QUESTION: Do you know Leonid Vasilievich NIKOLAEV?

ANSWER: Yes. I have known NIKOLAEV through his sister E. V. ROGACHEVA since 1926. My wife and NIKOLAEV's sister have known each other since childhood.

From 1926 I lived in the same building as NIKOLAEV (13/8 Lesnoy Prospekt). Until 1930 I knew only of NIKOLAEV's existence through my wife, without yet knowing him personally.

In 1930 I had a telephone installed in my apartment, and NIKOLAEV came several times to ask permission to use it. From then on we greeted each other when we met. From 1931 to 1932 I was in Moscow. When I returned to Leningrad, NIKOLAEV had already moved from our building to another apartment.

In all, I met NIKOLAEV about nine times.

QUESTION: What do you know of the anti-Soviet sentiments of your acquaintance Ekaterina Vasilievna ROGACHEVA?

ANSWER: I know nothing of anti-Soviet sentiments on the part of Ekaterina Vasilievna ROGACHEVA (NIKOLAEV's sister). I know of ROGACHEVA's degeneration in her private life.

QUESTION: When did you last meet L. V. NIKOLAEV?

ANSWER: This year I saw L. V. NIKOLAEV only three times. I first met NIKOLAEV in the street at the beginning of this year. Our second meeting was at my apartment on a date in the twenties of October this year — he came to use the telephone.

The third and last time I saw NIKOLAEV was around 26 October, when he again called at my apartment.

QUESTION: Relate the content of your conversations during the last two meetings with NIKOLAEV.

ANSWER: At the first meeting (around 20/X), NIKOLAEV told me he had been assigned to work in the countryside, but first wanted to improve his health and was trying to obtain a place at a sanatorium. NIKOLAEV stayed about five minutes and left.

At the second meeting (around 26/X), NIKOLAEV said he had obtained permission for a paid sanatorium voucher. He said that if he failed to obtain a free voucher from the Vyborg District Committee, he intended to obtain one from the Regional Union with Comrade KODATSKY's [1] help; if that too failed, he intended to go to Moscow and apply there. I understood that in Moscow NIKOLAEV intended to approach the VKP(b) Central Committee.

After telling me of his intention to go to Moscow, NIKOLAEV said he had no money for the journey and asked me to lend him some. I said that, after receiving my scholarship, I could give him no more than 15 rubles.

QUESTION: The investigation has information that, when meeting you, NIKOLAEV expressed his anti-Soviet sentiments.

ANSWER: L. V. NIKOLAEV never expressed anti-Soviet sentiments when meeting me.

QUESTION: The investigation also has information that in conversation with you NIKOLAEV spoke of the need to murder Comrade KIROV.

ANSWER: He did not speak to me of the need to murder Comrade KIROV.

I have read the testimony; it has been correctly taken down from my words.

I make the following corrections:

1\. I learned of NIKOLAEV's existence not from my wife but from his sister Ekaterina ROGACHEVA.

2\. I cannot speak of ROGACHEVA's degeneration in her private life, but in my opinion certain unethical acts in private life do occur.

YUSKIN

INTERROGATED BY:

DEPUTY HEAD OF THE SPO, GUGB — LYUSHKOV

OPERATIONAL OFFICER OF THE SPO, GUGB — FEDOROV
