INTERROGATION RECORD

OF SHVARTS, Grigory Ilyich,

1 January 1935.

QUESTION: Your testimony establishes that during the party purge in 1933 you were expelled for Trotskyism. Tell the investigation how you managed to deceive the party and secure reinstatement once again.

ANSWER: Throughout 1933 our group was preoccupied with preparations for our impending purge. We knew attention and vigilance toward us as Trotskyist-Zinovievist bloc participants were particularly heightened. Therefore all our meetings — mine, ROZOVSKY's and BULAKH's — concerned how to maneuver during the purge to avoid remaining outside party ranks. Our group's principal information passed through ROZOVSKY. We adopted the following proposal: in purge speeches, shift emphasis to an extensive criticism of the bloc's historical roots and dwell as little as possible on personal participation in fighting the party, concealing organizational connections preserved until recently. ROZOVSKY underwent the purge first. He structured his speech in accordance with our decisions, but could not fully carry through his line, since he was stopped and required to discuss his personal participation in the bloc. Nevertheless he passed successfully.

The purge commission expelled me from the party. I was charged with Trotskyism not yet overcome, substantiated also by examples from my practical work in Gosplan.

After expulsion our group repeatedly discussed my situation. We jointly drafted and corrected my appeals to higher party bodies. Through deceiving the party — I concealed Zinovievist connections retained until recently — I was once again reinstated in the party.

QUESTION: Report facts known to you of other organization members deceiving the party during the purge.

ANSWER: Information about who passed the purge and how circulated widely during its days. But now I remember only accounts of difficulties experienced by FAIVILOVICH (he and the Zinovievist REINGOLD were, as we said, “worked over” during the purge at the People's Commissariat of Agriculture). ROGOV underwent the purge twice and with great difficulty. I remember no other cases.

Correctly recorded from my words. Read by me.

G. SHVARTS.

INTERROGATED BY:

HEAD OF THE 4TH SECTION OF THE SPO — KOGAN L.

Certified correct: Kazakova
