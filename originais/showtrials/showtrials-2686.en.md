City of Leningrad, NKVD Directorate of the USSR for Leningrad Region.

INTERROGATION RECORD

On 6 December 1934, I, LULOV, Head of the 1st Section of the SPO, interrogated the citizen:

KOTOLYNOV, Ivan Ivanovich, born in 1905 in the city of Leningrad; father was a tailor before the revolution, now disabled; Russian. Resides at 65 Lesnoy Prospekt, building 1, apartment 19. First-year student at the Industrial Institute. Wife: Vera Yakovlevna DOLGIKH, VKP(b) member, student at the Industrial Institute; son aged 1 year 8 months; father: Ivan Afanasievich KOTOLYNOV, a dependant; brother: Pavel Ivanovich KOTOLYNOV, Red Army commander, serves in the Special Far Eastern Army in Nikolsk-Ussuriysk, expelled from the Party in 1929; brother: Nikolai Ivanovich, assistant mechanic at the “Krasny Treugolnik” factory. Financial circumstances: scholarship and wages. Higher education. VKP(b) member since 1921. Expelled from the VKP(b) by the Fifteenth Congress for factional activity (belonged to the Zinovievist opposition). Reinstated in early 1929. Until 1926 held leading Komsomol positions in Leningrad and Moscow.

QUESTION: With which former oppositionists have you maintained contact since your reinstatement in the Party?

ANSWER: Among the former Trotskyists, during 1929–1934 I maintained and continue to maintain contact with Maria NATANSON (until 1931), now in exile, and Ivan Ivanovich TARASOV, now a student at the Moscow Power Engineering Institute; at various times I met Ya. TSEITLIN, LUKIN, SUROV and others.

QUESTION: What is the nature of these contacts?

ANSWER: These contacts and meetings are of a comradely nature. In the past I was associated with all these people through our joint struggle against the Party.

QUESTION: A notebook containing quotations and theses was seized during the search of your premises. What is the nature of these quotations and theses, and who wrote them?

ANSWER: I selected the quotations and also wrote the theses. Both constitute, in their content, anti-Party counter-revolutionary material directed against the Party and Comrade STALIN. I compiled them in 1926–1927.

QUESTION: For what purpose did you keep this counter-revolutionary material?

ANSWER: This material was lost among my old notebooks.

QUESTION: How, then, do you assess the fact that you kept counter-revolutionary literature?

ANSWER: That is my fault.

QUESTION: Letters containing such phrases as “And people still shout ‘faster tempos’”; “this must be done”; “the same thing every day”; “why turn people into idiots, downtrodden, abandoned and wanted by nobody?” were seized during the search of your premises. Do you confirm that these letters were found there? Who is the author? What do the quoted phrases refer to?

ANSWER: Yes, I confirm it. The author of these letters is my brother, Pavel Ivanovich KOTOLYNOV, who serves in the OKDVA in Nikolsk-Ussuriysk in a command position (he wears 3 or 4 squares on his collar tabs). The phrases in quotation marks refer to the Red Army.

QUESTION: How do you assess the content of these letters?

ANSWER: These letters are counter-revolutionary in content.

QUESTION: What measures did you take, as a citizen and Party member, to protect the Red Army, particularly the OKDVA, from the counter-revolutionary Pavel KOTOLYNOV?

ANSWER: None.

QUESTION: Why?

ANSWER: That is my fault.

QUESTION: Had you previously corresponded with your brother Pavel? What influence did you exert on him?

ANSWER: In 1927, in correspondence with my brother Pavel KOTOLYNOV, who was already serving in the Red Army in the Far East, I worked on him in an oppositionist spirit. As a result, he joined the opposition there in the army. Subsequently he, Pavel KOTOLYNOV, was expelled from the Party. At present he is not a Party member.

QUESTION: To whom does the Browning-system revolver No. 2 found during the search of your premises belong?

ANSWER: To me. The former oppositionist Pekkero-Orlov [1] gave me this revolver in 1927. He is now in Moscow.

QUESTION: Do you have a permit for this weapon?

ANSWER: No.

QUESTION: On what grounds do you illegally keep a firearm?

ANSWER: I am guilty of that.

The record has been correctly taken down from my words and read by me, in confirmation of which I sign:

I. KOTOLYNOV

INTERROGATED BY — LULOV
