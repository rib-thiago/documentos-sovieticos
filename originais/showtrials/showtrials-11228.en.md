TOP SECRET.

TO THE SECRETARY OF THE CENTRAL COMMITTEE OF THE ALL-UNION COMMUNIST PARTY (BOLSHEVIKS)

Comrade STALIN

In addition to the arrests reported in No. 31074 of December 9, on December 12–13 of this year, on Comrade AGRANOV's instructions, apartment searches were conducted and the following persons arrested:

1\. GORSHENIN Ivan Stepanovich, born in 1994, a Party member since December 1919 (expelled in 1927 for factional work, but reinstated). Head of the Central Planning Bureau of the RSFSR Gosplan.

The search seized: the journal “Bolshevik,” No. 11 for 1925 and No. 11 for 1929, with anti-Party annotations; TROTSKY's pamphlet “The New Course” (1924 edition), inscribed: “from STEN to ASTROV, for edifying reading and instruction” (evidently relating to 1923).

2\. BRAVO Boris Lvovich, born in 1900, a Party member since 1919. Expelled from the Party in 1927 for factional work. In 1920 exiled by the OGPU for three years. Reinstated in the Party in 1929. Responsible editor of the journal “On the s/z Procurement Front,” published by the Procurement Committee under the USSR Council of People's Commissars.

3\. GERTSBERG Aleksandr Vladimirovich, born in 1892, a Party member since March 1917. Chairman of the All-Union Tekhnoeksport association.

The search seized: counterrevolutionary materials from the opposition's earlier struggle against the Party (from 1927, on forty-two leaves).

4\. FAIVILOVICH Leonid Yakovlevich, born in 1900, a Party member since 1913 (with an interruption from January to August 1928). Deputy head of the Main Cotton Administration of the USSR NKZ.

The search seized: a notebook with clippings from speeches by TROTSKY and ZINOVIEV published in “Pravda” in 1926.

5\. KOSTINA-BAKAEVA Anna Porfiryevna, born in 1895, a Party member since 1915. In 1927 expelled for factional work and subsequently reinstated. In 1932 expelled a second time by the MKK, but reinstated by the TsKK with transfer to candidate membership for one year. A lathe operator at the Stalin Factory.

The search seized: an archive of illegal Trotskyist documents from 1926–1928, including three copies of the “Opposition Platform,” and a counterrevolutionary poem.

6\. NAUMOV Ivan Kupriyanovich, born in 1895, a Party member since 1913. In 1927 expelled for factional work and exiled by the OGPU to the city of Penza; reinstated in 1928. Director of Aircraft Factory No. 20.

7\. BAKAUSHIN Aleksey Vasilyevich, born in 1907, a Party member since 1926. Senior engineer for planned preventive maintenance at Glavesprom in Leningrad. Arrived in Moscow on a work assignment.

8\. MANDELSHTAM Sergey Iosifovich, born in 1896, a Party member, head of the commissioning and construction organization sector of Gipromez in Leningrad. Arrived in Moscow on a work assignment.

NAUMOV I. K. and BAKAUSHIN A. V. were sent to Leningrad on December 12. The others will be sent on December 13.

PEOPLE'S COMMISSAR
