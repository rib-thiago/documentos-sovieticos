INTERROGATION RECORD

Of Georgy Fedorovich POPOV, dated 24/XII/1934

I consider it necessary to inform the investigation of the following: Annushka (I do not know her surname), V. RUMYANTSEV's apartment neighbor, told my wife, Evgenia Vladimirovna POPOVA, and her relatives that V. RUMYANTSEV engaged in petty trading in the early years of the revolution, and that one of V. RUMYANTSEV's brothers had been with the Whites and participated in the Kronstadt rebellion.

My wife's relatives formerly lived in the same building as RUMYANTSEV, on Astrakhanskaya Street.

I have not personally verified this and do not know how plausible it is.

POPOV.

Interrogated by:

HEAD OF THE SIXTH SECTION OF THE SPO — KORKIN

Certified true: Potrokhova

RGASPI, fond 671, inventory 1, file 121, folio 142.
