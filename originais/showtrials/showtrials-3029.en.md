INTERROGATION RECORD

OF RUMYANTSEV, Vladimir Vasilyevich

14 December 1934.

In my testimony of 13 December of this year I did not give the surnames of two members of the counterrevolutionary organization, former students of the Tolmachev Military-Political Academy. I have remembered that their surnames are LAPTEV and RYABOV Viktor. According to MANDELSHTAM, they have remained in contact with him until recently. LAPTEV works at the Baltic Shipyard; I do not know where RYABOV works.

V. RUMYANTSEV.

QUESTION: What do you know about YAKOVLEV Moisei?

ANSWER: I know YAKOVLEV from 1925–1927 as an active Zinovievist. Since his move to Moscow I have not been informed about his subsequent work. I can report a fact conveyed to me by KOTOLYNOV in 1933: YAKOVLEV sees and is connected with TARASOV Ivan Ivanovich.

V. RUMYANTSEV.

INTERROGATED BY:

DEPUTY HEAD OF THE SPO, GUGB — LYUSHKOV
