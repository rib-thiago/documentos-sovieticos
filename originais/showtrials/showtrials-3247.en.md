INTERROGATION RECORD

conducted by the DEPUTY HEAD OF THE SPO — Comrade STROMIN,

dated 14 December 1934.

GORSHENIN, Ivan Stepanovich, aged 40, born in 1894, from the petty-bourgeois estate of Dubovka settlement, Stalingrad Region, Dubovka District. Father was a shoemaker; Russian, USSR citizen. Resides in Moscow, 6/8 Plotnikov Lane, apartment 12. Head of the Planning Sector of the RSFSR Gosplan, professor at the Narimanov Institute of Oriental Studies and research fellow at the Institute of World Economy. Married, no children. Wife: Anna Evgenievna KOZHURO, 34, student at the Institute of Philosophy, VKP(b) member since 1919. My first wife is Evgenia Georgievna GORSHENINA, 35, living in Moscow, also a VKP(b) member. My brothers: Fedor Stepanovich, mechanic at a ship-repair yard in Gorky, VKP(b) member since 1928 [1]. No property. VKP(b) member since 1919, higher education. Not engaged in public work. Until 1933 a propagandist. Never tried. Removed from military registration on grounds of age. Did not serve with the Whites.

Question: Which former participants in the Trotskyist-Zinovievist bloc did you meet and maintain contact with?

Answer: I maintained contact and met the following former figures of the bloc: Grigory Evseevich ZINOVIEV, Ivan Petrovich BAKAEV, Artyom Moiseevich GERTIK and Aleksandr Sergeevich KUKLIN. I was also connected with their followers: Grigory Fedorovich FEDOROV, Sergei Mikhailovich GESSEN, Sergei Semionovich ZORIN, Regina Lvovna BUDZINSKAYA, Elkon LEIKIN, Ilya LUKYANOV and Boris BRAVO.

Question: With which Zinovievists living in Leningrad were you connected?

Answer: With Nikolai DMITRIEV, Moisei YAKOVLEV and Vladimir LEVIN.

Question: The investigation has information that you participated in illegal Zinovievist meetings which discussed Party and Soviet Government decisions in a critical spirit. What can you testify about this?

Answer: I attended only one such meeting, discussing ZINOVIEV's expulsion from the Party in connection with the RYUTIN case. It took place at Ivan Petrovich BAKAEV's apartment. Besides BAKAEV and myself, GORSHENIN, those present were Grigory Eremeevich EVDOKIMOV, Artyom Moiseevich GERTIK, Yakov Vasilievich SHAROV, Anna Porfirievna KOSTINA and Anna Evgenievna KOZHURO. We all believed ZINOVIEV should have been expelled under a different category, with the right to reinstatement after a year. We discussed this because we wished to clarify our line of conduct at the forthcoming Party meetings which would consider the Central Committee decision on Zinoviev's expulsion.

Question: What can you testify concerning your group's members' statements against Party decisions and leadership?

Answer: I must acknowledge that in the initial period after the Fifteenth Party Congress, in 1928–1929, I did indeed oppose Party decisions and leadership, criticising them and expressing the conviction that if ZINOVIEV, KAMENEV and others were in the leadership these decisions would be better, more correct and timely. I also said that the Central Committee was essentially implementing our proposals while attacking us Zinovievists. I was critical of Comrade MOLOTOV's appointment as Chairman of the Council of People's Commissars and of Comrade STALIN's personality. All these statements occurred only within a narrow Zinovievist circle, chiefly the persons listed above — ZINOVIEV, KAMENEV, EVDOKIMOV, BAKAEV, GERTIK, KOSTINA, SHAROV and KUKLIN — with whom I have maintained contact to this day.

Question: What can you testify concerning Zinovievist gatherings at GERTIK's apartment?

Answer: Such gatherings took place until early 1933, until Dina Abramovna GERTIK's death. They occurred once every three or four weeks. Persons from ZINOVIEV and KAMENEV's circle were always present. We discussed current topical questions, presenting them in an anti-Party form.

Question: Where else did your group's gatherings take place?

Answer: Mainly at my home, my apartment. Less often at BAKAEV's and KUKLIN's. The same persons attended: BAKAEV, EVDOKIMOV, KOSTINA, KUKLIN, GERTIK, myself — GORSHENIN — KOZHURO, ZALUTSKY, BUDZINSKAYA, GESSEN, ZORIN, KAPITONOV, LEIKIN and LUKYANOV. These gatherings occurred until recently. Our conversations revolved around current topical questions, often connected with the position and work of former participants in the Zinovievist opposition.

The record has been correctly taken down from my words.

GORSHENIN

Interrogated by — DEPUTY HEAD OF THE SPO, UGB — STROMIN.
