INTERROGATION RECORD

Of MIRONOV, Georgy Ivanovich,

dated January 3, 1935.

QUESTION: The investigation has information that, having declared your withdrawal from the opposition after the 15th Party Congress, you returned to the party’s ranks with factional views you had not overcome, maintaining organizational contacts with the opposition. Do you acknowledge that you thereby deceived the party and continued the struggle against it?

ANSWER: I deny this information.

QUESTION: During the interrogation of December 22 you testified that, after the Central Control Commission refused to consider the question of your readmission to the party’s ranks, you met the Zinovievists GERTIK and SHAROV, who advised you to address a new application to Comrade YAROSLAVSKY, which you in fact did. How can you explain your resort to the Zinovievists on the question of readmission to the party’s ranks after your own declared withdrawal from the opposition?

ANSWER: I confirm that, after the Central Control Commission refused in June 1928 to consider my readmission to the party, I went directly from the Central Control Commission to GERTIK’s apartment, where I found Yakov SHAROV as well as GERTIK himself. I described to them the circumstances of my appeal to the Central Control Commission and the subsequent decision in my case. I berated GERTIK and SHAROV as prominent figures in the opposition because the Trotskyist-Zinovievist bloc had led me into a dead end.

After hearing my outpourings, they both advised me to address a new application to Comrade YAROSLAVSKY; there and then, in their presence, I wrote an application addressed to Comrade YAROSLAVSKY and let them read its contents.

I myself can find no explanation for having approached the former Zinovievists GERTIK and SHAROV on the question of readmission to the party’s ranks.

QUESTION: The investigation knows that, when meeting GERTIK, you held anti-party, counterrevolutionary conversations concerning discussion of the party’s decisions on questions of domestic and foreign policy. What can you testify in this connection?

ANSWER: I affirm that, after the aforementioned meeting with GERTIK in 1928, I never met him again and had no conversations with him.

Recorded correctly from my words and read by me.

MIRONOV.

INTERROGATED BY:

ASSISTANT HEAD OF THE SPECIAL DEPARTMENT OF THE GUGB OF THE NKVD — GENDIN.

Certified correct: Kazakova
