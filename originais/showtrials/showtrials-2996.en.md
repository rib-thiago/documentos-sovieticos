INTERROGATION RECORD

OF TOLMAZOV Andrey Ilyich, dated December 12, 1934.

QUESTION: What do you know about the counterrevolutionary organization of participants in the former Trotskyist-Zinovievist bloc?

ANSWER: On the question of the counterrevolutionary organization of participants in the former Trotskyist-Zinovievist bloc, I testify: on the basis of anti-Party activity, RUMYANTSEV established connections with the leaders of the former Trotskyist-Zinovievist bloc ZINOVIEV, BAKAEV, and EVDOKIMOV.

He maintained these connections until recently.

In Leningrad, KOTOLYNOV, myself, Ya. TSEITLIN, ALBANSKY Dmitry, and ANTONOV gather around RUMYANTSEV, and he maintains systematic contact with us. These connections rest on our shared counterrevolutionary, anti-Party views.

Recorded faithfully from my words, and I sign in confirmation.

A. TOLMAZOV.

INTERROGATED BY:

HEAD OF SECTION 4 OF THE SPO — KOGAN.
