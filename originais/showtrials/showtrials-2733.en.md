INTERROGATION RECORD

OF KOTOLYNOV, Ivan Ivanovich — 11/XII/1934.

QUESTION: RUMYANTSEV testifies that he showed you ZINOVIEV's letter at his apartment in July 1928, in the presence of TARASOV and KHANIK. Do you continue to deny reading and discussing ZINOVIEV's directive letter?

ANSWER: I recall that RUMYANTSEV did show me a letter written in ZINOVIEV's own hand, in which he proposed that RUMYANTSEV return to the Party. This letter concerned RUMYANTSEV, and I do not consider it a directive.

QUESTION: For what purpose was the letter written, and why did RUMYANTSEV show it to you?

ANSWER: I cannot answer this question, since I do not know for what purpose RUMYANTSEV showed me this letter.

I. KOTOLYNOV

INTERROGATED BY:

HEAD OF THE 6TH SECTION, SPO, GUGB — KORKIN

Certified true: illegible
