INTERROGATION RECORD

OF NIKOLAEV, Leonid Vasilievich

dated 9 December 1934.

QUESTION: Which NKVD employees do you know?

ANSWER: I know the following employees of the NKVD for Leningrad Region: YAROLANTS, PONOMARENKO and PANKRATIEV.

QUESTION: Under what circumstances did you become acquainted with YAROLANTS?

ANSWER: I have known YAROLANTS since 1924, when he was engaged in Komsomol work as business manager of the VLKSM Regional Committee. From 1925, when I was business manager of the Luga Uezd Komsomol Committee, I had work-related contacts with YAROLANTS until 1926. Afterwards I had occasional chance meetings with him. I last saw him a year ago. He came to Smolny on some business; I too was in the Smolny building at the time. We exchanged views on the work each of us was doing and parted. My relations with YAROLANTS were never close. I assume he has worked in the OGPU-NKVD since 1927–1928.

QUESTION: Do you know YAROLANTS's political sentiments?

ANSWER: At present I do not. As regards the period of the struggle against the opposition in the Leningrad Party organisation, I know that YAROLANTS supported the VKP(b)'s general line.

QUESTION: Did you ask YAROLANTS for advice on how to keep your wife's brother, Pyotr DRAULE, who had been sentenced by a court, in Leningrad to serve his sentence?

ANSWER: Yes. This was in August 1934. Pyotr DRAULE had been sentenced to 3 years' imprisonment for embezzlement — he worked as an accountant at the 3rd district militia station. From Pyotr DRAULE's wife I learned that he would be sent to serve his sentence in the Far East, in the town of Svoboda. I decided to ask the NKVD to leave DRAULE in Leningrad and telephoned YAROLANTS for this purpose.

QUESTION: Why did you approach YAROLANTS specifically with this request?

ANSWER: I approached YAROLANTS rather than other NKVD employees known to me because PONOMARENKO and PANKRATIEV were newer employees and did not hold responsible positions.

QUESTION: Your relative was sentenced by a court; therefore you should have addressed any requests to the court, not to the NKVD. Why did you approach YAROLANTS, an NKVD employee?

ANSWER: I approached YAROLANTS as an old acquaintance; I did not enter the NKVD building, but spoke to him only by telephone.

QUESTION: Where did you obtain YAROLANTS's telephone number?

ANSWER: At the NKVD building's pass office, which I approached with this request.

QUESTION: What did YAROLANTS answer to your request?

ANSWER: He said on the telephone that I should address a petition to the Head of Corrective Labour Institutions of Leningrad Region. I did so. I did not speak to YAROLANTS again.

QUESTION: Did you plan to use YAROLANTS in any way to prepare the terrorist act?

ANSWER: No.

QUESTION: Under what circumstances did you become acquainted with PONOMARENKO, an employee of the NKVD for Leningrad Region?

ANSWER: I met PONOMARENKO in 1931–1932; he was then secretary of the Leningrad Regional Council of “ODN” (the Down with Illiteracy Society); the council was housed in Smolny. At the time I headed that council's financial sector and was connected with PONOMARENKO through work. Once during that period I visited his apartment: I proposed that he leave immediately on a business trip to Moscow, since a telegram had arrived from Moscow requesting that a representative be sent, and PONOMARENKO had been selected as that representative.

Since 1932 I have not seen PONOMARENKO again and have not met him even once.

QUESTION: Do you know what work PONOMARENKO and YAROLANTS perform in the NKVD Directorate for Leningrad Region?

ANSWER: No, I do not.

QUESTION: What work does PANKRATIEV do in the NKVD Directorate for Leningrad Region?

ANSWER: I know from his mother that PANKRATIEV is a worker in the NKVD Communications Department.

QUESTION: Do you know PANKRATIEV personally?

ANSWER: I do not know PANKRATIEV personally and have never spoken to him. PANKRATIEV lives at 13/8 Lesnoy Prospekt, apartment 56. I once lived in this building; that is how I know his mother.

QUESTION: If you lived in the same building as the PANKRATIEV family, how is it that you know PANKRATIEV's mother but not him personally?

ANSWER: PANKRATIEV is a young fellow, 5–8 years younger than me; I had no occasion to encounter him. As for his mother, I met her in the district Party organisations where she worked. She is a longstanding Party member.

QUESTION: Your explanations are unconvincing. At the beginning of this record you testified that you knew PANKRATIEV, and subsequently denied it. Moreover, your answer to the last question proves nothing. Finally answer correctly: were you acquainted with PANKRATIEV?

ANSWER: No, I was not; I state this categorically.

QUESTION: Do you know PONOMARENKO's political sentiments, both during your joint work in the Down with Illiteracy Society and after he joined the NKVD Directorate?

ANSWER: I know little of them during his work in the Down with Illiteracy Society, and nothing after he joined the NKVD. Regarding the Down with Illiteracy period, I can say I observed no anti-Party or anti-Soviet statements or sentiments on his part.

QUESTION: Did your plans include using PONOMARENKO as an NKVD Directorate employee to murder Comrade KIROV?

ANSWER: No, I did not pose such a question to myself.

QUESTION: We demand a truthful answer to this question, as also to the question of using YAROLANTS. Answer: did you take steps to use YAROLANTS and PONOMARENKO to carry out the terrorist act?

ANSWER: No, nothing of the kind occurred.

Correctly taken down from my words. I have read the record.

L. NIKOLAEV.

INTERROGATED BY:

DEPUTY PEOPLE'S COMMISSAR OF INTERNAL AFFAIRS OF THE USSR — AGRANOV

HEAD OF THE EKO, GUGB, NKVD OF THE USSR — MIRONOV

ASSISTANT HEAD OF THE EKO, GUGB, NKVD OF THE USSR — DMITRIEV
