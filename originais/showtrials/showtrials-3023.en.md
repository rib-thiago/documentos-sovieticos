INTERROGATION RECORD

OF NIKOLAEV, Leonid Vasilyevich

14 December 1934.

QUESTION: What do you know about Vladimir KLADOVIKOV, a member of the All-Union Communist Party (Bolsheviks)?

ANSWER: KLADOVIKOV Vladimir is currently deputy head of the regional supply organization of Leningrad Region. He was close to KOTOLYNOV, SOKOLOV, ANTONOV, me, TOLMAZOV and several others.

I met KLADOVIKOV during 1931–1933. He has anti-party sentiments. At one time I intended to use him in preparing a terrorist act in Moscow.

In autumn 1934 I called at his workplace but did not find him. I wanted to meet him for a discussion; furthermore, in connection with my attempt to establish contacts with foreign consulates in Leningrad and my wish to hand them material against the Soviet Government, I intended that KLADOVIKOV, who knows foreign languages, should translate this material into German, so that I could hand it to the consulates in that form.

Subsequently I did not approach KLADOVIKOV again.

Correctly dictated from my words and read by me.

L. NIKOLAEV

INTERROGATED BY:

DEPUTY PEOPLE'S COMMISSAR OF INTERNAL AFFAIRS OF THE USSR — AGRANOV

HEAD OF THE EKO, GUGB, NKVD OF THE USSR — MIRONOV

ASSISTANT TO THE HEAD OF THE EKO, GUGB, NKVD OF THE USSR — DMITRIEV

Certified correct: Kuzmin
