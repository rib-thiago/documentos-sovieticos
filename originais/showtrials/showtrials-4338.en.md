TO THE AUCP(b) CENTRAL COMMITTEE — Comrade STALIN

Dear Iosif Vissarionovich!

When, after the Fifteenth Party Congress, ZINOVIEV and his then closest like-minded associates, myself included, returned to the Party, all of us, with individual deviations, remained on the former anti-Party Zinovievist positions. We understood submission to the Party and its decisions formally. Moreover, seeking to preserve around himself a group of like-minded associates, myself included, as cadres for resuming open struggle against the Party and its leadership at a “suitable” moment, continuing to abuse and maliciously slander the Central Committee and you personally within the circle of his former associates while publicly approving the Party's line and leadership, ZINOVIEV not only practiced political double-dealing, not only continued his former counterrevolutionary struggle against the Party in a more concealed form, but also created and nourished those anti-Party and counterrevolutionary attitudes on whose soil fascists such as NIKOLAEV and their vile accomplices could grow and did grow.

I am personally guilty of maintaining ties and political meetings with ZINOVIEV and his group for approximately two or three years after my reinstatement in the Party, during which I was undergoing a process of overcoming the remnants of my former anti-Party positions and views.

To fulfill honestly, as a Bolshevik rather than formally, the obligations I undertook before the Party, I was obliged to break sharply with everything and everyone connected with the former opposition and its anti-Party activities. Knowing about ZINOVIEV's and others' double-dealing, I was obliged to report it to the Party Central Committee. I did not do so; on the contrary, I covered up Zinovievist double-dealing, covered up his slanderous exercises, and thus became an accomplice in this politically vile double-dealing. The fact, which the investigation can establish, that in my meetings and conversations then I took a distinct position reflecting the process of my departure from ZINOVIEV and Zinovievism does not diminish my political responsibility for the political double-dealing at that time of the Zinovievist group, which became the political center of a counterrevolutionary organization.

Having ended my former anti-Party attitudes and remnants of anti-Party positions and broken with ZINOVIEV and Zinovievism, for approximately the past three or four years I have felt and understood myself to be a Party member wholly and boundlessly devoted to the Party, its leadership, and you personally, Comrade STALIN. I became a man personally devoted to you because the facts of life convinced me that STALIN's path is the victorious and developed continuation of LENIN's path, that just as in our epoch one cannot be a revolutionary Marxist without being a Leninist, one cannot be a true Leninist without being personally devoted to STALIN, the best of the disciples and continuers of the cause of Marx–Lenin. And it is exceptionally hard and painful for me that precisely now, when I am wholly and undividedly devoted to the Party, devoted to the Central Committee, devoted to you personally as the Party's leader, when in my thoughts and actions at work and outside it, in my rare hours of leisure, there is not a gram of anything departing from the Party's line and interests, precisely now I have fallen into such a foolish and difficult position. If for my sins before the Party during those two or three years (1928–1931) I must bear additional punishment beyond what I have already borne, well, I shall submissively bear any punishment the Party imposes on me and blame no one but myself.

I only want you, Comrade STALIN, and the other Central Committee members to know that for approximately the past three or four years I have been and remain an honest Bolshevik devoted to the Party and its leadership, and will remain so whatever happens to me.

The class enemy, nourished by the dregs of the overthrown classes within and the entire bourgeoisie abroad, continues its counterrevolutionary sallies.

The most vile murder of Comrade KIROV by a handful of fascists who absorbed the serpent's venom of hatred and counterrevolutionary Zinovievist slander against the Party leadership will serve us as another grievous lesson and rally the entire Party, the entire working class, the whole country still more closely around the Central Committee and around you, Comrade STALIN, in a single impulse to finish off the enemy once and for all and ensure the proletariat's victory throughout the world.

Many more years of hard and persistent struggle for the final victory of communism lie ahead.

I ask the Party Central Committee and you personally, Comrade STALIN, to believe in the absolute sincerity of my words, to believe that all my crimes against the Party, which I have neither desire nor reason to conceal, gloss over, or diminish, since I now look at everything with entirely different eyes, that these crimes are all in the past, the relatively distant past, and to help me sooner take my place again in the honorable ranks of fighters for the cause of LENIN–STALIN.

I swear that I shall bear the title of AUCP(b) member with dignity, as befits a Bolshevik.

With communist greetings — M. KHARITONOV.

January 8, 1935.

Certified correct: Khvatov

RGASPI, fond 671, inventory 1, file 134, folios 51–54.
