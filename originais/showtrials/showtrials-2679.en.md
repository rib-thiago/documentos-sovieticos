INTERROGATION RECORD

OF NIKOLAEV Leonid Vasilyevich, dated 6/XII-1934.

QUESTION: When did you approach the German Consulate?

ANSWER: It was several days after my visit to the Latvian Consulate. I looked up the telephone number of the German Consulate in the telephone directory and called. I managed to speak with the consul only after repeated calls.

QUESTION: What conversation did you have with the consul?

ANSWER: I introduced myself to the consul as a Ukrainian writer, using a fictitious surname. I asked him to put me in touch with foreign journalists. I said that as a result of traveling around the Soviet Union I had various materials containing observations and hinted that I wanted to sell these materials to foreign journalists for use in the foreign press.

The consul responded to all this by suggesting that I contact the German mission in Moscow.

Thus, this attempt to establish contact with the German Consulate ended unsuccessfully.

The record was read to me and written down correctly.

Signed: L. NIKOLAEV

INTERROGATED BY:

DEPUTY PEOPLE'S COMMISSAR FOR INTERNAL AFFAIRS  AGRANOV

DEPUTY HEAD OF THE EKO OF THE GUGB, NKVD  DMITRIEV
