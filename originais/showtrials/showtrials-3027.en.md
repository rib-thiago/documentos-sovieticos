INTERROGATION RECORD

of the accused ZVEZDOV V. I. — 14 December 1934.

QUESTION: The investigation has information that you personally were an accomplice in preparing and devising the plan for the terrorist act against Comrade KIROV committed on 1 December of this year by your organization's member NIKOLAEV Leonid. What can you testify about this?

ANSWER: I personally took no part in preparing or devising the plan for the terrorist act and carried out no assignments in connection with it. However, I must sincerely confess that I knew of the very fact that a terrorist act was being prepared, but I was told about this in a veiled form.

QUESTION: When and under what circumstances did you learn about the terrorist act being prepared, and from whom exactly?

ANSWER: It was in early November of this year, in the library of our (Industrial) Institute. I was told by our organization's member ANTONOV Nikolai, who generally (constantly) kept me informed of affairs in the organization; he told me that, since our organization's activity had recently become ineffective, KOTOLYNOV Ivan had decided to intensify it and was now preparing a corresponding action against one of the members of the Politburo of the Central Committee of the All-Union Communist Party (Bolsheviks). I then understood ANTONOV to mean that KOTOLYNOV or some other organization member intended to make some statement at a meeting of the Leningrad organization's party activists, or issue some new document against the party leadership. Although I showed interest in this subject, ANTONOV and I did not discuss it again.

At present, analyzing this conversation with ANTONOV and connecting it with Comrade KIROV's murder, I reach the quite definite conclusion that the terrorist act committed by our organization's member NIKOLAEV Leonid was undertaken with the sanction of one of our organization's leading figures, KOTOLYNOV Ivan.

The record has been correctly taken down from my words and read to me —

ZVEZDOV

INTERROGATED BY: STROMIN.
