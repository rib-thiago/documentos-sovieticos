To Comrade Stalin.

I write to you as the Great Leader of the Party and of the entire world revolutionary proletariat, as the brilliant continuer of Leninism, as the Leader, spokesman, and standard-bearer of the greatest epoch in all of world history, the epoch of proletarian revolutions.

Uncomplainingly accepting retribution for my grave crimes against the Party, aware of the full gravity of my guilt and the need to atone for it, I give my sworn assurance that I am a sincerely convinced, selflessly devoted Stalinist, for which I will give my life to the last drop of my blood.

I implore you to believe that I was a counterrevolutionary Zinovievist only because of the errors of my convictions, and not in pursuit of any other aims.

I implore you to believe that, upon my first attentive study of your works on Leninism, I sincerely and with full force realized the full depth of the counterrevolutionary baseness, vileness, and betrayal of Zinovievism — of Leninism, but did not manage to do everything in time to atone for my grave crimes against the Party through an implacable struggle against counterrevolutionary Zinovievism, preserving its remnants within myself.

Great Leader of the Party, genius of Leninism — do not reject the sincere confessions of a man who has suffered his way to the correctness of his convictions in the struggle against his gravest errors and crimes, for which he is atoning; believe me that I am a sincerely convinced Stalinist, a prodigal son who has recognized his errors. Believe me that I will selflessly give my life for the Party, for you, its Great Leader, and for the Party's Central Committee.

The mere awareness of this trust will ease my fate. Do not reject the plea of one ready to die for you at any moment.

P. Zalutsky.

1935, January 22.

RGASPI, fond 17, inventory 171, file 210, leaf 10. Autograph.
