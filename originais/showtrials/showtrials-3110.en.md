STENOGRAPHIC TRANSCRIPT.

INTERROGATION RECORD

OF NIKOLAEV, Leonid Vasilyevich

dated December 16, 1934.

QUESTION: Explain why the notes found in your apartment, showing how you prepared the terrorist attack on Comrade KIROV, contain no indication that you had accomplices and acted on behalf of the counterrevolutionary Zinovievist organization.

ANSWER: Indeed, the documents contain no indication that the terrorist attack was prepared by a counterrevolutionary organization or that I had accomplices. Such things could, of course, not be allowed in the notes, since this would have constituted an incriminating document against the Zinovievist organization. On the other hand, in these notes I wanted to give vent to the terrorist sentiments that had arisen in me on the basis of sharp disagreement with Party policy.

I had direct instructions from KOTOLYNOV not to allow any mention of accomplices in the notes. Nevertheless, a careful reading of the notes reveals scattered hints that I was not acting alone. For example, in the document found in my possession, dated November 1 and entitled “plan,” under the heading “internal,” item No. 8 contains the passage “almost alone.” These words gave me away in the sense that I was not acting alone.

In the letter “My Answer Before the Party and the Fatherland,” I compared myself to Andrey ZHELYABOV, saying: “I am making preparations like Andrey ZHELYABOV.” Since ZHELYABOV did not act alone, these words contained hints that I too was not acting alone.

Taken down correctly in shorthand from my words; I have read the interrogation record. —

NIKOLAEV L.

INTERROGATED BY:

DEPUTY PEOPLE'S COMMISSAR FOR INTERNAL AFFAIRS OF THE USSR — AGRANOV

HEAD OF THE EKO OF THE GUGB OF THE NKVD OF THE USSR — MIRONOV

ASSISTANT HEAD OF THE EKO OF THE GUGB OF THE NKVD OF THE USSR — DMITRIEV
