TO THE INVESTIGATOR, Comrade KORKIN.

STATEMENT.

I request the following corrections to the testimony I gave on December 15, 1934, concerning my meeting with RUMYANTSEV:

1. Concerning my conversation with RUMYANTSEV at the beginning of 1928, when the readmission of the Zinovievists to the party was being discussed, I mistakenly testified that I was already a party member at the time. In fact, I was not yet a party member then, but had submitted an application to the Central Control Commission. I was readmitted later.

2. I testified that “I admit my guilt in not reporting at that time RUMYANTSEV’s double-dealing intentions and his organizing role.” I have recalled the circumstances of the conversation with RUMYANTSEV and consider it necessary to correct my testimony to the effect that, from the content of our conversation, I did not then understand his organizing role and testified about it mistakenly. RUMYANTSEV’s return to the party with double-dealing intentions, as I have already testified, was clear to me. —

In all other respects I fully confirm my testimony of that date. —

January 4, 1935.

POPOV.

Certified correct: Churbanova
