STENOGRAM

INTERROGATION RECORD

OF NIKOLAEV Leonid Vasilyevich \[1\].

18 December 1934.

QUESTION: What do you know about the preparation of the terrorist act against Comrade STALIN?

ANSWER: The terrorist act was being prepared by both terrorist groups existing in the organization: SHATSKY's group and KOTOLYNOV's group.

I first discussed this with SHATSKY when I met him in August 1934. SHATSKY told me that he was preparing to carry out the act and that, for this purpose, he was making use of RUMYANTSEV's position and connections.

In conversations with KOTOLYNOV in September and October, the latter indicated that he was preparing a terrorist act against STALIN and that he was involving me as well in carrying out this undertaking. KOTOLYNOV indicated that he had the necessary connections in Moscow, and we agreed that we would have to travel to Moscow to determine the possibility of carrying out the act. As I have testified, this trip did not take place because of the intensive preparations for the attempt on KIROV.

At the same time as preparing the attempt on KIROV, I sought to ascertain who could be used in preparing the act against STALIN. My conversations with KULISHER Roman and YUSKIN, and the preparations to recruit ERMOLAEVA, belong in this context. In addition, I intended to make use of TITOV Pavel, who lived in Moscow.

I have already given the investigation detailed testimony about the circumstances in which I negotiated with KULISHER and YUSKIN, and also prepared to recruit ERMOLAEVA.

Taken down in shorthand correctly from my words; I have read the record —

NIKOLAEV L.

INTERROGATED BY:

HEAD OF THE EKO OF THE GUGB OF THE NKVD OF THE USSR — MIRONOV.

ASSISTANT TO THE HEAD OF THE EKO OF THE GUGB OF THE NKVD OF THE USSR — DMITRIEV.
