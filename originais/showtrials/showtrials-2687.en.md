INTERROGATION RECORD

OF SHATSKY [1], Nikolai Nikolaevich, dated 6/XII/1934.

QUESTION: Which former oppositionists from Vyborg District do you know?

ANSWER: Andrei TOLMAZOV, who worked in the Leningrad cooperative system; Vladimir RUMYANTSEV, who works as secretary of the Vyborg District Soviet; FADEEV, whose first name I do not remember, who was, I believe, engaged in trade-union work; Vasily ZVEZDOV, who worked at the “Krasny Vyborzhets” factory in 1923–1927; ANTONOV, whose first name I do not remember, who is now studying, I believe, at the Polytechnic Institute; Mikhail KRYLOV, who works in an artisan metalworking workshop belonging to the Metalworking Association, at 2 Novaya Street.

QUESTION: Do you know Georgy SOKOLOV from Vyborg District?

ANSWER: No, I do not.

QUESTION: Did you meet the persons listed above, and how often?

ANSWER: In 1934 I met TOLMAZOV, FADEEV and KRYLOV.

QUESTION: What was the nature of your meetings?

ANSWER: When I met TOLMAZOV at his apartment, approximately in July 1934, our conversation concerned the possibility of returning to the Party, since I had previously been a Party member and, as TOLMAZOV knows, had been expelled in 1927. Specifically, I expressed to TOLMAZOV my wish to return to the Party, but said that this involved great difficulties. I also met FADEEV in July this year at a tram stop; I had no opportunity to talk to him because he was in a hurry, and merely wrote down his address. I last saw KRYLOV in September this year, at the apartment of our mutual acquaintance Nikolai RADCHENKO, a former VKP(b) member; according to his own account, he had left the Party two or three years earlier in the Moscow organisation.

There our conversation was mainly of a strictly work-related nature. We discussed setting up an artisan workshop, since RADCHENKO and KRYLOV were unemployed at the time. We touched on no other questions. On our way home, KRYLOV and I began talking about Party membership; we asked each other about joining the Party, what possibilities existed in this respect, and so on. I had no conversations on general political subjects during any of these meetings.

QUESTION: Do you know Leonid NIKOLAEV, a former worker of the Vyborg District Komsomol Committee?

ANSWER: No, I do not.

The record has been taken down from my words. Read by me.

N. SHATSKY

INTERROGATED BY —

DEPUTY HEAD OF THE SPO, GUGB — LYUSHKOV
