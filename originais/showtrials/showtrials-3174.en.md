INTERROGATION RECORD

On 19 December 1934, I, LULOV, head of the 1st Section of the SPO, interrogated SOKOLOV G. V.

Supplementary.

QUESTION: In the record of your interrogation of 10 December, you testified that your meeting with ANTONOV in late October at the KSI stadium had a definite character concerning the intensification of the organization's activities. What further testimony can you give on this question?

ANSWER: During this meeting, ANTONOV informed me that the members of the organization had decided to intensify their activities, and that contact with KOTOLYNOV and ZVEZDOV had been restored. At the same time, ANTONOV told me that the strengthening of contact with KOTOLYNOV was connected with a definite active operation which the organization had decided to undertake through him (KOTOLYNOV). ANTONOV remarked that this operation was to be sanctioned personally by KOTOLYNOV, while the other members of the organization would remain in the shadows. During this same conversation, the names RUMYANTSEV, TOLMAZOV, and KHANIK were mentioned.

Recorded correctly from my words and read to me.

SOKOLOV.

INTERROGATED BY — LULOV
