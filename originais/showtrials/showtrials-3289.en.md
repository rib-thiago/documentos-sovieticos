INTERROGATION RECORD

of GERTIK, Artyom Moiseevich

dated December 17, 1934.

QUESTION: State which Zinovievists were most hostile toward the Party leadership.

ANSWER: I consider that BAKAEV and GORSHENIN had the most hostile sentiments toward the Party leadership.

In their assessments of the Party leadership, they spoke of individual leaders — comrades STALIN and KAGANOVICH — in the harshest and most irreconcilable terms.

GERTIK

INTERROGATED BY: AGRANOV

RUTKOVSKY.

RGASPI, F. 671, Op. 1, D. 120, sheet 95.
