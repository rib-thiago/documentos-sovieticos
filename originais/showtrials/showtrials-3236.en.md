STENOGRAPHIC TRANSCRIPT

INTERROGATION RECORD

OF KOTOLYNOV, Ivan Ivanovich,

dated December 26, 1934.

QUESTION: How did feelings of bitterness toward the Party leaders develop among the members of the Zinovievist-Trotskyist counterrevolutionary organization, resulting in the shot fired by NIKOLAEV — a member of your organization — of which Comrade KIROV was the victim?

ANSWER: Our struggle against the Party was accompanied by the discrediting of the leadership of the All-Union Communist Party (Bolsheviks), and first and foremost of Comrade STALIN.

Our leaders systematically inflamed this dissatisfaction with the leadership of the All-Union Communist Party (Bolsheviks), not stopping short of the most reckless, outright counterrevolutionary slander against them and against Comrade STALIN.

Gradually, feelings of extreme bitterness grew ever stronger in our ranks and could assume a terrorist character in particular individuals. Thus this snowball gradually grew and increased, and at some point some kind of explosion was inevitable.

I recall how these feelings developed throughout the entire period of my participation in the Zinovievist organization, right up to the last days.

ZINOVIEV's statement that STALIN, by implementing organizational measures, was carrying out a massacre of the innocents was widely circulated in our ranks. “But remember,” ZINOVIEV added, “that nothing can stop revolutionaries — neither prisons nor exile.” In these words of ZINOVIEV's we saw direct encouragement of our anti-Party, counterrevolutionary talk about the Party leadership.

ZINOVIEV had a favorite expression, intended especially for the young: “Bruises adorn revolutionaries.”

I consider it necessary to say that this entire atmosphere of bitterness and hatred, encouraged by our leaders, amounted in essence to incitement to the most extreme forms of struggle against the Party leaders. I consider it beyond doubt that NIKOLAEV's shot was a direct consequence of this unrestrained incitement, and each of us — members of the Zinovievist-Trotskyist counterrevolutionary organization — bears grave responsibility for KIROV's death.

Taken down correctly in shorthand from my words; I have read the record.

KOTOLYNOV

INTERROGATED BY:

ASSISTANT HEAD OF THE EKO OF THE GUGB OF THE NKVD — DMITRIEV.

Certified true: Shugaeva
