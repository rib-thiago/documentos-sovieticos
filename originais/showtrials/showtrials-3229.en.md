RECORD

of a confrontation between the accused ANTONOV Nikolai Semyonovich and RUMYANTSEV Vladimir Vasilyevich, conducted on December 25, 1934.

After mutually confirming that they knew one another personally, the accused testified:

QUESTION TO THE ACCUSED ANTONOV: Describe the conversation you had when you met RUMYANTSEV in August 1934.

ANSWER: In mid-August 1934, at RUMYANTSEV's apartment, where I, ANTONOV, RUMYANTSEV, and FADEEV F. were present, after drawing up a list of former Komsomol members, RUMYANTSEV told us about the contents of the book by AGABEKOV, who had fled the USSR. During this conversation the question of war and of the USSR's relations with Japan arose. On this question, we all, including RUMYANTSEV, concluded that if war broke out, the present leadership of the All-Union Communist Party (Bolsheviks) would be even less able to cope with the tasks facing the country and would be compelled to bring our “leaders” ZINOVIEV and KAMENEV into the leadership of the country.

N. ANTONOV.

QUESTION TO THE ACCUSED RUMYANTSEV: Do you confirm this?

ANSWER: I confirm that ANTONOV and FADEEV did indeed visit my apartment in August 1934. They came to draw up with me lists of former Komsomol members for the International Youth Day celebration. This was done on the instructions of the secretary of the Vyborg District Komsomol Committee, Comrade RUBLEVSKY. I did indeed tell ANTONOV and FADEEV about the contents of AGABEKOV's book. I deny the conversation about war.

V. RUMYANTSEV.

Confrontation conducted by:

HEAD OF THE EKO OF THE GUGB OF THE NKVD OF THE USSR — MIRONOV.

DEPUTY HEAD OF THE SPO OF THE GUGB OF THE NKVD OF THE USSR — LYUSHKOV.

HEAD OF SECTION 1 OF THE SPO OF THE GUGB OF THE NKVD — PETROVSKY.

CERTIFIED TRUE: Kuznetsova
