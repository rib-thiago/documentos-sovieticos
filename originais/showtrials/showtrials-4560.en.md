TO THE NKVD DIRECTORATE FOR THE LENINGRAD REGION.

I fully and completely acknowledge my guilt, as well as the severe punishment I must undergo. My guilt is further aggravated by the fact that, having voluntarily assumed the obligations of a secret collaborator, I telephoned the GPU from KONDRATYEVA's apartment, thereby exposing myself to a number of oppositionists on whom I was supposed to report to the GPU organs (SUROV, TSEITLIN, and KONDRATYEVA). Because of this circumstance, my subsequent work as a secret collaborator became extremely difficult, and consequently I was unable to obtain the necessary information about a number of persons from the Zinovievist opposition. When I began working as a secret collaborator in 1932, I told the employees of Section 1 that I would report a number of interesting facts about the activity of the Trotskyist-Zinovievist underground; despite this undertaking, I concealed from the GPU organs several facts known to me, particularly my conversations with CHARNY, during which he spoke in favor of organizing a society of old workers, as well as the counterrevolutionary conversation in 1931 about building socialism in one country, during which I defended the old opposition views. Nor did I report that in 1931 BRAVO suggested I read Trotsky's book “My Life.” My guilt is extremely great, especially in the light of all the latest events connected with the counterrevolutionary Zinovievist organization's activity. I assure the NKVD organs that through all my future work, under any conditions, I will strive to earn the confidence of the Party and government.

V. LUKIN.

January 23, 1935.

RGASPI, fond 671, inventory 1, file 142, leaf 30.
