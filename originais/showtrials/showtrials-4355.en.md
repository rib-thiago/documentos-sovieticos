INTERROGATION RECORD

Of SAFAROV G. I., January 9, 1935.

In addition to my testimony, I inform the investigation of the following:

During my stay in Berlin in November 1927, when I organized an “international conference” of Trotskyists, Pyotr PEREVERZEV obtained money for organizing this conference, on my instructions, from N. N. KRESTINSKY, who at that time belonged to the counterrevolutionary Trotskyist-Zinovievist bloc. The initial sum, I believe 150 marks, was insufficient. I pointed this out to Pyotr PEREVERZEV and proposed that he speak more firmly to KRESTINSKY, threatening to hold him accountable to the center of the counterrevolutionary bloc, of which I was then an official member and representative. After this, KRESTINSKY provided additional funds for organizing the conference, I believe in the same amount or slightly more.

Written in my own hand — G. SAFAROV.

INTERROGATED BY:

HEAD OF THE 1ST SECTION OF THE SPO OF THE GUGB OF THE USSR NKVD — PETROVSKY.
