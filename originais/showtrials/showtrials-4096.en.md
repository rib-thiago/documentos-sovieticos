INTERROGATION RECORD

Of TSARKOV, Nikolai Alexeyevich.

Dated January 5, 1935.

Question: What do you know about KLADOVIKOV, Vladimir?

Answer: I know KLADOVIKOV, Vladimir, through working together in the Vyborg district. He was secretary (technical) of the cell at the “Krasnaya Zarya” plant in 1929. During our struggle against the party, KLADOVIKOV actively fought against us and was taken on for work at the Komsomol District Committee after we were dispersed. He was then promoted to the Komsomol Regional Committee; he did not belong to the Trotskyist-Zinovievist bloc and actively fought against it throughout. I have not seen him for a very long time.

Question: What do you know about KOZIN, Konstantin?

Answer: During the years of the Trotskyist-Zinovievist bloc, KOZIN, as secretary of the District Soviet’s Komsomol cell, initially sided with the Zinovievists. I do not know what work he did. FADEEV and VINOGRADOV know him better than I do. At that time I was sent to the Far East, and I have not seen him for a long time.

Recorded correctly from my words and read by me.

TSARKOV.

INTERROGATED BY:

HEAD OF THE 4TH SECTION OF THE SPO OF THE UGB: KOGAN, L.

Certified correct: Churbanova
