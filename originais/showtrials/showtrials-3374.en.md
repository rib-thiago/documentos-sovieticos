INTERROGATION RECORD

Of ALEKSANDROV, Aleksandr Ivanovich

December 21, 1934.

QUESTION: In the previous interrogation record you testified that in 1932, on the anniversary of the Leningrad Komsomol organization, you attended a gathering at SUROV's apartment composed overwhelmingly of former participants in the Zinovievist-Trotskyist bloc. How do you assess this gathering?

ANSWER: It must be noted that the overwhelming majority of those present had previously been connected with one another through participation in the Zinovievist-Trotskyist bloc, and undoubtedly some of them, having been expelled from the Party, submitted statements renouncing the opposition in order to preserve opposition cadres. Having been readmitted to the Party, they did not abandon their ideological and political directives, deriving

from the platform of the Zinovievist-Trotskyist bloc, aimed at undermining the authority of the existing Party leadership. United by common political directives, using others as a screen and taking cover behind the convenient pretext of the anniversary of the Leningrad Komsomol organization, they organized a gathering at SUROV's apartment which was essentially an organizational meeting of a number of participants in the Zinovievist-Trotskyist bloc, pursuing the aim of restoring former connections.

QUESTION: The investigation has information that the murder of Comrade KIROV was committed by an underground counterrevolutionary organization of former participants in the Zinovievist-Trotskyist bloc, and also that you belonged to this organization. What testimony can you give on this question?

ANSWER: While unaware of the existence of an underground, formally constituted counterrevolutionary organization of former participants in the Zinovievist-Trotskyist bloc, I acknowledge that, having formerly been a member of this bloc and having been educated by its leaders in a spirit of hatred toward the Party leaders, I bear full political responsibility for the terrorist act against Comrade KIROV committed by this organization.

The record was taken down correctly from my words and read by me.

ALEKSANDROV.

INTERROGATED BY: SHAKULEV

Certified correct: A. Svetlova
