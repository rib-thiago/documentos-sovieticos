INTERROGATION RECORD

OF RUMYANTSEV, Vladimir Vasilievich

dated 10 December 1934.

QUESTION: During the search of your premises, a letter addressed to you, dated 30 June 1928 and signed by G. ZINOVIEV, was found. It concerns the return of former Zinovievists to the VKP(b) and specifically instructs them to “write the application so that it will be considered satisfactory”. How do you assess this document?

ANSWER: This letter was sent because ZINOVIEV feared losing influence over the young people who had followed him during the Zinovievist-Trotskyist bloc period and who were partly disoriented and partly negotiating their return to the Party independently of ZINOVIEV. While courting the young people in this letter, ZINOVIEV also gave us former Zinovievists the double-dealing instruction to enter the Party while formally renouncing our anti-Party views, but in substance to remain in our old positions hostile to the Party.

QUESTION: When did you submit an application for your return to the VKP(b)?

ANSWER: I submitted the application to the Leningrad Regional Control Commission of the VKP(b) in August 1928, addressed to the VKP(b) Central Control Commission.

QUESTION: When submitting your application for admission to the Party, did you report, and to whom specifically, the ZINOVIEV document you possessed instructing a double-dealing return to the ranks of the VKP(b)?

ANSWER: No, I told no one.

QUESTION: How can you explain this behaviour?

ANSWER: I consider that, by failing when returning to the Party to report that I possessed such a double-dealing document from ZINOVIEV, I concealed it from the Party and committed a grave crime against the Party. I underestimated the significance of the letter.

QUESTION: Did you tell any of your associates of the existence of ZINOVIEV's letter and its instructions?

ANSWER: I showed this letter to KOTOLYNOV, KHANIK and Ivan TARASOV, who gathered at a party at my home in July 1928. I showed it to no one else.

QUESTION: Do you continue to assert that you did not use the ZINOVIEV letter mentioned in your testimony to acquaint Zinovievists widely with the double-dealing instructions it contained?

ANSWER: As far as I remember, I showed ZINOVIEV's letter to no one besides KOTOLYNOV, KHANIK and TARASOV, whom I have named.

QUESTION: The investigation has information that you also showed ZINOVIEV's letter to ANTONOV and Mikhail MURAVIEV in the summer of 1928. Do you confirm this?

ANSWER: I do not remember.

QUESTION: With which former participants in the Zinovievist-Trotskyist bloc who are in Moscow do you maintain contact?

ANSWER: Since returning to the Party in 1928, I have maintained no contact, either written or personal, with any former participants in the Zinovievist-Trotskyist bloc who are in Moscow, except for one meeting with EVDOKIMOV in 1931 in Leningrad at Pyotr TARASOV's [1] apartment. EVDOKIMOV was heavily intoxicated and engaged in no conversation. Besides myself, Pyotr TARASOV and his wife and my wife were present.

QUESTION: The BAKAEV letter of 8/X/1933 found during the search of your premises shows that you did have contact with him. What can you say about this?

ANSWER: I confirm that in October 1933 BAKAEV's daughter did visit me with a letter from him. Through RABINOVICH, Head of the District Soviet's Mass Work Department, I found her library work.

INTERROGATED BY:

DEPUTY HEAD OF THE SPO, GUGB — LYUSHKOV

[1] G. E. Evdokimov's son-in-law.
