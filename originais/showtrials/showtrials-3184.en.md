INTERROGATION RECORD

OF RUMYANTSEV, Vladimir Vasilyevich —

20 December 1934.

QUESTION: Do you know the NKVD employee GRANSKY?

ANSWER: I know GRANSKY, the former head of the GPU section in Vyborg District; he left for Moscow in 1932.

QUESTION: Did GRANSKY ever tell you that you appeared in the files of the former GPU?

ANSWER: No, he did not.

QUESTION: LEVIN V.'s testimony establishes that you told him how GRANSKY had warned you that the apparatus of the district section of the former GPU had information about you and LEVIN V. as persons around whom former participants in the so-called Zinovievist opposition were gathering?

ANSWER: GRANSKY summoned me to the district section of the former GPU in early 1932 and asked me how I was working in the District Soviet and whether I maintained contacts with the opposition. Employees of the district section of the former GPU, SINOZERSKY and NAKHAMKES, were present during my conversation with him. I do not remember precisely, but it is possible that I told LEVIN about my conversation with GRANSKY.

I deny that GRANSKY warned me of allegedly existing materials compromising me or exposing me as engaged in hostile activities.

V. RUMYANTSEV.

INTERROGATED BY:

DEPUTY HEAD OF THE SPO OF THE GUGB — LYUSHKOV. —
