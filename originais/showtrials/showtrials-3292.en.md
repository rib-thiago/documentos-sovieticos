INTERROGATION RECORD

conducted on December 17, 1934, by the head of the 4th Section of the SPO — L. KOGAN.

TSARKOV, Nikolai Alekseevich (SUPPLEMENTARY)

QUESTION: The investigation has information incriminating you in having returned to the Party for double-dealing purposes. What can you say on this question?

ANSWER: Yes, I acknowledge that I did indeed return to the Party while remaining on the positions of the Trotskyist-Zinovievist bloc. When ZINOVIEV submitted his application for admission to the Party, I and a group of my accomplices in counterrevolutionary work (S. TROFIMOV, SUROV, V. RUMYANTSEV, ZVEZDOV, ANTONOV, KOTOLYNOV, I. TARASOV, TOLMAZOV and others I do not remember) knew that ZINOVIEV was entering the Party as a double-dealer. We, however, considered it necessary to struggle against the Party while remaining outside it. At the time the group was known as the “leaderless” and was headed by SAFAROV. I knew from RUMYANTSEV that the Zinovievists were entering the Party with the direct assignment of deceiving it. I remember giving such an instruction to ZVEZDOV. Subsequently our group adopted the same position: all entered the Party, taking advantage of the Party's trust while essentially retaining their old counterrevolutionary positions.

QUESTION: Does the Zinovievists' counterrevolutionary organization continue to exist to this day, and do you belong to it?

ANSWER: The Zinovievist organization has continued to exist until recently. I, TSARKOV, belong to a group of this organization headed by RUMYANTSEV. In addition to myself and RUMYANTSEV, this group includes KOTOLYNOV, ANTONOV, ZVEZDOV, R. TROFIMOV, L. KHANIK, MURAVYOV, VINOGRADOV, TOLMAZOV and others whom I either do not know or partly do not remember. The main group of Zinovievists, employees of the Vyborg District Komsomol Committee, was concentrated around RUMYANTSEV. These were the most effective and actively disposed young people. My three-year absence from Leningrad (I returned to Leningrad only on November 10, 1934, and before that visited only occasionally on leave) prevents me from giving the investigation a complete picture of the membership and groups of the existing counterrevolutionary organization. But in the short time since my return I have already managed to contact RUMYANTSEV and receive material assistance from him for the initial period. I also know from RUMYANTSEV that until recently he has maintained contact with the organization's Center in Moscow — with ZINOVIEV and EVDOKIMOV. I have no information about the Center's complete membership at present.

TSARKOV

Interrogated by: L. KOGAN.
