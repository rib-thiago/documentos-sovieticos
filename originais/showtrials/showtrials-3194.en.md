INTERROGATION RECORD

OF SOSITSKY, Lev Ilyich, dated 21 December 1934.

QUESTION: What do you know about the existence in Leningrad of an organization of former participants in the Zinovievist-Trotskyist bloc?

ANSWER: After the party's defeat of the Zinovievist-Trotskyist bloc, a process of restoring organizational links among former participants in the Zinovievist-Trotskyist bloc has been under way since 1928, as they returned to the party (after their expulsion from its ranks) for double-dealing purposes. The restoration of these links led to their taking shape as a counterrevolutionary organization of former participants in the Zinovievist-Trotskyist bloc, which existed until the most recent period.

In fact, its organizational meeting was a gathering of active Zinovievists held after LILINA's funeral at the apartment of SHAROV Yakov. There were many people, at least thirty. I remember that LEVIN Vladimir, RUMYANTSEV Vladimir, MANDELSHTAM S., NATANSON Musya, and others were present. At this illegal conference, the question was discussed of further restoring organizational links among former participants in the so-called Zinovievist opposition and preserving these cadres from being broken up.

QUESTION: Who belonged to the leading center of the counterrevolutionary organization of former participants in the Zinovievist-Trotskyist bloc in Leningrad?

ANSWER: The leading center of the counterrevolutionary organization, which existed until the most recent period, consisted of LEVIN Vladimir, RUMYANTSEV Vl., MANDELSHTAM Sergei, MYASNIKOV Nikolai, and myself, SOSITSKY. With regard to me, LEVIN counted on my restoring the old links in enterprises among former participants in the so-called Zinovievist opposition.

QUESTION: Did KOTOLYNOV Ivan belong to the leading center?

ANSWER: I know KOTOLYNOV Ivan as a Zinovievist, a member of the counterrevolutionary organization, but I do not know whether he belonged to its leading center. He may have been connected with RUMYANTSEV, who conducted work among the youth.

QUESTION: What do you know about the existence of the Moscow center of former participants in the Zinovievist-Trotskyist bloc, and who belonged to it?

ANSWER: The center of the counterrevolutionary organization in Moscow consists of ZINOVIEV, KAMENEV, EVDOKIMOV, BAKAEV, GERTIK, and SHAROV.

QUESTION: How do you know of the existence of this Moscow center?

ANSWER: I know of the Moscow center's existence and composition from LEVIN V.'s words, since contacts with the Moscow center of the counterrevolutionary organization were concentrated in his hands.

QUESTION: Describe what you know about the practical activities of the counterrevolutionary organization among former participants in the Zinovievist-Trotskyist bloc in Leningrad.

ANSWER: The principal task consisted in selecting and preserving organizational links among former participants in the Zinovievist-Trotskyist bloc, involving them in the counterrevolutionary organization, systematically exerting counterrevolutionary influence upon them, and educating them in a spirit hostile to the leadership of the AUCP(b) and the Soviet Government. At periodic gatherings of small groups of participants in the counterrevolutionary organization, political information was communicated from Moscow, from the center of the counterrevolutionary organization, and individual questions were discussed, most often connected with current political issues. All meetings were accompanied by counterrevolutionary attacks against the leadership of the AUCP(b) and the Soviet Government, and especially against Comrade STALIN, whose name was associated with responsibility for crushing the so-called Zinovievist opposition and removing ZINOVIEV and KAMENEV from the leadership of the AUCP(b). The question of ZINOVIEV's and KAMENEV's possible return to the leadership of the AUCP(b) was almost always touched upon.

I recall individual, particularly characteristic gatherings in which I took part at different times. Thus, in 1928, at the home of a relative of organization member NALIVAIKO Foma, on Tikhvinskaya Street, besides NALIVAIKO there were RUMYANTSEV, myself, and many rank-and-file participants in the organization whose names I do not know. The question was then raised of the need to win confidence and strengthen our position in the party and at work in order to preserve our cadres. In 1931, ALEXANDROV A., NALIVAIKO, I think MOROZOV A., and I gathered at NALIVAIKO's apartment on Pushkinskaya Street. ALEXANDROV spoke of his meetings with ZINOVIEV and KAMENEV, saying they were embittered by their removal from the party leadership, that they were surrounded by distrust and were not given responsible work. At REM's home, I think in 1928, the following gathered: KORSHUNOV Efim, LEVIN Vladimir, MYASNIKOV Nikolai, LEONTIEV (who shared the apartment with REM), and two workers from the Khalturin factory. This was at REM's apartment, in a large room. LEVIN V. gave us directives about returning to the party in a double-dealing fashion. I remember his phrase to demonstrate that we must return to the party in order to preserve ourselves within it: “we must crawl into the party.” It was also said then that further repression would be directed against ZINOVIEV and KAMENEV, while workers who were former oppositionists would be drawn away from ZINOVIEV's and KAMENEV's influence. Our task was to preserve them (the workers) under our influence. After RUMYANTSEV returned from the Far East following his reinstatement in the party, either in 1928 or 1929, KHANIK, KOTOLYNOV, MANDELSHTAM, myself, and several persons unknown to me gathered at his apartment. In 1931, MANDELSHTAM, SAFRONOV P., and myself, SOSITSKY, gathered at RUMYANTSEV's apartment.

In both cases, these meetings were characterized by counterrevolutionary conversations about the situation in the party and discussions about returning ZINOVIEV and KAMENEV to the party leadership. In 1932, at his apartment, LEVIN conveyed information to me, MYASNIKOV, and RUMYANTSEV about the results of his meeting with ZINOVIEV. LEVIN then related that ZINOVIEV had described a visit by STEN, who brought some kulak platform.

In 1933, after the funeral of the Zinovievist SHURYGIN, I, LEVIN V., MANDELSHTAM, and RUMYANTSEV gathered at my apartment. Here LEVIN said that STALIN was intolerable at the head of the AUCP(b), that he had dealt with ZINOVIEV and KAMENEV, and that we had to continue preserving cadres for the future struggle. At the end of 1933, KORSHUNOV E. and I were at LEVIN's apartment, and the three of us expressed dissatisfaction with the treatment within the party of former participants in the Zinovievist-Trotskyist bloc and again indulged in counterrevolutionary attacks against the leadership of the AUCP(b).

QUESTION: According to the investigation's findings, the sharply hostile attitude toward the leaders of the party and the Soviet Government, displayed daily by members of the counterrevolutionary organization, gave rise to terrorist sentiments among members of the counterrevolutionary organization in Leningrad, which led to the murder of one of the leaders of the party and the Soviet Government, Comrade KIROV, organized by members of this organization.

Do you acknowledge responsibility for the fact that your hostile attacks against the leaders of the party and the Soviet Government led to the murder of Comrade KIROV by members of the counterrevolutionary organization you headed?

ANSWER: I knew nothing about the preparation of the terrorist act against Comrade KIROV. Since an atmosphere of intense hatred for the leaders of the AUCP(b) and the Soviet Government prevailed in our counterrevolutionary organization, which could not fail to create terrorist sentiments among individual members of the counterrevolutionary organization, I believe that the entire counterrevolutionary organization of Zinovievists and each of its members individually bear political and moral responsibility for NIKOLAEV's murder of Comrade KIROV.

SOSITSKY

INTERROGATED BY:

DEPUTY HEAD OF THE SPO OF THE GUGB OF THE NKVD OF THE USSR — LYUSHKOV
