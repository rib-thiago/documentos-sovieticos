INTERROGATION RECORD

OF FEDOROV Grigory Fedorovich

dated January 13, 1935.

QUESTION: What additional information can you give about the activities of the Zinovievist organization?

ANSWER: As I have already stated in my previous testimony, until my arrest I was a member of the Moscow Center of the Zinovievist organization. Until that time I continued to maintain organizational ties with participants in that organization, including

ZINOVIEV, EVDOKIMOV, BAKAEV, SHAROV, KUKLIN, GORSHENIN, GESSEN, RAVICH Olga, ELKOVICH, and others whom I do not now remember.

The Zinovievist organization and all its participants remained on their old counterrevolutionary positions until recently, that is, until the liquidation of the organization in December 1934. I knew this as a result of meetings, up to my arrest, with the participants in the organization listed above and as a result of their anti-Party statements.

Correctly recorded from my words and read by me — FEDOROV.

INTERROGATED BY:

Deputy People's Commissar for Internal Affairs of the USSR — AGRANOV.

SECTION HEAD OF THE EKO OF THE GUGB OF THE NKVD OF THE USSR — I. CHERTOK.

Certified correct: Kazakova
