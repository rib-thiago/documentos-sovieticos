INTERROGATION RECORD

OF NAUMOV, Ivan Kupriyanovich —

dated January 18, 1935.

QUESTION: How did your ties with the Zinovievist underground develop subsequently, after 1929?

ANSWER: As I testified in the interrogation record of January 14 of this year, I stopped meeting ZINOVIEV and KAMENEV approximately from 1929 or early 1930.

In the following years up to 1933 I did not consider myself a Zinovievist.

During this period I remain anti-Party-minded, continue meetings of a political character with a whole series of Zinovievists, with whom I express solidarity on a number of questions of Party policy.

Correctly recorded from my words. I have read the record —

NAUMOV.

INTERROGATED BY:

ASSISTANT HEAD OF THE EKO OF THE GUGB OF THE NKVD: (DMITRIEV).

CERTIFIED CORRECT: illegible
