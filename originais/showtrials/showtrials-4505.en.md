INTERROGATION RECORD

of KASPERSKY, Ivan Feliksovich (continued)

January 17, 1935.

QUESTION: Describe in detail the nature of your conversations with VASILIEV, Maxim Vasilyevich.

ANSWER: I know Maxim Vasilyevich VASILIEV as an active Zinovievist. I did not break off contact with him until the very last period. Our meetings took place in our apartments. During our meetings we constantly touched on the internal situation in the USSR, sharply criticizing the measures of the party and government, especially regarding industrialization and the collectivization of agriculture, proceeding from our former positions. The sharp dissatisfaction I expressed with the measures of the party and Soviet power, and my references to the “difficulties” experienced by the country, met with constant support from VASILIEV.

QUESTION: Describe the counterrevolutionary activities of individual participants in your organization.

ANSWER: KONDRATIEVA, Evdokia — works in the district civil registry office of the Vasileostrovsky district. Formerly an active Zinovievist, she maintained ties with members of the organization until the last period. She joined the opposition after the Fourteenth Party Congress and was our organization's representative at the Uritsky tobacco factory. She has retained her old contacts at the factory to this day. She was a regular, active participant in all the illegal meetings held at the apartments of YAKOVLEV, SEMYONOV, Semyon (her present husband), SEMYONOV, Ivan Semyonovich (“the Old Man”), and myself. At that time she displayed great hostility toward the party and especially Comrade STALIN. Recently she has not withdrawn from our organization and retains her hostility toward the party to this day. During the opposition she maintained close contact with the 3rd Lithographic Works. Whether she maintains these contacts to this day is unknown to me.

SEMYONOV, Semyon — retained his old contacts with members of the Zinovievist organization until the last period and has not changed his hostile attitude toward the party.

FOKIN, Alexei — formerly an active Zinovievist, he has not changed his sharply hostile attitude toward the party up to the last period.

PANKO, Adam — also formerly an active Zinovievist. He has not changed his hostile attitude toward the line of the party Central Committee. He displays constant dissatisfaction with the “difficulties,” expressing a sharply negative attitude toward the measures of the party and government, chiefly in assessing the pace of industrialization and the collectivization of agriculture. He did not sever his ties with the organization until the last period.

NOTMAN, Konstantin — formerly an active Zinovievist, he led the organization's circles. Up to the last period he maintains contact with members of the organization: YAKOVLEV, Moisei, KONDRATIEVA, and SEMYONOV.

Recorded correctly from my words, read by me, in confirmation of which I sign.

KASPERSKY.

INTERROGATED BY:

OPERATIONS OFFICER OF THE 4TH SECTION, SPO, UGB, M. KOGAN.

Certified true: A. Svetlova
