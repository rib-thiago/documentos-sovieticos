STENOGRAPHIC TRANSCRIPT

INTERROGATION RECORD

OF NIKOLAEV, Leonid Vasilyevich — December 17, 1934.

QUESTION: You testified that you began preparing the terrorist attack on Comrade KIROV in July 1934 and that you encountered Comrade KIROV during September and October. Please clarify when you began preparing the terrorist attack and whether you actually kept Comrade KIROV under observation during September.

ANSWER: Since I have now recalled a number of circumstances connected with the preparation of the terrorist attack and the surveillance of Comrade KIROV, I ask that the following corrections be made to my testimony of December 8, 1934.

I tested the serviceability of the revolver and cartridges not in July 1934, as stated in the record, but later, after meeting SHATSKY and KOTOLYNOV. I assert this on the grounds that I tested the revolver some time after sending my children to the dacha, which was in August 1934.

During August Comrade KIROV was not in Leningrad. In September 1934 I established, at the Regional Committee through the woman distributing newspapers, that Comrade KIROV was on leave.

As I now recall, I began keeping Comrade KIROV under observation on October 1, 1934, and on that date I had my first encounter with him at the entrance to the building where he lived, at 86/28 Krasnykh Zor Street.

Taken down correctly in shorthand from my words; I have read the interrogation record.

NIKOLAEV L.

INTERROGATED BY:

HEAD OF THE EKO OF THE GUGB OF THE NKVD OF THE USSR MIRONOV

ASSISTANT HEAD OF THE EKO OF THE GUGB OF THE NKVD OF THE USSR DMITRIEV
