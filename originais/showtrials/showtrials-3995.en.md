INTERROGATION RECORD

OF PANFILOV Mikhail Stepanovich, January 2, 1935.

(supplementary).

Question: The investigation has information that during the interrogation of January 1, 1935, you gave false testimony by stating that you did not know RUMYANTSEV Vladimir and ZVEZDOV at all. Do you confirm that testimony?

Answer: No, I do not. I know both RUMYANTSEV and ZVEZDOV, and that part of my testimony of January 1 does not correspond to reality, because I did not understand the nature of my acquaintance with these persons.

Question: Did you know RUMYANTSEV, ZVEZDOV and KOTOLYNOV Iv\<an\> as Zinovievists?

Answer: Yes, I knew them as former participants in the Zinovievist opposition. RUMYANTSEV and KOTOLYNOV, in particular, played an active role.

Question: Have you ever been to the city of Novosibirsk?

Answer: Yes, I passed through Novosibirsk in 1927.

Question: Did you meet members of the so-called Zinovievist opposition there?

Answer: Yes, I met only TARASOV Pyotr.

Question: The investigation knows that while in Novosibirsk you met MYASNIKOV Nikolai there. Do you confirm this?

Answer: I do not deny that I may have met him.

Question: Why, then, do you testify that, among members of the Zinovievist organization, you met only TARASOV in Novosibirsk?

Answer: I simply forgot.

Question: When did you last see MYASNIKOV in Leningrad?

Answer: I last saw MYASNIKOV in October 1934. In 1932, while at Smolny, where he worked, I specifically called at his office, and until 1930 I visited his apartment. —

The record has been written correctly from my words; read by me.

PANFILOV.

INTERROGATED BY:

OFFICER OF THE SPO OF THE GUGB OF THE NKVD OF THE USSR—ZAIKO.

Certified correct: Churbanova
