CONFRONTATION RECORD

between the accused Alexander Fabianovich BASHKIROV

and Artem Moiseevich GERTIK,

conducted on January 7, 1935.

The accused A. F. BASHKIROV and A. M. GERTIK, summoned to the confrontation, after mutually acknowledging their personal acquaintance, testified:

QUESTION TO A. F. BASHKIROV: What do you know about A. M. GERTIK's membership in the Zinovievist organization and his role in it?

ANSWER BY BASHKIROV: I know that A. M. GERTIK is a member of the Zinovievist organization and one of the persons close to ZINOVIEV. GERTIK came from Moscow to Leningrad to provide information.

QUESTION TO GERTIK: Do you confirm BASHKIROV's testimony?

ANSWER BY GERTIK: I confirm BASHKIROV's testimony except that I came to Leningrad for the exclusive purpose of providing information. I came to Leningrad on institutional business and, incidentally, informed the Zinovievists about Party policy in the spirit of the Zinovievist organization.

QUESTION TO A. F. BASHKIROV: Tell the investigation about the nature of A. M. GERTIK's information.

ANSWER BY BASHKIROV: In 1932 GERTIK came from Moscow to provide information. He was at Vladimir LEVIN's apartment. Besides the persons listed above, I and Alexei PERIMOV were present. We discussed the prospects of bringing ZINOVIEV into the Party leadership to secure our influence over the Party and change the composition of the leadership.

QUESTION TO A. M. GERTIK: Do you confirm this testimony by BASHKIROV?

ANSWER BY GERTIK: I have no grounds to deny this testimony by BASHKIROV, but I do not remember the conversation's content. Besides the persons listed, MIRONOV, a former Zinovievist, was at Vladimir LEVIN's. At that time MIRONOV headed the bread-baking department of the LSPO [1].

BASHKIROV                                                                          GERTIK
