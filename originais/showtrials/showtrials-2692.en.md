INTERROGATION RECORD

OF YUSKIN, Ignaty Grigorievich, dated 8/XII/1934.

Conducted by LYUSHKOV, Deputy Head of the SPO, GUGB, NKVD, and FEDOROV, operational officer of the SPO, GUGB. —

QUESTION: The investigation has information that, in conversations with NIKOLAEV, you expressed your anti-Soviet views and sentiments, which gave NIKOLAEV grounds to speak to you of terror against the Communist Party leaders?

ANSWER: I admit that I did indeed express to NIKOLAEV my anti-Soviet sentiments and dissatisfaction with the Party leadership.

I have read the testimony; it has been correctly taken down from my words.

YUSKIN.

INTERROGATED BY:

DEPUTY HEAD OF THE SPO, GUGB, NKVD — LYUSHKOV

OPERATIONAL OFFICER OF THE SPO, GUGB — Fedorov.

Certified true: illegible
