INTERROGATION RECORD

of BRAVO, Boris Lvovich,

dated December 19, 1934.

In 1928 I was serving exile in the city of Kainsk for participation in anti-Party activities in the ranks of the former Zinovievist opposition. Many Trotskyists engaged in active struggle against the Party were serving exile with me, including a certain SIROTA, a Trotskyist formerly working in the Kiev organization. He once told me about a letter he had received from Ukraine reporting the discovery in the Donbass of a youth group of Trotskyists that had organized an expropriation and was preparing terrorist acts against the KP(b)U leadership. From SIROTA's account of this fact, it followed that he sympathized with the actions of the group uncovered in the Donbass, and to my direct question about his attitude toward expropriations and terror SIROTA answered: “Such methods can be used, but one must not talk about them.”

QUESTION: Did you report this fact to Party authorities after your reinstatement in the Party's ranks?

ANSWER: No, I informed no one about it.

QUESTION: How can you explain not having informed the Party of the terrorist tendencies of the Trotskyist SIROTA known to you?

ANSWER: I forgot at the time to report this to Party authorities. I now understand that I acted wrongly by not informing them of SIROTA's terrorist tendencies.

I ask that it be entered in the record that, after SIROTA expressed solidarity with the actions of the terrorist Trotskyist group liquidated in the Donbass, I expressed my indignation to him, saying that for this alone (meaning his attitude toward terror) he should be shot. —

Recorded correctly from my words and read by me. — BRAVO.

INTERROGATED BY: ASSISTANT HEAD OF THE OO OF THE GUGB OF THE NKVD — GENDIN.

Certified correct:
