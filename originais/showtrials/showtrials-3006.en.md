INTERROGATION RECORD

OF NIKOLAEV, L. V. — dated December 13, 1934.

QUESTION: Do you acknowledge that you belonged to a counterrevolutionary group of former oppositionists existing in Leningrad, consisting of KOTOLYNOV, SHATSKY, YUSKIN, and others?

ANSWER: Yes, I confirm that I belonged to a group of former oppositionists consisting of KOTOLYNOV, SHATSKY, YUSKIN, and others, which carried out counterrevolutionary work.

QUESTION: What political views did the group's members hold?

ANSWER: The group's members stood on the platform of the Trotskyist-Zinovievist bloc. They considered it necessary to replace the existing Party leadership by every possible means.

QUESTION: Who authorized KIROV's murder?

ANSWER: KIROV's murder was authorized by the group's members KOTOLYNOV and SHATSKY on behalf of the entire group.

QUESTION: What instructions did you receive from KOTOLYNOV and SHATSKY on how to conduct yourself during the investigation?

ANSWER: I was to portray KIROV's murder as an individual act, in order to conceal the Zinovievist group's participation in it.

Recorded correctly from my words. The record has been read aloud to me.

L. NIKOLAEV

INTERROGATED BY:

DEPUTY PEOPLE'S COMMISSAR FOR INTERNAL AFFAIRS OF THE USSR — AGRANOV

HEAD OF THE EKO OF THE GU GB OF THE NKVD OF THE USSR — MIRONOV

ASSISTANT HEAD OF THE EKO OF THE GUGB OF THE NKVD OF THE USSR — DMITRIEV
