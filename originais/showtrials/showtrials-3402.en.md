INTERROGATION RECORD

Of TARASOV, Ivan Ivanovich. —

Dated December 23, 1934.

I consider it necessary to report the following additional fact to the investigation: at the end of 1927, during discussion of questions of opposition work, the Komsomol member STREMYAKOV expressed this thought: “it is time to put dynamite into action.” This conversation took place in the presence of myself and KOTOLYNOV in Moscow, at Aleksandr BATASHOV's apartment on Sadovaya-Triumfalnaya Street, in the Tirol Hotel, no. 44.

I draw the investigation's attention to this fact because I consider there to be an unquestionable political connection between NIKOLAEV's shot at Comrade KIROV and the extremist statements of STREMYAKOV, who was then associated with the opposition. Personally I never saw STREMYAKOV again; at that time he worked on the editorial staff of Komsomolskaya Pravda. We reacted negatively to his statements; I simply threw him out.

I recall that in later years such conversations took place at meetings among the Zinovievists: “BUKHARIN has been brought back into work, but ZINOVIEV is not allowed to work.” At the same time anti-Soviet statements were made against Comrade STALIN.

On the question of the country's defense capability, around 1927–1928 it was said: “We are not strong, our army is not up to standard, and therefore a possible war will bring the defeat of the Soviet Union.”

I do not remember who held these conversations or under what circumstances.

In 1932 KOTOLYNOV and I visited M. NATANSON; among other conversations, NATANSON then told us about the decision to build the VIEM, accompanying this report with remarks approximately as follows: they are building without regard for difficulties, without regard for the material position of the working people in the country, and are guided not by considerations of state, but “the leaders of the All-Union Communist Party (Bolsheviks) are building monuments to themselves.”

TARASOV. —

INTERROGATED BY: HEAD OF THE 6TH SECTION OF THE SPO OF THE GUGB, KORKIN.
