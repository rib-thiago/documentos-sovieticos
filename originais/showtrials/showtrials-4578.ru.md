| 5. | [ПАЙО,](<https://ru.openlist.wiki/%D0%9F%D0%B0%D0%B9%D0%BE_%D0%A4%D0%B5%D0%BB%D0%B8%D0%BA%D1%81_%D0%90%D0%BB%D0%B5%D0%BA%D1%81%D0%B0%D0%BD%D0%B4%D1%80%D0%BE%D0%B2%D0%B8%D1%87_(1902)>) [Федор Александрович](<https://ru.openlist.wiki/%D0%9F%D0%B0%D0%B9%D0%BE_%D0%A4%D0%B5%D0%BB%D0%B8%D0%BA%D1%81_%D0%90%D0%BB%D0%B5%D0%BA%D1%81%D0%B0%D0%BD%D0%B4%D1%80%D0%BE%D0%B2%D0%B8%D1%87_(1902)>) | – 1902 г\<ода\> р\<ождения\>, чл\<ен\> ВКП(б) с 1924 г.
