RECORD

of a confrontation between the accused MYASNIKOV Nikolai Petrovich

and LEVIN Vladimir Solomonovich, conducted on December 25, 1934.

After mutually confirming that they knew one another personally, the accused testified:

QUESTION TO THE ACCUSED MYASNIKOV: What do you know about LEVIN V. S.'s membership in the organization of former participants in the so-called Zinovievist opposition and his role in it?

ANSWER: I know LEVIN V. as a member of the center of the organization of former participants in the Zinovievist opposition through working with him in that center until recently. LEVIN controlled communications with the organization's Moscow center and was in fact the authorized representative of the organization's Moscow center.

MYASNIKOV.

QUESTION TO THE ACCUSED LEVIN V. S.: Do you confirm MYASNIKOV's testimony?

ANSWER: I confirm MYASNIKOV's testimony that I was indeed the authorized representative of the organization's Moscow center and a member of the center of the Leningrad organization of former participants in the Zinovievist opposition. I remained the leader of the Leningrad organization and the authorized representative of the Moscow center until recently. Although the center of the Leningrad organization had in fact ceased to exist from the middle of 1933, I nevertheless continued my contacts with individual members of that center until recently.

LEVIN.

Confrontation conducted by:

Head of the EKO of the GUGB of the NKVD of the USSR — MIRONOV.

DEPUTY HEAD OF THE SPO OF THE GUGV OF THE NKVD OF THE USSR LYUSHKOV

HEAD OF SECTION I OF THE SPO OF THE GUGB OF THE NKVD OF THE USSR PETROVSKY.

Certified true: Churbanova
