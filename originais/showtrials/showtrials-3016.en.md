INTERROGATION RECORD

OF NIKOLAEV Leonid Vasilyevich

14 December 1934.

Question: Whom can you name as accomplices in preparing the terrorist act against Comrade STALIN?

Answer: An accomplice in preparing the terrorist act against STALIN is ERMOLAEVA, a member of the All-Union Communist Party (Bolsheviks) and a third-year student at the Leningrad Industrial Academy.

When KOTOLYNOV and SHATSKY told me in confidence that they were preparing a terrorist act against STALIN, I began to find out whom among my acquaintances could be used for this purpose. From conversations with YUSKIN I established that ERMOLAEVA might be suitable. From conversations with ERMOLAEVA I know that she has openly anti-party sentiments; from YUSKIN's words I know that ERMOLAEVA has outright terrorist sentiments. Knowing ERMOLAEVA more closely than I did, YUSKIN recommended using her in preparations for the terrorist act in Moscow, especially since she has many acquaintances there.

I was unable to speak with her personally, since at that time I was intensely occupied with establishing contact with consulates.

However, I telephoned ERMOLAEVA and asked her, without saying for what purpose, to provide material support because I intended to leave for Moscow shortly. ERMOLAEVA asked how I had learned her telephone number; I replied that I had obtained it from YUSKIN.

Since I was not certain it would be possible to involve ERMOLAEVA in preparing the act, I did not tell KOTOLYNOV about her.

Subsequently, occupied with preparing the terrorist act against KIROV, I no longer approached ERMOLAEVA. —

Correctly dictated from my words and read by me.

\<L. NIKOLAEV\>

INTERROGATED BY:

DEPUTY PEOPLE'S COMMISSAR OF INTERNAL AFFAIRS OF THE USSR — AGRANOV

HEAD OF THE EKO, GUGB, NKVD OF THE USSR — MIRONOV

ASSISTANT TO THE HEAD OF THE EKO, GUGB, NKVD OF THE USSR — DMITRIEV
