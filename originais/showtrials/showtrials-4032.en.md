INTERROGATION RECORD

Of TARTAKOVSKAYA, Yulia Borisovna, dated January 3, 1935.

Question: Do you acknowledge that the conversation SHAROV had with you at his apartment in Moscow, in the presence of SHEPSHELEVA [1] and ILYINA, was counterrevolutionary in character?

Answer: Yes, I acknowledge that this conversation was counterrevolutionary in character. —

TARTAKOVSKAYA.

INTERROGATED BY THE HEAD OF THE 1ST SECTION OF THE SPO OF THE UGB — LULOV.

Certified correct: Churbanova

RGASPI, fond 671, inventory 1, file 126, folio 254.

[1] In the text, erroneously: “Shepshelevich”.
