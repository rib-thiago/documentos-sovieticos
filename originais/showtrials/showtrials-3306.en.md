RECORD

of the supplementary interrogation of GORSHENIN, I. S.,

dated December 18, 1934.

Question: In your testimony of December 17 of this year you stated that, in Moscow, on the initiative of G. E. ZINOVIEV and L. B. KAMENEV, two illegal seminars were created to study all decisions of the VKP(b) Central Committee in economics and politics. Describe in greater detail everything you know about these seminars.

Answer: Following ZINOVIEV's and KAMENEV's reinstatement in the Party and their move from Kaluga to Moscow, approximately in September 1928, two seminars were organized under the personal direction of ZINOVIEV and KAMENEV. Both seminars aimed at a thorough study of Party decisions and the actual economic and political situation of the country. The seminars met in the private apartments of ZINOVIEV (Kaloshny Lane \[1\]) and KAMENEV (Karmanitsky Lane), with the following membership:

Political seminar: G. E. ZINOVIEV (leader), EVDOKIMOV, KOSTINA, KHARITONOV, KAREV, KOZHURO, Olga RAVICH, GERTIK, KUKLIN; I do not now remember the others; Bogdan, I think.

Economic seminar: L. B. KAMENEV (leader); KAPITONOV, Ivan Petrovich, former teacher at the Institute of Red Professors, now in Novosibirsk as deputy chairman of the regional planning body; SHAROV; REINGOLD, Isaak Isaakovich, former deputy people's commissar of Agriculture of the USSR; LEIKIN, Elkon, who works in the Committee for Higher Technical Education attached to the USSR TsIK; BABAKHAN, Sergei Yakovlevich, deputy head of the Planning Department of the RSFSR People's Commissariat of Agriculture; myself, GORSHENIN; SOKOLNIKOV, Grigory Yakovlevich; SHVALBE, Filipp, former secretary to KAMENEV. I do not now remember the seminar's other members.

Question: What tasks did these seminars set themselves?

Answer: Apart from a general and thorough study of the country's economy and political situation, the seminars envisaged revising the old platform of the Trotskyist-Zinovievist bloc in light of the changed situation.

Question: What assignments from ZINOVIEV and KAMENEV did you personally carry out?

Answer: I supplied them with statistical information on current conditions which I possessed through my work in the RSFSR Gosplan.

Question: How long did these seminars' sessions continue?

Answer: There were only two meetings — an organizational meeting and a practical one; approximately in October a broad underground meeting took place at Grigory Evseevich ZINOVIEV's apartment, attended by about 35 to 40 former oppositionists of the so-called Leningrad opposition. At this meeting, following L. B. KAMENEV's report, it was decided not to meet again in such large numbers, to avoid unpleasant consequences. ZINOVIEV personally held that meetings should not be organized, but connections should not be lost and must certainly be preserved.

I sign in confirmation of the correctness of my testimony: GORSHENIN.

Interrogated by: DEPUTY HEAD OF THE SPO OF THE UGB — STROMIN

Certified correct:
