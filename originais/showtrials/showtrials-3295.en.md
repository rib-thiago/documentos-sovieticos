SUPPLEMENTARY TESTIMONY

of ALEKSANDROV, Aleksandr Ivanovich.

I consider it necessary to inform the investigation that, during the opposition years (1926–1927), former participants in the counterrevolutionary Zinovievist-Trotskyist bloc were educated by the leaders of this bloc in the fiercest hatred toward the Party leaders — comrades STALIN, KAGANOVICH, MOLOTOV, KIROV and others.

The leaders of the Zinovievist opposition nurtured particular hatred for comrade STALIN, accusing him of dictatorial rule and Bonapartism.

The leaders of the Zinovievist-Trotskyist bloc always referred to the Party leaders — comrades STALIN, MOLOTOV, KIROV and KAGANOVICH — in grossly insulting terms, calling comrade KIROV, for example, comrade STALIN's “echo” and the embodiment of the “Thermidor” which, in these leaders' opinion, had set in within the Party. We were imbued with the idea that the Party leaders, comrades STALIN, MOLOTOV, KIROV, KAGANOVICH and others, had replaced internal Party democracy with dictatorial rule; in connection with this, the leaders of the counterrevolutionary Zinovievist-Trotskyist bloc repeatedly expressed the view that it was necessary to “remove comrade STALIN” and replace him with ZINOVIEV or TROTSKY. As a result of the cultivation of such hatred toward the Party leaders, extremist sentiments were bound to arise among individual participants in the counterrevolutionary Zinovievist-Trotskyist bloc.

Recorded correctly from my words; read by me.

A. ALEKSANDROV.

December 17, 1934.

INTERROGATED BY:

Authorized officer of SPO-2 — SHAKULEV.

CERTIFIED CORRECT: M. Glazkov
