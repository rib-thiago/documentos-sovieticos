INTERROGATION RECORD

OF NIKOLAEV LEONID VASILYEVICH, 14 DECEMBER 1934.

QUESTION: Whom can you name as accomplices in carrying out the terrorist act against Comrade STALIN?

ANSWER: An accomplice was Roman KULISHER. KULISHER Roman is the husband of Olga DRAULE, my wife's sister. In 1929 KULISHER worked at the Vozrozhdenie factory, where he participated in anti-party activities and organized an unprincipled feud against the factory's party leadership, for which he was removed together with a group of his sympathizers and expelled from the party. After leaving the Vozrozhdenie factory, KULISHER joined the Marti Works in 1930. There he contacted a number of former oppositionists who had formed a counterrevolutionary Zinovievist group at the works.

KULISHER held meetings at his apartment with members of this group; on one occasion, when I was at his apartment, he and the workers from the Marti Works whom he had invited expressed openly anti-party views in my presence on the principal questions of party policy, sharply condemning the existing party leadership.

I know that members of the group were arrested at the works in 1931, but KULISHER was not arrested; apparently he escaped arrest by chance.

During 1934 I met KULISHER many times. In conversations both at his apartment and mine, KULISHER suggested that I engage in counterrevolutionary work. I replied that I preferred committing a terrorist act. Subsequently I told KULISHER in confidence that I was preparing a terrorist act against KIROV. KULISHER spoke approvingly of these preparations.

In view of KULISHER's sentiments, I decided to involve him in preparing the terrorist act against STALIN and obtained his consent to travel with me to Moscow to ascertain the circumstances and possibilities for committing it.

This trip to Moscow with KULISHER did not take place because I was occupied with preparing the terrorist act against KIROV.

I did not tell KOTOLYNOV of my intention to involve KULISHER in organizing the terrorist act, intending to do so only when carrying it out became a concrete prospect.

Correctly dictated from my words and read by me.

L. NIKOLAEV

INTERROGATED BY:

DEPUTY PEOPLE'S COMMISSAR OF INTERNAL AFFAIRS OF THE USSR — AGRANOV

HEAD OF THE EKO, GUGB, NKVD OF THE USSR — MIRONOV

ASSISTANT TO THE HEAD OF THE EKO, GUGB, NKVD OF THE USSR — DMITRIEV
