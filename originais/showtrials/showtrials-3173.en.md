INTERROGATION RECORD

OF NIKOLAEV, Leonid Vasilyevich.

19 December 1934.

QUESTION: Do you know of the existence in other cities of the Union of counterrevolutionary groups belonging to the Zinovievist-Trotskyist organization?

ANSWER: I know from KOTOLYNOV that a central group of the Zinovievist-Trotskyist organization exists in Moscow. This group includes ZINOVIEV and KAMENEV. He named no other persons to me.

QUESTION: Did the central group of the organization know that you had been assigned to kill Comrade KIROV?

ANSWER: I do not know. When KOTOLYNOV assigned me to murder KIROV, he told me, without naming names, that this was being done on the instructions of the organization's leaders.

ADDED: I entered the counterrevolutionary organization with terrorist sentiments and with recruited accomplices and assistants in the persons of YUSKIN, KULISHER, and ERMOLAEVA.

As a result of meetings and conversations with members of the organization, beginning in IX–X I drew up a plan for carrying out a terrorist act against KIROV, which was implemented with the sanction of the Zinovievist-Trotskyist organization.

The last part of the record was written in my own hand, and the beginning of the record in the investigator's hand.

L. NIKOLAEV

INTERROGATED BY:

HEAD OF THE EKO OF THE GUGB OF THE NKVD OF THE USSR: MIRONOV.

ASSISTANT TO THE HEAD OF THE EKO OF THE GUGB OF THE NKVD OF THE USSR — DMITRIEV.
