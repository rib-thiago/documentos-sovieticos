INTERROGATION RECORD

OF GERTIK Artyom Moiseyevich, conducted by RUTKOVSKY

December 10, 1934.

1\. GERTIK

2\. Artyom Moiseyevich

4\. city of Kazan

5\. city of Moscow, 2nd Kolobovsky Lane, building No. 2, apartment 7.

7\. Daughter Nina FISHMAN, aged twenty-eight; her husband FISHMAN Ilya Mironovich works at the People's Commissariat of Foreign Trade.

8\. None

9\. Secondary school

10\. A Party member since 1902, with a six-month interruption from the Fifteenth Party Congress, and from February 1933 to August 1934.

QUESTION: With which members of the former Leningrad opposition were you connected?

ANSWER: I had no organizational connection with anyone. In recent years I met Comrades GORSHENIN, EVDOKIMOV, KUKLIN, BAKAEV, KHARITONOV (before his departure abroad), FEDOROV, and SHAROV, who live in Moscow, and BASHKIROV, LEVIN, and TSVIBAK. After returning from exile, from September 1933 onward, I met regularly only with GORSHENIN.

QUESTION: What do you know about the anti-Party sentiments of the persons mentioned above?

ANSWER: I observed no anti-Party sentiments in any of them.

QUESTION: What do you know about meetings attended by the persons listed above?

ANSWER: I know of an occasion in 1932 when the following comrades gathered at BAKAEV's apartment: myself, BAKAEV, KOSTINA, EVDOKIMOV, KUKLIN, and, I believe, GORSHENIN.

KOSTINA stated that she would speak at her cell meeting to protest ZINOVIEV's expulsion from the Party. She proposed that those present also speak at their cell meetings. KOSTINA's proposal was rejected, and she too was advised not to speak. The refusal to speak was justified on the grounds that such a statement would be wrong, since ZINOVIEV had failed to inform the Party about the document of RYUTIN's organization, and also because speaking in ZINOVIEV's defense would not be supported by the masses and would worsen rather than improve his position.

QUESTION: Did you inform the Party's control bodies about the meeting and KOSTINA's intentions?

ANSWER: No, I did not.

A. GERTIK.

Interrogated by: RUTKOVSKY.
