JUDGMENT

IN THE NAME OF THE UNION OF SOVIET SOCIALIST REPUBLICS

THE TRAVELING SESSION OF THE MILITARY COLLEGIUM OF THE SUPREME COURT

OF THE UNION OF SOVIET SOCIALIST REPUBLICS,

composed of:

PRESIDING JUDGE V. V. Ulrikh,

Members: I. O. MATULEVICH and A. D. GORYACHEV,

With A. A. BATNER as secretary,

without the participation of the prosecution or defense in the court hearing, in the city of Leningrad,

on December 28–29, 1934, examined the case against:

1\. NIKOLAEV Leonid Vasilyevich, aged thirty, an employee, a former member of the All-Union Communist Party (Bolsheviks), expelled from the Party for violating Party discipline but reinstated in view of his statement of repentance;

2\. KOTOLYNOV Ivan Ivanovich, aged twenty-nine, a student, a former Party member expelled for belonging to the Zinovievist anti-Soviet group but reinstated following his official statement of full solidarity with the policies of the Party and Soviet power;

3\. SHATSKY Nikolai Nikolaevich, aged thirty-five, an engineer, expelled from the Party in 1927 for belonging to the Zinovievist anti-Soviet group, non-Party;

4\. RUMYANTSEV Vladimir Vasilyevich, aged thirty-two, an employee, a former Party member expelled for belonging to the Zinovievist anti-Soviet group but reinstated following his official statement of full solidarity with the policies of the Party and Soviet power;

5\. MANDELSHTAM Sergey Osipovich, aged thirty-eight, an employee, a former Party member expelled for belonging to the Zinovievist anti-Soviet group but reinstated on the same grounds;

6\. MYASNIKOV Nikolai Petrovich, aged thirty-four, an employee, twice expelled from the Party for belonging to the Zinovievist anti-Soviet group but reinstated on the same grounds;

7\. LEVIN Vladimir Solomonovich, aged thirty-seven, an employee, a former Party member expelled for belonging to the Zinovievist anti-Soviet group but reinstated on the same grounds;

8\. SOSITSKY Lev Ilyich, aged thirty-five, an employee, a former Party member expelled for belonging to the Zinovievist anti-Soviet group but reinstated on the same grounds;

9\. SOKOLOV Georgy Vasilyevich, aged thirty, a student, a former Party member;

10\. YUSKIN Ignaty Grigoryevich, aged thirty-six, an employee, a former Party member;

11\. ZVEZDOV Vasily Ivanovich, aged thirty-two, a student, a former Party member;

12\. ANTONOV Nikolai Semyonovich, aged thirty-one, a student, a former Party member;

13\. KHANIK Lev Osipovich, aged thirty-two, an employee, a former Party member;

14\. TOLMAZOV Andrey Ilyich, aged thirty-five, a student, a former Party member,

all fourteen charged with crimes under Articles 58-8 and 58-11 of the RSFSR Criminal Code.

The traveling session of the Military Collegium of the Supreme Court of the Union of Soviet Socialist Republics established the following through the judicial investigation:

Despite the capitulation of the former Zinovievist anti-Soviet group, the underground work of its most active members did not cease and continued until the most recent times. In particular, in Leningrad in 1933–1934, an underground counterrevolutionary terrorist group formed and operated among former members of the Zinovievist anti-Soviet group; having lost all hope of support from the masses, and seeking to disorganize the leadership of the Soviet Government and change the policies of the Party and government in the spirit of the so-called Zinovievist-Trotskyist platform, it turned to methods of terrorist struggle against the leaders of Soviet power.

All activity of the Leningrad terrorist counterrevolutionary group was directed by the “Leningrad Center,” which included former active figures of the Zinovievist anti-Soviet group: LEVIN V., RUMYANTSEV V., KOTOLYNOV, MANDELSHTAM, MYASNIKOV, SOSITSKY, SHATSKY, and L. NIKOLAEV.

The “Leningrad Center,” so constituted, proceeding from the criminal aims described above and also motivated by revenge against Comrade S. M. KIROV, a member of the Presidium of the USSR Central Executive Committee, a member of the Politburo of the Party Central Committee, and secretary of the Party's Central and Leningrad Committees, who had previously ideologically and politically defeated the Leningrad anti-Soviet Zinovievist group, devised the plan and organized Comrade KIROV's murder.

An active member of the terrorist counterrevolutionary group, L. NIKOLAEV, under the direct leadership of “Leningrad Center” member KOTOLYNOV, with the active assistance and support of members of the Leningrad underground counterrevolutionary terrorist group SHATSKY, YUSKIN, SOKOLOV, ANTONOV, ZVEZDOV, TOLMAZOV, and KHANIK, after lengthy preparations and carrying out a direct assignment from the “Leningrad Center,” killed Comrade Sergey Mironovich KIROV with a revolver shot on December 1, 1934, in Leningrad, at Smolny, on the premises of the Leningrad Regional Committee of the All-Union Communist Party (Bolsheviks).

Not expecting to achieve their criminal aims solely through terrorist actions within the country, the leaders of the terrorist counterrevolutionary group under their patronage placed their direct hopes on armed intervention by foreign states.

The same L. NIKOLAEV, at the suggestion of “Leningrad Center” member KOTOLYNOV, repeatedly visited the Latvian consul in Leningrad, BISSENEKS, in September of this year, and negotiated with him to place at his, the consul's, disposal material of an openly provocative nature on the political and economic condition of the Soviet Union, stipulating that financial assistance be given to him, NIKOLAEV L., for the needs of the counterrevolutionary group, to which the consul agreed. On visiting the consul, NIKOLAEV L. received 5,000 rubles from him, of which he gave 4,500 rubles to KOTOLYNOV for expenses in organizing terrorist attacks.

Thus the guilt of NIKOLAEV L., KOTOLYNOV, MYASNIKOV, SHATSKY, MANDELSHTAM, SOKOLOV, ZVEZDOV, YUSKIN, RUMYANTSEV, ANTONOV, KHANIK, TOLMAZOV, LEVIN, and SOSITSKY in committing crimes under Articles 58-8 and 58-11 of the RSFSR Criminal Code is established.

On the basis of the foregoing and pursuant to the resolution of the Central Executive Committee of the Union of Soviet Socialist Republics of December 1, 1934, THE TRAVELING SESSION OF THE MILITARY COLLEGIUM OF THE SUPREME COURT OF THE UNION OF SOVIET SOCIALIST REPUBLICS

SENTENCED:

1\) NIKOLAEV Leonid Vasilyevich,

2\) KOTOLYNOV Ivan Ivanovich,

3\) SHATSKY Nikolai Nikolaevich,

4\) RUMYANTSEV Vladimir Vasilyevich,

5\) MANDELSHTAM Sergey Osipovich,

6\) MYASNIKOV Nikolai Petrovich,

7\) LEVIN Vladimir Solomonovich,

8\) SOSITSKY Lev Ilyich,

9\) SOKOLOV Georgy Vasilyevich,

10\) YUSKIN Ignaty Grigoryevich,

11\) ZVEZDOV Vasily Ivanovich,

12\) ANTONOV Nikolai Semyonovich,

13\) KHANIK Lev Osipovich, and

14\) TOLMAZOV Andrey Ilyich,

all fourteen to the supreme penalty — execution by shooting.

All property belonging to them personally shall be confiscated.

The judgment is final, not subject to appeal, and shall be carried out immediately.
