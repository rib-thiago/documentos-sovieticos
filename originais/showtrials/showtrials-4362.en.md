CONFRONTATION RECORD

Between the accused EVDOKIMOV Grigory Yeremeevich and GORSHENIN Ivan Stepanovich

January 9, 1935.

The accused EVDOKIMOV G. E. and GORSHENIN I. S., after confirming that they know each other, testified:

QUESTION TO EVDOKIMOV: What do you know about GORSHENIN I. S.'s membership in the Moscow center of the counterrevolutionary Zinovievist organization?

ANSWER: I know for certain that GORSHENIN Ivan Stepanovich was, during the recent period, a member of the Moscow center of our organization.

QUESTION TO GORSHENIN: Do you acknowledge your membership in the center of the counterrevolutionary organization?

ANSWER: Yes, I do. Contrary to my previous testimony, I inform the investigation that I was indeed, during the recent period, a member of the Moscow center of the Zinovievist organization.

QUESTION TO EVDOKIMOV: Describe GORSHENIN's activity in your organization.

ANSWER: Within the organization, GORSHENIN was one of the persons best informed on questions of the country's economic situation and often presented these questions to us tendentiously, using for this purpose data he obtained from Gosplan.

In addition, he had more connections in Moscow than some of us members of the center, which he also used in the organization's interests. GORSHENIN's apartment served as a place for our clandestine meetings.

QUESTION TO GORSHENIN: Do you confirm this testimony by EVDOKIMOV?

ANSWER: Yes, I confirm it in full.

The confrontation was conducted by:

DEPUTY HEAD OF THE SPO OF THE GUGB OF THE USSR NKVD — LYUSHKOV

ASSISTANT HEAD OF THE SPO OF THE GUGB OF THE USSR NKVD — RUTKOVSKY

DEPUTY HEAD OF THE SPO OF THE UGB FOR THE LENINGRAD REGION — STROMIN.

Certified true: A. Svetlova
