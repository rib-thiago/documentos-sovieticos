INTERROGATION RECORD

OF ROGACHEVA, Ekaterina Vasilyevna

dated December 12, 1934.

QUESTION: In previous interrogations you stated that you had last seen Pyotr NIKOLAEV more than a month ago. However, the investigation knows that you met Pyotr NIKOLAEV in the last days of November. What can you testify about this?

ANSWER: I admit that, in fact, until now I concealed my meeting with Pyotr NIKOLAEV in the last days of November. This meeting took place under the following circumstances: at about six in the evening — I do not remember the exact date, apparently November 26 — when I arrived at work at the landscaping trust, our gardener KLIMOV told me that my brother Leonid had asked for me in my absence. Soon afterward Leonid came to my workplace a second time and, calling me out into the street, pointed out Pyotr NIKOLAEV standing at the corner of Chugunnaya and Uspenskaya Streets, saying that he wanted to speak to me. By this time I already knew that Pyotr NIKOLAEV had deserted from the army and was hiding from the authorities.

Together with Leonid I approached Pyotr, and we briefly discussed Pyotr's situation. I expressed my view that it was necessary and advisable for Pyotr NIKOLAEV to return to his unit. At Pyotr NIKOLAEV's request I gave him three packets of cigarettes.

QUESTION: Why, knowing that Pyotr NIKOLAEV had deserted from the army and was hiding from the authorities, did you take no steps to have him detained when you met him on November 26?

ANSWER: I cannot explain my conduct. I hoped that Pyotr would return to his unit of his own accord.

QUESTION: What did Leonid NIKOLAEV tell you about his intention to murder Comrades STALIN and KIROV?

ANSWER: Leonid NIKOLAEV told me nothing about an intention to kill STALIN and KIROV. In conversations with Leonid NIKOLAEV during our latest meetings, he expressed his hostility toward the Communist Party leadership in harsh terms, complained about the internal Party regime, was extremely embittered, and considered it necessary to bring about a change in the Party leadership by force.

QUESTION: How did you react to these expressions of his feelings and intentions by Leonid NIKOLAEV?

ANSWER: I tried in every way to dissuade him, arguing that as soon as he started working his mood would change, and recommended that he apply directly to the Party Control Commission to have any doubts clarified. To this Leonid NIKOLAEV replied: “I know myself what I must do.”

Recorded faithfully from my words and read by me:

E. ROGACHEVA.

INTERROGATED BY:

DEPUTY HEAD OF THE OO OF THE UGB OF THE NKVD — SOSNOVSKY

ASSISTANT HEAD OF THE OO OF THE GUGB OF THE NKVD — GENDIN
