INTERROGATION RECORD

OF MYASNIKOV, Nikolai Petrovich,

18 December 1934.

Confirming my previous testimony about the Zinovievist-Trotskyist counterrevolutionary organization and my counterrevolutionary activities as a member of the center of the Leningrad counterrevolutionary organization, I consider it necessary to bring the following to the investigation's attention.

I deeply repent of the grave crime I committed against the party and Soviet power by taking the counterrevolutionary path. If, during the first period of our counterrevolutionary struggle against the party, we Zinovievists still had some semblance of a platform, under the impact of the successes of socialist construction this so-called platform dissipated like smoke. Having no program whatsoever, our counterrevolutionary organization, under the direct influence of its “leaders,” ZINOVIEV and KAMENEV, took the path of unprincipled struggle against the party leadership. The blind hatred for the leadership of the AUCP(b), particularly Comrade STALIN, that our “leaders” fostered in us in the name of their undisguised desire to come to power, inevitably led us to terror. The logic of struggle without ideas, without principles, inevitably led us to fascist methods of struggle against the party.

Concealing our counterrevolutionary core behind phrases about the struggle for Leninism, for a Leninist Politburo, as our “leaders” ZINOVIEV and KAMENEV liked to put it, we had essentially slid into fascist methods of action.

I ask the party and the Soviet government to give me the opportunity, through persistent and honest work wherever the Soviet authorities deem possible, to remove the shameful stain of membership in the counterrevolutionary Zinovievist organization and obtain the right to bear the lofty title of citizen of the socialist motherland.

MYASNIKOV.

Interrogated by —

DEPUTY HEAD OF THE SPO OF THE GUGB — LYUSHKOV.

certified true:

RGASPI, fond 671, inventory 1, file 120, folio 173.
