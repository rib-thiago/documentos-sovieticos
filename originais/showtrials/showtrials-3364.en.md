Leningrad: SPO of the UGB of the UNKVD for Leningrad Region

INTERROGATION RECORD

Conducted by the Head of Section of the SPO of the GUGB of the NKVD, RUTKOVSKY.

December 20, 1934.

GERTIK, Artem Moiseevich.

SUPPLEMENTARY.

QUESTION: NAUMOV's testimony has established that in recent years you participated in creating a counterrevolutionary organization in Leningrad composed of former participants in the Zinovievist-Trotskyist bloc. What testimony can you give on this question?

ANSWER: I participated in creating the organization in 1925 and January 1926. After that I met only individual persons: Vladimir LEVIN, BASHKIROV and TSVIBAK. I knew that LEVIN met former Zinovievists in Leningrad, but I did not know of the existence of a formally constituted organization.

QUESTION: Tell us about your meetings and conversations with LEVIN, BASHKIROV and TSVIBAK.

ANSWER: LEVIN: I cannot now recall all my past meetings with him before 1932. In 1932 I saw him in Leningrad, I think two or three times, and while on business trips to Moscow LEVIN sometimes called at my home. In conversations with LEVIN I informed him of rumors circulating among us. I testified in the previous record about the trip LEVIN and I made together to ZINOVIEV's dacha in 1932; I can add nothing to my previous testimony. LEVIN's sentiments were as anti-Party as those of the participants in the former Zinovievist-Trotskyist bloc living in Moscow.

BASHKIROV: I met him repeatedly. He told me about the political sentiments of the students. I testified in the previous records about the details of my conversations with BASHKIROV. I can add nothing to my previous testimony.

MYASNIKOV: I saw him once in 1932 at LEVIN's apartment. In conversation he told me that many letters were coming to the Executive Committee from peasants in the countryside of Leningrad Region saying that the peasants were starving and fleeing the villages. The peasants were also writing similar letters from the countryside directly to workers. I had no political conversations with TSVIBAK, whom I saw in a psychiatric hospital in 1932.

QUESTION: The investigation has established that the terrorist act against Comrade KIROV was committed by L. NIKOLAEV, a participant in the Zinovievist organization in Leningrad. Do you acknowledge that the center of the Zinovievist counterrevolutionary organization and, in particular, you as a member of that center bear responsibility for this murder?

ANSWER: I regard this act as the most abominable crime, directed at the heart of the Party leadership. If L. NIKOLAEV was indeed connected with the Zinovievist counterrevolutionary organization in Leningrad, our entire Moscow Zinovievist group must bear moral and political responsibility for this terrorist act. —

A. GERTIK.

Interrogated by: RUTKOVSKY.
