INTERROGATION RECORD

Of KASPERSKY Ivan Feliksovich —

January 9, 1935.

Question: The investigation has information that you are a member of the Zinovievist organization. Relate what you know about the organization's work.

Answer: As I already testified in my record of January 7 of this year, our meetings and conversations were intended to gather the old Zinovievist cadres. The preservation of these contacts led to the formation of the Zinovievist organization, of which I, KASPERSKY Ivan Feliksovich, was also a member.

Our group of old Zinovievists, consisting of the active members of the Vasilyevsky Ostrov district, was headed by YAKOVLEV Moisey Nakhimovich. This group included its leader and ideological inspirer YAKOVLEV Moisey and, as active members of the group, myself, KASPERSKY, FOKIN Aleksey Kirillovich, PANKO Adam Pavlovich, SKOROMNY Aleksey Fomich, SEMYONOV Semyon, KONDRATYEVA Yevdokiya, and SHEINBERG David Samoylovich (now deceased).

Question: In the record of January 7, 1935, you testified that at the apartments of your like-minded associates — YAKOVLEV Moisey, FOKIN, PANKO, BELONOGOV, SKOROMNY, and others — you held counterrevolutionary conversations.

Describe the nature of these conversations in detail.

Answer: As I have already testified, at our gatherings certain measures of the Party and government were subjected to criticism. Criticism was directed chiefly at the unbearable rates of industrialization, with the simultaneous lag of light industry and consumer goods, and the question of difficulties with bread was raised as confirmation of our views. At the same time, we informed one another about the position of our “leaders,” ZINOVIEV, KAMENEV, and EVDOKIMOV, showing interest in their fates and expressing sympathy.

Taken down correctly from my words and read by me, in confirmation of which I sign —

KASPERSKY.

Interrogated by: KOGAN M.

Certified true: Biryukov
