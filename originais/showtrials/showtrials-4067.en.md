INTERROGATION RECORD

Of TSATUROVA-BOLSHAKOVA, Vera Nikolaevna,

(supplementary).

Dated January 4, 1935.

Question: Which of your acquaintances visited your apartment from the beginning of July 1934 until the day of your arrest?

Answer: During this period (from July 1934 until the day of my arrest), the following persons visited our apartment:

1) ELKOVSKY, David, member of the All-Union Communist Party (Bolsheviks);

2) ZADORINA, Elena, member of the All-Union Communist Party (Bolsheviks); both visited several times.

3) BRENER, Mikhail, member of the All-Union Communist Party (Bolsheviks), works as the cultural and propaganda officer in the political department of the October Railway; visited once.

4) SARKISOV, came from Moscow and called on us with his wife about two months ago.

5) KYUTEMAYER, visited us once or twice in July or August 1934. —

The testimony has been recorded correctly from my words and read by me.

V. TSATUROVA.

INTERROGATED BY:

OFFICER OF THE 2ND SECTION OF THE SPO OF THE UGB — GOLUBEV.

Certified correct: Churbanova

RGASPI, fond 671, inventory 1, file 127, folio 73.
