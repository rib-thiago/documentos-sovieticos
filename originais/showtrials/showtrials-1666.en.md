RECORD OF INTERROGATION

On December 4, 1934, I, Deputy People’s Commissar for Internal Affairs — AGRANOV, interrogated the citizen NIKOLAEV Leonid Vasilyevich as an accused person, and he gave the following answers to the questions put to him:

QUESTION: What influence did your connections with f\<ormer\> Trotskyist oppositionists have on your decision to kill Comrade KIROV?

ANSWER: My decision to kill Comrade KIROV was influenced by my connections with the Trotskyists SHATSKY, Vanya KOTOLYNOV, Nikolai BARDIN, and oth\<ers\>. However, I knew these people as individuals, not as members of a group. I met with them over a number of years and, in particular, recently. I had not met with BARDIN lately.

QUESTION: Did these persons participate in your crime?

ANSWER: No, they did not. Around August th\<is\> y\<ear\>, while I was keeping watch during the day on the house where KIROV and CHUDOV lived — on Krasnykh Zor Street — I met SHATSKY. He complained to me about being cut off from the Party and about his dissatisfaction. He said that someone else in his place would be ready for anything. During our conversation, the secretary of the Regional Committee, CHUDOV, drove past in car No. 713. CHUDOV was traveling from work to his dacha in Sestroretsk. Comrade CHUDOV’s car stopped at a tram stop and then drove on. We both noticed the car and exchanged glances.

I saw KOTOLYNOV at the Polytechnic Institute in Leningrad before the October celebrations (November 4), but we did not speak. Shortly before that I had met SOKOLOV, a member of the AUCP(b), at Smolny. We left Smolny by tram and went home to the Vyborg Side. SOKOLOV has close ties with former oppositionists. He is a student at the Naval Academy. As a result of our conversation, we decided to establish closer relations. At Smolny we wanted to obtain tickets for the ceremonial meeting at the Mariinsky Theater on 6/XI. I had intended to attempt to assassinate KIROV on November 5 or 6, but later postponed my intention because of the October celebrations. My conversation with SOKOLOV amounted to mutual probing. SOKOLOV lives on Nizhegorodskaya Street. He graduated from an industrial technical school.

L. NIKOLAEV

Interrogation conducted by:

Deputy People’s Commissar for Internal Affairs AGRANOV

Head of the Economic Department (EKO) of the Main Directorate of State Security (GUGB), NKVD of the USSR, MIRONOV
