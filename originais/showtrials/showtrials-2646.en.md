VERDICT

IN THE NAME OF THE UNION OF SOVIET SOCIALIST REPUBLICS

TRAVELING SESSION OF THE MILITARY COLLEGIUM OF THE SUPREME COURT OF THE UNION OF SOVIET SOCIALIST REPUBLICS

composed of:

PRESIDING JUDGE V. V. ULRIKH

MEMBERS: I. O. MATULEVICH and A. D. GORIACHEV,

WITH SECRETARY A. A. BATNER,

without the participation of either prosecution or defense, in a court session held in the city of Leningrad on January 15–16, 1935, examined the case against:

1\. ZINOVIEV, Grigory Evseevich, 1883 y\<ear\> of b\<irth\>, an employee, former member of the AUCP(b), expelled from the Party in 1932 and reinstated in 1933 in connection with his declaration of complete solidarity with Party policy;

2\. EVDOKIMOV, Grigory Eremeevich, 1884 y\<ear\> of b\<irth\>, an employee, former member of the AUCP(b), previously expelled from the Party but reinstated for the same reasons;

3\. GERTIK, Artyom Moiseevich, 1879 y\<ear\> of b\<irth\>, an employee, former member of the AUCP(b), expelled from the Party in 1927 but reinstated for the same reasons;

4\. BAKAEV, Ivan Petrovich, 1887 y\<ear\> of b\<irth\>, former member of the AUCP(b), previously expelled from the Party but reinstated after submitting a statement of complete solidarity with the Party's general line;

5\. KAMENEV, Lev Borisovich, 1883 y\<ear\> of b\<irth\>, an employee, former member of the AUCP(b), expelled from the Party in 1932 but reinstated in 1933 for the same reasons;

6\. KUKLIN, Aleksandr Sergeevich, 1876 y\<ear\> of b\<irth\>, a for\<mer\> member of the AUCP(b), expelled from the Party in 1927 but reinstated in 1928 for the same reasons;

7\. SHAROV, Yakov Vasilievich, 1884 y\<ear\> of b\<irth\>, an employee, a for\<mer\> member of the AUCP(b), expelled from the Party in 1927 but reinstated in 1928 for the same reasons;

9\. GESSEN, Sergei Mikhailovich, 1898 y\<ear\> of b\<irth\>, an employee, a for\<mer\> member of the AUCP(b), expelled from the Party in 1927 for factional activity but reinstated for the same reasons;

10\. SAKHOV, Boris Naumovich, 1900 y\<ear\> of b\<irth\>, an employee, a for\<mer\> member of the AUCP(b), who had received a severe reprimand with warning for factional activity;

11\. TARASOV, Ivan Ivanovich, 1902 y\<ear\> of b\<irth\>, a student, a for\<mer\> member of the AUCP(b), expelled from the Party in 1927 for factional activity but reinstated in 1929 in connection with his declaration of complete solidarity with the policy of the Party and Soviet power;

12\. FAIVILOVICH, Leonid Yakovlevich, 1900 y\<ear\> of b\<irth\>, an employee, a for\<mer\> member of the AUCP(b), expelled from the Party in 1928 for factional activity but reinstated in 1929 for the same reasons;

15\. ANISHEV, Anatoly Isaevich, 1899 y\<ear\> of b\<irth\>, an employee, a for\<mer\> member of the AUCP(b), expelled in 1927 for factional activity but reinstated in 1928 in connection with his declaration of complete solidarity with the policy of the Party and Soviet power;

16\. TSARKOV, Nikolai Alekseevich, 1903 y\<ear\> of b\<irth\>, an employee, a for\<mer\> member of the AUCP(b), expelled from the Party for factional activity but reinstated in 1929 for the same reasons;

18\. PERIMOV, Aleksei Viktorovich, 1897 y\<ear\> of b\<irth\>, an employee, a for\<mer\> member of the AUCP(b), expelled from the Party in 1928 but reinstated in the same year for the same reasons, and

19\. FEDOROV, Grigory Fedorovich, 1891 y\<ear\> of b\<irth\>, an employee, a for\<mer\> member of the AUCP(b), expelled from the Party in 1927 for factional activity but reinstated in 1929 for the same reasons —

ALL NINETEEN FOR CRIMES UNDER ARTICLES 17, 58-8 AND 58-11 OF THE CRIMINAL CODE OF THE RSFSR.

THE JUDICIAL INVESTIGATION ESTABLISHED THE FOLLOWING:

The leaders of the former anti-Soviet Zinovievist group, who had not ceased their underground work aimed at achieving counterrevolutionary objectives in the spirit of the so-called Zinovievist–Trotskyist platform despite their capitulation, gathered their most active supporters around themselves and created an underground counterrevolutionary group.

For a number of years, from 1928 until December 1934, this underground counterrevolutionary group carried out counterrevolutionary work in Moscow, Leningrad, and other cities, its principal objective being to change the leadership of the Party and the Soviet government.

Until very recently, all the activities of this counterrevolutionary group were directed by the so-called “Moscow Center,” consisting of ZINOVIEV, GERTIK, KUKLIN, EVDOKIMOV, BAKAEV, SHAROV, GORSHENIN, FEDOROV, and KAMENEV. The “Moscow Center” not only directed all the counterrevolutionary group's activities in Moscow and Leningrad but also maintained constant contact with individual members of the counterrevolutionary group living in other cities.

The judicial investigation in this case established that the leaders of the so-called “Leningrad Center” of the same counterrevolutionary group — V. LEVIN, RUMYANTSEV, KOTOLYNOV, and others — repeatedly traveled to Moscow in recent years and received counterrevolutionary policy directives, instructions, and relevant information from members of the “Moscow Center.” The same investigation established that representatives of the “Moscow Center” — KUKLIN, GERTIK, GESSEN, SHAROV, EVDOKIMOV, and BAKAEV — with ZINOVIEV's knowledge, traveled for the same purposes to Leningrad, where they met with the above-mentioned leaders of the “Leningrad Center,” subsequently convicted by the verdict of the Military Collegium of the Supreme Court of the Union of Soviet Socialist Republics of December 28–29, 1934, in the case concerning the assassination of Comrade S. M. KIROV.

The immediate tasks of the counterrevolutionary group led by the “Moscow Center” were to preserve its “Zinovievist cadres,” accumulate strength, and reinforce among members of the group feelings of bitterness and open hatred toward the leaders of Soviet power.

Having begun its counterrevolutionary work on ZINOVIEV's direct instructions through a deceptive, double-dealing return to the Party, the counterrevolutionary group led by the “Moscow Center,” throughout the years of its underground activity, sought to achieve its main objective — the replacement of the leadership of Soviet power and a change in the current policy of the Soviet government — by systematically spreading among its members reports and rumors about various events in domestic and foreign policy presented in an overtly counterrevolutionary interpretation, with deliberate distortion of facts and figures, stirring up hatred and anger toward the leaders of Soviet power while at the same time promoting ZINOVIEV and KAMENEV everywhere as the future “leaders” of the Party and government.

The defendants in this case — GESSEN, SAKHOV, TSARKOV, ANISHEV, PERIMOV, TARASOV, GERTSBERG, FAIVILOVICH, BRAVO, and BASHKIROV — are incriminated as active members of the counterrevolutionary group who, over a number of years up until their arrest in December 1934, systematically engaged in counterrevolutionary agitation and stirred up anger and hatred against the leaders of the Soviet government on the direct instructions of the “Moscow Center.”

As a result of the counterrevolutionary activities of the “Moscow Center,” distinctly fascist methods of struggle developed in certain parts of the Zinovievist counterrevolutionary underground; terrorist sentiments directed against the leaders of the Party and government appeared and grew stronger. The consequence was the assassination of Comrade S. M. KIROV, prepared and organized by the “Leningrad Center” of the same counterrevolutionary group.

All defendants in this case fully admitted their guilt.

On the basis of the foregoing, the Military Collegium of the Supreme Court of the Union of Soviet Socialist Republics found: 1) ZINOVIEV, 2) EVDOKIMOV, 3) GERTIK, 4) BAKAEV, 5) KUKLIN, 6) SHAROV, 7) FEDOROV, 8) GORSHENIN, 9) KAMENEV, 10) TARASOV, 11) FAIVILOVICH, 12) GERTSBERG, 13) BRAVO, 14) PERIMOV, 15) GESSEN, 16) BASHKIROV, 17) ANISHEV, 18) SAKHOV, and 19) TSARKOV guilty of committing the crimes provided for in Articles 17, 58-8, and 58-11 of the Criminal Code of the RSFSR.

Guided by the resolution of the Central Executive Committee of the Union of Soviet Socialist Republics of December 1, 1934, the Military Collegium of the Supreme Court of the Union of Soviet Socialist Republics

SENTENCED:

1\. ZINOVIEV, Grigory Evseevich,

as the principal organizer and most active leader of the “Moscow Center,” which directed the activities of the underground counterrevolutionary groups in Moscow and Leningrad —

TO TEN YEARS' IMPRISONMENT;

2\. GERTIK, Artyom Moiseevich, and

3\. KUKLIN, Aleksandr Sergeevich, —

as the most active members of the “Moscow Center,” who for a number of years until recently played a leading role in the underground counterrevolutionary activities of the Zinovievist group —

TO TEN YEARS' IMPRISONMENT EACH;

4\. SAKHOV, Boris Naumovich,

as one of the most active participants in the underground counterrevolutionary activities of the Moscow group, who repeatedly conveyed to the group various secret information known to him through his service in the organs of the Procuracy and used his official position in the interests of this underground counterrevolutionary group —

TO TEN YEARS' IMPRISONMENT;

5\. SHAROV, Yakov Vasilievich,

6\. EVDOKIMOV, Grigory Eremeevich,

7\. BAKAEV, Ivan Petrovich, and

8\. GORSHENIN, Ivan Stepanovich, —

as active leaders of the above-mentioned “Moscow Center” —

TO EIGHT YEARS' IMPRISONMENT EACH;

9\. TSARKOV, Nikolai Alekseevich,

as an active member of the underground counterrevolutionary Zinovievist group —

TO EIGHT YEARS' IMPRISONMENT;

10\. FEDOROV, Grigory Fedorovich,

who was a member of the above-mentioned “Moscow Center” but had not recently taken an active part in it —

TO SIX YEARS' IMPRISONMENT;

11\. KAMENEV, Lev Borisovich,

who was one of the leading members of the “Moscow Center” but had not recently taken an active part in its activities —

TO FIVE YEARS' IMPRISONMENT;

12\. GERTSBERG, Aleksandr Vladimirovich,

13\. GESSEN, Sergei Mikhailovich,

14\. TARASOV, Ivan Ivanovich,

15\. PERIMOV, Aleksei Viktorovich,

16\. ANISHEV, Anatoly Isaevich, and

17\. FAIVILOVICH, Leonid Yakovlevich, —

for participation in the underground counterrevolutionary Zinovievist group —

TO SIX YEARS' IMPRISONMENT EACH;

18\. BASHKIROV, Aleksandr Fabianovich, and

19\. BRAVO, Boris Lvovich —

for participation in the underground counterrevolutionary Zinovievist group —

TO FIVE YEARS' IMPRISONMENT EACH.

The personally owned property of all those convicted shall be confiscated.

The period spent in pretrial detention shall be credited toward the sentences of the convicted persons. \[1\]

THE VERDICT IS FINAL AND NOT SUBJECT TO APPEAL.
