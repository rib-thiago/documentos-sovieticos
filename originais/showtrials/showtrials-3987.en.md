INTERROGATION RECORD

OF KHARITONOV, Moisei (Mikhail) Markovich

January 2, 1935.

QUESTION: What do you know about SAKHOV's activity in the Zinovievist organization?

ANSWER: I remember one episode: it was either during the Fifteenth Congress or shortly afterward. I met SAKHOV on the street. In conversation, he told me that he still had about twenty copies of the counterrevolutionary Trotskyist-Zinovievist platform. When asked what he intended to do with them, he said he was handing them over for safekeeping in reliable hands. in reliable hands. He had in mind a party member who had never openly appeared as an oppositionist, had not sympathized with the opposition. As far as I remember, he did not tell me the surname. In any event, I do not remember the surname even if he did mention it. As far as I remember, it concerned a justice official.

QUESTION: Who else visited ZINOVIEV?

ANSWER: In the initial period after our reinstatement in the party (the second half of 1928 and in 1929), I remember FYODOROV Grigory often going to see ZINOVIEV.

For a time, approximately in that same period, BELYAYS visited him very often. Before the Fifteenth Congress, BELYAYS was connected with rank-and-file oppositionists in one Moscow district, I think Zamoskvorechye. I do not know whether he preserved any connections after the congress, but he himself visited ZINOVIEV very often.

Another visitor to ZINOVIEV was one of his former secretaries, GRINBERG, who informed him about CC affairs that he knew through the CC apparatus. Often these were simply rumors about impending appointments and transfers. I know this from ZINOVIEV's words.

M. KHARITONOV.

INTERROGATED BY:—KORKIN.

Certified correct: A. Svetlova
