INTERROGATION RECORD

dated January 18, 1935, conducted by the SPO operative officer FEDOROV.

KONDRATIEVA, Evdokia Andreevna, born in 1898 (1896 according to her passport), native of Miti-Grivki village, Ostrov District, former Pskov Province, Russian, citizen of the USSR, residing in Leningrad, Vasilievsky Island, 15th Line, No. 74, apartment 31. Head of the NKVD civil registry office of Vasilievsky District. Married; husband SEMENOV, Semyon Georgievich, arrested; brother KONDRATIEV, Mikhail Andreevich, metalworker, lives with me; brother ILYIN, Nikolai Andreevich, foreman at the Samoilov confectionery factory. Elementary education, parish school in 1910, former member of the All-Union Communist Party (Bolsheviks) from January 1919, expelled as an active participant in the Zinovievist opposition on January 9, 1935.

QUESTION: The investigation has information that you are a participant in the counterrevolutionary Zinovievist organization?

ANSWER: After the Fifteenth Party Congress I remained in the Party without overcoming my opposition disagreements or ceasing the struggle against the Party, being a participant in the Vasilievsky counterrevolutionary Zinovievist organization.

The Vasilievsky Zinovievist organization was formed from the old oppositionists of this district and headed by YAKOVLEV Moisei.

During 1928 the organization actively conducted its counterrevolutionary work, holding illegal meetings at YAKOVLEV's apartment.

My husband, SEMENOV Semyon, and I repeatedly attended these illegal meetings.

In the autumn of 1934 I initiated the organization of a meeting of participants in the Zinovievist organization at the memorial gathering for the deceased SHEINBERG, attended by the participants in the Zinovievist organization: YAKOVLEV Moisei, KASPERSKY I. F., PANKO Adam, my husband SEMENOV, FOKIN Alexei, and myself, KONDRATIEVA.

I have read the testimony; it was correctly recorded from my words:

KONDRATIEVA.

INTERROGATED BY — FEDOROV.
