INTERROGATION RECORD

OF YUSKINA, Anna Yakovlevna

dated 12 December 1934.

QUESTION: What were the relations between your husband, I. G. YUSKIN, and L. V. NIKOLAEV?

ANSWER: My husband, I. G. YUSKIN, has known Leonid Vasilievich NIKOLAEV well for a long time; they met particularly often when NIKOLAEV still lived in the same building as us. After NIKOLAEV moved to another building, the acquaintance continued. NIKOLAEV frequently visited his mother and sister's apartment and also called at ours. Sometimes, when he called and did not find my husband, Nikolaev asked me when he would be there, where he was, and so on.

QUESTION: What did your husband YUSKIN and NIKOLAEV talk about when they met?

ANSWER: I was rarely present at my husband YUSKIN's conversations with NIKOLAEV, but during one of them I heard NIKOLAEV complain to my husband that he was unemployed, ill and in need, and that nobody paid him any attention.

My husband was very sympathetic towards him, expressed indignation at the Party organisation's indifference to NIKOLAEV and said it was unfair.

In conversation with my husband, NIKOLAEV expressed dissatisfaction with Party organisations, said that bureaucracy was everywhere, that the living human being had been forgotten, and so forth. My husband YUSKIN supported NIKOLAEV, expressed sympathy for him and spoke of his own grievances.

QUESTION: What do you know of your husband YUSKIN's anti-Soviet sentiments?

ANSWER: My husband I. G. YUSKIN is a very unstable person. Although a Communist, he sometimes made anti-Soviet attacks. When he worked at the Izhevsk factory, he received a Party reprimand. In connection with this, at home my husband expressed his dissatisfaction with the Party organisation's leadership very sharply.

QUESTION: What do you know about E. V. ROGACHEVA?

ANSWER: We have known ROGACHEVA (NIKOLAEV's sister) since childhood. ROGACHEVA is a degenerate Communist with anti-Soviet sentiments, who expressed dissatisfaction with Soviet power over supply matters. I many times stopped ROGACHEVA and told her that it was unbecoming for a Communist to conduct anti-Soviet agitation.

ROGACHEVA frequently held drinking parties at her apartment, attended by SOLOVIEV, KLEMENTIEV and a number of her colleagues. These parties usually involved unrestrained drunkenness, various anti-Soviet conversations, and so on.

QUESTION: Did you attend these parties at ROGACHEVA's?

ANSWER: I attended these parties very rarely.

I have read the testimony; it has been correctly taken down from my words.

YUSKINA.

Interrogated by —

Operational officer of the SPO, GUGB, NKVD — FEDOROV.
