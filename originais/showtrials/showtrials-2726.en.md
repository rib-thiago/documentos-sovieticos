RECORD OF SUPPLEMENTARY INTERROGATION

OF V. I. ZVEZDOV,

conducted by the Deputy Head of the SPO — STROMIN.

10 December 1934.

QUESTION: Which participants in the Zinovievist opposition, formerly workers in Vyborg District, are now in Leningrad?

ANSWER: The following participants in the opposition are in Leningrad:

1\. Vladimir RUMYANTSEV, former secretary of the Leningrad Provincial VLKSM Committee — now secretary of the Vyborg District Soviet;

2\. Ivan KOTOLYNOV [1], former secretary of the Vyborg District VLKSM Committee — now not working but studying, a student in the Physics and Mechanics Faculty of the Industrial Institute;

3\. Lev KHANIK, former secretary of the Vyborg District VLKSM Committee — now an instructor of the Kronstadt District VKP(b) Committee. In 1926 KHANIK worked abroad in Turkey as secretary of our Trade Representation; he returned in 1928;

4\. Nikolai TSARKOV, former secretary of the Vyborg District VLKSM Committee — now works at Gosalyuminstroy; in 1933 TSARKOV studied at the Leningrad Industrial Academy;

5\. Vasily VINOGRADOV, former head of the District Committee's Sports Department — now works at Kalinin Factory No. 4;

6\. Mikhail NADEL, former head of the Children's Communist Organisation (Young Pioneer work), a student in the Physics and Mechanics Faculty of the Industrial Institute;

7\. Nikolai ANTONOV, former organiser of the VLKSM organisation at the Metal Works — a student in the Mechanical Faculty of the Industrial Institute;

8\. Egor POPOV — “GOSHA”, former Head of the Organisational Department of the Vyborg District VLKSM Committee and secretary of the Suburban District VLKSM Committee. He is now a Bureau member of the “Combat Veterans' Association” of former Komsomol members of Leningrad;

9\. Mikhail MURAVIEV, former head of the House of Communist Education of Youth in Vyborg District. Worked at the “Landrin” factory;

10\. Grigory POTASHNIKOV, former District Committee instructor — now works at some factory in Vyborg District;

11\. Konstantin KOZIN, former organiser of the VLKSM organisation on the Irinovka railway section; I do not know where he works now; in 1928 he was an employee of the Criminal Investigation Department;

12\. Fedor PAIO, former secretary of the Murmansk Okrug VLKSM Committee — now a teacher at Stalin Communist University;

13\. Pyotr LOGINOV, former organiser of the VLKSM organisation at the “Neva” factory, now studies at the Industrial Institute;

14\. Fedor FADEEV [2], former VLKSM secretary for the Central City District and one of the first Komsomol members in Vyborg District;

15\. Andrei TOLMAZOV, former organiser of the VKP(b) organisation at the “Krasny Vyborzhets” factory — now works in the cooperative sector.

In essence, all participants in the Komsomol Zinovievist opposition of Vyborg District now live in Leningrad.

QUESTION: With which former oppositionists specifically did you maintain contact and meet?

ANSWER: I mainly met Nikolai ANTONOV, NADEL, KOTOLYNOV and LOGINOV. We all study at the same Institute and therefore have the opportunity to meet frequently. In addition, this year there was one meeting at Pyotr SEVEROV's apartment, where ANTONOV was present besides myself and SEVEROV; in 1933 there was one meeting at my apartment. Vasily VINOGRADOV and his wife, Fedor FADEEV, Nikolai ANTONOV and Mikhail NADEL visited me.

QUESTION: Which former oppositionists are members of the Leningrad Komsomol “Combat Veterans' Association”?

ANSWER: Egor POPOV, Lev KHANIK, Andrei TOLMAZOV and Fedor FADEEV; I do not remember others now.

QUESTION: Why did you go to this “Association's” premises, and how many times were you there?

ANSWER: I went there to submit an application to join the “Association”; I was there twice.

QUESTION: How do you explain your meetings with former opposition participants?

ANSWER: We are united by our personal friendship.

QUESTION: Why, for what purpose, did you keep the anti-Party documents found during the search at your apartment? Do you know that the material seized from you contains almost all the documents issued by the Zinovievist opposition?

ANSWER: These materials remained with me from the period of my factional work. I acknowledge my guilt in keeping these materials and regard this act of mine as profoundly anti-Party.

QUESTION: The investigation has information about the existence in Leningrad of an underground anti-Party group of former Zinovievists and about your membership in this group. What can you testify on this matter?

ANSWER: I personally did not know of an underground, formally organised anti-Party group of former Zinovievists. However, I acknowledge that the meetings I mentioned, which occurred throughout this time, undoubtedly had a political character, since our attraction to one another was based on a desire to preserve old opposition ties.

QUESTION: To which former participants in the Zinovievist opposition was Leonid NIKOLAEV personally close?

ANSWER: Leonid NIKOLAEV encountered and was connected through work in the apparatus of the Vyborg District VLKSM Committee with Nikolai TSARKOV, Vasily VINOGRADOV, Grigory POTASHNIKOV, Fedor PAIO, Mikhail NADEL and Egor POPOV. NIKOLAEV met PAIO and POPOV outside the District Committee, since their joint work took place in Luga and at the House of Party Activists.

The record has been correctly taken down from my words and read by me.

ZVEZDOV.

INTERROGATED BY:

DEPUTY HEAD OF THE SPO, UGB — STROMIN.

Certified true: G. Maksimov
