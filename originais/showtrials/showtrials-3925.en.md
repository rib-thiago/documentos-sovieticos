RECORD

Of the supplementary interrogation of GORSHENIN I. S.

December 30, 1934

Question: What additional testimony can you give concerning the sources of information of the Moscow Zinovievist center that supplied you with information on the internal and external situation of the USSR?

Answer: Besides MAGYAR Ludwig Ignatyevich, who informed ZINOVIEV about the situation in the Comintern, in individual countries, and about the foreign policy of the Central Committee of the All-Union Communist Party (Bolsheviks), the following persons were also our sources of information:

1\) LEIKIN Elkon — a research associate of the Communist Academy and an inspector of the Academic Committee for Higher Technical Education under the Council of People's Commissars of the USSR. He informed ZINOVIEV and other members of the center about the situation in the Moscow Committee of the All-Union Communist Party (Bolsheviks) and about decisions on various matters taken by the Bureau of the Party Committee. He, in turn, received this information from his relative Makar LEMANOV, a member of the Bureau of the Moscow Committee.

LEIKIN is an active Zinovievist and a participant in the gatherings held at my apartment until recently. LEIKIN is very critical of the policy of the Central Committee of the All-Union Communist Party (Bolsheviks).

2\) KAPITONOV Ivan Petrovich — deputy chairman of the Regional Planning Commission of Western Siberia (Novosibirsk). As head of the sector for the distribution of productive forces of the USSR Gosplan until June 1933, he constantly informed KAMENEV Lev Borisovich, BAKAEV Ivan Petrovich, and ZINOVIEV G. E. about the economy of the USSR and the prospects for the development of the national economy under the existing government plans. He takes a very pessimistic view of planning projections in agriculture.

KAPITONOV is an active Zinovievist.

3\) ELKOVICH Yakov Rafailovich — editor of the regional collective-farm newspaper in Sverdlovsk. During his visits to Moscow, he reports on the state of agriculture in the Urals and on the mood among leading officials of the All-Union Central Council of Trade Unions, in particular SHVERNIK and DOGADOV. ELKOVICH obtained his information about the All-Union Central Council of Trade Unions from ZHARIKOV Vasily, the academic secretary of Comrade SHVERNIK.

4\) SAKHOV Boris — procurator of the Northern Territory. During his stay in Moscow as assistant procurator of the Republic, he frequently visited KUKLIN. SAKHOV was an active Zinovievist who maintained contact with KUKLIN, a member of the Moscow Zinovievist center, until recently. I do not know on what matters SAKHOV informed the center of your organization.

SAKHOV also met with members of your organization BRAVO Boris and ROZNER Myacheslav.

Question: What do you know about the connections of GERTSBERG, POZDEEVA, and DYAKOV with the Moscow center of your organization?

Answer: All these persons were closely connected with GERTIK Artyom, a member of the Moscow center, while DYAKOV was connected with the center through GESSEN Sergey Mikhailovich.

I personally met all these persons at GERTSBERG's apartment after his return from abroad in 1933.

I visited DYAKOV Vasily with GESSEN at his private apartment. All are active Zinovievists and take a negative attitude toward the policy of the Central Committee of the All-Union Communist Party (Bolsheviks). —

The record has been taken down correctly from my words and read by me.

GORSHENIN.

INTERROGATED BY STROMIN.

Certified true: Churbanova
