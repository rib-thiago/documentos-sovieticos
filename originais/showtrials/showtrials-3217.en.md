INTERROGATION RECORD

OF SOSITSKY, Lev Ilyich, December 23, 1934.

I ask the investigation to accept the following statement:

Confirming my previous testimony about my counterrevolutionary activity in the Zinovievist organization of the city of Leningrad, in which I remained a participant and a member of its leading center until recently, I deeply repent of my grave crime against the Party and the socialist homeland. I recognize the full depth of my moral and political downfall, which resulted from my remaining in the ranks of the counterrevolutionary Zinovievist organization.

The hostile attitude toward the leaders of the All-Union Communist Party (Bolsheviks) and the Soviet Government, based on an unprincipled struggle to overthrow the Party leadership and constantly encouraged in this direction by our “leaders” ZINOVIEV and KAMENEV, led our organization to descend to fascist methods of struggle — terrorism.

I again confirm that the entire counterrevolutionary Zinovievist organization, myself included, bears political and moral responsibility for the vile atrocity committed — the murder of Comrade KIROV.

I sincerely ask the Party and the Soviet Government to accept my repentance and give me an opportunity, under any conditions, to prove that I can reform and be useful to the socialist homeland.

SOSITSKY.

INTERROGATED BY:

DEPUTY HEAD OF THE SPO OF THE GUGB OF THE NKVD OF THE USSR — LYUSHKOV.

CERTIFIED TRUE: M. Glazkov
