December 23, 1934.

Esteemed Comrade Stalin,

Naumov's testimony about the activities of the Zinovievist opposition mentions my presence at a New Year's gathering at Kamenev's welcoming 1929. In this connection I consider it necessary to make the following clarification:

I did indeed attend an evening gathering at Kamenev's once, but it was not a New Year's celebration and the people present were different. The evening I attended was arranged in Gorky's honor. I came precisely because I wanted to meet Gorky and considered that this character of the evening excluded the possibility of a factional meeting. Gorky and Peshkova were present. Kamenev explained Gorky's invitation by their friendship in Italy. In conversation at the table Gorky emphasized this character of his presence. In response to some hint from Kamenev or Zinoviev he said, to the effect, that you are likable people, as it were, but engaged in harmful business, and expressed the hope of their complete unity with the Party. I left before the end of the evening; in my presence, at any rate, no factional plans of the Zinovievists were discussed.

In light of the subsequent conspiratorial activities of Z<inoviev> and K<amenev>, it is clear that I should not have gone after all, just as I should not have maintained personal relations with Kamenev for a certain time. However, I have already written to you, and now confirm, that I broke them off completely about four years ago.

G. Sokolnikov.

RGASPI, fond 17, inventory 171, file 200, folio 88. Autograph.
