INTERROGATION RECORD

OF TAITS\<A\>, David Arkadievich ,

dated September 1, 1936.

TAITS D. A. , 1894 y\<ear\> of b\<irth\>, nat\<ive\> of the city of Warsaw, m\<ember\> of the AUCP(b) since 1932, physician, and before his arrest director of the Central Polyclinic of the Kremlin Medical Administration.

Question: At the interrogation of 27/VIII-36, you did not give exhaustive testimony concerning your connection with the terrorist Nathan LURIE , concealing from the investigation the organizers of terrorist acts against the leaders of the Soviet Government known to you.

Stop withholding the truth and give exhaustive testimony.

Answer: In telling the investigation about my meeting with N. LURIE in Berlin in 1928 and about the fact that LURIE had already informed me at that time of his membership in a Trotskyist organization, I indeed did not tell the investigation the whole truth.

I have now decided, in the interests of truth, to tell the investigation the whole truth.

Question : Proceed.

Answer: After N. LURIE arrived in the USSR from abroad in the m\<onth\> of April 1932, he stayed in Moscow with my relative TAITS Ts. L. \[1\] A few days after his arrival he contacted me, and we began meeting periodically.

In one of our conversations, N. LURIE raised the question of obtaining employment at the Kremlin Polyclinic. As I already testified at the previous interrogation, I told N. LURIE that I could not arrange employment for him at the Kremlin Polyclinic, since he had no practical experience as a physician.

Question: Did you know at that time about N. LURIE 's terrorist intentions?

Answer: No, I did not. I knew only that N. LURIE was a Trotskyist. N. LURIE informed me of his terrorist plans somewhat later.

Question: When was that, and under what circumstances?

Answer: This was in May 1932. The conversation took place at my apartment, when the two of us were alone. He again raised the question of his employment at the Kremlin Polyclinic, and I told him that I could arrange it if he were a member of the AUCP(b). My reasoning was that the staff of the Kremlin Medical Administration was then undergoing communization, which seemed to present me with a real opportunity to place LURIE in a job at the Kremlin Polyclinic, even though he lacked the required medical experience.

In response, N. LURIE told me that his transfer from the German Communist Party to the AUCP(b) had been delayed, most likely because the Comintern knew about his Trotskyist activities.

In connection with this I naturally wondered why he was so insistent about finding employment at the Kremlin Polyclinic. Then N. LURIE, speaking in strict confidence, revealed to me the true reasons for his arrival in the USSR, telling me that he had come to the Soviet Union on instructions from L. TROTSKY.

Question: Reconstruct with complete accuracy everything N. LURIE told you concerning the assignment given by L. TROTSKY.

Answer: N. LURIE informed me that he had come to the USSR with direct instructions from L. TROTSKY to organize terrorist acts against the leaders of the Soviet Government and the AUCP(b), first and foremost against STALIN.

Question: What did LURIE tell you about the circumstances in which he received these instructions from TROTSKY?

Answer: He did not let me into those details and proceeded directly to the question of obtaining my assistance.

Question; What practical assistance did you give N. LURIE in preparing terrorist acts against the leaders of the Soviet Government and the AUCP(b)?

Answer: My assistance to the terrorist N. LURIE in his preparation of terrorist acts against the leaders of the Soviet Government and the AUCP(b) consisted, first, in my attempt to secure him employment at the Kremlin Polyclinic, where he could carry out his terrorist intentions. For reasons beyond my control, LURIE did not manage to secure employment at the polyclinic.

Question: What plan had N. LURIE devised for carrying out a terrorist act at the Kremlin Polyclinic?

Answer: N. LURIE told me that all he needed was to secure employment at the Kremlin Polyclinic and that he would find the means of carrying it out once there, taking the circumstances into account. At the same time, LURIE assured me that he would take all necessary measures to conceal my role in the matter.

When LURIE asked which leaders of the AUCP(b) and the Soviet Government visited the Kremlin Polyclinic, I answered that a number of members of the government attended the polyclinic and specifically named VOROSHILOV.

Question: In what other ways did you assist the terrorist N. LURIE ?

Answer: Since the attempt to get N. LURIE employed at the Kremlin Polyclinic failed, the question arose of finding other opportunities to carry out a terrorist act.

For this purpose, it was necessary above all for N. LURIE to establish himself in Moscow. With my assistance, he obtained employment at the University Clinic and thus settled in Moscow.

In the autumn of 1932, during a session of the All-Russian Central Executive Committee (VTsIK), N. LURIE asked me to obtain a pass for him to attend the session so that he could attempt to kill STALIN there.

LURIE again assured me that my role in the matter would not be exposed, since, if circumstances at the VTsIK session were favorable for carrying out the terrorist act, he would first destroy the pass he had obtained from me. I obtained a pass for LURIE to attend the VTsIK session, and he attended it, but according to him STALIN was not present at the session on that occasion.

Question: By what means did you obtain a pass for LURIE to attend the VTsIK session?

Answer: As a senior employee of the Kremlin Medical Administration, in 1932 I received passes for various Congresses of Soviets and sessions from the secretariat of the Kremlin Medical Administration. Ordinarily, a named official pass was accompanied by single-use passes without names, and that was the case on this occasion. I received from the secretariat of the Kremlin Medical Administration a named official pass for the VTsIK session, together with an unnamed single-use pass. I handed the latter to LURIE .

Question: Does that exhaust your assistance to the terrorist LURIE ?

Answer: Yes, that is all, except that in late 1935 I gave LURIE a note addressed to the heads of the Leningrad Medical Commission asking them to arrange employment for him. I should note that N. LURIE , after unsuccessful attempts to carry out terrorist acts against the leaders of the Soviet Government and the AUCP(b), of which I was aware and about which I have testified above, left in mid-1933 to take up work in Chelyabinsk.

I do not know what other steps he took in that direction or with whom else he was connected for that purpose.

Question: What motives led you to become an accomplice in the counter-revolutionary terrorist activities of the Trotskyist LURIE ?

Answer: I became an accomplice to the Trotskyist LURIE in his terrorist activities because I was hostile toward the leadership of the AUCP(b).

My meetings and conversations with the Trotskyist LURIE strengthened those sentiments, and so when he raised the question of assisting him in his terrorist activities, I gave LURIE that assistance.

Question: You have still not told everything. In particular, you are attempting to minimize your own role and conceal other accomplices of the terrorist LURIE known to you.

The investigation categorically demands that you tell the whole truth and identify all the terrorists known to you.

Answer: I have testified only to the truth. I have told the investigation everything I knew.

Correctly recorded from my words and read by me.

TAITS .

Tsetsilia Solomonovna Taits, née Aruin, was the wife of Samuil, David Taits's brother (David Taits's own wife was called Sonya (Sofia)).
