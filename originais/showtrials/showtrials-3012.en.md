INTERROGATION RECORD

conducted by the Head of the 1st Section of the SPO, GUGB — PETROVSKY. —

13 December 1934.

LEVIN, Vladimir Solomonovich, aged 37 (born 1897), native of Nevel, Western Region; father had a small trading business, LEVIN Zalman Pinkhosovich; mother TAUBE; brother Moisei Solomonovich LEVIN; brother Boris Solomonovich NEVELSKY — resident in Kharkov; sister KRYLOVA Basya Solomonovna, resident in Moscow; wife LEVINA N. M.; citizen of the USSR, resident in Leningrad, 55 Krasnykh Zor Street, apartment 13; chairman of the Leningrad Rental Housing Union, employee, secondary education, member of the All-Union Communist Party (Bolsheviks) since 1917; expelled from the party in 1926 and 1927 for belonging to the Zinovievist opposition. In the Red Army from 1918 to 1926, in the Medical Supply Trust from 1926 to 1930, in Lenzhilarendsoyuz from 1930 to 1933. No convictions; military registration — reserve, senior commanding personnel; did not serve with the Whites.

QUESTION: Tell us about your connections with the Zinovievists.

ANSWER: Over the last two years I met certain party members who belonged to the Zinovievist opposition in 1925–1927: BASHKIROV Aleksandr, works at the Krasnaya Zarya factory; MYASNIKOV Nikolai — works in the Leningrad Soviet; KORSHUNOV Efim, studies at the Industrial Academy; DMITRIEV Timofei — works in Rostov-on-Don, I believe at the Canning Trust; RUMYANTSEV Vladimir; GAIDEROVA Zinaida; GERTIK Artyom; KHANIK Lev — works in the party District Committee in Kronstadt; ZELIKSON Isaak — works as secretary of the party committee at the Electrotechnical Institute; ZILBERMAN Aleksandr; ZALUTSKY Pyotr — works in Moscow within the People's Commissariat of Heavy Industry system; RAVICH Olga and GESSEN \[1\] Sergei — works in Smolensk.

QUESTION: Which of those you named did you meet frequently?

ANSWER: Until recently I frequently met BASHKIROV Aleksandr and MYASNIKOV Nikolai. —

LEVIN.

INTERROGATED BY:

HEAD OF THE 1ST SECTION OF THE SPO, GUGB — PETROVSKY
