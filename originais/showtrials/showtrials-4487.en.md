INTERROGATION RECORD

OF MIRONOV Vladimir Davydovich

(supplementary)

dated January 14, 1935.

QUESTION: Under what circumstances did you meet Trotskyists, and what was the character of these meetings?

ANSWER: I met the active Trotskyist BARANOV Sergei in late 1927; BARANOV was then working as an instructor of the Central Committee of the All-Union Communist Party (Bolsheviks). My attitude toward his Trotskyist talk gave him reason to think that I sympathized with him.

I met BARANOV several times at his apartment. During meetings and conversations with me BARANOV cast doubt on everything; he said that the revolution was perishing, and so on. BARANOV's conversations contained negative attacks against the Central Committee of the All-Union Communist Party (Bolsheviks). BARANOV complained to me that he was not trusted in the Central Committee, and so on.

In 1927 I also met the Trotskyist GRAUSBART Aron several times in Moscow. During these meetings GRAUSBART told me that he was conducting active counterrevolutionary Trotskyist work; I did not firmly rebuff GRAUSBART and continued meeting him.

I met BARANOV and GRAUSBART until my departure to work in the North Caucasus. Since then I have not met BARANOV or GRAUSBART.

Later, I do not remember exactly when, UFIMTSEV told me that BARANOV had been exiled for counterrevolutionary Trotskyist activities.

In 1933, after UFIMTSEV's arrest, GRAUSBART telephoned my apartment; he asked me over the telephone how I was living and told me that he was working in Moscow, but since I treated him unfriendlily, the conversation ended there. GRAUSBART did not telephone me again, and I did not see him.

In 1931 I moved to work in Moscow; a short time later I met UFIMTSEV and from that moment renewed my ties with him. In 1932, at UFIMTSEV's apartment, I met the Zinovievists KOSTINA and BAKAEV, whom I subsequently often encountered at UFIMTSEV's. In the autumn of 1932 I visited BAKAEV's apartment once. I went to BAKAEV's from UFIMTSEV's apartment with the Trotskyist SIMASHKO. At BAKAEV's apartment we found KOSTINA, UFIMTSEV, and a friend of theirs whose surname I do not know; later the Trotskyist DITYATEVA, Comrade PYATAKOV's wife, and others arrived at BAKAEV's.

I met UFIMTSEV, SIMASHKO, KOSTINA, and BAKAEV until early 1933, that is, until UFIMTSEV's arrest. After UFIMTSEV's arrest I broke off ties with SIMASHKO, KOSTINA, and BAKAEV.

QUESTION: Tell the investigation what conversations you had with the Trotskyists UFIMTSEV and SIMASHKO and the Zinovievists BAKAEV and KOSTINA.

ANSWER: During meetings with UFIMTSEV, KOSTINA, SIMASHKO, and BAKAEV we discussed political questions. After the January plenum of the Central Committee, UFIMTSEV telephoned my apartment and asked permission to come for information, since for some reason he had been unable to get into the plenum, though he had a ticket. I agreed to tell him about Comrade STALIN's speech, after which UFIMTSEV, SIMASHKO, and KOSTINA came to my home late at night. I informed them in detail about Comrade STALIN's speech on the situation in the countryside. I related that Comrade STALIN had sharply castigated KHATAEVICH in his speech for excesses. I do not remember the tone or the comments I gave on this speech.

Recorded from my words; the record was read to me.

MIRONOV.

INTERROGATED BY:

HEAD OF THE 1ST SECTION OF THE SPO OF THE GUGB OF THE NKVD OF THE USSR — PETROVSKY

Certified correct: A. Svetlova
