INTERROGATION RECORD

Of KORSHUNOV, Yefim Konstantinovich

(Supplementary)

December 26, 1934.

QUESTION: Describe in greater detail the meetings held at LEVIN's apartment and who was present at them.

ANSWER: As I already testified in my previous record of December 17, 1934, meetings of the Zinovievist-Trotskyist organization repeatedly took place at LEVIN's apartment. These meetings were usually led, that is, questions were put up for discussion and given the appropriate direction, by LEVIN Vladimir. Usually present at these meetings were myself, KORSHUNOV, MYASNIKOV, SOSITSKY, ZILBERMAN, DMITRIEV, BASHKIROV, and LEVIN. Of the questions discussed at these meetings, I remember the following:

1\. LEVIN repeatedly raised the question that it was entirely wrong that ZINOVIEV and KAMENEV had been removed from the Party leadership. The main conclusion we reached in discussing this question was that it was necessary to return to the so-called “Leninist Politburo,” that is, to bring ZINOVIEV, KAMENEV, RYKOV, and BUKHARIN into the Politburo. LEVIN emphasized that the main task of our organization was to secure ZINOVIEV's and KAMENEV's return to the Party leadership.

2\. During one of the meetings at LEVIN's apartment, around 1933, KUKLIN was also present. I do not now remember whether KUKLIN gave any information. As far as I now recall, the meeting amounted to LEVIN informing KUKLIN about our sentiments and that we were strengthening contacts with a number of oppositionists returning to Leningrad.

3\. Commenting at some meeting on ZINOVIEV's speech at the Seventeenth Congress, LEVIN considered that it was now possible to hope that ZINOVIEV would be elected to the Central Committee of the All-Union Communist Party (Bolsheviks).

All the meetings I attended at LEVIN's apartment were usually held under the guise of tea gatherings. It was also usual for us to drink quite a lot at these meetings.

QUESTION: Whom did you know among the members of the leading center of the Zinovievist-Trotskyist organization in Leningrad?

ANSWER: I knew only LEVIN. I did not know the others.

QUESTION: What can you testify about the terrorist act against Comrade KIROV, committed on the direct instructions of individual members of the Leningrad center of the Zinovievist-Trotskyist organization by NIKOLAEV, a member of your organization?

ANSWER: The malicious hatred and hostile attitude toward the leaders of the All-Union Communist Party (Bolsheviks) cultivated in our organization led one of its members to take the path of terror and murder Comrade KIROV. For this crime, both our entire organization and I in particular bear political and moral responsibility.

Taken down correctly from my words and read by me:

KORSHUNOV.

INTERROGATED BY:

HEAD OF THE 3RD SECTION OF THE EKO OF THE GUGB OF THE USSR NKVD — CHERTOK.
