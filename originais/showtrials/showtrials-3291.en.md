RECORD

of the supplementary interrogation of the accused GORSHENIN, I. S.,

conducted by the deputy head of the UGB — STROMIN — on December 17, 1934.

QUESTION: In your previous testimony you stated that the Moscow group of Zinovievists held regular illegal meetings. Describe in greater detail where and how often these meetings took place.

ANSWER: Our meetings took place more or less regularly once a month (sometimes twice a month). They were held in private apartments, chiefly mine (Moscow, 6/8 Plotnikov Lane, apartment 12). Sometimes the apartments of BAKAEV, Ivan Petrovich; KUKLIN, Aleksandr Sergeevich; GERTIK, Artyom Moiseevich; SHAROV, Yakov Vasilievich; and EVDOKIMOV, Grigory Eremeevich, were used for this purpose. These meetings took place throughout the period from 1928 and continued until our arrest in December of this year.

QUESTION: Who attended these meetings?

ANSWER: Only Zinovievists attended. At first our meetings had a broader character, with about fifteen or sixteen people present. Lately we restricted our meetings to a very narrow circle, namely: I. P. BAKAEV, A. P. KOSTINA, A. M. GERTIK, A. E. KOZHURO \[1\], myself, GORSHENIN; sometimes A. S. KUKLIN, Ya. V. SHAROV, G. E. EVDOKIMOV, S. M. GESSEN, R. L. BUDZINSKAYA \[2\], P. ZALUTSKY, S. S. ZORIN, Elkon LEIKIN \[3\] and LUKYANOV attended.

QUESTION: What was the nature of these meetings, and what questions were discussed?

ANSWER: At these meetings we discussed questions connected with the domestic and foreign policy of the VKP(b) Central Committee and touched on the situation of individual Zinovievists.

QUESTION: Did you meet with ZINOVIEV and KAMENEV? What did you talk about with them?

ANSWER: Such meetings did take place. I met both KAMENEV and ZINOVIEV. I supplied KAMENEV with statistical materials on the state of agriculture and industry. I obtained this information at my workplace in Gosplan. I also supplied ZINOVIEV with some economic information. I last visited ZINOVIEV three (3) days before my arrest, that is, on December 9 of this year.

I visited him at his request, in connection with the question of the anti-Party views expressed by his son Stepan Grigoryevich in the scholarly lectures he gives at the Planning Academy. I last saw KAMENEV on December 12 of this year.

QUESTION: What do you know about the political seminars organized by ZINOVIEV and KAMENEV?

ANSWER: In 1928, following ZINOVIEV's and KAMENEV's reinstatement in the Party, two seminars were organized on their initiative: an economic seminar and a political seminar. KAMENEV personally headed the first, and ZINOVIEV the second.

Only one session of each took place, since it was decided by common agreement not to continue these sessions.

QUESTION: State the membership of both seminars.

ANSWER: I personally participated only in the economic seminar and therefore do not know the membership of ZINOVIEV's seminar. As for the economic seminar, its members were KAMENEV, L. B.; KAPITONOV, Ivan Petrovich; BABAKHAN \[4\], Sergei Yakovlevich; REINGOLD, Isaak; LEIKIN, Elkon; SHAROV, Yakov Vasilievich; and SOKOLNIKOV, Grigory Yakovlevich. At this seminar's first session, KAPITONOV delivered a report on the economic situation in the countryside at that time. This report provoked an active exchange of opinions with a view to defining our disagreements with the line of the VKP(b) Central Committee.

I sign in confirmation of the correctness of my testimony:

GORSHENIN

Interrogated by: STROMIN

Certified correct:

\[1\] Kozhuro, Anna Evgenievna, wife of I. Gorshenin. Repressed. Fate unknown.

\[2\] Budzinskaya, Regina Lvovna, wife of V. Vujovich. Arrested on January 25, 1935. Sentenced twice to imprisonment, then to execution by shooting.

\[3\] Erroneously given in the text as “Elken”. Leikin, Elkon Grigoryevich, economist, was repressed but survived the Great Terror. Died in 1986.

\[4\] Erroneously given in the text as “Bobokhan”.
