SUPPLEMENTARY TESTIMONY

OF IZRAILITIN, Aleksandr Abramovich.

Dated January 13, 1935.

QUESTION: How long have you known the former oppositionist MAGDARYEV Nikolai, and what do you know about him?

ANSWER: I have known MAGDARYEV Nikolai since late 1924 through Komsomol work in the Moscow-Narva District of Leningrad. At the time of the Fourteenth Congress of the All-Union Communist Party (Bolsheviks), he joined the opposition and actively participated in opposition work.

At the end of 1929 (approximately) I met MAGDARYEV N. at the Moscow-Narva District Committee of the All-Union Communist Party (Bolsheviks); he proudly declared: “I have been reinstated in the Party; I did not acknowledge any mistakes, only declared the correctness of the decisions of the Fifteenth Congress and the Sixteenth Party Conference; and, you know, Trotsky is still a revolutionary and will be in the Party.” From this conversation I concluded that he was double-dealing, held opposition views, and was aligning himself with the Trotskyists, which I reported to Comrade Gaaze Iv. Iv. (then working as secretary of the district committee of the All-Union Communist Party (Bolsheviks)).

In the first half of 1930 Comrade Nikitin I. K. (secretary of the Moscow-Narva District Committee of the All-Union Communist Party (Bolsheviks)) told me that RUMYANTSEV had sent him a note requesting that he find MAGDARYEV N. work at the “Red Triangle.” I told Comrade Nikitin not to send him, as they would build an opposition nest at the “Red Triangle.” Then, in that same year of 1930, his wife Milda (a worker at the “Red Triangle”) approached me in my capacity as deputy secretary of the “Red Triangle” Party committee, asking that I find MAGDARYEV work at the factory, and said she would also speak about this to the Party committee secretary. I informed Comrade Komarov Vasily (then secretary of the Party committee), warning him of the need to refuse admission to an oppositionist linked to Rumyantsev's group.

The record was signed personally by me — A. IZRAILITIN.

INTERROGATED BY:

ASSISTANT HEAD OF THE 1ST SECTION OF THE OO OF THE GUGB — BRICCI.
