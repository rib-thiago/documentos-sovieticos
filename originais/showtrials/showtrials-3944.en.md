INTERROGATION RECORD

OF REM, Mikhail Semyonovich — 31 December 1934.

I consider it necessary to report the following fact: on 17 December, after arrest, I was taken to the House of Preliminary Detention of the NKVD Directorate of Leningrad Region and met Mikhail LEVIN. During a conversation about the reasons for arrest and which Criminal Code article had been charged, he asked me, as if seeking advice: “May I declare a hunger strike if I consider Article 58 of the Criminal Code to have been wrongly charged against me?” I answered negatively and the conversation ended.

QUESTION: Did you meet M. LEVIN before his arrest, and what conversations did you have?

ANSWER: Throughout recent years I saw LEVIN Mikhail only once, on 1 May 1934 on the reviewing stand. We had no conversations and I know nothing about him.

REM.

INTERROGATED BY: KORKIN.

Certified correct: Kazakova
