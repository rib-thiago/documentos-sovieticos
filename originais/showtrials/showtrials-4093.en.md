TESTIMONY

Of the accused ANISHEV, Anatoly Isaevich.

January 5, 1935.

About ZAKS-GLADNEV I know only that during the 14th Congress he was an active participant in the opposition. I did not meet him later and do not remember conversations about him. Apparently he was abroad. If I am not mistaken, ZAKS-GLADNEV is some sort of relative of ZINOVIEV [1]. —

ANISHEV.

INTERROGATED BY: — LULOV.

Certified correct: Churbanova

RGASPI, fond 671, inventory 1, file 127, folio 44.

[1] S. M. Zaks-Gladnev was the husband of G. Zinoviev’s sister, Liya Aronovna Radomyslskaya.
