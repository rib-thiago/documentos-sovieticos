INTERROGATION RECORD

Of SHAROV, Yakov Vasilyevich,

dated January 8, 1935.

QUESTION: What can you testify about the murder of Comrade KIROV?

ANSWER: As I already testified in the interrogation record of January 8, 1935, the Zinovievist organization cultivated malice and hatred toward the leadership of the All-Union Communist Party (Bolsheviks). This ultimately led, over several years of the Zinovievist organization’s struggle against the party, to the upbringing of participants in the organization who took the path of violent actions against leaders of the All-Union Communist Party (Bolsheviks).

NIKOLAEV, KIROV’s murderer, is precisely one of these scoundrels, participants in our organization, who, as a result of being educated in a spirit of hatred toward leaders of the All-Union Communist Party (Bolsheviks), took the path of terror. Therefore the entire Zinovievist organization and I, as one of its participants, bear political and moral responsibility for NIKOLAEV’s murder of KIROV.

Recorded correctly from my words and read by me — SHAROV.

INTERROGATED BY:

HEAD OF THE 3RD SECTION OF THE EKO OF THE GUGB OF THE NKVD OF THE USSR — CHERTOK.

Certified correct:

RGASPI, fond 671, inventory 1, file 127, folio 104.
