INTERROGATION RECORD

OF LELEVICH Labory Gilelevich

dated January 27, 1935.

QUESTION: What do you know about TARTAKOVSKAYA, Yulia Borisovna?

ANSWER: I became acquainted with TARTAKOVSKAYA in Moscow in 1926, where she was one of the prominent organizers of the Zinovievist faction of the opposition bloc. I remember that Olga Naumovna RAVICH, with whom I was connected through underground work, passed her contact with TARTAKOVSKAYA on to me when she left. In 1927 TARTAKOVSKAYA was regarded as an extremist, one of the most radical and most uncompromising toward the Party leadership. I remember that in November 1927, at a meeting of the united opposition's Zinovievist subfactional center, ZINOVIEV said that the most extremist among the Trotskyists was YATSEK and among the Zinovievists TARTAKOVSKAYA. In January 1928, when SAFAROV, VARDIN, and others were leaving for exile, they designated TARTAKOVSKAYA as one of the principal activists of the Moscow center of the “leaderless.” Since then I lost sight of her. But I remember that in 1933, during a conversation in Zinovievist circles, when we exchanged information on how Zinovievists were getting through the purge (often interpreted in Zinovievist circles as a means of dealing with “dissenters”), someone said that TARTAKOVSKAYA was having trouble in the purge and that apparently she was accused of having served in KERENSKY's shock troops.

Written by me in my own hand.

LELEVICH

Interrogated by:

HEAD OF SECTION I OF THE SPO — LULOV.
