INTERROGATION RECORD

conducted by the Head of the 4th Section, SPO — L. KOGAN, on 14 December 1934.

BASHKIROV, Aleksandr Rabinovich [1], born in 1903, from Vilna. Father a worker, died in 1914; Jewish, USSR citizen; Leningrad, 26 Herzen Street, apartment 4. Assistant shop head at the “Krasnaya Zarya” factory. Diploma student at the Electrotechnical Institute. Married; wife Elena Yakovlevna BASHKIROVA, VKP(b) member, Head of the FO of the Smolny District Committee; daughter Nina, 11. Without property. Graduated from the Electrotechnical Institute in 1934; VKP(b) member since 1920. Expelled in 1927 for belonging to the Trotskyist-Zinovievist bloc. Reinstated in June 1928. Until 1926, senior instructor in the Political Directorate of the Leningrad Military District. In Kazakhstan, senior inspector of the Workers' and Peasants' Inspectorate until 1930. From 1930 to 1932, deputy secretary of the Institute Party committee. From 1932 until recently, a member of its bureau. Never tried. Registered at the Institute under category T-8; did not serve with the Whites.

QUESTION: With which former participants in the Trotskyist-Zinovievist bloc do you maintain contact?

ANSWER: Among the former participants I maintain contact with Vladimir LEVIN, Nikolai MYASNIKOV, Isaak ZELIKSON, secretary of the Electrotechnical Institute Party committee and member of the Petrograd District VKP(b) Committee, and Georgy FEDOROV, with whom I studied at home, preparing for classes at the Institute.

After my expulsion from the Party in 1927 I was sent to Kazakhstan. There I shared an apartment with Mikhail KHARITONOV, now working in England. Living in Moscow in 1926–1927, I maintained contact with ZINOVIEV, BAKAEV, EVDOKIMOV, KUKLIN, GERTIK and ZALUTSKY. In 1930, while in Moscow, I met KHARITONOV after a long interval. He then worked in the People's Commissariat of the Workers' and Peasants' Inspectorate.

QUESTION: The investigation has information that all the former participants in the Trotskyist-Zinovievist bloc listed above, including yourself, returned to the Party for double-dealing purposes on ZINOVIEV's directive. What can you testify about this?

ANSWER: Yes. I and the former participants listed, except ZELIKSON, who was not expelled and dissociated himself from the opposition before the Fifteenth Congress, returned to the Party on ZINOVIEV's direct instructions, retaining our old positions. I do not remember who personally conveyed this directive. Thus the Trotskyist-Zinovievist opposition, ideologically defeated at the Fifteenth Congress, survived after the congress until today as an anti-Party counter-revolutionary organisation built on personal connections. My own connections are indicated above.

QUESTION: What can you testify about Comrade KIROV's murder by Leonid NIKOLAEV, a member of your organisation?

ANSWER: I did not know NIKOLAEV personally and had never heard of him. I heard and knew nothing of preparations for the terrorist act against KIROV. At the same time, I consider that NIKOLAEV's shot resulted from his membership and upbringing in the counter-revolutionary Trotskyist-Zinovievist organisation, and therefore that the entire organisation bears political responsibility for the act.

Correctly taken down from my words, in confirmation of which I sign.

BASHKIROV

INTERROGATED BY — L. KOGAN.
