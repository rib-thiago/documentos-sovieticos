STATEMENT

To the investigation in the case of the organization of Zinovievists.

By way of concluding the investigation into my participation in the organization of Zinovievists, I ask that the following statement be attached to the case:

1\. In my testimony I informed the investigation of everything I knew about the organization of Zinovievists and my personal participation in it. I confirm this testimony wholly and completely and can add nothing to it. I could not inform the investigation of more than I have reported, because in 1930, 1931 and 1932 I was absent from Leningrad, working on the construction of the Volkh\<ov\> Al\<uminum\> Combine.

2\. I belonged to the group of Zinovievists headed by V. RUMYANTSEV, with whom, after almost a year's absence, I renewed contact in November 1934 and from whom I received material assistance. I know that hostile attitudes toward the party leaders were cultivated in this organization, and that as a result NIKOLAEV fired the shot at Sergei Mironovich KIROV.

3\. During the investigation I related with the utmost sincerity everything I knew, and finally and irrevocably tore out all my Zinovievism like the most harmful growth of flesh in my body, and I sincerely rejoice that Zinovievism has now been destroyed finally and forever.

I ask the party to give me, still a young working-class lad, the opportunity to prove in deeds my devotion to the party and Comrade STALIN. I shall lay down my bones for socialist construction, but I shall prove my devotion to the party of Lenin–Stalin. I linked my fate with the party from the age of thirteen and, as a teenager, was its member with full rights; if father, mother, wife and children were taken from me, it \<would\> be hard, agonizing, but to take away the party is more than that.

TSARKOV.

Statement received by:

HEAD OF THE 4TH SECTION OF THE SPO — L. KOGAN.

Certified correct: A. Svetlova
