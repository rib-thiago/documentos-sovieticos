INTERROGATION RECORD

OF KOSTINA, Anna Porfiryevna

dated January 10, 1935.

QUESTION: Describe how the Zinovievist organization's contacts were restored in Moscow and with other cities.

ANSWER: During the factional struggle before the Fifteenth Party Congress, the Zinovievist organization's members began intensively restoring contacts. Particularly clear connections were established among the organization's active members. After the Fifteenth Congress, with the gradual move of the organization's members to Moscow, our connections survived and were maintained until recently. When GESSEN Sergey, who had lately worked in Smolensk; DMITRIEV Nikolai, who worked in Saratov; ELKOVICH, who worked in the Urals; the Leningrad people LEVIN Vladimir, ANISHEV, RUMYANTSEV Vladimir, REPNIKOV Pyotr, and MANDELSHTAM Sergey; ZILBERMAN Aleksandr (Moscow); SHURYGIN; GORDON Nikolai, who worked in Rostov; RAVICH Olga, who worked in Voronezh; ROTSKAN; KOROLEV Aleksandr, who worked at the aluminum combine; and KAPITONOV from Western Siberia came to Moscow — all of them, arriving from outside the capital, visited me — KOSTINA — as well as GERTIK and GORSHENIN. KHARITONOV and KAREV also visited these same apartments; the latter did so until his arrest.

QUESTION: What conversations took place when members of the Zinovievist organization met?

ANSWER: At meetings with the Zinovievist organization's members, those arriving from outside the capital informed us about the local situation. Most visitors gave a tendentious account of conditions; the presentation of particular questions was counterrevolutionary in character. At meetings we also discussed the situation in industry, concluding that industrial work lacked planning, proceeded in fits and starts, and that all questions were decided from above without taking account of the views of people working in the particular branch of industry: initiative was stifled and enormous capital investments were frozen; each of those present always cited tendentious facts. I — KOSTINA — BAKAEV, OLKHOVSKY, GORSHENIN, and others discussed these questions. When informing us, GORSHENIN gave a particularly negative, counterrevolutionary account of the state of agriculture. He worked at the RSFSR Gosplan. GORSHENIN supplied material and counterrevolutionary comments when explaining particular facts, bringing the material to my apartment, to his own home. On the basis of this material, GORSHENIN frequently informed not only me — KOSTINA — BAKAEV, EVDOKIMOV, GERTIK, KUKLIN, and KOZHURO, but also DMITRIEV Nikolai, GESSEN, ELKOVICH, and others arriving from the localities.

Recorded correctly from my words.

The record was read aloud to me

A. KOSTINA.
