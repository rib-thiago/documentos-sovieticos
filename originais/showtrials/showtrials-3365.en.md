INTERROGATION RECORD

Of SAFAROV, Georgy Ivanovich, December 20, 1934.

SAFAROV, Georgy Ivanovich, born in 1891, son of an official, writer, Russian, citizen of the USSR, Moscow, Spiridonyevskaya Street, 26, apartment 30. Wife: Valentina Sergeevna SAFAROVA; mother: Maria Osipovna DAVYDOVSKAYA. Incomplete higher education. Member of the All-Union Communist Party (Bolsheviks) since 1908; expelled from the Party in 1927. Party, Soviet and military work. Information on convictions: in 1910, 1912, 1913. In 1910 sentenced to exile in Vologda Province, commuted to permission to go abroad; arrested for the second time in 1912, released on surety and went into hiding.

QUESTION: Tell us with which participants in the Zinovievist-Trotskyist bloc you maintained contact in Moscow, and what this contact consisted of.

ANSWER: Of the former participants in the Zinovievist-Trotskyist bloc, I met TARKHANOV, BUROV and NAUMOV most often, having developed closer personal relations with them. I met EVDOKIMOV twice at the Kremlin Hospital; I met ZINOVIEV at Ilyinskoye in the summer of 1932 and saw him another time at Bolshevik; I met BRAVO at NAUMOV's home, where I had a quarrel with him; I met MRACHKOVSKY at the time of his appointment to railway construction. Ivan KOTOLYNOV and Ivan TARASOV called at my apartment during a visit to Moscow and inquired about the situation in Germany. I told them about the state of affairs from the Comintern's standpoint. Vladimir RUMYANTSEV came to the Comintern to invite a speaker to the Vyborg House of Culture. In the summer of 1932 I met ZINOVIEV at a dacha in Ilyinskoye; during the conversation I raised the question of how he viewed the state of affairs. ZINOVIEV began saying that the country felt anxiety in the face of difficulties, that elements of dissatisfaction were growing in the country, that the Party lacked a clear orientation; he expressed to me his fears for the future. I essentially made no objection and remained silent. There ZINOVIEV also said: “it would be good to meet more often.” —

Recorded from my words and read to me.

SAFAROV.

INTERROGATED BY:

Head of the 1st Section of the SPO of the GUGB, PETROVSKY. —
