INTERROGATION RECORD

Of KOZHURO, Anna Yevgenyevna —

January 9, 1935.

Question: What do you know about KOSTINA Anna Porfiryevna, a participant in your organization?

Answer: KOSTINA was a member of the underground Zinovievist organization until recently and participated in all our clandestine meetings and gatherings held at her apartment (BAKAEV's), mine (GORSHENIN's), and those of VUYOVICH, SHAROV, and GERTIK.

As the wife of a member of the Moscow Zinovievist center, KOSTINA was informed of all the center's instructions and practical activity.

At our gatherings, when various Central Committee resolutions were discussed, KOSTINA, like the others, spoke against these resolutions. Remaining on her former Zinovievist positions and a supporter of ZINOVIEV's and KAMENEV's return to the Party leadership, KOSTINA had a negative attitude toward the present Party leadership.

At individual meetings, KOSTINA's statements were particularly striking; this was so, for example, during discussion of the Central Committee resolution expelling ZINOVIEV and KAMENEV from the Party, when she demanded that the entire organization take an organized stand against this Central Committee resolution.

An equally sharp statement occurred in connection with GERTIK's exile; at that time she was sharply opposed to the Central Control Commission's decision.

A. KOZHURO.

Interrogated by: STROMIN.

Certified true: Biryukov
