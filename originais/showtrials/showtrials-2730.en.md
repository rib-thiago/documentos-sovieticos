RECORD OF SUPPLEMENTARY INTERROGATION

of the accused V. I. ZVEZDOV, conducted on 11 December 1934

by the DEPUTY HEAD OF THE SPO, UGB — STROMIN.

QUESTION: What do you know of the existing counter-revolutionary organisation of former Zinovievists?

ANSWER: I know that during 1933–1934 the former leaders of the Zinovievist opposition — BAKAEV, KHARITONOV, GERTIK and ZINOVIEV himself — resumed work to reconstitute the organisation.

The former leaders of the Zinovievist opposition, being in Moscow and closely connected with Leningrad, hoped to draw into the struggle against the Party and its leadership our former youth group of former Zinovievists, among others. Nikolai ANTONOV, who studies at the same institute as I do, told me this.

QUESTION: How did ANTONOV learn this, and when exactly did he tell you?

ANSWER: ANTONOV learned it from KOTOLYNOV. Our conversation took place at the Industrial Institute in October 1934.

QUESTION: What else did ANTONOV tell you about KOTOLYNOV?

ANSWER: He conveyed KOTOLYNOV's instruction that we should not draw close to Mikhail NADEL, who, according to KOTOLYNOV's information, was connected with the OGPU authorities.

QUESTION: What did ANTONOV tell you about the political instructions received from Moscow?

ANSWER: He told me that old contacts were now being intensified and that a new action against the Party leadership was being prepared.

The record has been correctly written from my words — ZVEZDOV

INTERROGATED BY — STROMIN
