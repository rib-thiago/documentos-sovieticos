INTERROGATION RECORD

of POPOV, Georgy Fedorovich, December 16, 1934.

To clarify my earlier testimony concerning SHATSKY's aggressive sentiments, I consider it necessary to report to the investigation: from what SHATSKY told me on the way back from the meeting of the association of former comrades-in-arms (see testimony of December 15), I could see that his (SHATSKY's) sharply hostile sentiments toward the Party leaders bordered on terrorist sentiments. —

POPOV.

Interrogated by:

HEAD OF THE 6TH SECTION OF THE SPO OF THE GUGB — KORKIN.

Certified correct:

\[Annotation: at the top of the document, the inscription: to comrade Fedorov.\]
