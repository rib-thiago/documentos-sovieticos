INTERROGATION RECORD

Of BOGRACHEV, Solomon Naumovich — dated January 4, 1935.

QUESTION: Do you know SHEININ, director of the “Krasny Vodnik” plant, and what are your relations?

ANSWER: I have known SHEININ, Isaak, since approximately 1930 through working together at the Volodarsky factory, where he performed administrative and economic work, in fact fulfilling the functions of assistant director. SHEININ is a member of the All-Union Communist Party (Bolsheviks) and, as far as I know, was not involved in the opposition. I have close, even friendly relations with SHEININ. Our families are acquainted, and we have visited one another throughout our acquaintance.

QUESTION: List the persons you met at Sheinin’s apartment?

ANSWER: Until approximately 1932, when I frequently visited SHEININ’s apartment at 6 Trade Unions Boulevard, I met there AGRANOVSKY, director of the Volodarsky garment factory, KUBLANOV, Yakov Veniaminovich, deputy director of the same factory, and CHIZH, the current director of this same factory. From time to time we all gathered at SHEININ’s apartment to drink together. Sometimes we gathered at my apartment.

QUESTION: The investigation knows that leaders of the Zinovievist organization arriving from Moscow, EVDOKIMOV, Grigory, and SHAROV, Yakov, attended gatherings held at SHEININ’s apartment. Do you confirm this circumstance?

ANSWER: I confirm that in 1928, after one of the demonstrations (May or October), EVDOKIMOV did indeed call on me at the “Astoria” hotel. At that time the following were with me: AGRANOVSKY, KUBLANOV, SHEININ, GONCHAROV, chairman of the Leningrad Provincial Department of Garment Workers, SHAROV, Yakov, and several other persons whose surnames I cannot now recall.

EVDOKIMOV’s and SHAROV’s visit to me had been agreed upon between us beforehand. I do not now remember the content of our conversation.

I also confirm that during 1931–33, when SHAROV came from Moscow to Leningrad, we sometimes gathered at my apartment in the company of SHEININ, AGRANOVSKY, and KUBLANOV.

QUESTION: Do you confirm that, during SHAROV’s visits to your apartment, anti-party conversations took place between you and questions of struggle against the party were discussed, in particular the return to leadership of the former leaders of the opposition Trotskyist-Zinovievist bloc?

ANSWER: I acknowledge that, when visiting my apartment, SHAROV expressed dissatisfaction with his personal situation, felt aggrieved, and declared in a resentful tone that the party did not trust him as a former oppositionist, did not give him work corresponding to his knowledge and experience, and that he had no choice but to retire. I do not remember other conversations of an anti-party character taking place between us.

QUESTION: Which participants in the Zinovievist organization visited your apartment when SHAROV visited it?

ANSWER: In the summer of 1933 I met SHAROV, who had arrived from Moscow. This coincided with the arrival in Leningrad of RESHETKO, Ilya, head of the RSFSR garment industry association and a mutual acquaintance of ours. We decided to make a joint trip to Peterhof, to which I also invited LEVIN, Vladimir, and AGRANOVSKY.

We gathered at my home and all went to Peterhof together. From conversations with SHAROV one could conclude that he retained the same feelings of dissatisfaction with his personal situation. We tried to cheer him up, arguing that he should by no means retire.

QUESTION: Your answers to the investigation’s questions, set out in previous interrogation records and in particular the record of January 1, establish that you conducted anti-party, counterrevolutionary work, maintaining organizational contacts with the Zinovievist organization. Do you acknowledge this?

ANSWER: I deny it. I consider all my activity to be fully in keeping with the party, although I maintained contact with individual participants in the former Zinovievist opposition.

QUESTION: The investigation knows that you belonged to the Zinovievist organization in Leningrad. Do you confirm this circumstance?

ANSWER: No, I deny belonging to the Zinovievist organization. Since withdrawing from the opposition, that is, since 1928, I have had nothing to do with the Zinovievist opposition.

QUESTION: The testimony of the arrested leaders of the Zinovievist organization precisely establishes your membership in this organization. The investigation demands a truthful answer from you about your anti-party, counterrevolutionary activities in the ranks of the Zinovievist organization.

ANSWER: I deny this information.

Recorded correctly from my words and read by me —

BOGRACHEV.

INTERROGATED BY:

ASSISTANT HEAD OF THE SPECIAL DEPARTMENT OF THE GUGB OF THE NKVD — GENDIN.

Certified correct: Kazakova
