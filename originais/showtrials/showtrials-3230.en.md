RECORD

of a confrontation between the accused TOLMAZOV Andrey Ilyich

and KOTOLYNOV Ivan Ivanovich —

dated December 25, 1934.

After mutually confirming that they knew one another personally, the accused testified:

QUESTION TO TOLMAZOV: What do you know about KOTOLYNOV I. I.'s membership in the counterrevolutionary Zinovievist organization and his role in it?

ANSWER: I know personally that KOTOLYNOV I. I. is a member of the counterrevolutionary Zinovievist organization. In 1933, I was present at RUMYANTSEV's apartment, where KOTOLYNOV gave an account of his latest trip to Moscow. At that same meeting I learned that KOTOLYNOV was a member of the Center of the Leningrad organization.

A. TOLMAZOV.

QUESTION TO KOTOLYNOV: Do you confirm TOLMAZOV's testimony?

ANSWER: I confirm that I was indeed a member of the Zinovievist organization. I deny that I was a member of the Center of that organization. The conversation to which TOLMAZOV testifies did indeed take place at RUMYANTSEV's apartment, but it did not touch on the question of my role in the organization.

QUESTION TO KOTOLYNOV: What do you know about TOLMAZOV's membership in the counterrevolutionary organization?

ANSWER: I personally know of TOLMAZOV's membership in the organization.

I. KOTOLYNOV,

Confrontation conducted by:

HEAD OF THE EKO OF THE GUGB OF THE NKVD OF THE USSR — MIRONOV.

DEPUTY HEAD OF THE SPO OF THE GUGB OF THE NKVD OF THE USSR — LYUSHKOV.

ASSISTANT HEAD OF THE EKO OF THE GUGB OF THE NKVD OF THE USSR — DMITRIEV.

HEAD OF SECTION IV OF THE SPO OF THE UGB — KOGAN.

CERTIFIED TRUE: Kuznetsova
