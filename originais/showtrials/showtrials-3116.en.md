INTERROGATION RECORD

conducted on December 16, 1934, by the Head of Section 1 of the SPO — LULOV.

SOKOLOV Georgy Vasilyevich (SUPPLEMENTARY).

QUESTION: When and where did you last meet ANTONOV Nikolai, a member of your counterrevolutionary organization?

ANSWER: I last met ANTONOV Nikolai in the last days of October of this year at the KSI stadium.

QUESTION: What did ANTONOV inform you about during this meeting?

ANSWER: ANTONOV informed me about the intensification of contacts. In particular, he told me that the comrades were strengthening their connections with one another, that RUMYANTSEV was meeting TOLMAZOV, and that contact with ZVEZDOV had been restored. At the same time ANTONOV invited me to visit him, remarking that VINOGRADOV and other people of our circle would also be there.

QUESTION: Was KOTOLYNOV's name mentioned during this conversation, and in what connection?

ANSWER: During this conversation KOTOLYNOV's name was mentioned in connection with the question of intensifying contacts.

QUESTION: When did you last meet NIKOLAEV Leonid?

ANSWER: I last met NIKOLAEV Leonid at Smolny on the eve of the October celebrations.

QUESTION: What did you discuss with NIKOLAEV L. during this meeting at Smolny?

ANSWER: During this conversation I tried, with NIKOLAEV's help, to obtain a pass for the reviewing stand on Uritsky Square for the October parade.

QUESTION: Which member of your organization did you discuss with NIKOLAEV during this meeting?

ANSWER: NIKANOROV Vasily.

QUESTION: Was that the extent of your meeting with NIKOLAEV at Smolny?

ANSWER: No. After leaving Smolny, I met NIKOLAEV again at the stop for tram No. 33, and we boarded the tram together and rode to the corner of Komsomola and Nizhegorodskaya Streets.

QUESTION: What was the nature of your conversation with NIKOLAEV?

ANSWER: Our conversation consisted of sounding one another out.

QUESTION: Did you meet NIKOLAEV L. in 1932?

ANSWER: Yes, in the middle of 1932 I met and talked with NIKOLAEV at Lenpishchegort.

Recorded correctly from my words.

SOKOLOV.

Interrogated by: LULOV.
