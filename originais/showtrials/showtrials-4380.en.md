INTERROGATION RECORD

OF EVDOKIMOV Grigory Yeremeyevich

January 10, 1935.

QUESTION: What do you know about SMILGA I. T.'s political sentiments?

ANSWER: Personally, in recent years I have had no conversations with SMILGA on political subjects, but from KAMENEV's and ZINOVIEV's remarks I knew that SMILGA remained at his former Trotskyist positions until recently. I cannot establish exactly when I spoke with KAMENEV and ZINOVIEV about SMILGA's sentiments, but in any event it was no later than 1932.

From my conversations with KAMENEV and ZINOVIEV about SMILGA and his sentiments, it was clear to me that one of them had met SMILGA; possibly both had met him.

QUESTION: What can you tell the investigation to supplement your testimony?

ANSWER: I recalled that SHATSKIN, during his negotiations with me in 1932 about establishing an exchange of information between the Zinovievist center and the group he represented, proposed that I and BAKAEV be designated on behalf of the Zinovievists; on behalf of his group, besides himself, he named two other persons who were to substitute for him if he left Moscow. I do not remember one of the surnames SHATSKIN named at all; the other was apparently VARDIN, but I am not firmly certain of this.

QUESTION: To which members of the Zinovievist organization did you speak about the negotiations with SHATSKIN?

ANSWER: Before my conversations with SHATSKIN, KAMENEV told me that SHATSKIN had proposed mutual contact to him in Gagra in that same year, 1932. Then, after consulting ZINOVIEV and KAMENEV, I rejected SHATSKIN's proposal. I did not tell the other members of the Zinovievist organization's center about the negotiations with SHATSKIN, in accordance with my agreement with him: when I rejected his proposal, he asked me to tell no one except KAMENEV and ZINOVIEV about these negotiations with him.

The record was taken down correctly from my words, read by me —

EVDOKIMOV.

INTERROGATED BY:

ASSISTANT HEAD OF THE SPO OF THE GUGB — RUTKOVSKY

Certified true: A. Svetlova
