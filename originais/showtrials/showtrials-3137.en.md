RECORD

of the confrontation between the accused ANTONOV, Nikolai Semenovich, and KOTOLYNOV, Ivan Ivanovich, conducted on 17 December 1934. —

The accused ANTONOV N. S. and KOTOLYNOV I. I., summoned for a confrontation, after mutually acknowledging that they knew each other personally, testified:

QUESTION TO ANTONOV: Do you confirm your testimony about KOTOLYNOV I. I.'s membership in the counterrevolutionary organization of Zinovievists?

ANSWER: Yes, I confirm it. KOTOLYNOV I. is a member of the counterrevolutionary organization of Zinovievists and belonged to the leading center of the counterrevolutionary organization in Leningrad. KOTOLYNOV himself told me this.

QUESTION TO KOTOLYNOV: Do you confirm this?

ANSWER: Yes, I confirm that I belong to the Zinovievist-Trotskyist counterrevolutionary organization. I did not tell ANTONOV that I belonged to the center of the Zinovievist-Trotskyist counterrevolutionary organization.

QUESTION TO ANTONOV: State when, from whom, and under what circumstances you learned of the terrorist act against Comrade KIROV being prepared by the counterrevolutionary organization of Zinovievists.

ANTONOV'S ANSWER: At the end of October 1934, I. KOTOLYNOV, a member of the center of the counterrevolutionary organization, told me that our organization was preparing a decisive act against one of the members of the Politburo of the CC of the AUCP(b). He explained this by the need to intensify the counterrevolutionary organization's activity. At the same time, KOTOLYNOV told me that NIKOLAEV Leonid, a member of the Zinovievist-Trotskyist counterrevolutionary organization, harbored great hatred for Comrades STALIN, KAGANOVICH, and KIROV and that he, KOTOLYNOV, knew of NIKOLAEV's terrorist intentions.

This conversation took place at the Industrial Institute. In speaking of NIKOLAEV's terrorist intentions against the leaders of the AUCP(b), the expression used was that “these party leaders must be removed.”

QUESTION TO KOTOLYNOV: Do you confirm this testimony by ANTONOV?

KOTOLYNOV'S ANSWER: I deny ANTONOV's testimony.

ANTONOV.                             KOTOLYNOV.

Confrontation conducted by:

HEAD OF THE EKO OF THE GUGB — MIRONOV.

DEPUTY HEAD OF THE SPO OF THE GUGB — LYUSHKOV.

HEAD OF THE 6TH SECTION OF THE SPO OF THE GUGB — KORKIN.

HEAD OF THE 1ST SECTION OF THE SPO OF THE GUGB — PETROVSKY.
