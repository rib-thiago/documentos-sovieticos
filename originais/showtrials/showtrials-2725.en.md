INTERROGATION RECORD

conducted on 10 December 1934 by the Head of the 1st Section, SPO — LULOV.

SOKOLOV, Georgy Vasilievich (supplementary).

QUESTION: What do you know of the activity of the group of former oppositionists associated with you?

ANSWER: In my testimony of 7/XII I stated that there was a group of former oppositionists in Leningrad associated with me, comprising RUMYANTSEV, KHANIK, NIKANOROV, TOLMAZOV, ANTONOV, VINOGRADOV, Vasily ZVEZDOV and Leonid NIKOLAEV. Beginning in October this year, the members of this group began to intensify their contacts and establish closer relations with one another. As early as the end of last year, ANTONOV called on me at the Student Section of the Regional Trade Union Council. He wanted to discuss something with me, but the people around us prevented it. In January this year, during my conversation with RUMYANTSEV on the District Soviet's premises, he asked me to visit him and expressed willingness to help me. My meeting with ANTONOV in October this year was already more definite in character, in terms of intensifying contacts. It took place in the last days of October 1934 at the KSI stadium. ANTONOV told me that the comrades were strengthening their contacts with one another, that RUMYANTSEV was meeting TOLMAZOV and that contact with KOTOLYNOV [1] and ZVEZDOV had been restored. At the same time ANTONOV invited me to visit him, noting that VINOGRADOV and other lads of our circle would also be there. Finally, in November this year, on the eve of the October anniversary, I met Leonid NIKOLAEV at Smolny; he was associated with the group named above. This meeting, as I have already testified, was a mutual sounding-out. In this conversation NIKOLAEV showed particular interest in Vasily NIKANOROV, insistently asking where he could meet him and where he lived.

Correctly taken down from my words and read by me, in confirmation of which I sign.

SOKOLOV.

Interrogated by: G. LULOV.
