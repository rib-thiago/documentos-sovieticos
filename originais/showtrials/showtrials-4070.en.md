CONFRONTATION RECORD

Between the accused SEVEROV, Pyotr Petrovich, and POPOV, Georgy Fedorovich.

Conducted on January 4, 1935.

The accused SEVEROV and POPOV, summoned for a confrontation, after confirming that they knew each other personally, testified:

QUESTION TO SEVEROV: What do you know about POPOV, G. F.’s membership in the Zinovievist organization?

SEVEROV’S ANSWER: I know personally that POPOV, G. F., was a member of the counterrevolutionary Zinovievist organization until the most recent period.

QUESTION TO POPOV: Do you confirm this testimony by SEVEROV?

POPOV’S ANSWER: Yes, I was a member of the counterrevolutionary organization, and I confirm SEVEROV’s testimony.

QUESTION TO POPOV: Describe what SEVEROV told you about RUMYANTSEV’s contacts?

POPOV’S ANSWER: In one of his conversations with me, SEVEROV said that RUMYANTSEV “had met one of the big people.” I do not know with whom RUMYANTSEV maintained personal contact; SEVEROV did not say, but the contacts concerned the organization.

QUESTION TO SEVEROV: Do you confirm this testimony by POPOV?

SEVEROV’S ANSWER: Either NATANSON or MIRONOV told me about RUMYANTSEV’s contacts with one of the big people, either ZINOVIEV or KAMENEV. I met POPOV frequently and fully allow that I may have told him about this contact of RUMYANTSEV’s.

SEVEROV.                              POPOV.

The confrontation was conducted by:

HEAD OF THE 6TH SECTION OF THE SPO OF THE GUGB OF THE NKVD — KORKIN

HEAD OF THE 1ST SECTION OF THE SPO OF THE GUGB OF THE NKVD — LULOV.
