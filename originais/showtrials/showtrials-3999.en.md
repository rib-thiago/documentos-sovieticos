RECORD

of confrontations \[1\] between the accused GERTIK Artyom Moiseyevich and ANISHEV Anatoly Isayevich, held

on January 2, 1935.

The accused, summoned to the confrontation, after mutually acknowledging that they knew each other personally, testified:

Question to ANISHEV A.I.: Describe the connections of GERTIK Artyom Moiseyevich, who came from Moscow, with you, NATANSON and other Zinovievists living in Leningrad.

Answer: I remember two visits by GERTIK A.M. to Leningrad. On his first visit, we went together (myself, Gertik, Natanson) to BASHKIROV's home; on the second visit, GERTIK called at our apartment. This was in 1932; I do not remember the exact time.

Question to GERTIK: Do you confirm what ANISHEV A.I. said?

Answer: I remember being at ANISHEV's and NATANSON's apartment during one of my visits. I also remember visiting BASHKIROV during one of my trips to Leningrad in 1932. I do not remember whether NATANSON and ANISHEV were with me, but I admit the possibility.

Question to ANISHEV A.I.: What can you tell us about political conversations with GERTIK at your apartment in 1932?

Answer: I remember a conversation about collectivization. GERTIK, NATANSON and I expressed the view that peasants were being forced into collective farms and that a situation was being created in which the peasant could not leave the collective farm. In connection with this conversation, I remember GERTIK's information about famine in Ukraine. GERTIK drew the conclusions from all this that party policy in the countryside was a crude maneuver by the party leadership, that the situation was grave, and that all this could lead the country to ruin.

Question to GERTIK A.M.: Do you confirm ANISHEV's testimony?

Answer: I do not remember the details of the conversation; I admit that I may have informed ANISHEV and NATANSON about famine in Ukraine, but I did not speak about coercive methods of collectivization at all.

Question to ANISHEV A.I.: Describe your conversations with GERTIK concerning the “problem of leaders.”

Answer: In connection with these conversations about collectivization, NATANSON and I raised the question of whether we could counterpose to party policy not only criticism of its line but also something positive. GERTIK A.M. replied that the question was not a positive platform, but who pursued party policy and by what methods. Here GERTIK also gave a hostile assessment of Comrade STALIN as party leader. He then said that the Zinovievists were so isolated in the party that they could offer nothing positive.

Question to GERTIK: What can you say about this?

Answer: I do not remember. In my view, I could not have had a conversation of this nature with ANISHEV.

Question to ANISHEV: What role did GERTIK perform in the Zinovievist organization?

Answer: We regarded him as a member of the Moscow center directly connected with ZINOVIEV.

Question to GERTIK: Do you confirm this testimony of ANISHEV A.I.?

Answer: I met ZINOVIEV; I knew nothing about the existence of the center.

ANISHEV                              GERTIK

Confrontation conducted by: ASSISTANT HEAD OF THE SPO OF THE GUGB—RUTKOVSKY.

Certified correct: Biryukov
