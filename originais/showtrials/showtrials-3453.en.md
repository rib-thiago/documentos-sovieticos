INTERROGATION RECORD

Of FAIVILOVICH Leonid Yakovlevich, December 26, 1934.

Question: The investigation knows that when meeting participants in the counterrevolutionary Zinovievist-Trotskyist organization, you systematically held conversations directed against the leadership of the All-Union Communist Party (Bolsheviks). What can you testify about this?

Answer: As I have already testified, I had a counterrevolutionary conversation discrediting the Party leadership with I. KOTOLYNOV and I. TARASOV in December 1933. I

do not remember holding similar conversations with anyone else.

Personally, my sentiments were such that I considered it necessary to bring ZINOVIEV into the Party leadership. It seemed to me that he could and should be utilized, especially in the event of any difficulties in the country. During my stay in Kazakhstan, I shared these sentiments with Comrade KAKHIANI; the latter explained to me the incorrectness of my viewpoint. I do not remember whether I conveyed these sentiments of mine to any members of the organization. —

L. FAIVILOVICH.

INTERROGATED BY KORKIN.

Certified true: Churbanova
