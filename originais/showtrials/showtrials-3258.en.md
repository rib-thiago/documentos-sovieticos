INTERROGATION RECORD

OF POTASHNIKOV, Grigory Petrovich

dated 15 December 1934.

POTASHNIKOV, Grigory Petrovich, born in 1903, native of Grodno, permanent resident of Leningrad. Father a former worker and watchman at the Stalin Metal Works, died in 1918; mother a worker at the “Krasny Vyborzhets” factory. I reside at 13 Zhukov Street, apartment 17. At Factory No. 103, head of a group in the Tooling and Production Planning Department. Divorced. Close relatives: 1) mother Revekka Abramovna POTASHNIKOVA, 65, VKP(b) member since 1924; 2) brothers: Samuil, 37, non-Party, fitter at the VARZ factory; Veniamin, 24, non-Party, Red Navy sailor on the “Marat”, artillery electrician; Abram, 20, non-Party, worker at the “Progress” factory, template-making fitter; Aron, 18, non-Party, fitter at the “Krasnaya Zarya” factory; sister Bronislava, 21, non-Party, accounts clerk at the “Krasny Vyborzhets” factory. Without property. In 1913 completed a three-class primary school and in 1916 three classes at a town school. VKP(b) member since 1923, Party card No. 1063479. Expelled in 1927 for belonging to the Zinovievist opposition. In the Komsomol since 1918, joined at a fittings factory in Leningrad. According to his statement, no convictions. Exempted from military service for illness. Did not serve with the Whites.

QUESTION: Relate with which Zinovievists you maintain contact and the nature of these contacts.

ANSWER: I maintain contact with Nikolai TSARKOV, TOLMAZOV, Fedor FADEEV, Nikolai ANTONOV, Vladimir RUMYANTSEV, SEVEROV, Ivan KOTOLYNOV, Mikhail MURAVIEV and Mikhail NADEL. I did not break organisational ties with them until recently. These ties consisted in our having been expelled for counter-revolutionary activity and returned to the Party while retaining the counter-revolutionary positions of the Trotskyist-Zinovievist bloc.

In particular, I returned to the Party remaining a double-dealer within it and retaining the platform of the counter-revolutionary Trotskyist-Zinovievist bloc.

QUESTION: The investigation has information that you were a member of the counter-revolutionary Zinovievist organisation. Relate your organisation's activity.

ANSWER: Contacts among Zinovievists did not cease until recently. These contacts were organisational in character. Among the Leningrad organisation's members I mainly know the group of former Vyborg District Komsomol workers mentioned above, headed by Vladimir RUMYANTSEV.

I adhered to the counter-revolutionary Zinovievist organisation, which formed on the basis of dissatisfaction with the present Party leadership and the need for active struggle against it.

QUESTION: Did you know Leonid NIKOLAEV?

ANSWER: I knew Leonid NIKOLAEV through the Vyborg Komsomol organisation until 1922. I have not met him since 1922.

Correctly taken down from my words and read to me, in confirmation of which I sign.

POTASHNIKOV.

Interrogated by —

Operational officer of the 4th Section, SPO — M. KOGAN.
