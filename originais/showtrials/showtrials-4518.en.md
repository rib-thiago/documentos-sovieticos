INTERROGATION RECORD

conducted by the Head of the 1st Section of the SPO of the GUGB of the NKVD of the USSR —

PETROVSKY.

January 18, 1935.

supplementary.

MIRONOV Vladimir Davydovich.

QUESTION: The investigation has information that, while holding a responsible post in the Central Committee of the All-Union Communist Party (Bolsheviks), you maintained ties with participants in the Zinovievist group and active Trotskyists, to whom you gave political information not intended for disclosure, and that they used this information for counterrevolutionary purposes. Do you confirm this?

ANSWER: Yes, I confirm it. I was indeed connected with the active Trotskyists UFIMTSEV and SIMASHKO, as well as the Zinovievists BAKAEV and KOSTINA. I informed these persons about certain political questions I encountered through my work in the Central Committee. They apparently used this information for counterrevolutionary purposes. I did not know that they were continuing the struggle against the Party.

I also plead guilty to having concealed these ties of mine with Trotskyists and Zinovievists from the Party when summoned to the Party Control Commission under the Central Committee of the All-Union Communist Party (Bolsheviks) in August or September 1934.

Recorded from my words and read to me.

MIRONOV.

INTERROGATED BY:

HEAD OF THE 1ST SECTION OF THE SPO OF THE GUGB OF THE NKVD OF THE USSR — PETROVSKY

Certified correct: A. Svetlova
