December 16, 1934.

Deeply respected Iosif Vissarionovich, great leader of the Party and the country.

I would rather not live than have lived to see this day of a fatal, terrible misunderstanding. I honestly and sincerely broke with counterrevolutionary Trotskyism and sought, with everything I could, to help in the struggle against it. The comrades before whose eyes my work proceeded can say that all this work was imbued with the desire to do everything to atone for my crime before the Party. There is no life for me outside the Party, and I ask you to examine and clarify this fatal misunderstanding. In the remotest corner, at the most arduous work, as a rank-and-file worker in the great army of the great Lenin and the great Stalin, I shall prove that I deserve your trust.

Comrade Stalin! Believe that there is not a grain of falsehood here, and help me!

G. Safarov

RGASPI, F. 17, Op. 171, D. 199, sheet 46.

Published in: The Echo of the Shot at Smolny. The History of the Investigation into S. M. Kirov's Murder According to Documents of the CPSU Central Committee // Moscow, International Democracy Foundation, 1917, p. 212.
