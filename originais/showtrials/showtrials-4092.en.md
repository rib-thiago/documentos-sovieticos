TESTIMONY OF THE ACCUSED

ANISHEV, Anatoly Isaevich, January 5, 1935.

I know DMITRIEV, Nikolai, from Samarkand, where we conducted factional work together in 1927. Later he left for somewhere on the Volga.

I last met him in 1932 (or 1933) at the Regional Executive Committee in Leningrad, when he was moving to Leningrad. He told me that he was being transferred to Leningrad at a request from Leningrad arranged for him by one of his old friends from the Vyborg district. I do not remember whom he was talking about; it may have been KOMAROV, in any case someone from Vyborg who was not a Zinovievist. DMITRIEV and I agreed to meet; I gave him my telephone number, but he did not call me and we did not meet again.

ANISHEV

INTERROGATED BY: — LULOV.

Certified correct: A. Svetlova

RGASPI, fond 671, inventory 1, file 127, folio 18.
