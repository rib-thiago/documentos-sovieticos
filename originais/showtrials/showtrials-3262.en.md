INTERROGATION RECORD

conducted by the assistant head of the OO of the GUGB of the NKVD — GENDIN

December 15, 1934

Accused: BRAVO, Boris Lvovich, born in 1900, a native of Leningrad, Jewish, son of a timber merchant, residing in Moscow, 4 Oktyabrskaya Street, apartment 60; journalist, responsible editor of the magazine “On the Agricultural Procurement Front”; married. Wife: Irina Efimovna KOGAN, correspondence education consultant at the Procurement Committee of the USSR SNK, living in Moscow; son Vladimir, aged 10. Father: Lev Moiseevich BRAVO, supported by his children, living in Moscow on Dzerzhinsky Street, NKID building (5/15), apartment 16. Mother: Fanya Yakovlevna BRAVO, Leningrad, 13 Saperny Lane, apartment 17. Brother: BRAVO, Lazar Lvovich, engineer, Leningrad, 13 Saperny Lane, apartment 17; sister: Raisa Lvovna, married name ULMAN, Leningrad, 8 Proletkulta Street, apartment 4. Before the revolution his father had a firewood business in Harbin. Graduated from a gymnasium in Harbin in 1918. Was enrolled as a student at Tomsk Polytechnic Institute for about a year (1918–1919); a member of the VKP(b) since 1919. Was outside the Party from 1927 to 1929, having been expelled for belonging to the former Zinovievist opposition. By a decision of the Special Conference attached to the OGPU Collegium, in April 1928 he was exiled to the city of Kansk for three years for participation in anti-Party activities. Returned from exile in May 1929. Registered for military service as a private. Served with the Whites as a private from June to December 1919 in the guard of the Chinese Eastern Railway.

QUESTION: List the participants in the Zinovievist opposition whom you met after your reinstatement in the ranks of the VKP(b).

ANSWER: Following my reinstatement in the Party and my return to Moscow from exile, I met the following participants in the Zinovievist opposition:

1\) EVDOKIMOV, Grigory Eremeevich — we are personal friends, and I visited him very frequently at the “Paris” Hotel, where he lived until 1933. After he moved into an apartment (in the Tverskaya-Yamskaya Street area), I visited him less frequently. After his return from vacation (autumn 1934), I saw him four or five times.

2\) BAKAEV, Ivan Petrovich — I met him several times in 1933 at EVDOKIMOV's in the “Paris”. I did not meet him subsequently.

3\) GERTIK, Artyom Moiseevich — before his deregistration from residence in Moscow (1932), I met him very frequently at his apartment. Following GERTIK's return to Moscow from exile, I saw him two or three times in the GORT dining room on Nikolskaya Street.

4\) GORSHENIN, Ivan — in 1934 I met him two or three times in the GORT dining room on Nikolskaya.

5\) SHAROV, Yakov — I met him several times at EVDOKIMOV's in the “Paris”. I visited SHAROV's apartment once, when EVDOKIMOV was also present. I have not seen SHAROV since 1933.

6\) KUKLIN, Aleksandr — in 1932–1933 I met him repeatedly through work at “Khlebotsentr”. In 1934 KUKLIN came to see me at work several times.

7\) KORSHUNOV, Efim — in 1932 I met him in Leningrad during a business trip; I visited his apartment then and met him at the Academy (I do not recall its name). In the spring of this year KORSHUNOV was in Moscow on a business trip and visited my apartment. He also stayed overnight at my place on that occasion.

8\) BASHKIROV — I do not know his full name; he was called “ALKA” — I saw him in Moscow in the spring of this year during his business trip. He came to my home and intended to stay overnight, but did not turn up.

9\) LEVIN, Vladimir — I met him in April 1933 while on a business trip to Leningrad. I met him a second time in Leningrad in February 1934 while on a business trip. I then arranged a gathering at my sister's apartment on Proletkulta Street, attended by LEVIN, the SNK Procurement Committee's representative for the Leningrad Region; ITS, Ferdinand Samoilovich; ITS's deputy, GORBUNTSOV; and a “Zagotzerno” representative (I do not remember his surname). In October 1934 LEVIN was in Moscow on a business trip and visited me at work.

I do not now recall any other meetings with Zinovievists.

Recorded correctly from my words and read to me. — BRAVO.

INTERROGATED BY:

ASSISTANT HEAD OF THE OO OF THE GUGB OF THE NKVD — GENDIN
