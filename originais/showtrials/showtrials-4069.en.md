CONFRONTATION RECORD

Between the accused SAFAROV, G. I., and SAKHOV, B. N.,

conducted on January 4, 1935.

The accused SAFAROV and SAKHOV, summoned for a confrontation, after acknowledging that they knew each other personally, testified:

Question to SAFAROV: What do you know about SAKHOV, B. N.’s membership in the Zinovievist organization?

Answer: I know personally that SAKHOV was a member of the counterrevolutionary Zinovievist organization until the most recent period and that he was connected with the members of the center EVDOKIMOV and especially KUKLIN, A. S.; he was also connected with GERTIK.

Question to SAKHOV: Do you confirm this testimony by SAFAROV?

SAKHOV’s answer: Yes, I belonged to the organization and, as I have already testified, in the initial period I was connected with KUKLIN, EVDOKIMOV, and GERTIK, and from approximately the middle of 1931 with KUKLIN and EVDOKIMOV.

Question to SAFAROV: Describe the content of your conversation when meeting SAKHOV.

SAFAROV’s answer: I met SAKHOV on Comintern Street in 1932, apparently in the autumn. When I asked how he was getting on, SAKHOV replied that he visited Leningrad, met the “old boys,” informed them, and obtained information from them himself. At the same time SAKHOV told me that he had managed to extricate the former oppositionist ABRAMSON from some affair connected with an apartment matter. I helped more than just ABRAMSON, SAKHOV told me. From the entire conversation with SAKHOV it was apparent that he continued to wage counterrevolutionary struggle against the party.

Question to SAKHOV: Do you confirm this testimony by SAFAROV?

SAKHOV’s answer: I confirm the fact of my meeting with SAFAROV on Comintern Street, but it was apparently in November 1931, not in 1932. I deny SAFAROV’s testimony about the content of our conversation.

SAKHOV. —

I request that the following be added to the confrontation record: I categorically affirm that my meeting with SAKHOV took place in 1932, not in 1931, and that the nature of the conversation was exactly as I testified —

SAFAROV.

The confrontation was conducted by:

DEPUTY HEAD OF THE SPO OF THE GUGB — LYUSHKOV.

HEAD OF SECTION VI OF THE SPO OF THE GUGB — KORKIN.

HEAD OF THE 1ST SECTION OF THE SPO OF THE GUGB — PETROVSKY.
