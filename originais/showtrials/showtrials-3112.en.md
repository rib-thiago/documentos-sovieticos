INTERROGATION RECORD

OF KULISHER R. M., DATED DECEMBER 16, 1934.

QUESTION: What do you have to add to the testimony you have given and to the questions put to you by the investigation?

ANSWER: I wish to inform the investigation of what I consider myself guilty. From what I have set out in my testimony, it is evident that, beginning in June 1934, I knew facts characterizing Leonid NIKOLAEV's departure from the Party and his transformation into an enemy of the Party.

I acknowledge myself guilty of not responding, in a manner befitting a Party member's duty, to Leonid NIKOLAEV's political statements and outright anti-Party attacks (his attitude toward possible intervention against the USSR and others), confining myself to isolated objections to particular statements of his. I had sufficient grounds to assess substantively and politically that Leonid NIKOLAEV's sentiments over the past half-year, although gradually and, in particular, during my last meeting with Leonid NIKOLAEV on November 10, I noted and saw that Leonid NIKOLAEV's anti-Party, despondent sentiments had reached their utmost limits. Neither earlier nor recently (in November of this year) did I put to him the question of where this line of conduct and his sentiments were leading him.

I also consider myself guilty of failing to inform the Party organization of Leonid NIKOLAEV's statements and sentiments, although I understood well enough that he was probing and studying my political views and sentiments, seeking sympathy and support in me.

All this is aggravated by the fact that, as I testified, over the past year Leonid NIKOLAEV, having grown close to me and listened to me attentively, came increasingly under my influence as time passed; consequently, I had a greater opportunity to prevent his sliding into and finally crossing over to the counterrevolutionary camp.

Thus, through the course of events, I placed myself alongside the terrorist and failed to divert the hand that fired at the secretary of the Party Central Committee.

QUESTION: Do you further acknowledge that you knew what measures of struggle against the Party Leonid NIKOLAEV was contemplating, and that you discussed with him the objective and plans for attempts on the lives of the Party leaders?

ANSWER: No, I repeat that Leonid NIKOLAEV did not discuss with me any intentions to prepare and carry out a terrorist attack on the leaders.

Recorded correctly from my words, read by me.

KULISHER.

INTERROGATED BY:

DEPUTY HEAD OF THE OO OF THE GUGB OF THE NKVD — SOSNOVSKY

Certified true: M. Koroleva
