INTERROGATION RECORD

of GERTIK, Artyom Moiseevich, dated December 16, 1934.

QUESTION: Do you plead guilty to counterrevolutionary, double-dealing, anti-Party activities?

ANSWER: Yes, I plead guilty to double-dealing, anti-Party activities.

QUESTION: Describe your activities directed against the Party line and the Party leadership.

ANSWER: I met with EVDOKIMOV, BAKAEV, KOSTINA \[1\], KUKLIN, GORSHENIN, KHARITONOV, SHAROV, FEDOROV, ZINOVIEV and KAMENEV, that is, with people who had previously belonged to the Zinovievist-Trotskyist bloc and had not completely overcome their anti-Party attitudes.

Personally, I rarely met ZINOVIEV, but I knew of his anti-Party attitudes and conversations from EVDOKIMOV, BAKAEV, GORSHENIN and others who met him more frequently.

The essence of our anti-Party attitudes was that, when discussing current issues of Party policy, we criticized many Party actions and circulated among ourselves all sorts of rumors about disagreements within the Party leadership, individual failures in the Party leadership's actions, and so forth.

Our anti-Party attitudes depended directly on the country's economic situation and, becoming more acute as difficulties in the country increased, subsided once those difficulties were overcome.

QUESTION: Describe in detail your connections with former oppositionists in Leningrad.

ANSWER: In Leningrad I met LEVIN, BASHKIROV and TSVIBAK; I saw MYASNIKOV and MIRONOV once. At these meetings I informed them of what I knew about ZINOVIEV's views on questions of domestic and international policy, and I told people connected with me in Moscow about my conversations with the Leningrad people.

Of the Leningrad people, LEVIN visited me in Moscow; KORSHUNOV sometimes dropped in; since 1930 TSVIBAK and BASHKIROV had visited once or twice. In August 1932 LEVIN and I visited ZINOVIEV at his dacha in Ilyinskoye. In my presence LEVIN informed ZINOVIEV about the worsening situation of Leningrad workers; I do not remember what ZINOVIEV said to LEVIN.

I admit the possibility that I conveyed to LEVIN our group's decisions that they should not speak in defense of ZINOVIEV at meetings in Leningrad, but I do not remember the circumstances in which this occurred.

The record has been read to me. Recorded correctly from my words.

GERTIK

INTERROGATED BY: RUTKOVSKY.

Certified correct:

RGASPI, F. 671, Op. 1, D. 120, sheets 66–67.

\[1\] There is an obvious error in the text: “Kostinym”.
