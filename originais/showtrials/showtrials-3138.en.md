RECORD

of the confrontation between the accused ANTONOV Nikolai Semenovich and ZVEZDOV Vasily Ivanovich

conducted on 17 December 1934.

The accused ZVEZDOV V. I. and ANTONOV N. S., summoned for a confrontation, after mutually acknowledging that they knew each other personally, testified:

Question to the accused ZVEZDOV: Do you know that ANTONOV N. S. is a member of the counterrevolutionary organization of Zinovievists?

Answer: Yes, I know this. ANTONOV is a member of our organization of Zinovievists.

Question to the accused ANTONOV: Do you confirm this?

Answer: Yes, I confirm it; I was indeed a member of the counterrevolutionary organization of Zinovievists.

Question to the accused ANTONOV: Do you know of ZVEZDOV V. I.'s membership in the counterrevolutionary organization of Zinovievists?

Answer: Yes, I know that ZVEZDOV is a member of the counterrevolutionary organization of Zinovievists.

Question to the accused ZVEZDOV: When and under what circumstances did you learn of the terrorist act being prepared against Comrade KIROV, and who precisely told you about it?

Answer: In early November, in the library of the Industrial Institute, at about four o'clock, ANTONOV Nikolai informed me that KOTOLYNOV Ivan, a member of our organization, was preparing a consequential and decisive action against one of the members of the Politburo of the CC of the AUCP(b). This information from ANTONOV was preceded by a general conversation about our organization's activities having recently become ineffective and about decisive actions being prepared in order to intensify our activities.

ZVEZDOV.

Question to the accused ANTONOV: What can you testify regarding this?

Answer: Yes, I confirm that in early November, in the library of our (Industrial) Institute, I indeed told ZVEZDOV that KOTOLYNOV Ivan, a leading figure in our organization, was preparing an act — a decisive action by our organization against one of the members of the Politburo of the CC of the AUCP(b). This conversation of mine with KOTOLYNOV Ivan took place in late October of this year, also at the Leningrad Industrial Institute.

ANTONOV.

Confrontation conducted by:

DEPUTY HEAD OF THE SPO OF THE GUGB OF THE NKVD OF THE USSR — LYUSHKOV.

DEPUTY HEAD OF THE SPO OF THE UGB — STROMIN.

HEAD OF THE 1ST SECTION OF THE SPO OF THE GUGB — PETROVSKY.
