INTERROGATION RECORD

Of FEDOROV, Grigory Fedorovich (supplementary)

Dated December 21, 1934.

QUESTION: What were the directives of the leadership of the Zinovievist-Trotskyist organization concerning the readmission of participants in that organization to the Party?

ANSWER: I know that ZINOVIEV issued the directive that one must enter the Party at all costs. It was pointed out that one's opposition cadres must be preserved within the Party in case of further likely disagreements with the Party. Alongside this a directive was given to abandon the practice of holding conferences of the organization's participants, replacing them with comradely tea parties at which, behind the innocent form of these gatherings, current political questions would be discussed.

As I indicated in my previous testimony, until 1931 I participated with other oppositionists in discussions at a number of such conferences. In 1931 I withdrew from this Zinovievist-Trotskyist counterrevolutionary organization, but since I did not declare this to any of the organization's participants, I consider that formally I continued to belong to the organization until my arrest, all the more so since until my arrest I preserved and maintained connections with participants in this counterrevolutionary organization.

QUESTION: The investigation has established that the terrorist act against Comrade KIROV was committed by NIKOLAEV, a participant in the Zinovievist-Trotskyist counterrevolutionary organization. Do you acknowledge that the organization as a whole, and you in particular as one of its participants, bear responsibility for committing this terrorist act?

ANSWER: Yes, I fully acknowledge both the responsibility of the entire Zinovievist-Trotskyist organization as a whole and, in particular, my responsibility for committing the terrorist act against Comrade KIROV.

Recorded correctly from my words and read to me.

FEDOROV.

INTERROGATED BY:

HEAD OF THE 3RD SECTION OF THE EKO OF THE GUGB OF THE NKVD OF THE USSR, CHERTOK.
