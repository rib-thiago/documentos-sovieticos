INTERROGATION RECORD

Of POZDEEVA, Maria Vasilyevna, January 4, 1935.

Question: What do you know about GORSHENIN, a member of the Zinovievist organization?

Answer: I know that GORSHENIN is close to the center of the Zinovievist organization. He meets BAKAEV, GERTIK, and apparently ZINOVIEV. During the purge he was expelled from the All-Union Communist Party (Bolsheviks). During one of my meetings with BUDZINSKAYA (VUJOVIC’s wife), I expressed my opinion of GORSHENIN in connection with his expulsion. I said that I had never trusted him, that he gave me the impression of a man who used his membership in the party for his personal interests, a bureaucrat who disliked work. BUDZINSKAYA agreed with my opinion and added that he did not inspire her trust either: “He joined the party late and immediately became a professor. His past is unknown to us. It was right to expel him.”

Question: What else do you wish to add for the investigation?

Answer: I remembered a conversation of mine with GERTIK that may be of interest to the investigation: once I asked GERTIK about NAUMOV’s former wife, NATANSON. GERTIK told me that she had been arrested and banished, and that it was forbidden to mention her name in NAUMOV’s house. GERTIK told me at the same time that, according to EVDOKIMOV, NATANSON had behaved very badly during the investigation, meaning that she had told the truth.

Recorded from my words — POZDEEVA.

INTERROGATED BY: HEAD OF THE 4TH SECTION OF THE SPO OF THE GUGB, KOGAN.

Certified correct: Churbanova

RGASPI, fond 671, inventory 1, file 126, folio 244.
