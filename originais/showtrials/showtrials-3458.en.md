INTERROGATION RECORD

Of POPOV, Georgy Fedorovich —

December 26, 1934.

QUESTION: Describe your meetings with ZHESTYANNIKOV and the nature of your conversation with him.

ANSWER: I have known ZHESTYANNIKOV since 1921 through our work together in the Petrograd district committee of the Komsomol. We had not seen each other for a long time and met at Istmol during preparations for the Komsomol's fifteenth anniversary. I remember that at that time ZHESTYANNIKOV expressed dissatisfaction that political distrust had been expressed toward him in his unit, which he had learned about in connection with some promotion in the service. I do not remember other conversations from that meeting.

POPOV. —

INTERROGATED BY:

HEAD OF THE 6TH SECTION OF THE SPO: KORKIN.

Certified true: M. Glazkov
