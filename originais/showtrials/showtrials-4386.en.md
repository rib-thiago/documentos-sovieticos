CONFRONTATION RECORD

between EVDOKIMOV Grigory Eremeevich and SHAROV Yakov Vasilyevich

10 January 1935.

The accused EVDOKIMOV G. E. and SHAROV Ya. V., acknowledging that they knew each other, testified:

QUESTION TO EVDOKIMOV: Do you acknowledge that until the latest period you and SHAROV belonged to the Moscow center of the counterrevolutionary Zinovievist organization?

EVDOKIMOV'S ANSWER: Yes, I acknowledge that together with SHAROV I belonged until the latest period to the Moscow center of the counterrevolutionary Zinovievist organization.

QUESTION TO SHAROV: Do you confirm this testimony by EVDOKIMOV?

SHAROV'S ANSWER: Yes, I confirm it in full.

QUESTION TO SHAROV: What do you know of the composition of the Moscow center of the counterrevolutionary Zinovievist organization?

SHAROV'S ANSWER: The Moscow center of the Zinovievist organization consisted of ZINOVIEV, KAMENEV, EVDOKIMOV, BAKAEV, FEDOROV, KUKLIN, KHARITONOV, GORSHENIN, GERTIK, and myself, SHAROV.

QUESTION TO EVDOKIMOV: Do you confirm SHAROV's testimony about the Moscow center's composition?

EVDOKIMOV'S ANSWER: Yes, I confirm it. Besides those named by SHAROV, the Moscow center of the Zinovievist organization also included GESSEN, who had lived in Smolensk in recent years.

QUESTION TO SHAROV: Do you acknowledge the Zinovievist organization's activities as counterrevolutionary?

SHAROV'S ANSWER: Yes, I unconditionally acknowledge the Zinovievist organization's activities as counterrevolutionary.

QUESTION TO EVDOKIMOV: Do you confirm this?

EVDOKIMOV'S ANSWER: Yes, I confirm that the Zinovievist organization's activities were counterrevolutionary.

QUESTION TO SHAROV: Name the participants in the Zinovievist organization in Moscow known to you.

SHAROV'S ANSWER: Besides the members of the Moscow center, I knew the following as participants in the Zinovievist organization in Moscow: ZALUTSKY, KOZHURO, KOSTINA, SAKHOV, BRAVO.

QUESTION TO EVDOKIMOV: Do you confirm SHAROV's testimony?

EVDOKIMOV'S ANSWER: Yes, I confirm it. Besides the participants in the Zinovievist organization named by SHAROV, the organization also included GERTSBERG, POZDEEVA, and PERIMOV.

QUESTION TO SHAROV: Name the participants in the Zinovievist organization in Leningrad known to you.

SHAROV'S ANSWER: In Leningrad the Zinovievist organization included LEVIN Vladimir, RUMYANTSEV, MANDELSHTAM, KOTOLYNOV, ALEXANDROV Alexander, KOSTRITSKY, KORSHUNOV, and REM.

QUESTION TO EVDOKIMOV: Do you confirm SHAROV's testimony?

EVDOKIMOV'S ANSWER: Yes, I confirm it. Besides those named by SHAROV, I also knew NALIVAIKO Foma and MIRONOV as participants in the Zinovievist organization in Leningrad. Personally I had not seen a considerable number of these persons recently.

QUESTION TO EVDOKIMOV: Name the participants in the Zinovievist organization in the provinces, apart from Moscow and Leningrad, known to you.

EVDOKIMOV'S ANSWER: Among participants in the organization in the provinces I knew ELKOVICH in Sverdlovsk; SAKHOV in Arkhangelsk; ROZNER also in Arkhangelsk. I last met ROZNER in Moscow on the eve of the October anniversary in 1932; although it is possible that inwardly he had not overcome his sentiments, there was no conversation on political subjects; GORDON and ROTSKAN in Rostov-on-Don.

QUESTION TO SHAROV: Do you confirm this testimony by EVDOKIMOV?

SHAROV'S ANSWER: To the persons named in Rostov-on-Don I can add SOLOVIEV Pyotr. In addition I knew GESSEN in Smolensk and RAVICH Olga in Voronezh as participants in the Zinovievist organization.

Ya. SHAROV.                                                    G. EVDOKIMOV.

Confrontation conducted by:

DEPUTY PEOPLE'S COMMISSAR FOR INTERNAL AFFAIRS OF THE USSR — AGRANOV

DEPUTY HEAD OF THE SPO OF THE GUGB OF THE NKVD OF THE USSR — LYUSHKOV

ASSISTANT TO THE HEAD OF THE SPO OF THE GUGB OF THE NKVD OF THE USSR — RUTKOVSKY

HEAD OF A SECTION OF THE EKO OF THE GUGB OF THE NKVD OF THE USSR — CHERTOK
