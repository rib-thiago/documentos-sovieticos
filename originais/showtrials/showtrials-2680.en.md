CONTINUATION OF THE INTERROGATION

OF NIKOLAEV Leonid Vasilyevich

dated 6/XII-1934.

QUESTION: When did you approach the British Consulate to establish contact?

ANSWER: Alongside my attempt to establish contact with the German Consulate, I also tried to make contact with the British one. I began observing the consul arriving at and leaving the consulate building. I also intended to approach one of the consulate employees near the building and ask that person to put me in contact with the consul. But nothing came of this plan because I was unable to meet any of the consulate employees.

Incidentally, to make it easier to establish contact with the British Consulate, I spent some time studying English (using a dictionary).

Recorded correctly from my words and read to me.

L. NIKOLAEV

INTERROGATED BY:

DEPUTY PEOPLE'S COMMISSAR FOR INTERNAL AFFAIRS –  AGRANOV

DEPUTY HEAD OF THE EKO OF THE GUGB, NKVD –  DMITRIEV
