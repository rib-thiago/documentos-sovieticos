INTERROGATION RECORD

OF ANTONOV, Nikolai Semionovich

dated 11 December 1934.

QUESTION: In your testimony of 9/XII this year, you stated that the group of former Zinovievists with whom you were associated was united on the basis of anti-Party views. Explain what specifically these anti-Party views consisted of.

ANSWER: In our conversations we touched on the Party's policy in the countryside and the industrialisation of the country — the question of tempos. Regarding the Party's rural policy, we said at our meetings that the policy of liquidating the kulak as a class by administrative means and artificially imposing collective farms led to agricultural decline and the impoverishment of the countryside; that the unbearable tempos of industrialisation were being carried out by squeezing the lifeblood out of the working class; that the country's difficulties were compounded by the regime within the Party and repressions against ZINOVIEV and other major figures removed from the country's leadership.

Taken down from my words.

N. ANTONOV.

INTERROGATED BY —

HEAD OF THE 1ST SECTION, SPO, GUGB, PETROVSKY.
