INTERROGATION RECORD

Of GAIDEROVA Zinaida Nikandrovna, conducted by the head of the 4th Section of the SPO of the UGB of the NKVD of the Leningrad Region — KOGAN L.

January 11, 1935.

QUESTION: The investigation possesses information that until recently you belonged to the Zinovievist group in Leningrad. Do you confirm this?

ANSWER: Yes, I do. After my return to the party, having formally renounced the Zinovievist platform, I continued to be connected with a number of Zinovievists with whom I had struggled against the party during the years of our extensive legal struggle (1926–1928). This connection was anti-Soviet in character. From 1930 until recently I was personally connected above all with DMITRIEV Timofei Dmitrievich, who was in Rostov-on-Don and with whom I corresponded. I carried out a number of assignments for DMITRIEV, finding and informing him of the addresses of Zinovievists who interested him. I informed him about MYASNIKOV, LEVIN and KORSHUNOV. During these same years I had a number of meetings with LEVIN Vl. and BASHKIROV Al. In Moscow I visited GERTIK's apartment. In late 1931, at a health resort, I stayed together (in the same sanatorium) with OLGA RAVICH, GERTIK and ZALUTSKY.

QUESTION: What do you know about the connections between the Leningrad Zinovievist group and the Moscow group?

ANSWER: I know that GERTIK came to Leningrad from Moscow. I do not know whom he met here. LEVIN Vl. traveled to Moscow. I likewise do not know whom among the Moscow group he visited.

Recorded correctly from my words and read by me. —

Z. GAIDEROVA.

INTERROGATED BY: KOGAN.

Certified correct: Kazakova

RGASPI F. 671, Op. 1, D. 134, fol. 119.
