TESTIMONY

OF GESSEN Sergei Mikhailovich

dated January 13, 1935.

In addition to the testimony I previously gave concerning the Zinovievist group's ties with other anti-Party groupings, I must report the following:

In 1932 SHLYAPNIKOV visited ZINOVIEV and spoke with him on various political subjects. One of SHLYAPNIKOV's visits took place, as far as I remember, at the time of the Ivanovo events of 1932. I do not know the content of the conversations in greater detail, since I was not present myself, but heard about SHLYAPNIKOV's visits from ZINOVIEV and BOGDAN. In the spring of 1932 VARDIN told ZINOVIEV about his conversation with S. MEDVEDEV, in which the latter maliciously attacked not only STALIN but also LENIN. What was new in MEDVEDEV's statements was not the fact that he upheld the correctness of the “Workers' Opposition” in its struggle against LENIN (this had been known earlier), but precisely the extreme harshness of the attacks on LENIN, according to VARDIN. After this ZINOVIEV told me: “When SHLYAPNIKOV comes, I shall demand an explanation from him. If he thinks as MEDVEDEV does, I shall have to tell him: ‘Let us cease to be acquainted.’” I do not know whether SHLYAPNIKOV visited ZINOVIEV afterward.

Written in my own hand: S. GESSEN.

INTERROGATED BY: — RUTKOVSKY.

Certified correct: Kazakova

RGASPI, F. 671, Op. 1, D. 134, fol. 111.
