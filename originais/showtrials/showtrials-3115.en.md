SUPPLEMENTARY INTERROGATION RECORD

OF THE ACCUSED KHANIK L. O., CONDUCTED BY THE DEPUTY HEAD OF THE SPO — STROMIN

16 December 1934.

QUESTION: In your testimony of 13 December of this year you stated that the organization's centers in Moscow and Leningrad took organizational shape in 1933. Indicate all circumstances preceding this organizational formation and how you learned of it.

ANSWER: In my previous testimony I identified 1933 as the time of the greatest influx of former Zinovievists and the beginning of a broad intensification directed at renewing and organizationally consolidating old connections. In 1933 representatives of the Moscow center (GERTIK, KUKLIN, GESSEN) began visiting most frequently from Moscow and demanded that RUMYANTSEV Vladimir and LEVIN Vladimir intensify and expand the organization's work in Leningrad. On that basis I indicated that organizational formation dated to 1933.

Essentially, however, it would be more correct to consider the organization as existing before 1933, since the ideological and political defeat of the Trotskyist-Zinovievist bloc in 1927–1928 did not disarm our organization's members, especially the “leaders” ZINOVIEV, KAMENEV and others, and did not eliminate our underground activity.

I received this information about the organization's renewed activity, visits by Moscow center representatives and directives to strengthen underground work from RUMYANTSEV.

QUESTION: In that testimony of 13 December you state that your organization's leaders cultivated feelings of bitterness and hatred toward the party leadership among its members, especially toward Comrade STALIN and Comrades MOLOTOV, KAGANOVICH and KIROV. Explain in detail who exactly instilled such feelings in organization members.

ANSWER: It must be said frankly that bitterness and hatred toward Comrade STALIN and Comrades MOLOTOV, KAGANOVICH and KIROV increased as the party and country overcame difficulties in fulfilling the First and Second Five-Year Plans. The spread of this blind, I would say bestial, hatred originated in Moscow with the center's leading figures — ZINOVIEV, KAMENEV, EVDOKIMOV and BAKAEV — and in Leningrad with RUMYANTSEV Vladimir, LEVIN Vladimir and KOTOLYNOV Ivan. There were no limits to the attacks and malicious fabrications used by these persons against the party leadership. Constant criticism of party policy and measures, combined with violent attacks against party leaders, produced a system of views bordering on fascism.

QUESTION: What objectives did your organization pursue?

ANSWER: Changing the party line in the spirit of the Zinovievist platform, by replacing the party leadership with former leaders of the Trotskyist-Zinovievist bloc.

QUESTION: The investigation has information that on 25 November of this year a meeting between you, MYASNIKOV and LEVIN Vladimir took place in the Smolny building, in MYASNIKOV Nikolai Petrovich's office; indicate the character of this meeting.

ANSWER: On instructions from the Kronstadt District Party Committee I was coordinating with MYASNIKOV the number of Kronstadt seats in the Leningrad Soviet in connection with the Soviet elections. While I was speaking with him, LEVIN Vladimir happened to come in. We settled no questions concerning the organization. Comrade LAZURKINA, an outsider, was then in MYASNIKOV's office.

The record was correctly taken down from my words and read by me, in confirmation of which I sign.

L. KHANIK

INTERROGATED BY — STROMIN

Certified correct: M. Koroleva
