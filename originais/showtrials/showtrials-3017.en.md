INTERROGATION RECORD

OF NIKOLAEV LEONID VASILYEVICH, 14 DECEMBER 1934.

QUESTION: Did you adhere to the opposition in 1925–1928?

ANSWER: In 1925–1928 I did not openly belong to the opposition, but I must say that on a number of questions I shared its views: on the removal of opposition leaders from leading work and on ways of overcoming the country's economic difficulties (I favored increasing grain procurement prices and the like).

I can give the following example illustrating my vacillation toward the opposition. In the Komsomol organization at the KRASNY ARSENAL factory, where I was then working, an oppositionist was discovered at one meeting; I proposed at the meeting that he should not be expelled from the Komsomol.

Beginning in 1931, I moved further away from the party line, culminating in my joining the counterrevolutionary Zinovievist organization in August 1934.

I shall testify about my political views during the most recent period in a separate record.

Correctly taken down in shorthand from my words. I have read the interrogation record —

L. NIKOLAEV.

INTERROGATED BY:

DEPUTY PEOPLE'S COMMISSAR OF INTERNAL AFFAIRS OF THE USSR: (AGRANOV)

HEAD OF THE EKO, GUGB, NKVD OF THE USSR: (MIRONOV)

ASSISTANT TO THE HEAD OF THE EKO, GUGB, NKVD OF THE USSR: (DMITRIEV)
