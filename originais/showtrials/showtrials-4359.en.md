CONFRONTATION RECORD

Between BAKAEV Ivan Petrovich and EVDOKIMOV Grigory Yeremeevich

January 9, 1935.

The accused BAKAEV I. P. and EVDOKIMOV G. E., having acknowledged that they know each other, testified:

QUESTION TO BAKAEV: Do you acknowledge that, until recently, you were, together with EVDOKIMOV, a member of the Moscow center of the counterrevolutionary Zinovievist organization?

ANSWER: I acknowledge that I was indeed, until recently, together with EVDOKIMOV, a member of the Moscow center of the counterrevolutionary Zinovievist organization.

QUESTION TO EVDOKIMOV: Do you confirm this testimony by BAKAEV?

ANSWER: I confirm this testimony by BAKAEV.

QUESTION TO BAKAEV: What do you know about the membership of the Moscow center of the counterrevolutionary Zinovievist organization?

ANSWER: The Moscow center of the counterrevolutionary Zinovievist organization included ZINOVIEV, KAMENEV, myself, BAKAEV, EVDOKIMOV, KUKLIN, FEDOROV, SHAROV, GORSHENIN, and GERTIK; KHARITONOV was a member until 1932.

QUESTION TO EVDOKIMOV: Do you confirm BAKAEV's testimony about the membership of the Moscow center?

ANSWER: Yes, I confirm that the Moscow center of the counterrevolutionary Zinovievist organization did indeed consist of the persons named above.

QUESTION TO EVDOKIMOV: Do you acknowledge that immediately after the Fifteenth Congress of the All-Union Communist Party (Bolsheviks), together with BAKAEV and other former participants in the Zinovievist-Trotskyist bloc, headed by ZINOVIEV and KAMENEV, you created a Zinovievist organization that waged a struggle against the Party and Soviet power until recently?

ANSWER: Yes, I acknowledge that immediately after the Fifteenth Congress of the All-Union Communist Party (Bolsheviks), together with BAKAEV and other former participants in the Zinovievist-Trotskyist bloc, headed by ZINOVIEV and KAMENEV, I created a Zinovievist organization that waged a struggle against the Party and Soviet power until recently.

QUESTION TO BAKAEV: Do you confirm EVDOKIMOV's testimony?

ANSWER: Yes, I confirm it; the organization's activity was, in essence, counterrevolutionary and anti-Soviet in character.

QUESTION TO BAKAEV: Name the participants in the Zinovievist organization in Moscow known to you.

ANSWER: There were participants in the Zinovievist organization in Moscow; they were BRAVO, my wife KOSTINA Anna, GERTSBERG Aleksandr, POZDEEVA Mariya, PERIMOV, and MAGYAR, whose participation, in my opinion, consisted only in supplying ZINOVIEV with information about the Comintern.

QUESTION TO EVDOKIMOV: Do you confirm BAKAEV's testimony?

ANSWER: Yes, I confirm that there were participants in the Zinovievist organization in Moscow; I confirm that the persons named by BAKAEV came to them \[1\], except for MAGYAR, about whom I did not know this.

QUESTION TO BAKAEV: What do you know about the participants in the Zinovievist organization in Leningrad?

ANSWER: I know the following participants in the organization in Leningrad: LEVIN Vladimir, RUMYANTSEV Vladimir, ANISHEV, SHIRYAEV, and SOLOVYOV K.

QUESTION TO EVDOKIMOV: Do you confirm BAKAEV's testimony?

ANSWER: Yes, I confirm it; in addition, there was MIRONOV.

QUESTION TO EVDOKIMOV: Name the participants in the Zinovievist organization known to you in the provinces, outside Moscow and Leningrad.

ANSWER: I know of the membership of ELKOVICH in Sverdlovsk, ROZNER in Arkhangelsk, and ROTSKAN, DMITRIEV T., and GORDON N. in Rostov-on-Don.

QUESTION TO BAKAEV: Do you confirm this testimony?

ANSWER: In Rostov-on-Don, the members of the organization were ROTSKAN Pyotr, DMITRIEV T., and GORDON Nikolay.

In Sverdlovsk — ELKOVICH Yakov.

In Voronezh — Olga RAVICH.

In Arkhangelsk — ROZNER and SAKHOV.

EVDOKIMOV. BAKAEV.

The confrontation was conducted by:

HEAD OF THE EKO OF THE GUGB OF THE USSR NKVD — MIRONOV

DEPUTY HEAD OF THE SPO OF THE USSR NKVD — LYUSHKOV

ASSISTANT HEAD OF THE EKO OF THE GUGB OF THE USSR NKVD — DMITRIEV

ASSISTANT HEAD OF THE SPO OF THE GUGB OF THE USSR NKVD — RUTKOVSKY

Certified true: A. Svetlova
