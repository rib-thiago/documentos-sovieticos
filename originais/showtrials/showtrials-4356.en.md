INTERROGATION RECORD

Of SAFAROV G. I., January 9, 1935.

I additionally testify:

In November 1927, Ya. LIVSHITS worked as the representative of the Ukrainian SSR in the USSR trade mission. He belonged to the Trotskyist organization, was connected with Pyotr PEREVERZEV, and carried out Trotskyist assignments on his instructions. The trade representative BITKER also belonged to the counterrevolutionary bloc; he arranged my fictitious official trip and subsequent journey to the USSR, and provided money for this journey.

Fritz PLATTEN, removed from the leadership of the Swiss Communist Party and connected with ZINOVIEV by an old “friendship,” retained, by all indications, his old “Zinovievist” sympathies. He visited the Comintern until recently, and I suppose that during visits by HUMBERT-DROZ, one of the leaders of the Right, he could have transmitted his own and others' assignments and “information” abroad. This is all the more likely since PLATTEN had not acknowledged his errors and deviations until recently. ZINOVIEV knew PLATTEN well and repeatedly referred to him as a “firm man.”

PEVZNER, who worked at Gosbank in Kharkov, had been an active Trotskyist since 1923 and belonged to the counterrevolutionary Trotskyist-Zinovievist bloc in 1925–1927.

KUTUZOV, who worked in Central Asia in 1919–1921 in Poarm 1, a Trotskyist since 1923, took the most active part in the struggle against the Party in 1926–1927. Meeting me at an underground gathering in Moscow during the Fifteenth Congress, he defended, in essence, the counterrevolutionary views of the “Decists” on the Party, its leadership, and Soviet power.

Written in my own hand — SAFAROV.

INTERROGATED BY:

HEAD OF THE 1ST SECTION OF THE SPO OF THE GUGB OF THE USSR NKVD — PETROVSKY.
