CONFRONTATION RECORD

between YUSKIN Ignaty and NIKOLAEV, Leonid Vasilyevich

19 December 1934.

The accused YUSKIN I. and NIKOLAEV L. V., summoned for a confrontation, after mutually confirming that they knew each other personally, testified:

QUESTION TO YUSKIN: What did you know from NIKOLAEV about his preparations to murder Comrade KIROV?

ANSWER: In October 1934, while at my home, NIKOLAEV confided to me that he was preparing to murder KIROV.

QUESTION TO NIKOLAEV: Do you confirm that you did indeed tell YUSKIN about preparations to murder Comrade KIROV?

ANSWER: Yes, I confirm that I did indeed confide to YUSKIN that I was preparing to murder KIROV.

QUESTION TO NIKOLAEV: Did YUSKIN participate in preparing the murder of Comrade KIROV?

ANSWER: Yes, he did; his participation consisted in discussing with me precisely where the attack on KIROV should be carried out and advising at which point in KIROV's movements this should be done.

QUESTION TO YUSKIN: Do you confirm that you participated in preparing and carrying out the murder of Comrade KIROV?

ANSWER: No, I deny it.

QUESTION TO NIKOLAEV: Did you know that YUSKIN was a member of the Zinovievist-Trotskyist counterrevolutionary organization, and did you tell him that you yourself belonged to this organization?

ANSWER: Yes, I knew; YUSKIN personally told me this; I told him that I too belonged to this organization.

QUESTION TO YUSKIN: Do you confirm NIKOLAEV's testimony that you told him of your membership in the Zinovievist-Trotskyist counterrevolutionary organization and that he told you about his participation in this organization?

ANSWER: No, I deny it.

QUESTION TO YUSKIN: Did you propose to NIKOLAEV that he organize a terrorist act against Comrade STALIN, and did you agree to participate in preparing this act?

ANSWER: Yes, in a conversation with NIKOLAEV I told him that to kill KIROV, one must kill STALIN.

QUESTION TO NIKOLAEV: Do you confirm this testimony by YUSKIN?

ANSWER: Yes, I confirm it; I add that YUSKIN at that time arranged with me to travel to Moscow to take measures to prepare a terrorist act against STALIN.

The answers were recorded correctly from our words; each confirms his own answers.

NIKOLAEV L.                                  YUSKIN I.

Confrontation conducted by:

HEAD OF THE EKO OF THE GUGB OF THE NKVD OF THE USSR — MIRONOV

ASSISTANT TO THE HEAD OF THE EKO OF THE GUGB OF THE NKVD OF THE USSR — DMITRIEV

OPERATIONAL OFFICER OF THE SPO OF THE GUGB OF THE NKVD OF THE USSR — FEDOROV
