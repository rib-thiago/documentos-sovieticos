INTERROGATION RECORD

OF RUMYANTSEV Vladimir Vasilyevich

14 December 1934.

In addition to my testimony of 13 December 1934, I consider it necessary to inform the investigation of another fact confirming that, after the defeat of the Zinovievist-Trotskyist bloc, the counterrevolutionary Zinovievist organization was reestablished. In August or September 1928, ZINOVIEV came to Leningrad for LILINA's funeral. After the funeral, organization members LEVIN Vladimir, MANDELSHTAM Sergei, REM \[1\] — now director of the ORS on the October Railway —, SAFRONOV Pyotr, MIKHAILOV (recently deceased), RYABOV or LAPTEV (both former students of the Tolmachev Military-Political Academy), I — RUMYANTSEV —, NALIVAIKO Foma and several others whose surnames I do not remember gathered at SHAROV Yakov's apartment on the Petrograd Side. SHAROV was absent. LEVIN V. brought us all together. ZINOVIEV attended this illegal meeting and gave us directives for the organization's further work, setting us the principal task of keeping in touch and preserving our cadres. This was after ZINOVIEV had submitted his statement renouncing his anti-party views and struggle against the party. In essence this was an organizational meeting, since afterward LEVIN V. told me that NALIVAIKO Foma had begun work in the Narva District, REM in the Central District, LAPTEV in Vasileostrov, NATANSON in Petrograd, SOSITSKY in Vyborg, and MIKHAILOV with RYABOV in Volodarsky.

Question: Who else among the counterrevolutionary organization's members, besides those you previously reported, displayed the sharpest hatred toward the leaders of the All-Union Communist Party (Bolsheviks), and especially Comrade STALIN?

Answer: LEVIN Vladimir, SOSITSKY Lev and MYASNIKOV Nikolai, especially the last, who reflected LEVIN's sentiments. MYASNIKOV Nikolai was very close to BAKAEV.

RUMYANTSEV.

INTERROGATED BY:

DEPUTY HEAD OF THE SPO, GUGB — LYUSHKOV
