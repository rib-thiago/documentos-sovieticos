INTERROGATION RECORD

OF ANTONOV Nikolai Semenovich

21 December 1934.

In addition to my testimony, I consider it necessary to inform the investigation of the following:

In mid-August 1934, at RUMYANTSEV's apartment, where I, ANTONOV, RUMYANTSEV, and FADEEV F. were present, after drawing up a list of old Komsomol members, RUMYANTSEV recounted to us the contents of the book by AGABEKOV, who had fled the USSR. During this conversation, the question arose of war and the USSR's relations with Japan. On this question we all concluded that, should war break out, the present leadership of the AUCP(b) would be even less able to cope with the tasks facing the country and would be forced to bring our “leaders,” ZINOVIEV and KAMENEV, into the country's leadership. It must be said that we hoped to take advantage of war in order to come into the leadership.

There were individual conversations about this among members of our organization of Zinovievists, and, if it was not openly expressed, a definite desire to hasten war showed through, since war might contribute to the maximum intensification of our organization's work, and we would use the situation complicated by war to replace the leadership of the AUCP(b).

Recorded correctly from my words and read to me.

ANTONOV N.

INTERROGATED BY:

HEAD OF THE 1ST SECTION OF THE SPO OF THE GUGB — PETROVSKY.

certified true: A. Svetlova
