INTERROGATION RECORD

OF ANTONOV, Nikolai Semionovich

dated 10 December 1934.

QUESTION: Do you know, and from whom, of the existence and nature of a directive letter from ZINOVIEV instructing Zinovievists on the advisability of returning to the Party?

ANSWER: After the Fifteenth Congress, in 1928, I came to Leningrad from Vologda and called at Volodya RUMYANTSEV's apartment. Mikhail MURAVIEV, who had also adhered to the Zinovievist opposition, was with me. At that time RUMYANTSEV had not yet broken with the opposition. We then discussed the advisability of returning to the Party. RUMYANTSEV was dissatisfied with ZINOVIEV's position, which favoured returning to the Party under certain conditions. RUMYANTSEV then showed me a letter from ZINOVIEV, written in red ink on a small sheet of white paper, in which ZINOVIEV gave RUMYANTSEV instructions about reinstatement in the Party.

Correctly taken down from my words.

N. ANTONOV

INTERROGATED BY: HEAD OF THE 1ST SECTION, SPO, GUGB — PETROVSKY
