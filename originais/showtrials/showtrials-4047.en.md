INTERROGATION RECORD

Conducted by GOLUBEV, officer of the 2nd Section of the SPO, on January 3, 1935.

DMITRIEV, Nikolai Alexandrovich.

Question: Which other former Zinovievists did you meet in Leningrad, apart from those mentioned in your previous testimony?

Answer: In the previous interrogation records I did not indicate that in 1933 and 1934 I was also in contact in Leningrad with NALIVAIKO, F., a former participant in the Zinovievist-Trotskyist bloc. I visited NALIVAIKO’s apartment twice, once with the former Zinovievist LUKIN and my brother DMITRIEV, Yevgeny. In the autumn of 1933, the former Zinovievist TARASOV, Pyotr, came to Leningrad from Magnitogorsk. On his arrival he contacted LUKIN in Leningrad; the latter, that is, LUKIN, telephoned my home and told me that TARASOV had arrived and would be at his apartment, and invited me to call on him. I went and met TARASOV at LUKIN’s.

Question: What conversations did you have when meeting NALIVAIKO and TARASOV, Pyotr?

Answer: NALIVAIKO told me that ALEKSANDROV, Alexander, and LEOSHKO worked with him in the Regional Supply Department, and complained that ALEKSANDROV, A., did not treat him and LEOSHKO well enough. He also reported that, during the party purge, he, NALIVAIKO, had been accused of an opportunist interpretation of the possibilities of fulfilling planned targets for the Fermentation Trust. NALIVAIKO pointed out that, during the purge, this question had been linked to his membership in the opposition, which made it considerably more acute.

When I met TARASOV, he inquired about SOBOL’s attitudes and whereabouts, and he and LUKIN recalled their activities and contacts in factional work in Kharkov.

Question: When and where was your brother DMITRIEV, Yevgeny, abroad?

Answer: From 1923 to 1925 my brother DMITRIEV, Yevgeny, was in England, where he worked at Arcos Bank. In 1926 he came to the USSR for about two months and then left for China and Harbin, where he worked as a representative of Tsentrosoyuz until 1929.

Question: Did your brother Yevgeny DMITRIEV join any opposition?

Answer: In 1923 DMITRIEV, Yevgeny, joined the Trotskyist opposition; I do not know what his participation in this opposition consisted of.

Question: Did DMITRIEV, Yevgeny, receive any party penalties, and for what?

Answer: DMITRIEV, Yevgeny, told me that he had received a penalty from the Central Control Commission for his conduct in Harbin; I do not know exactly for what, but apparently for drunkenness.

I know that in 1933 he gave a mechanistic interpretation of certain questions in his article “The Dialectics of Technology,” but received no party penalties for this. —

The testimony has been recorded correctly from my words and read by me.

DMITRIEV.

INTERROGATED BY — GOLUBEV.

Certified correct: Churbanova
