INTERROGATION RECORD

Of POPOV, Georgy Fedorovich, dated January 4, 1935.

Question: What do you know about G. M. DRYAZGOV’s membership in the counterrevolutionary organization?

Answer: I know nothing about G. M. DRYAZGOV’s membership in our counterrevolutionary organization.

Question: What can you testify about ZHESTYANNIKOV and P. BURMISTROV?

Answer: They are likewise unknown to me as members of the counterrevolutionary Zinovievist organization. Concerning ZHESTYANNIKOV, I testified that he expressed dissatisfaction with the distrust shown toward him. I had no political conversations with BURMISTROV. —

POPOV. —

INTERROGATED BY: KORKIN.

Certified correct: Churbanova

RGASPI, fond 671, inventory 1, file 127, folio 51.
