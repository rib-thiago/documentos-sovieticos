INTERROGATION RECORD

conducted on December 16, 1934, by the head of the 4th Section of the SPO — L. KOGAN.

MURAVYOV, Mikhail Vasilievich, born in 1902, a native of Leningrad; his father worked as a shop assistant and died in 1914. Russian, a citizen of the USSR. Residence: 13 Kalyaev Street, apartment 34. Head of the Commercial Department of the 3rd Confectionery Factory. Wife: Valentina Ivanovna, a member of the VKP(b), administrative manager of the Vyborg RAYPO. Son Yuri, aged 3. Mother: Praskovya Alekseevna, Slavyanka station on the October Railway; brother Aleksei, a member of the VKP(b), Prombank; brother Arseny, the Leningrad Soviet's Transport Administration; brother Dmitry, in the Red Army; sister Maria, lives with their mother; sister Natalia, works at the Lentrikotazh cooperative as assistant accountant. Has no property. Education: a three-class school in Leningrad. A member of the VKP(b) since 1925. Never expelled. I have a severe reprimand for active participation in the Trotskyist-Zinovievist bloc. Withdrew at the end of 1927. Was a member of the Komsomol provincial committee until 1925. Never tried. Registration category II (combat-duty sailor). Did not serve with the Whites.

QUESTION: The investigation has information that former participants in the Trotskyist-Zinovievist bloc did not cease their struggle against the Party and that a counterrevolutionary Zinovievist organization, of which you are a member, has existed until recently. What can you testify about this?

ANSWER: Yes. I acknowledge it. The Trotskyist-Zinovievist bloc, ideologically defeated at the Fifteenth Party Congress, did not cease its struggle until recently. It exists as a counterrevolutionary organization standing on the platform of the Trotskyist-Zinovievist bloc and directed against the Party leadership. Personally, I belong to a group of this organization led by V. RUMYANTSEV, with whom, apart from myself, ZVEZDOV, TSARKOV, KOTOLYNOV and VINOGRADOV are connected.

All those listed, while remaining in the Party, carry out ZINOVIEV's directive to deceive the Party and preserve the cadres, which he, ZINOVIEV, conveyed to RUMYANTSEV. I personally know of this instruction from RUMYANTSEV.

MURAVYOV.

Interrogated by: L. KOGAN.
