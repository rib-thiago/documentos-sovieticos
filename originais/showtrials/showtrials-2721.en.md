INTERROGATION RECORD

conducted by the Head of the 1st Section, SPO, GUGB, NKVD,

PETROVSKY,

on 9 December 1934.

ANTONOV, Nikolai Semionovich, aged 31 (1903), city of Leningrad, Kondratievsky Prospekt, building 10, apartment 10, Russian, USSR citizen, passport issued by the 21st Militia Station, No. ЛЕ 2644742, fifth-year student at the Leningrad Industrial Institute, milling-machine worker; mother: Matriona ANTONOVA; brother: Vladimir Semionovich; sister: Valentina Semionovna ANTONOVA; brother: Ivan Semionovich ANTONOV, living in Khabarovsk; sister: Maria Semionovna VOLYNINA. Incomplete higher education, VKP(b) member since 1922. Registered with the Military Commissariat, category 6. Public activity: Komsomol and Party work from 1918 to 1925; Soviet work from 1925 to 1927; Party work until 1930.

QUESTION: Name your acquaintances who adhered to the Zinovievist opposition.

ANSWER: I maintain contact with Vladimir RUMYANTSEV, secretary of the Vyborg District Soviet; Andrei TOLMAZOV, director of the ORS at “Krasny Putilovets”; Fedor FADEEV, who works in the Railway Department of the Murmansk Railway; Vasily VINOGRADOV, shop head at Factory No. 4; Ivan KOTOLYNOV [1], a student at the Industrial Institute; and Vasily ZVEZDOV, a student at the Industrial Institute.

QUESTION: How can you explain this grouping of former Zinovievists?

ANSWER: Our mutual attraction as former Zinovievists is not accidental; it followed the line of preserving old opposition ties, and the revival of these ties occurred on the basis of our anti-Party views.

N. ANTONOV.

Interrogated by:

Head of the 1st Section, SPO, GUGB, NKVD, PETROVSKY.

Certified true: illegible
