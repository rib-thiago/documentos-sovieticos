RECORD

of the confrontation between the accused GORSHENIN Ivan Stepanovich and PERIMOV Aleksei Viktorovich, conducted on January 10, 1935.

The accused GORSHENIN I.S. and PERIMOV A.V., summoned for the confrontation, after mutually confirming that they knew each other personally, testified:

QUESTION TO GORSHENIN: What do you know about PERIMOV A.V.'s membership in the counterrevolutionary Zinovievist organization?

ANSWER: I know very well that PERIMOV A.V. was a member of the counterrevolutionary Zinovievist organization until recently, and that he maintained organizational ties with the Moscow center of the Zinovievist organization, in particular personally with ZINOVIEV, GERTIK and myself. PERIMOV actively participated in our clandestine gatherings and spoke negatively about the principal decisions of the CC of the VKP(b) and about the party leadership.

QUESTION TO PERIMOV: Do you confirm GORSHENIN I.S.'s testimony?

ANSWER: GORSHENIN I.S.'s testimony corresponds to reality. I confirm it in full, with the exception of one point: I considered myself a member not of the organization, but of the counterrevolutionary Zinovievist grouping.

QUESTION TO GORSHENIN: How do you know that PERIMOV was a member of the organization?

ANSWER: PERIMOV was linked to the “Moscow Zinovievist center”; he participated, as I have already stated above, in clandestine gatherings and spoke negatively about the CC's decisions. During his visits to Moscow he always called on ZINOVIEV, BAKAEV, GERTIK and myself, received information on the state of the organization, and in turn informed us about the situation in the localities.

QUESTION TO PERIMOV: Do you acknowledge your membership in the counterrevolutionary Zinovievist organization?

ANSWER: I do. Amending the testimony I have just given, I declare that I was indeed a member of the underground Zinovievist organization, which I consider counterrevolutionary.

PERIMOV.                                                      GORSHENIN.

The confrontation was conducted by:

Assistant Head of the SPO of the GUGB of the NKVD of the USSR — RUTKOVSKY.

Deputy Head of the SPO of the UGB — STROMIN.

Certified correct: Khvatov

RGASPI F. 671, Op. 1, D. 134, fols. 55–56.
