INTERROGATION RECORD

OF TARASOV Ivan Ivanovich,

January 3, 1935.

Question: Do you know BULAKH, ROZOVSKY, SHVARTS G., YAKOVLEV N. and GRINBERG, and what can you testify about their activity?

Answer: I know all of them. All were active oppositionists at one time. I know little of their activity after the Fifteenth Party Congress; this may be explained by the fact that they did not belong to the “leaderless” group; they always followed ZINOVIEV.

ROZOVSKY Samuil—after returning from Samara to Moscow in 1928, I lived at ROZOVSKY's apartment for about a year. I do not remember exactly, but I think that at that time his sentiments were the same as mine and those of other Zinovievists, that is, non-party sentiments. BULAKH Viktor often visited ROZOVSKY's apartment; his sentiments did not differ from ROZOVSKY's. SHVARTS Grigory lived nearby, with the same sentiments as ROZOVSKY. In recent years, I met BULAKH and ROZOVSKY at BULAKH's apartment and at FAIVILOVICH's. I do not remember anti-Soviet conversations on their part. Dissatisfaction with their position was expressed; in particular, when meeting in 1933, I said that I had been summoned to the District Committee several times to be given work, but as soon as they learned that I had been in the opposition, they did not give me responsible work.

YAKOVLEV Nikolai—in my view, he did not meet any of the persons listed. I have also had no conversations with YAKOVLEV in recent years. In the first period after the Fifteenth Congress, YAKOVLEV was more irreconcilable in his sentiments and returned to the party, I think, later and alone.

GRINBERG—I know that he was in ZINOVIEV's secretariat. About a year and a half ago, when meeting at the institute, he invited me to visit him at home. Once I asked him why ZINOVIEV's surname was no longer among the editorial staff of “Bolshevik,” and where he was now; GRINBERG told me that ZINOVIEV had gone away to rest. There were no other conversations. GRINBERG is a student at the Moscow Power Engineering Institute.

Question: Do you know YAKOVLEV Moisei?

Answer: I do not know YAKOVLEV Moisei.

TARASOV.

Interrogated by: HEAD OF THE 6TH SECTION OF THE SPO OF THE GUGB OF THE NKVD—KORKIN.

Certified correct: Biryukov
