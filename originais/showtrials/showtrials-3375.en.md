INTERROGATION RECORD

Of citizen KAMENEV, Lev Borisovich, dated December 21, 1934.

QUESTION: The testimony of I. P. BAKAEV of December 20, 1934, has established that in late summer 1932 you showed him the Ryutinites' platform at your apartment. Do you confirm this?

ANSWER: I do not. ZINOVIEV showed me RYUTIN's document at the end of September 1932 at our dacha. I looked through this document there and then in ZINOVIEV's presence and returned it to ZINOVIEV. Thus the Ryutinites' platform did not remain in my possession.

QUESTION: The testimony of I. P. BAKAEV and G. E. EVDOKIMOV contradicts your testimony of December 17 that you did not meet or talk with them after returning from Minusinsk. Both testify that they saw and talked with you in 1933–1934. Do you confirm their testimony?

ANSWER: I am certain that I did not see BAKAEV either in 1933 or in 1934. As I have already testified, I saw EVDOKIMOV at the dacha in Ilyinskoye in 1933 or 1934. I had no political conversations with him.

QUESTION: The investigation has information about defeatist sentiments and conversations by ZINOVIEV, yourself and others in recent years in connection with the threat of war against the USSR by the imperialist powers. Do you plead guilty to this?

ANSWER: I had no conversations on such subjects with anyone except ZINOVIEV. In my conversations with ZINOVIEV, however, we did not touch on questions of the situation in the country, since neither of us was sufficiently competent in this matter. Working at Bolshevik, ZINOVIEV had the opportunity to read the foreign press, and our conversations usually concerned questions of an international nature.

My firm conviction in our country's stability and defense capability rested on the fact that the peasantry had accepted the collective-farm system and become convinced of its real advantages, while the government's measures in international policy, particularly entry into the League of Nations and rapprochement with France, strengthened our international position and reduced the dangers of war. I viewed all these measures positively.

The record was read. Recorded correctly from my words.

KAMENEV.

INTERROGATED BY: HEAD OF THE 1ST SECTION OF THE SPO OF THE GUGB OF THE NKVD OF THE USSR, RUTKOVSKY.

In addition, concerning my meetings with BAKAEV and EVDOKIMOV, I report that in 1933 or 1934 EVDOKIMOV did indeed visit me and explained his unexpected arrival by saying that he had been to ZINOVIEV's home, had not found him there, and thought he would find him at mine.

EVDOKIMOV stayed with me about ten minutes; I had no conversations on political subjects with him.

KAMENEV.

INTERROGATED BY: RUTKOVSKY.
