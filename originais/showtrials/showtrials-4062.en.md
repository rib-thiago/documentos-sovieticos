INTERROGATION RECORD

Of the accused SEREDOKHIN, A. K., dated January 4, 1935.

QUESTION: The investigation has information that you are a member of the underground Zinovievist organization. What can you testify about this?

ANSWER: I confess that I am indeed a member of this organization. Our organization consists of former participants in the Zinovievist opposition, united in Leningrad under the leadership of a small group of five to seven persons constituting a kind of Leningrad center.

The members of the Leningrad center, in creating the Zinovievist organization, were in turn connected with the all-Union Zinovievist center existing in Moscow, which directed the struggle against the party to achieve the goals set by our organization.

QUESTION: Precisely what goals did your organization pursue? Tell everything you know about this?

ANSWER: Our organization, remaining generally on the old Zinovievist positions and disagreeing with the decisions of the Central Committee of the All-Union Communist Party (Bolsheviks) on a number of fundamental questions connected with international and domestic policy, pursued the goal of changing the existing party leadership and returning our organization’s leaders, ZINOVIEV and KAMENEV, to the leadership of the party.

QUESTION: What do you know about the membership of the Leningrad organization, and who personally belonged to the Leningrad center?

ANSWER: The following persons are members of our organization in Leningrad:

1) ALEKSANDROV, Alexander, aged 35, an employee of the Leather Trust in the post of commercial director;

2) KORSHUNOV, Yefim Konstantinovich, about 40, a student at the Industrial Academy;

3) KATSNELSON, Mulya, aged 34–35, an employee of the Regional Supply Department;

4) LEIKIN, Aron, aged 37, manager of Soyuzbumsnabsbyt in Leningrad;

5) TOLMAZOV, Andrei Ilyich, aged 34, assistant director of the Workers’ Supply Department at the “Krasny Putilovets” plant;

6) NALIVAIKO, Foma, aged 40, a former employee of the Fermentation Trust;

7) REM, about 45, an employee of the Political Department of the October Railway;

8) SOLOVYOV, Konstantin Stepanovich, about 40, manager of the Fittings Trust;

9) FEDOROV, Alexander, about 32, an employee of the Housing Department of the October district;

10) KHANIK, Lev Osipovich, aged 32, worked as an instructor of the Kronstadt District Committee of the All-Union Communist Party (Bolsheviks);

11) KHALUPO, Boris, aged 32, an employee of the public catering system.

I do not remember any others at present.

As for the personal membership of the Leningrad center of our organization, according to my information it includes: LEVIN, Vladimir, KOTOLYNOV, Ivan, MANDELSHTAM, Sergei, and RUMYANTSEV, Vladimir.

QUESTION: From whom precisely, and when, did you learn of the existence and activities of the Zinovievist organization?

ANSWER: Everything I know about the existence and activities of the organization I learned from TOLMAZOV, Andrei; through TOLMAZOV I personally joined the organization in 1932.

I have read the record; it has been recorded correctly from my words, in confirmation of which I sign.

<SEREDOKHIN>

INTERROGATED BY —

DEPUTY HEAD OF THE SPO OF THE UGB — STROMIN.

Certified correct: V. Stoiko
