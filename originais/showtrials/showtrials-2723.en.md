INTERROGATION RECORD

OF ANTONOV, Nikolai Vasilievich

dated 10 December 1934.

QUESTION: Did you speak with Georgy SOKOLOV at the KSI stadium in October this year?

ANSWER: I spoke with G. SOKOLOV at the stadium in October 1934.

QUESTION: What did you discuss with SOKOLOV at the KSI stadium?

ANSWER: When I met Georgy SOKOLOV, I told him that our comrades maintained contact with one another, that TOLMAZOV met RUMYANTSEV and KOTOLYNOV met ZVEZDOV; I invited SOKOLOV to call at my apartment, saying that VINOGRADOV and FADEEV would be there.

QUESTION: Do you know Leonid NIKOLAEV, a former Komsomol worker?

ANSWER: I know NIKOLAEV, whose first name I do not remember, short and dark-haired. In 1922–1923 NIKOLAEV worked as business manager of the Vyborg District Komsomol Committee.

N. ANTONOV

INTERROGATED BY:

HEAD OF THE 1ST SECTION, SPO, GUGB — PETROVSKY
