INTERROGATION RECORD

OF TOLMAZOV Andrey Ilyich, conducted by the Head of Section 4 of the SPO

KOGAN L.

dated December 12, 1934.

QUESTION: Describe the organizational forms and the organization of which you were a member.

ANSWER: The counterrevolutionary organization to which I belonged has its center in Moscow, consisting mainly of former leaders of the Trotskyist-Zinovievist bloc. According to RUMYANTSEV and KOTOLYNOV, its members include ZINOVIEV, EVDOKIMOV, BAKAEV, and GERTIK. I do not know whether KHARITONOV is a member of the center. Personally, I have never met any of the center's members.

The Leningrad group consisted mainly of RUMYANTSEV, KOTOLYNOV, ANTONOV, ZVEZDOV, FADEEV, and myself — TOLMAZOV.

The contacts and mutual support existing among us operated in such a way that we could very rarely all meet together. Contacts were therefore arranged as follows: I personally was connected with RUMYANTSEV and saw ANTONOV less frequently. RUMYANTSEV in turn met with KOTOLYNOV. KOTOLYNOV did not maintain contact with ANTONOV and ZVEZDOV. Contact with Moscow was maintained by KOTOLYNOV and RUMYANTSEV. This year EVDOKIMOV came from Moscow. He met with RUMYANTSEV and they talked. RUMYANTSEV himself told me about this. He asked me whether I had met EVDOKIMOV, and when I said I had not, he said that he had talked with him. As far as I remember, the conversation between RUMYANTSEV and me took place at the Vyborg District Soviet. I do not know the contents of EVDOKIMOV and RUMYANTSEV's conversation. RUMYANTSEV also showed me a personal letter he had received from ZINOVIEV. Apparently this letter had not arrived by post. I do not remember its contents exactly; I remember only that it concerned the organization's state of affairs. The letter began by addressing RUMYANTSEV by his first name and patronymic, “Vladimir Vasilyevich.” RUMYANTSEV also traveled to Moscow. I do not know whom he met there. I have already testified about KOTOLYNOV's trip to Moscow and his meetings there with FAIVILOVICH and TARASOV I. Personally, on one of my trips to Moscow, I also tried to meet FAIVILOVICH. I called at his home but did not find him in. This was at the beginning of 1934. One of the legal forms of our activity was the “Combatants' Fellowship,” established and operating under the Leningrad branch of Istomol. We took advantage of the fact that the principal cadres of former Leningrad Komsomol officials had belonged to the Trotskyist-Zinovievist bloc and remained in the organization until recently to establish, under Istomol, which was headed by the organization's member KHANIK Lev, a group of the counterrevolutionary organization disguised as the “Combatants' Fellowship.” RUMYANTSEV V., ANTONOV, FADEEV, SEREDOKHIN, and I — TOLMAZOV — belonged to the “Combatants' Fellowship.”

I also forgot to mention that, though I do not remember exactly, I believe it was in 1930 that SEREDOKHIN and I, while in Moscow, called on KUKLIN. We drank tea and discussed general questions. When we asked whether KUKLIN met with ZINOVIEV, he answered in the affirmative. On the same trip we met ZAKHAROV in Moscow, who, in very harsh terms and without any restraint in our presence, abused ZINOVIEV, calling him a “filthy rag.”

QUESTION: With which members of the organization was NIKOLAEV Leonid directly connected?

ANSWER: I do not personally know NIKOLAEV Leonid. I left Komsomol work in the Vyborg district in 1922. In my time NIKOLAEV did not work in the district committee. ZVEZDOV, ANTONOV, and KOTOLYNOV worked with him and know him. I do not know where or how they met. During the opposition struggle, NIKOLAEV associated with the Trotskyist-Zinovievist bloc.

QUESTION: What can you testify concerning the organization of the terrorist attack on Comrade KIROV by NIKOLAEV, a member of your organization?

ANSWER: Our organization bears full political and moral responsibility for the murder of Comrade KIROV; from its midst emerged, and on its ideological heritage was educated, Comrade KIROV's murderer, NIKOLAEV Leonid.

Recorded correctly from my words, and I sign in confirmation

A. TOLMAZOV.

INTERROGATED BY — KOGAN.
