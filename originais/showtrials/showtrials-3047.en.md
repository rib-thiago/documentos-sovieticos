INTERROGATION RECORD

conducted by the Head of the 4th Section of the SPO — KOGAN L.

15 December 1934.

TOLMAZOV, Andrei Ilyich (continued).

QUESTION: What can you testify about members of your counterrevolutionary organization MURAVYOV and MANDELSHTAM?

ANSWER: Organization member MURAVYOV Mikhail is particularly close to ANTONOV, with whom he lived in the same apartment for a time and was connected on organizational business. MANDELSHTAM Sergei is an active organization member, connected above all with RUMYANTSEV and KOTOLYNOV. Some time ago I met him and began talking with him, and he expressed the most pessimistic political sentiments. He is undoubtedly an actively disposed type, and these despondent sentiments of his were connected with his anti-party outlook and disbelief in the organization's victory and ZINOVIEV's return to the leadership.

Correctly recorded, in confirmation of which I sign.

A. TOLMAZOV.

INTERROGATED BY — KOGAN
