INTERROGATION RECORD

Of the accused KOZHURO, Anna Yevgenyevna, dated January 5, 1935.

QUESTION: The investigation has information about your membership in the underground Zinovievist organization. What can you testify about this?

ANSWER: I confirm this information. I did indeed belong to the Zinovievist organization existing in Moscow, which pursued the objectives of changing the present party leadership and returning the leaders of our organization, ZINOVIEV and KAMENEV, to leadership.

I also admit my guilt in deceiving the party and Soviet power, since the statement I submitted to the Central Control Commission about breaking with the Zinovievist opposition was merely a formal act, a maneuver, so to speak, to retain the status of a party member; in reality I remained on the Zinovievist positions of struggle against the party.

QUESTION: What do you know about illegal gatherings of members of your organization held in Moscow? Did you attend these gatherings?

ANSWER: Such gatherings took place, and I personally participated in them. They were arranged at the apartments of EVDOKIMOV, BAKAEV, GERTIK, and myself, and were always camouflaged as family parties. At these gatherings opinions were exchanged on questions of the international and domestic policy of the Central Committee of the All-Union Communist Party (Bolsheviks); when discussing the Central Committee’s decisions, members of our organization took a negative attitude toward them. We were all of the opinion that ZINOVIEV and KAMENEV should be returned to the party leadership.

QUESTION: Who exercised leadership over your organization?

ANSWER: The organization was headed by the Moscow Zinovievist center, consisting of ZINOVIEV, KAMENEV, EVDOKIMOV, BAKAEV, KUKLIN, and SHAROV.

In my subsequent testimony I intend to give a truthful and complete account of every fact known to me connected with the existence and activities of our organization.

The record has been written correctly from my words and read by me, in confirmation of which I sign.

KOZHURO.

INTERROGATED BY:

DEPUTY HEAD OF THE SPO OF THE UGB — STROMIN.

Certified correct: A. Svetlova
