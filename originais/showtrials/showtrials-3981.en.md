INTERROGATION RECORD

OF YAKOVLEV, Moisei Nakhimovich

January 2, 1935.

QUESTION: In your testimony of January 1 of this year, you state that after the Fifteenth Party Congress, although you submitted a statement renouncing the opposition, you did not overcome your old views, continued organizational meetings with former members of the United Opposition and engaged in anti-party conversations with them. Do you acknowledge that you thereby deceived the party and did not cease your counterrevolutionary activity?

ANSWER: After the Fifteenth Party Congress, I was convinced of the correctness of its decisions, understood that accusing the party of Thermidor was slander and that continuing factional struggle was counterrevolution; at the same time, not having fully overcome my old views, throughout 1928 I allowed myself a series of organizational meetings with former members of the United Opposition and conversations on counterrevolutionary subjects, thereby deceiving the party and failing to fulfill my undertaking to cease anti-Soviet activity.

QUESTION: Inform the investigation of the membership of the old opposition group of the Vasileostrovsky district, of which you were the leader.

ANSWER: The total number of United Opposition members in the Vasileostrovsky district at the time of the Fifteenth Party Congress was approximately sixty to eighty people at different factories. I was personally connected with a small group of six or seven opposition activists from different factories. The principal activists of the Vasileostrovsky district were as follows: KASPERSKY, Ivan Feliksovich; BOGOMOLNY Ya., a researcher; SKOROMNY—a worker at the Kazitsky factory; VINOGRADOV and VASILYEV—workers at the tube factory; DMITRIYEVA and DMITRIYEV—workers at the cable factory; KONDRATYEVA Evd\<okiya\>—a worker at the Uritsky fac\<tory\>; FOKIN A.—a worker at the Elektroapparat fac\<tory\>; SEMYONOV S.—a worker at the Comintern radio factory; TARAN P.—a former foreman at the Baltic Works, now deceased.

QUESTION: The investigation has information that your meeting at SHEINBERG's funeral \[1\] in the autumn of 1934 with members of the old opposition group of the Vasileostrovsky dist\<rict\> was organizational in nature. Do you acknowledge this?

ANSWER: Categorically not. However, although the presence of a number of former oppositionists at the memorial gathering for SHEINBERG (myself—YAKOVLEV, KASPERSKY, SKOROMNY, SEMYONOV, KONDRATYEVA and FOKIN \[2\]) was accidental, it nevertheless objectively constitutes an anti-party fact, since it facilitated SKOROMNY's anti-Soviet intervention.

M. YAKOVLEV.

INTERROGATED BY:

OPERATIONS OFFICER OF THE SPO OF THE GUGB OF THE NKVD—FYODOROV.

Certified correct: Kazakova

RGASPI, F. 671, Op. 1, D. 126, fols. 183–184.

\[1\] Here and subsequently in the text, erroneously “Shemberg.”

\[2\] Erroneously “Kondratyev and Fokina” in the text.
