SUPPLEMENTARY INTERROGATION RECORD

OF LEVIN Vladimir Solomonovich

15 December 1934.

QUESTION: Which Zinovievists did you meet and under what circumstances?

ANSWER: I met SOSITSKY Lev rarely; on the eve of the seventeenth anniversary of the October Revolution he invited me to visit, but I did not manage to go. I know KOTOLYNOV but have not met him since 1931.

QUESTION: Under what circumstances did you meet ZILBERMAN Aleksandr and GAIDEROVA Zinaida?

ANSWER: I met ZILBERMAN Aleksandr in July or August 1934 during his business trip to Leningrad. He called at my dacha in Sestroretsk. He told me that he had been arrested by the GPU in Moscow; I do not remember in connection with what case. GAIDEROVA Zinaida telephoned me two and a half months ago, asking for advice on housing; I visited her apartment and during the conversation she asked me to help with her housing problem. GAIDEROVA was arrested by the GPU in 1933. She told me she had been arrested for contact with a counterrevolutionary group.

V. LEVIN.

INTERROGATED BY

HEAD OF THE 1ST SECTION OF THE SPO, GUGB — PETROVSKY.
