INTERROGATION RECORD

OF NAUMOV, Ivan Kupriyanovich —

dated January 14, 1935.

In response to the investigation's question about my participation in the counterrevolutionary Zinovievist underground, I testify:

after my reinstatement in the Party following the Fifteenth Congress of the All-Union Communist Party (Bolsheviks), my opposition past weighed upon me for several years: in 1929 it led me into the counterrevolutionary Zinovievist underground; already at the celebration of the New Year of 1929, known to the investigation, ZINOVIEV saw in me an associate who, after a certain zigzag, had nevertheless returned to him.

This return of mine to ZINOVIEV explains my considerable knowledge of the “secrets” of the Zinovievist underground, about which I gave a series of statements.

During 1929 I repeatedly met ZINOVIEV, KAMENEV, EVDOKIMOV, FEDOROV, KUKLIN, and a number of other leading figures of the Zinovievist underground.

All the Zinovievists placed their hopes on growing difficulties in the country and the Party — they had in mind disagreements within the Party — believing that these would bring them to the leadership of the country and the Party.

Furthermore, the entire underground was united by hostility and hatred toward the leading section of the Party, especially against STALIN.

I stopped seeing ZINOVIEV and KAMENEV after the publication, as I recall, of material about attempts at a bloc between the Rights and the Zinovievists, that is, from 1929-1930 onward (the letter about BUKHARIN's negotiations with KAMENEV).

I continued to maintain political ties with BAKAEV, KUKLIN, EVDOKIMOV, GERTIK, GORSHENIN, BRAVO, and a number of other persons.

Correctly recorded from my words; I have read the record — NAUMOV.

INTERROGATED BY:

ASSISTANT HEAD OF THE EKO OF THE GUGB OF THE NKVD OF THE USSR — DMITRIEV.
