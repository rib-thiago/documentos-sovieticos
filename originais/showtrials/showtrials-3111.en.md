INTERROGATION RECORD

OF NIKOLAEV, Leonid Vasilyevich

16 December 1934.

QUESTION: Since when have you known SOKOLOV Georgy?

ANSWER: I have known SOKOLOV Georgy since 1923, when I worked in the District Komsomol Committee and he worked in physical culture at enterprises in the Vyborg District. Since then I have maintained friendly relations with him. Until 1933 we lived near each other. At the end of 1933 I had a conversation with SOKOLOV and ANTONOV in which I established that both held openly anti-Soviet views on food supply difficulties and other questions of party policy. ANTONOV gave me his address and asked me to visit his apartment (Kondratyevsky Avenue) to continue the conversations. I did not go because I was convinced that he and SOKOLOV were working against the party, while my anti-party sentiments, though already present, were not yet strong enough for me to begin anti-party work. At this meeting ANTONOV and SOKOLOV asked me to help them obtain food for the closed workers' cooperative of the institute where they studied. I helped them and accompanied them to the board of the Smolninsky District consumers' society. I was able to assist because at that time I was an instructor in the trade group of the Regional Control Commission and Workers' and Peasants' Inspectorate.

During 1934 I had only three meetings with SOKOLOV.

The first meeting was in April on Lesnoy Avenue, outside the building where SOKOLOV lives. I had no conversations with him about anti-party work; the meeting was entirely accidental;

the second was in September, before the conversation with KOTOLYNOV about which I testified, in the vicinity of the Finland Station. Trusting me fully, SOKOLOV said that he belonged to the Zinovievist organization and was a member of the terrorist group headed by KOTOLYNOV;

the third was in November. On 4 November, as I testified, I saw KOTOLYNOV and asked him to assign me an assistant for watching KIROV. KOTOLYNOV said he was assigning SOKOLOV, who would be at Smolny at four in the afternoon on 6 November, waiting for me on the first floor by the exit from the main entrance.

Indeed, I met SOKOLOV at the agreed time and place. We went around every floor of Smolny, hoping to meet KIROV to carry out the terrorist act; I was to fire the shot. First SOKOLOV went around the building and did not encounter KIROV; then I went from the other side and also failed to encounter him.

We consulted each other. Both simultaneously thought of getting into the Mariinsky Theater, where a ceremonial meeting marking the October anniversary was to take place that day. We reckoned KIROV would attend. We rushed to obtain tickets, but it was already late and the employees had left.

Seeing the failure, I dismissed SOKOLOV and decided to keep watch on my own and encounter KIROV as he approached the Mariinsky Theater. I did see him, but he was far away; nothing came of this attempted attack either. After this I did not see SOKOLOV again and decided I could manage without him.

Correctly recorded from my words. I have read the record.

L. NIKOLAEV.

INTERROGATED BY:

HEAD OF THE EKO, GUGB, NKVD OF THE USSR — MIRONOV

ASSISTANT TO THE HEAD OF THE EKO, GUGB, NKVD OF THE USSR — DMITRIEV
