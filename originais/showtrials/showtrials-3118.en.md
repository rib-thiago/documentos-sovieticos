INTERROGATION RECORD

OF MYASNIKOV, Nikolai Petrovich

dated December 16, 1934.

QUESTION: Do you know any employees of the NKVD organs who belong to the counterrevolutionary Zinovievist organization or are connected with it?

ANSWER: ISAEV Gennady worked at the NKVD “Red Star” cooperative. He was a former Trotskyist who was expelled from the Party in either 1929 or 1930 for sending letters that gave misleading guidance on questions of the class struggle in the countryside and expressed dissatisfaction with Party policy.

He wrote the letters using carbon paper, in nine copies, and sent them to different addresses. I know this from LEVIN V. ISAEV was connected with LEVIN V. up to and including 1933; then LEVIN told me that ISAEV was beginning to withdraw from counterrevolutionary work and that we had to be more cautious with him.

ISAEV was head of the cooperative's organizational department.

MYASNIKOV.

INTERROGATED BY —

DEPUTY HEAD OF THE SPO OF THE GUGB — LYUSHKOV
