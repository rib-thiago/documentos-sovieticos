RECORD

of the supplementary interrogation of GORSHENIN, I. S.,

dated December 19, 1934.

Question: What do you know about the existence and activities of a Zinovievist organization in Leningrad and its connection with the Moscow Zinovievist Center?

Answer: From G. E. ZINOVIEV I know that a secret Zinovievist organization did indeed exist in Leningrad, headed by Vladimir RUMYANTSEV and Vladimir LEVIN.

ZINOVIEV described this organization as a youth organization with a militant disposition. As for RUMYANTSEV, who maintained a personal connection with ZINOVIEV, it was said that he was irreconcilably disposed toward the Party leadership, particularly against comrade STALIN. This conversation between ZINOVIEV and myself took place at the end of 1928. I was not spoken to on this subject again, since I personally was not from Leningrad and did not know the situation there.

Concerning the connection between our Moscow Zinovievist Center and the Leningrad organization in recent times, I likewise know nothing except for one journey by KUKLIN and SHAROV at the end of 1928, when they went to Leningrad with direct assignments from ZINOVIEV for negotiations with the leaders of the Leningrad organization, particularly Vladimir RUMYANTSEV. Later EVDOKIMOV (1934), GESSEN (1933) and GERTIK (1934) also traveled from Moscow to Leningrad. What assignments they carried out for the organization, or whether any assignments had been given them, I personally do not know.

Question: How do you assess the terrorist act committed against comrade KIROV, and who is responsible for this crime?

Answer: Immediate responsibility rests on the members and, chiefly, the leaders of the Leningrad Zinovievist organization. As for the organization's Moscow Center, since one of its principal methods of struggle against the Party was discrediting decisions of the VKP(b) Central Committee and cultivating feelings of resentment and hatred toward the Party leadership among Zinovievists, the entire political and moral responsibility for the terrorist act unquestionably falls on ZINOVIEV and KAMENEV, who headed the Moscow Center.

I acknowledge that the Moscow Zinovievist Center, continuing its struggle against the Party and Party leadership and spreading a hostile attitude toward members of the Central Committee Politburo among its like-minded associates, created conditions for individual terrorist actions, thus descending toward fascist methods of struggle. One manifestation of this struggle was the murder of comrade KIROV.

As for myself personally, being a person closely connected with members of the Moscow Zinovievist Center and informed about most matters connected with its anti-Party activities, I acknowledge my enormous guilt before the Party and Soviet power, and deeply repent of it.

I ask to be given the opportunity to atone for this guilt through persistent labor in any field of socialist construction and under any conditions.

GORSHENIN

Interrogated by: DEPUTY HEAD OF THE SPO OF THE UGB — STROMIN.

RGASPI, F. 671, Op. 1, D. 120, sheets 174–175.
