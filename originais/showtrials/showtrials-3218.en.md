CONFRONTATION RECORD

between the accused MYASNIKOV, Nikolai Petrovich, and SOSITSKY, Lev Ilyich.

Dated December 23, 1934.

After mutually confirming that they knew one another personally, the accused MYASNIKOV and SOSITSKY testified:

QUESTION TO MYASNIKOV: What do you know about SOSITSKY Lev's membership in the organization of former participants in the Zinovievist-Trotskyist bloc and his role in it?

ANSWER: I confirm my previous testimony to the investigation that I know SOSITSKY as a member of the center of the counterrevolutionary organization of former participants in the Zinovievist-Trotskyist bloc. Until 1931, I had direct dealings with him through work in the organization's center. After that time, I knew that he remained a member of the organization and was directly connected with LEVIN V. through the organization's center.

QUESTION TO SOSITSKY: Do you confirm this?

ANSWER: Yes, I confirm that I did indeed belong to the center of the Zinovievist organization and was connected with LEVIN V. on the center's business.

QUESTION TO SOSITSKY: What do you know about MYASNIKOV's membership and role in the Zinovievist organization?

ANSWER: I personally knew that MYASNIKOV, as a member of the Zinovievist organization, belonged to the organization's center until recently and was connected with LEVIN.

MYASNIKOV.                                    SOSITSKY.

Confrontation conducted by:

DEPUTY HEAD OF THE SPO OF THE GUGB OF THE NKVD OF THE USSR: (LYUSHKOV).

Certified true: M. Glazkov
