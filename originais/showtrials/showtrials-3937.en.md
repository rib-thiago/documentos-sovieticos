INTERROGATION RECORD

OF FAIVILOVICH Leonid Yakovlevich —

31 December 1934.

QUESTION: What do you know about CHERNY Grigory's membership in the counterrevolutionary Zinovievist organization?

ANSWER: I know that during the open struggle against the party G. CHERNY conducted very active work. It seemed to me that he returned to the party sincerely. After 1928 I met him in Central Asia and several times in Moscow while he worked at Tsentrosoyuz. In Moscow CHERNY met PERIMOV. Whether he saw any other Zinovievists I do not know.

I last saw CHERNY at the end of 1933, when he arrived from the Far Eastern Territory and called on me. At this meeting he told me he had been arrested in Khabarovsk on charges of creating an anti-party group. He said he had conducted no anti-party work and was rehabilitated following the investigation. —

L. FAIVILOVICH.

INTERROGATED BY: KORKIN.
