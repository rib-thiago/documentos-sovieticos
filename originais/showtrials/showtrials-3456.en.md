INTERROGATION RECORD

Conducted by the head of the 4th Section of the SPO, KOGAN L., December 26, 1934.

GERTSBERG Aleksandr Vladimirovich.

QUESTION: The investigation has information that until recently you belonged to the counterrevolutionary Zinovievist organization and were connected in Moscow with persons who headed it. What can you testify on this matter?

ANSWER: Returning to Moscow in late 1931 after four years' absence from the USSR, during which I was abroad, I reestablished my connections with a number of Zinovievists whose surnames I have already named. In the course of meeting these people, I learned that the Zinovievists, despite formally renouncing their views, had not merged with the Party but continued to cherish old thoughts and sentiments. I do not know the positive program of this counterrevolutionary organization, but the main thing uniting everyone was hatred of the Party leadership and especially STALIN. To settle accounts, when a convenient opportunity arose, with those whose fault it was that the Zinovievists were not in the leadership, some said, while others sympathized with them. In one form or another, I heard such conversations from GERTIK, ZALUTSKY, and KUKLIN. I shall give additional specific testimony about my personal meetings with them.

My grave crime against the Party and working class consists in the fact that after a complete ideological break I did not break sharply and hostilely, organizationally as well, with the counterrevolutionary Zinovievists, continuing to meet and converse with them and therefore to remain connected with them.

For this reason, together with all these counterrevolutionaries, I bear full political responsibility for the murder of Comrade KIROV.

Written in my own hand.

A. GERTSBERG.

Interrogated by the head of the 4th Section of the SPO — KOGAN L.
