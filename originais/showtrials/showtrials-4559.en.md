INTERROGATION RECORD

Of POLUYAN, Dmitry Vasilyevich (supplementary).

January 23, 1935.

During my visits to SMILGA's apartment in 1930–1932, I repeatedly met there former active participants in the Zinovievist-Trotskyist bloc: ALSKY, BAYAN, Roman (I do not remember his surname), Vyacheslav or Vecheslov, and another one or two whose surnames I do not now remember.

At a number of meetings, SMILGA, ALSKY, and other oppositionists, in varying combinations, held conversations on questions of the country's economic and political life which were, in essence, anti-Party in character. Vyacheslav-Vecheslov had the most anti-Party sentiments.

Of the questions discussed at these meetings, I now remember sharply anti-Party statements on collectivization. The conclusion drawn was that the forcible implementation of collectivization had a mass character and that excessive rates of collectivization had been adopted. To substantiate this proposition, individual shortcomings were put forward and generalized.

Statements on questions of industrialization also had the character of sharp anti-Party criticism. The rates of industrialization were likewise sharply condemned. To substantiate this, it was argued that the adopted rates undermined the country's economic resources. These statements led to the conclusion that the leadership of the All-Union Communist Party (Bolsheviks) was incapable.

SMILGA and the former participants in the Zinovievist-Trotskyist bloc gathering around him constituted a concealed anti-Party group.

My participation in this group consisted in my covering up its activity by not reporting it to the Party.

Taken down correctly from my words. The record has been read by me. —

D. POLUYAN.

INTERROGATED BY:

ASSISTANT HEAD OF THE EKO OF THE GUGB OF THE USSR NKVD — DMITRIEV.

SECTION HEAD OF THE EKO OF THE GUGB OF THE USSR NKVD — CHERTOK.
