INTERROGATION RECORD

Of SHAROV, YAKOV FEDOROVICH, dated January 5, 1935.

Question: Do you admit your guilt in belonging to the anti-party Zinovievist organization?

Answer: Yes, I admit my guilt in having belonged to the anti-party Zinovievist group, consisting of participants in the former Zinovievist-Trotskyist organization. This anti-party group included ZINOVIEV, KAMENEV, EVDOKIMOV, BAKAEV, KUKLIN, GERTIK, GORSHENIN, KHARITONOV, FEDOROV, ZALUTSKY, and myself.

The impetus for organizing this anti-party group was a meeting of several participants in the Zinovievist-Trotskyist organization held shortly after the 15th Congress at ZINOVIEV’s apartment. At this meeting were ZINOVIEV, KAMENEV, EVDOKIMOV, BAKAEV, KUKLIN, GERTIK, KHARITONOV, FEDOROV, ZALUTSKY, and myself. I do not now remember who else was there. ZINOVIEV and KAMENEV gave reports at this meeting. The essence of their reports was that serious political and economic difficulties were inevitable, that the party leadership would be unable to cope with these difficulties, that in connection with this the gravest complications were likely within the party itself, and that we needed to preserve our opposition organization so that we could prevent the party’s turn to the Right, which the party leadership would attempt as a way out of the difficulties.

It was decided not to dissolve the organization and that its main participants should maintain firm contacts with one another.

For the sake of secrecy, it was decided to hold no more meetings but to meet under the guise of various “tea parties,” family celebrations, and so forth. It was also decided to agitate for all oppositionists who had not yet joined the party to join it, so as to preserve our opposition cadres.

The Zinovievist organization, including a number of its members — ZINOVIEV, KAMENEV, EVDOKIMOV, BAKAEV, KUKLIN, GERTIK, KHARITONOV, FEDOROV, ZALUTSKY, and myself — entered the party as double-dealers, with double-dealing tasks. Our entire anti-party Zinovievist group was bound together by our struggle against the party in the ranks of the counterrevolutionary Zinovievist-Trotskyist organization; by the fact that we entered the party with double-dealing objectives; by the fact that we shared ZINOVIEV’s and KAMENEV’s anti-party positions; and by the fact that we separated ourselves from the whole party, existing as a distinct anti-party group whose members trusted one another completely and knew that none would expose another before the party.

Recorded correctly from my words and read by me. —

SHAROV.

INTERROGATED BY: HEAD OF A SECTION OF THE EKO OF THE GUGB OF THE NKVD OF THE USSR — CHERTOK.

Certified correct: Churbanova
