INTERROGATION RECORD

OF LEVIN Vladimir Solomonovich (supplementary)

21 December 1934.

QUESTION: Describe in greater detail the character of the gathering at SOSITSKY's apartment.

ANSWER: After SHURYGIN's funeral, in February or March 1933, the following gathered at SOSITSKY's apartment: myself, LEVIN, MANDELSHTAM Sergei, SOSITSKY, and RUMYANTSEV. During the conversation the question arose of how we should formulate our attitude toward ZINOVIEV and KAMENEV in connection with their expulsion from the party. Both SOSITSKY and I stated that ZINOVIEV's and KAMENEV's expulsion from the party should be regarded as a continuation of the party leadership's line aimed at cutting off the “leaders,” at any cost and by all means. We stated that the task remained to continue working to bring ZINOVIEV and KAMENEV into the leadership.

Recorded correctly from my words.

LEVIN.

INTERROGATED BY:

HEAD OF THE 1ST SECTION OF THE SPO OF THE GUGB — PETROVSKY

HEAD OF THE 1ST SECTION OF THE SPO OF THE UGB — LULOV.
