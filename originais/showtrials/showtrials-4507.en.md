INTERROGATION RECORD

OF KORSHUNOV Efim Konstantinovich

dated January 17, 1935.

QUESTION: What additional information can you give the investigation about your activities in the Zinovievist organization?

ANSWER: Fully confirming my testimony of December 17 and December 26, 1934, I add the following:

gatherings of participants in the Zinovievist organization, in which I too participated, took place regularly at LEVIN's apartment up to the arrest. As I have already testified, the participants in the organization usually present were LEVIN Vladimir, MYASNIKOV, SOSITSKY, BASHKIROV, DMITRIEV Timofei, and ZILBERMAN. The last two attended less frequently.

At all these gatherings we discussed the principal economic and political questions and sharply criticized them. The principal conclusion drawn was that the plan of industrialization and collectivization was failing and that the material position of the working class was increasingly deteriorating. The failure of the policy of industrialization and collectivization was explained by the inept and incorrect policy of the leadership of the All-Union Communist Party (Bolsheviks), and STALIN in particular. LEVIN made the most malicious attacks against the leadership of the All-Union Communist Party (Bolsheviks), and STALIN in particular.

In 1932 LEVIN traveled to Moscow, where he saw ZINOVIEV. I do not now remember the substance of the information LEVIN gave me about their conversation. I remember only that LEVIN told me that ZINOVIEV had suggested that he not visit him (ZINOVIEV) anymore, since this might arouse suspicions and cause trouble.

After visiting ZINOVIEV, LEVIN's attitude toward the Rights changed completely. Whereas LEVIN had previously stressed on various occasions his disagreement with the Rights' line, after the trip to ZINOVIEV he began saying that RYKOV, BUKHARIN, and TOMSKY were good Bolsheviks and that efforts should be made to have them, as well as ZINOVIEV and KAMENEV, brought into the Politburo of the Central Committee of the All-Union Communist Party (Bolsheviks).

Correctly recorded from my words and read by me.

KORSHUNOV.

INTERROGATED BY:

SECTION HEAD OF THE EKO OF THE GUGB OF THE NKVD OF THE USSR (CHERTOK)

Certified correct: A. Svetlova
