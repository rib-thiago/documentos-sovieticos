INTERROGATION RECORD

OF TSATUROVA-BOLSHAKOVA Vera Nikolayevna

31 December 1934.

Question: When did your husband TSATUROV return from Moscow?

Answer: About three or four months ago.

Question: Do you know that upon arriving from Moscow TSATUROV visited Smolny, where his employment was being arranged?

Answer: Yes. From his words I know he tried to obtain an audience with Comrade KIROV, wishing to discuss his affairs personally. Comrade KIROV did not receive him and, through his secretary Comrade SVESHNIKOV, directed him to Comrade KASPAROV. On the latter's instructions, Smolny official Comrade TSYGANOV provided TSATUROV with work.

Question: How did TSATUROV react to Comrade KIROV's not receiving him?

Answer: He was very dissatisfied but did not display this outwardly.

Question: Did TSATUROV try to speak with Comrade KIROV after taking employment?

Answer: No.

Question: Which other Smolny officials did TSATUROV meet during this period?

Answer: He met KODATSKY at Smolny, KASPAROV and TSYGANOV. There he also encountered Comrade ALEKSEEV, secretary of the Kirov District Party Committee.

Question: Which Smolny officials visited your home?

Answer: None visited us.

Question: Why did you and your husband TSATUROV receive news of Comrade KIROV's murder with malicious pleasure?

Answer: That is untrue. We were very saddened.

Question: Perhaps this reflected former Zinovievists' common hatred of Comrade KIROV, who smashed the remnants of counterrevolutionary Zinovievist groups in Leningrad?

Answer: No, that is not so.

Question: Do you assert that your husband TSATUROV conducted no counterrevolutionary work and was connected with no former Zinovievists?

Answer: I cannot assert this, but I know no such facts.

Question: Do you know that TSATUROV met TOLMAZOV, and where exactly?

Answer: I do not know.

Question: Has TOLMAZOV visited your apartment recently?

Answer: Yes, he visited us about three months ago. —

V. TSATUROVA.

INTERROGATED BY

OPERATIONS OFFICER OF THE SPO — TSAMAEV.

Certified correct: Churbanova
