INTERROGATION RECORD

Of SAFAROV, G. I.,

dated January 6, 1935.

In addition to my testimony to the investigation, I testify:

YANIN, former director of “Treugolnik,” was a participant in the counterrevolutionary Trotskyist-Zinovievist bloc but skillfully concealed this from the party while constantly rendering services to Zinovievists. He was close to the former organizer of the collective at the former Putilov plant, Alexander ALEKSANDROV, both through their shared inclination toward drunkenness and dubious romantic adventures and because he particularly trusted ALEKSANDROV, confiding his anti-party attitudes to him. At one time ALEKSANDROV, through his patronage, was appointed to leading work at “Treugolnik,” apparently in 1930–1931. I met YANIN once on a train bound for Leningrad, in 1931 as I recall. He took a keen interest in the “fate” and situation of Zinovievists who had previously worked in Leningrad and gave every indication that he “remained the same” and “sympathized.” The YANIN–ALEKSANDROV circle was undoubtedly one of the channels for spreading counterrevolutionary gossip and “information,” especially among those who had participated in the struggle against the party in the former Narva district.

Written in my own hand — G. SAFAROV.

INTERROGATED BY:

HEAD OF THE 1ST SECTION OF THE SPO OF THE GUGB OF THE NKVD OF THE USSR — PETROVSKY.

Certified correct: A. Svetlova
