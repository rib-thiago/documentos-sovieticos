INTERROGATION RECORD

OF BURMISTROV, Pavel Petrovich

January 2, 1935.

QUESTION: Tell us what you know about PADVA M.B.

ANSWER: LUKIN V. or SOBOL introduced me to PADVA in 1929 or 1930 at a meeting at Istomol. One of them told me that PADVA had belonged to the opposition, but I do not clearly remember which opposition PADVA belonged to. But it is certain that he belonged to the opposition.

I met PADVA two or three times; I called on him at the “Music Hall” and always received complimentary tickets from him.

At Istomol, I met PADVA in the company of LUKIN and SOBOL.

In my view, he was particularly well connected with LUKIN, since I met the latter at the “Music Hall” with PADVA.

In a conversation about the history of the Leningrad Komsomol, PADVA said that he was not a native Leningrader and did not know the history of the Komsomol.

I have read the testimony. Recorded correctly from my words—

BURMISTROV.

INTERROGATED BY:

OPERATIONS OFFICER OF THE SPO OF THE GUGB OF THE NKVD—FYODOROV.

CERTIFIED CORRECT: Kazakova

RGASPI, F. 671, Op. 1, D. 126, fol. 261.
