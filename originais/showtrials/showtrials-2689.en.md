INTERROGATION RECORD

OF I. I. KOTOLYNOV, dated 7 December 1934.

QUESTION: Do you know Leonid NIKOLAEV, a former Komsomol worker in Vyborg District?

ANSWER: I recall that during my Komsomol work in Leningrad in 1921–1924 I encountered NIKOLAEV through work; I do not remember his first name or patronymic. NIKOLAEV is short, with large drooping lips and dark hair. NIKOLAEV gave the impression of a sullen, rude person. During conversation he interjected sarcastic remarks and inappropriate jokes. I remember clearly that NIKOLAEV held no leading position; I do not remember where he worked.

I. KOTOLYNOV

QUESTION: Did you meet NIKOLAEV; when and where?

ANSWER: I briefly met NIKOLAEV in the summer of 1932 or summer of 1933, I do not remember exactly, on the premises of the Military Medical Academy (Canteen No. 1 of the Vyborg District Committee).

I. KOTOLYNOV

INTERROGATED BY: HEAD OF THE 1ST SECTION, SPO, GUGB — V. PETROVSKY
