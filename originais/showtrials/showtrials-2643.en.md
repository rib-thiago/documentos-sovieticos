FROM THE PROCURACY OF THE UNION OF SOVIET SOCIALIST REPUBLICS

During the investigation of the case of Bakaev I. P., Gertik\<a\> A. M., Kuklin A. S., and others held accountable in connection with the discovery in the city of Leningrad of an underground counterrevolutionary group that had prepared and carried out the assassination of Comrade S. M. Kirov, new information was obtained concerning the underground counterrevolutionary activities of Zinoviev G. E., Evdokimov G. E., Kamenev L. B., and Fedorov G. F., whose cases had initially been referred for consideration by the NKVD Special Conference.

In view of these findings and, in particular, the testimony of Bakaev I. P., exposing the participation of Zinoviev G. E., Evdokimov G. E., Kamenev L. B., and Fedorov G. F. in the underground counterrevolutionary “Moscow Center,” and of Safarov G. I., who supplied the investigation with a number of facts concerning the underground counterrevolutionary activities of the aforementioned persons up until recently, the case against Zinoviev G. E., Evdokimov G. E., Kamenev L. B., and Fedorov G. F. has been referred for consideration by the Military Collegium of the Supreme Court of the Union of Soviet Socialist Republics.

Alongside the aforementioned persons, the following have also been brought to trial before the Military Collegium of the Supreme Court of the USSR: Bakaev I. P., Gertik A. M., Kuklin A. S., Gorshenin I. S., Tarasov I. I., Tsarkov N. A., Bravo B. L., Sharov Ia. V., Faivilovich L. Ia., Gertsberg A. V., Gessen S. M., Sakhov B. N., Bashkirov A. F., Perimov A. V., and Anishev A. I.

The trial in this case began on January 15 of the current year at the Supreme Court in Leningrad. Composition of the Military Collegium of the Supreme Court of the USSR: presiding judge, Comrade Ulrikh V. V.; members of the Collegium, Comrades Matulevich I. O. and Goriachev A. D.

Reproduced from the publication in the newspaper “Izvestia” of January 16, 1935.
