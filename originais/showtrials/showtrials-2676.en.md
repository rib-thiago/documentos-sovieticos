INTERROGATION RECORD

OF NIKOLAEV L. V., dated 5/XII-1934.

Question: What conversations did you have with the form\<er\> oppositionist SHATSKY, apart from the one mentioned in the interrogation record of 4/XII?

Answer: At the meeting about which I testified, SHATSKY, as I recall, after CHUDOV’s car had driven away, turned to a more serious subject: displaying terrorist sentiments, he bitterly condemned STALIN’s statement at the 17th Congress of the AUCP(b) on the need to remove oppositionists from their posts. SHATSKY added that he was registered with the OGPU and had recently been dismissed from his job. –

L. NIKOLAEV.

INTERROGATED BY:

DEPUTY PEOPLE’S COMMISSAR FOR INTERNAL AFFAIRS, IA. AGRANOV

ASSISTANT HEAD OF THE ECONOMIC DEPARTMENT, MAIN DIRECTORATE OF STATE SECURITY (EKO GUGB), DMITRIEV
