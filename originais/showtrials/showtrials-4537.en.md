INTERROGATION RECORD

of the accused GORSHENIN, I. S.

January 20, 1935.

In answer to the investigation's question as to whom I know among the persons connected with the counterrevolutionary “Moscow Center” of the Zinovievist organization, I report that the following persons are known to me:

LUKYANOV, Ilya — an employee of the Tsentrosoyuz system (Plodoovoshch), an active Zinovievist, a member of the organization. He was connected with me and, at gatherings held in my apartment, met BAKAEV, GERTIK, and others. He holds anti-party sentiments and shared the organization's directives.

Lives in Moscow in the area of the Smolensk market.

FURTICHEV, Yakov Abramovich — a graduate of the Institute of Red Professors, an active member of the Zinovievist organization. Until the last period he was connected with me and, until 1932, with ZINOVIEV.

Lives in Gorky, where he teaches dialectical materialism at local higher educational institutions.

GORDON, Nikolai — an active Zinovievist. He was connected with GERTIK, KUKLIN, and GORSHENIN and, until 1933, always visited ZINOVIEV personally. Lives in Rostov-on-Don, where he is connected with other members of the Zinovievist organization, ROTSKAN and Timofei DMITRIEV.

VAKHOSHIN, Ivan Mikhailovich — an active Zinovievist. Until the last period he met me both at my apartment and at my place of work in Gosplan. He holds anti-party sentiments. During the 1933 purge he was expelled from the party and has not been reinstated.

Lives in Moscow near Kursk Station, I believe on 2nd Syromyatnicheskaya Street.

KAPITONOV, Ivan Petrovich — a graduate of the Institute of Red Professors, an active member of our organization. While in Moscow he informed KAMENEV and ZINOVIEV on questions of AUCP(b) economic policy, using his official position in USSR Gosplan, where he headed the sector for the location of productive forces. He is actively hostile to the party leadership. He was connected with the organization until the last period.

Lives in Novosibirsk, where he holds the post of deputy chairman of the regional planning body.

GORODSKOI, Pyotr — an active Zinovievist. He remained on anti-party Zinovievist positions until the last period. He was connected with KAPITONOV and myself.

Lives in Moscow. Works as an engineer-instructor at the Military Engineering Academy.

KAPITONOVA, Antonina Petrovna — an active participant in the Zinovievist organization. She remained on her old anti-party positions until the last period. She was connected with KAPITONOV and GORSHENIN. She is GORODSKOI's wife and KAPITONOV's sister.

Lives in Moscow in the Sokolniki area.

SELIVANOV — head of the teaching section of the Military Engineering Academy in Moscow. An active Zinovievist. He remained on his old anti-party positions. He was connected with KAPITONOV, GORODSKOI, and partly with me.

Lives in Moscow at the corner of Sivtsev Vrazhek and Bolshoi Vlasyevsky Lane.

VUJOVIC — an active Zinovievist; he remained on his old positions until the last period, was connected with BAKAEV and GORSHENIN, and informed them about the situation in the Comintern. He participated in illegal gatherings at GORSHENIN's and BAKAEV's apartments. He frequently met NAUMOV and ZORIN.

Lives in Moscow on Taganskaya Street.

UNSHLIKHT, Zosya — she remained on her old anti-party Zinovievist positions. She is closely connected with Comintern officials and well informed about Comintern affairs, especially Polish matters.

Until the last period she was closely connected with R. L. BUDZINSKAYA and participated in gatherings held at her home.

Until 1932 she visited me and participated in illegal gatherings at my apartment.

LELEVICH — a writer, he remained on anti-party positions. I met him only twice, at anti-party gatherings at BUDZINSKAYA's apartment. At one of these gatherings in 1933, GESSEN, in LELEVICH's presence, recited his anti-Soviet poem (the so-called “Penitential”), over which LELEVICH was in indescribable rapture and made sharp remarks directed against the party leadership and, in particular, Comrade STALIN.

SOLOVYOV, Pyotr Petrovich — a graduate of the Institute of Red Professors, he remained on his old Zinovievist positions, was an active member of the Zinovievist organization until 1922 (until moving from Rostov to Moscow), and was connected with GORSHENIN and ZINOVIEV. Lives in Moscow. Works in the food industry as deputy head of the personnel training department.

MALYSHEV, Andrei — a graduate of the Institute of Red Professors, an active Zinovievist. While in Moscow he was connected with SOLOVYOV. In Leningrad he was connected with TSVIBAK, KAREV, and KARTASHEV. Lives in Leningrad, where he teaches Russian history at higher educational institutions.

GRUDSKAYA, Anna — a graduate of the Institute of Red Professors. A Zinovievist. Wife of KAREV, Nikolai Afanasyevich. Until 1932 she was connected with KAPITONOV. Lives in Moscow. Works in literature and publishing.

LADOKHA, Grigori — a graduate of the Institute of Red Professors. Before the Fifteenth Congress he belonged to the Zinovievist group and remained on his old anti-party positions until the last period.

He submitted a statement “recognizing” the general line only in 1931 and was reinstated in the party in 1932. After this he visited my apartment, engaged in anti-party conversations, expressed dissatisfaction with our organization's methods of struggle, and declared himself a supporter of more active measures. At the same time (the end of 1932) he told me that he had kept a suitcase containing printing type, which he intended to use to print leaflets against the party leadership. Shortly afterward, at the beginning of

1933, he was assigned to Novozýbkov, where he teaches at a technical school to this day. He had previously worked in the USSR People's Commissariat of Agriculture and in teaching in Ivanovo-Voznesensk.

Furthermore, I know persons who were not members of our Zinovievist organization and had not even been Zinovievists in the past, but who were regularly visited by organization member Yakov Rafailovich ELKOVICH during his trips to Moscow, from whom he received information and communicated it to members of the “Moscow Center.”

ZHARIKOV, Vasili — academic secretary to Comrade SHVERNIK. A graduate of the Institute of Red Professors. He did not belong to the Zinovievist opposition, but somewhere in Sverdlovsk was called to account for some anti-party statement; he was connected with ELKOVICH, whom he informed about various decisions of the Central Committee Politburo and personal relations among leading officials. In 1933 he gave ELKOVICH and GORSHENIN the secret stenographic record of the Thirteenth Plenum of the Executive Committee of the Communist International to read.

GONIKMAN, Solomon — an employee of the Main Directorate of the Civil Air Fleet. He was connected with ELKOVICH, who received political information on various questions from GONIKMAN. GONIKMAN apparently adhered to the Sten–Lominadze group.

SONKIN — deputy head of the Agricultural Department of the AUCP(b) Central Committee. He was connected with ELKOVICH, who received information on various political questions from him. He apparently did not belong to the opposition.

TUMARKIN — editor of the newspaper “Trud.” He was connected with ELKOVICH, who received various information on political questions from him.

KANATCHIKOV, Semyon Ivanovich — an active Zinovievist. Until 1932, up to GERTIK's last exile, KANATCHIKOV, Semyon Ivanovich, maintained contact with members of the “Moscow Center,” predominantly GERTIK, at whose home he met other members of the Center and activists of the counterrevolutionary Zinovievist group. Lives in Moscow.

CHERNY, Grigori — an active Zinovievist. Until the last period he maintained contact with the counterrevolutionary Zinovievist organization through PERIMOV and GAUSMAN. His place of residence is unknown to me. It can be learned from GAUSMAN and PERIMOV.

SHEPSHELEVA, Maria — an active Zinovievist. A member of the counterrevolutionary Zinovievist organization until the last period. She participated in anti-party gatherings of Zinovievists. She holds anti-party sentiments toward both the general line and the leadership. Wife of “Moscow Center” member SHAROV, Ya. V. [1] Lives in Moscow.

GORSHENIN.

INTERROGATED BY —

DEPUTY HEAD OF THE SPO, UGB — STROMIN.

CERTIFIED TRUE: V. A.

January 20, 1935.

RGASPI, F. 17, Inv. 171., File 210, Folios 57–61.

[1] In the text, incorrectly: “Ya. G.”
