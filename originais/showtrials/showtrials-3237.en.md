TO THE CENTRAL COMMITTEE OF THE ALL-UNION COMMUNIST PARTY (BOLSHEVIKS)

from a former member of the All-Union Communist Party (Bolsheviks) —

SOSITSKY, Lev Ilyich.

STATEMENT.

I have given the investigation detailed testimony about my grave counterrevolutionary crimes against the Party and the working class. I do not think there is any need to take up your attention by repeating it. Nevertheless, I wish to emphasize once more that the unheard-of, villainous murder of Comrade KIROV, the best-loved leader of the Leningrad Bolsheviks and the workers of Lenin's city, resulted from the atmosphere of slander against the Party and fierce hatred of its leaders, especially Comrade Stalin, which all of us cultivated, above all our “leaders ZINOVIEV and KAMENEV.” All of us, and especially our “leaders” ZINOVIEV and KAMENEV, bore full political and moral responsibility for this vile murder. I personally am doubly guilty. I had already been expelled from the Party once, in 1927, for taking part in the Zinovievist opposition. The Party believed in my repentance, readmitted me to its ranks, and entrusted me with responsible work. I proved to be a double-dealer and an enemy of the Party and criminally kicked the Party. Instead of working honestly, I again established connections with LEVIN Vladimir, MANDELSHTAM Sergey, and Myasnikov Nikolai and took part in counterrevolutionary subversive work against the Party. Acknowledging my immense crime against the Party and the Soviet Country, I ask you to accept my sincere and complete repentance for my crime, and I am ready to help destroy to the very end this accursed counterrevolutionary swamp, which brought so much harm to the Party and the working class. I beg to be given an opportunity to break forever with this accursed past, which led me into the abyss, and an opportunity someday to join the ranks of the working class and work together with the entire Party and working class, as honestly and devotedly as I worked before I ever entered the opposition, before 1926. I served in the Red Army from 1919 to 1926, took part in suppressing the Kronstadt uprising and the Karelian uprising and served on the Latvian front, for which I was commended in the relevant orders. A former member of the All-Union Communist Party (Bolsheviks) since 1919. I once more ask the Central Committee of the All-Union Communist Party (Bolsheviks) to give me an opportunity to make amends for my vile crime and someday be able to join the ranks of the fighters for communism.

Written in his own hand — SOSITSKY.

December 26, 1934.

Certified true: Shugaeva

RGASPI, fond 671, inventory 1, file 121, leaves 73–74.
