INTERROGATION RECORD

OF NIKOLAEV L. V., dated December 6, 1934.

QUESTION: What form did the participation of the form\<er\> oppositionist KOTOLYNOV V. \[1\] take in preparing and carrying out the terror\<ist\> act against Comrade KIROV?

ANSWER: I met with KOTOLYNOV V. in 1934 at the Polytechnic Institute, on the Vyborg Side, in Sosnovka, where he was studying.

In the first days of November of th\<is\> y\<ear\>, I went to the Institute to meet KOTOLYNOV. The meeting took place outdoors, in the Institute garden, at about four o’cl\<ock\> in the afternoon.

I told KOTOLYNOV about my conversation with SHATSKY: that SHATSKY was keeping KIROV’s apartment under observation; that he was preparing a terror\<ist\> act against Comrade KIROV and also against STALIN; and that SHATSKY strongly condemned STALIN’s statement at the 17th Congress concerning oppositionists.

KOTOLYNOV responded favorably to all this and declared that SHATSKY should be supported in this undertaking.

After saying this, he stated that he would have to meet with SHATSKY and take part in carrying out his plans.

At the end of the conversation, I informed KOTOLYNOV about the work I was personally conducting in preparation for the murder of KIROV. He approved of my actions.

Signed: L. NIKOLAEV.

I supplement my testimony concerning KOTOLYNOV with the following point:

KOTOLYNOV told me that he was working in Moscow to prepare a terror\<ist\> act against STALIN and that he had contacts for this purpose. His words were to be understood as meaning that he was doing so independently of SHATSKY.

L. NIKOLAEV.

INTERROGATED BY:

DEPUTY PEOPLE'S COMMISSAR FOR INTERNAL AFFAIRS Ya. AGRANOV
