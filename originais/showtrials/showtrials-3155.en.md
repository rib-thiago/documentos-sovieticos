RECORD

of the confrontation between the accused ZVEZDOV, Vasily Ivanovich, and SOKOLOV, Georgy Vasilyevich, conducted on 18/XII-34.

The accused ZVEZDOV V. I. and SOKOLOV G. V., after mutually acknowledging that they knew each other personally, testified:

QUESTION to the accused ZVEZDOV: What do you know about SOKOLOV G. V.'s membership in the counterrevolutionary organization of Zinovievists?

ANSWER: I know that SOKOLOV is a member of our counterrevolutionary organization of Zinovievists. SOKOLOV G. personally told me this, as did ANTONOV Nikolai. SOKOLOV and I spoke repeatedly throughout 1933–1934 about his participation in the organization's work.

QUESTION to the accused SOKOLOV: Do you confirm this?

ANSWER: I confirm that I was indeed a member of the counterrevolutionary organization of Zinovievists.

QUESTION to the accused ZVEZDOV: What did SOKOLOV tell you about his meetings with NIKOLAEV, Leonid Vasilyevich, a member of your organization, and did he, SOKOLOV, know that NIKOLAEV was also a member of your organization?

ANSWER: SOKOLOV personally told me that he frequently met with NIKOLAEV Leonid, a member of our organization. We spoke about this in the student section of the Regional Trade Union Council in the spring of this year. SOKOLOV and I worked together in the student section of the Regional Trade Union Council until May 1934. SOKOLOV undoubtedly knew that NIKOLAEV belonged to our organization; he could not have been unaware of it, since all of us members of the organization knew this and, furthermore, it was clear from my conversation with SOKOLOV about NIKOLAEV.

QUESTION to the accused SOKOLOV: What can you testify regarding this?

ANSWER: I confirm that I did indeed meet ZVEZDOV in the student section of the Regional Trade Union Council. I knew that NIKOLAEV belonged to this counterrevolutionary organization of Zinovievists.

SOKOLOV                               ZVEZDOV

Confrontation conducted by:

DEPUTY PEOPLE'S COMMISSAR FOR INTERNAL AFFAIRS OF THE USSR — AGRANOV

DEPUTY HEAD OF THE SPO OF THE GUGB — STROMIN
