RECORD

Of a confrontation between the accused BASHKIROV, Aleksandr Fabianovich, and SOSITSKY, Lev Ilyich

Dated December 23, 1934.

After mutually confirming that they knew one another personally, the accused BASHKIROV and SOSITSKY testified:

QUESTION TO BASHKIROV:

What do you know about SOSITSKY's membership in the organization of former participants in the Zinovievist-Trotskyist bloc and his role in it?

ANSWER BY BASHKIROV:

I personally know SOSITSKY as a member of the counterrevolutionary organization of former participants in the Zinovievist-Trotskyist bloc; I met him, in particular, at V. LEVIN's home. I know from V. LEVIN's words that SOSITSKY belongs to the center of our organization in Leningrad and worked in the center until recently.

(BASHKIROV).

QUESTION TO SOSITSKY:

Do you confirm BASHKIROV's testimony?

ANSWER BY SOSITSKY:

Yes, I confirm that until recently I belonged to the Zinovievist organization in Leningrad and was a member of its center. I did indeed meet BASHKIROV at LEVIN's home on the organization's business. (SOSITSKY).

QUESTION TO SOSITSKY:

What do you know about BASHKIROV's membership in the Zinovievist organization?

ANSWER BY SOSITSKY:

I know personally that BASHKIROV belonged to the Zinovievist organization in Leningrad and was connected with V. LEVIN on the organization's business until recently. —

(SOSITSKY).

The confrontation was conducted by:

DEPUTY HEAD OF THE SPO OF THE GUGB OF THE NKVD OF THE USSR: (LYUSHKOV)

HEAD OF THE FOURTH SECTION OF THE SPO OF THE UGB: (KOGAN)
