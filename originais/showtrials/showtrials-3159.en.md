RECORD

of the confrontation between the accused MYASNIKOV, Nikolai Petrovich, and RUMYANTSEV, Vladimir Vasilyevich, conducted on 18/XII-34.

The accused MYASNIKOV N. P. and RUMYANTSEV V. V., summoned for a confrontation, after acknowledging that they knew each other personally, testified:

QUESTION TO MYASNIKOV: Do you know of RUMYANTSEV's membership in the Zinovievist-Trotskyist counterrevolutionary organization and of his role in it?

ANSWER: Yes, as a member of the center of the Leningrad Zinovievist-Trotskyist counterrevolutionary organization, I know that RUMYANTSEV V. V. is a member of the counterrevolutionary organization I have named and has belonged to its leading center until the most recent period.

QUESTION TO RUMYANTSEV: Do you confirm MYASNIKOV's testimony?

ANSWER: I acknowledge that until the most recent period I was connected with, and thereby belonged to, the Zinovievist-Trotskyist counterrevolutionary organization. I belonged to the leading center of the Leningrad counterrevolutionary organization of Zinovievists until 1929; afterward, until the most recent period, I maintained contact with LEVIN V., the representative of the Moscow center of the Zinovievist-Trotskyist counterrevolutionary organization and leader of the Leningrad Zinovievist-Trotskyist counterrevolutionary organization.

The confrontation record was taken down correctly from our words.

V. RUMYANTSEV.                                N. MYASNIKOV.

Confrontation conducted by:

DEPUTY PEOPLE'S COMMISSAR FOR INTERNAL AFFAIRS — AGRANOV.

HEAD OF THE EKO OF THE GUGB — MIRONOV.

DEPUTY HEAD OF THE SPO OF THE GUGB — LYUSHKOV.
