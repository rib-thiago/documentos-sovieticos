INTERROGATION RECORD

of POPOV, Georgy Fedorovich,

dated December 18, 1934.

In addition to my previous testimony, I consider it necessary to report the following fact: I think it was in 1929 that I visited Pyotr SEVEROV's apartment (Shirokaya Street), where I found SITKIN and GEDVABNY \[1\]. I think SITKIN had some letter from TROTSKY which they intended to read aloud. I objected to reading and discussing this document at the time, and therefore the letter was not read in my presence. Shortly afterward I left SEVEROV's apartment, and SEVEROV himself went somewhere with me. GEDVABNY and SITKIN remained there (SITKIN lived in the same apartment).

QUESTION: Did you inform anyone at the time about this illegal gathering?

ANSWER: At the time I informed no one. In 1930 I was summoned to the OGPU Plenipotentiary Representation and asked about GEDVABNY and SITKIN, and then I testified about this gathering.

POPOV.

INTERROGATED BY:

HEAD OF THE 6TH SECTION OF THE SPO OF THE GUGB — KORKIN.
