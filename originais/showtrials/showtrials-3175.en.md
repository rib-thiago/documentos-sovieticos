CONFRONTATION RECORD

between SOKOLOV, Georgy Vasilyevich, and NIKOLAEV, Leonid Vasilyevich

19 December 1934.

The accused SOKOLOV Georgy and NIKOLAEV Leonid, summoned for a confrontation, after mutually confirming that they knew each other personally, testified:

1\. QUESTION TO SOKOLOV: Do you confirm that you are a member of the Zinovievist-Trotskyist counterrevolutionary organization?

ANSWER: Yes, I confirm it.

2\. QUESTION TO NIKOLAEV: Do you know that SOKOLOV is a member of the Zinovievist-Trotskyist counterrevolutionary organization?

ANSWER: Yes, I know that SOKOLOV is a member of the Zinovievist-Trotskyist counterrevolutionary organization.

3\. QUESTION TO NIKOLAEV: Do you confirm that you are a member of the Zinovievist-Trotskyist counterrevolutionary organization?

ANSWER: Yes, I confirm that I am a member of the Zinovievist-Trotskyist counterrevolutionary organization.

4\. QUESTION TO SOKOLOV: Do you know that NIKOLAEV is a member of the Zinovievist-Trotskyist counterrevolutionary organization?

ANSWER: Yes, I know that NIKOLAEV is a member of the Zinovievist-Trotskyist counterrevolutionary organization.

5\. QUESTION TO SOKOLOV: Do you know about the terrorist activities of the Zinovievist-Trotskyist counterrevolutionary organization to which you belonged?

ANSWER: Yes, I do. During a conversation with ANTONOV, a member of the organization, at the KSI stadium in late October about intensifying the organization's activities, ANTONOV informed me that the organization had decided to undertake a definite active operation through KOTOLYNOV. ANTONOV explained that this operation was to be sanctioned personally by KOTOLYNOV. The other members of the organization would remain in the shadows.

6\. QUESTION TO NIKOLAEV: Do you know anything about this conversation between ANTONOV and SOKOLOV?

ANSWER: I know of this conversation between ANTONOV and SOKOLOV from KOTOLYNOV and SOKOLOV.

7\. QUESTION TO NIKOLAEV: Did SOKOLOV participate in preparing the terrorist act against Comrade KIROV?

ANSWER: SOKOLOV participated; he helped me keep watch on KIROV in the Smolny.

8\. QUESTION TO SOKOLOV: Do you confirm this testimony by NIKOLAEV?

ANSWER: No, I do not confirm it.

9\. QUESTION TO SOKOLOV: Did you see NIKOLAEV in the Smolny between 2 and 4 November?

ANSWER: Yes, I saw NIKOLAEV in the Smolny between 2 and 4 November.

10\. QUESTION TO SOKOLOV: When you met NIKOLAEV on the eve of the October celebrations, did you speak with him about obtaining a ticket for admission to the October celebrations?

ANSWER: I asked NIKOLAEV to obtain a ticket to Uritsky Square for admission to the parade, which NIKOLAEV refused to do for me.

11\. QUESTION TO NIKOLAEV: Do you confirm that you saw SOKOLOV in the Smolny by NIKOLAEV on the eve of the October celebrations and spoke with him about obtaining tickets for admission to the October celebrations?

ANSWER: I have already testified that I did indeed meet SOKOLOV in the Smolny building on 6 November. We spoke about the need for both of us to obtain tickets for admission to the Mariinsky Theater for the ceremonial meeting marking the October anniversary. We wanted to enter the theater for terrorist purposes, intending to encounter KIROV there. We did not obtain tickets because we arrived at the Smolny late.

12\. QUESTION FROM NIKOLAEV TO SOKOLOV: How did you, SOKOLOV, react when you learned from ANTONOV of the terrorist act being prepared?

ANSWER: I attached no particular importance to it.

Confrontation conducted by:

HEAD OF THE EKO OF THE GUGB OF THE NKVD OF THE USSR — MIRONOV

ASSISTANT TO THE HEAD OF THE EKO OF THE GUGB OF THE NKVD OF THE USSR — DMITRIEV

HEAD OF THE 1ST SECTION OF THE SPO OF THE UGB OF THE NKVD DIRECTORATE FOR LENINGRAD REGION — LULOV
