INTERROGATION RECORD

OF RUMYANTSEV, Vladimir Vasilyevich

13 December 1934.

QUESTION: Do you know SHATSKY Nikolai, a former Komsomol official in the Vyborg District?

ANSWER: I have known SHATSKY very well since 1925, when he was secretary of the party organization at the VOLOKNO factory. SHATSKY participated in the counterrevolutionary Zinovievist-Trotskyist bloc; from early 1926 he was an active Zinovievist, and subsequently, after ZINOVIEV and KAMENEV retreated, being irreconcilably disposed and unwilling to accept any compromise, he left the Zinovievists for the Trotskyists. After returning from the Chelyabinsk Tractor Works, where he worked as an engineer, he came to visit me with his wife during the May Day holidays in 1934. Since I was drunk, I did not talk with him. Then, after the May Day holidays, he came to see me at the Soviet and asked me to find him a job, citing good references from the factory's leadership triumvirate. —

RUMYANTSEV.

INTERROGATED BY:

DEPUTY HEAD OF THE SPO, GUGB — LYUSHKOV.
