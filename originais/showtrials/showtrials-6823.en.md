TOP SECRET.

TO THE SECRETARY OF THE CC OF THE VKP(b)

Comrade STALIN

On the night of January 1–2 of this year, searches were conducted at the apartments of, and the following persons were arrested:

1\. ZORIN Sergei Semyonovich, born in 1890, VKP(b) member since 1917; from 1911 to 1917, was a member of the American Social Democratic Party. In 1927, was expelled from the VKP(b) for factional work as a participant in the Zinovievist opposition; reinstated in 1930. Director of the Scientific Research Institute of Housing Construction.

Seized during the search: counterrevolutionary materials from the Zinovievists' past struggle against the party, the platform of the “13”; stenographic transcripts of speeches by KAMENEV, ZINOVIEV, TROTSKY and EVDOKIMOV from 1927; ZINOVIEV's theses “Lessons of the British Strike”; pamphlets by TROTSKY, “What Was Expected and What Resulted,” ZINOVIEV, “What Happened,” TREN, “The Truth About China,” SARKIS, “On LENIN's Testament,” and ZALUTSKY, “For Genuine Unity” (1927); leaflets—appeals and statements on behalf of the opposition and signed by ZINOVIEV, TROTSKY, PREOBRAZHENSKY, BAKAYEV and RADEK.

2\. SHLYAPNIKOV Aleksandr Gavrilovich, born in 1884, former VKP(b) member, expelled from the party during the purge in 1933. Has not worked anywhere for the last two years.

Seized during the search: a letter from MEDVEDEV dated August 3, 1934, and a diary containing attacks against Soviet power, as well as the manuscript of the pamphlet “In Search of Bread and Oil.”

3\. YEMELYANOV Nikolai Aleksandrovich, born in 1871, VKP(b) member since 1898, member of the Society of Old Bolsheviks, personal pensioner. In 1917, sheltered V.I. LENIN in his home in Razliv.

4\. SMILGA Ivar Tenisovich, born in 1892, VKP(b) member since 1907; expelled from the VKP(b) in 1927 for factional work; reinstated in 1930. At the end of 1927, was exiled for THREE years for counterrevolutionary anti-party activity, and in May 1929 was released from exile ahead of schedule in connection with his submission of a statement breaking with Trotskyism.

During the search of SMILGA's apartment, ALSKY A.O. was present there; he works as deputy head of Karabugazstroy, is a former VKP(b) member expelled from the party in 1934, and is on leave in Moscow.

The search of SMILGA's apartment has not yet been completed.

PEOPLE'S COMMISSAR

OF INTERNAL AFFAIRS OF THE USSR: G. Yagoda (G. YAGODA)
