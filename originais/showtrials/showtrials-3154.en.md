INTERROGATION RECORD

(supplementary) OF ZVEZDOV V. I.

18 December 1934.

QUESTION: What can you add to your previous testimony?

ANSWER: I acknowledge my guilt before the party and the proletarian state in that, having taken the path of struggle against the party, I joined the counterrevolutionary organization of Zinovievists, which pursued the aims of changing the political course of the CC and replacing the current party leadership with the leaders of the Trotskyist-Zinovievist bloc.

I was aware of our organization's political and tactical directives, which generally amounted to preparing conditions within the party for the rise of a broad movement in favor of implementing the aims we had outlined. I knew that our organization in Leningrad was closely linked to the Moscow Center in the persons of ZINOVIEV and KAMENEV, had undertaken extensive recruitment work in Leningrad, and employed clandestine methods in its activities.

I also knew that, to intensify the organization's activities, KOTOLYNOV Ivan, a member of the Leningrad center, was preparing a decisive action against Comrade KIROV.

As I have already testified, the fundamental methods of our struggle against the party and the party leadership were to discredit in every way the decisions and measures of the CC of the AUCP(b) and to cultivate feelings of bitterness and hatred against Comrade STALIN and Comrades MOLOTOV, KAGANOVICH, and KIROV.

In the circumstances of Leningrad, we particularly hated Comrade KIROV, who most consistently carried out Comrade STALIN's line here.

Comrade KIROV, who had taken charge of the Leningrad party organization after ZINOVIEV's expulsion, embodied for us the cruel policy of administrative repression by the party apparatus.

Having begun the struggle in the ranks of the so-called Leningrad opposition, I realize that I slid into the camp of the enemies of the working class. I acknowledge that our struggle was unprincipled and caused colossal damage to the party and the state.

The ideological foundations of our struggle, once formulated in the well-known platform of the Trotskyist-Zinovievist bloc, had long since exhausted themselves completely and were refuted by the successful course of socialist construction.

Our organization's struggle, despite the entirely evident correctness of the party's general line, nevertheless continued with particular intensity and was fueled by our “leaders” ZINOVIEV, KAMENEV, and others, generating a system of views characteristic of fascism.

In concluding my testimony, I ask the investigation to take account of my sincere confessions and deep repentance.

In addition, I ask that consideration be given to my statement of 13 December, in which I request the opportunity to atone for my guilt through persistent labor and earn the forgiveness of the socialist motherland.

The record was taken down correctly from my words; read by me.

ZVEZDOV.

INTERROGATED BY:

DEPUTY HEAD OF THE SPO OF THE UGB — STROMIN
