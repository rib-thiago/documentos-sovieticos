INTERROGATION RECORD

OF BRAVO Boris Lvovich —

January 10, 1935.

QUESTION: During the interrogation of January 9 of this year, you testified that the Zinovievist organization discussed the possibility of ZINOVIEV and KAMENEV returning to the Party leadership on the basis of the country's economic difficulties.

Explain in greater detail what conclusions the organization reached when discussing economic difficulties.

ANSWER: As I have already testified, various rumors and fabrications about emerging or impending economic difficulties were constantly circulated among the organization's members (about failure to fulfill the metallurgy program, the unsatisfactory progress of the sowing and grain-harvesting campaigns, declining livestock numbers, famine in particular regions, and the like).

As various difficulties arose in the country, our anti-Party and anti-Soviet activity increased, slander against the Party and its leadership intensified, and rumors of disagreements within the Party leadership were circulated more insistently.

Conversely, as the difficulties were overcome, the wave of our activity receded and our spirits fell.

In discussing economic difficulties, we concluded that the Party, unable to overcome them, would be compelled to bring the former opposition leaders into the leadership. We exchanged thoughts of this kind with EVDOKIMOV several times.

Recorded faithfully from my words, read — BRAVO.

INTERROGATED BY:

ASSISTANT HEAD OF THE OO OF THE GUGB OF THE NKVD — GENDIN.
