INTERROGATION RECORD

Of BASHKIROV, Aleksandr Fabianovich, December 23, 1934.

(SUPPLEMENTARY)

QUESTION: What do you know about SOSITSKY, a member of the counterrevolutionary organization of Zinovievists?

ANSWER: I know SOSITSKY as a member of the counterrevolutionary organization of Zinovievists. I met him at LEVIN's home and have already testified about him. I know from LEVIN's words that SOSITSKY belonged to the organization's Leningrad center.

Recorded correctly from my words, in confirmation of which I sign.

A. BASHKIROV.

Interrogated by: HEAD OF THE 4TH SECTION OF THE SPO, L. KOGAN.

Certified correct: Potrokhova

RGASPI, fond 671, inventory 1, file 121, folio 143.
