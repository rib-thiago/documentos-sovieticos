RECORD

of the supplementary interrogation of ANTONOV Nikolai Semenovich

17 December 1934.

QUESTION: Of what do you plead guilty?

ANSWER: Repenting before the party and the proletarian state, I plead guilty:

a) to having belonged to the counterrevolutionary organization of Zinovievists, which was essentially fascist in character;

b) to having known of the existence of the center of the counterrevolutionary organization of Zinovievists in Leningrad and Moscow;

c) to having maintained contact with KOTOLYNOV Ivan, a member of the center of the counterrevolutionary organization;

d) to having learned from KOTOLYNOV Ivan of NIKOLAEV Leonid's terrorist intentions against the leaders of the AUCP(b), Comrades STALIN, KAGANOVICH, and KIROV.

Recorded correctly from my words and read to me.

ANTONOV.

Interrogated by:

HEAD OF THE 1ST SECTION OF THE SPO OF THE GUGB OF THE NKVD OF THE USSR PETROVSKY.

Certified true:

RGASPI, fond 671, inventory 1, file 120, folio 92.
