RECORD OF SUPPLEMENTARY INTERROGATION

OF ANTONOV, Nikolai Semionovich.

12 December 1934.

QUESTION: How did the counter-revolutionary Zinovievist organisation conduct its work in practice?

ANSWER: I know that the combat veterans' association at Leningrad Istomol served as a centre for gathering opposition forces and was used as a legal opportunity for our counter-revolutionary organisation. Through Istomol we established contact with Zinovievists. We also used Istomol to restore Zinovievist contacts and bring together old Komsomol members. In 1933 there were several meetings. Leningrad Istomol's leaders were the Zinovievists Lev KHANIK, a member of our counter-revolutionary organisation, and Georgy POPOV, a Zinovievist. To establish contacts and draw new people into the organisation, we undertook assignments to write articles on Komsomol history. We undertook to write the articles collectively so as to have a legal opportunity to gather. Vladimir RUMYANTSEV, a member of our counter-revolutionary organisation, organised us, always involving me, FADEEV, Andrei TOLMAZOV, Pavel BAKA, a former Zinovievist, and Aleksandr SEREDOKHIN for this purpose; I believe the latter is also a member of our counter-revolutionary organisation. The organisation used members working in the Soviet and Party apparatus, namely Vladimir RUMYANTSEV, responsible secretary of the Vyborg District Soviet, who helped us obtain employment — he placed me as head of personnel at the District Soviet and ANTONOV [i] as sector head; I cannot now recall which other organisation members he placed. Whenever we gathered and met, we conducted counter-revolutionary conversations — condemned the Party's policy and said that its present leadership pursued a policy disastrous for the country. At the meetings there were constant discussions of the Party leadership, particularly Comrade STALIN and KIROV. On the basis of these conversations, sharp extremist sentiments grew among Zinovievist youth. NIKOLAEV, Comrade KIROV's murderer, grew up and was educated among these Zinovievist young people from the former active members of the Vyborg Komsomol.

QUESTION: What do you know about the organisation of terror against the VKP(b) leadership by the counter-revolutionary Zinovievist organisation in which you participated?

ANSWER: I consider that political responsibility for Comrade KIROV's murder rests with our counter-revolutionary Zinovievist organisation, which in fact brought up Leonid NIKOLAEV, who remained associated with our counter-revolutionary organisation until recently and belonged to it.

Correctly taken down from my words and read to me. —

N. ANTONOV.

Interrogated by —

HEAD OF THE 1ST SECTION, SPO, GUGB — PETROVSKY.
