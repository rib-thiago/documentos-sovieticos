INTERROGATION RECORD

Conducted on December 20, 1934, by the Assistant Head of Section of the GUGB of the NKVD, RUTKOVSKY.

KUKLIN, Aleksandr Sergeevich

Question: What do you know about the existence in Leningrad of a counterrevolutionary organization of participants in the former Zinovievist-Trotskyist bloc?

Answer: The former Zinovievists RUMYANTSEV, Vladimir LEVIN, MYASNIKOV and RUSANOV, whom I met, did not tell me about the existence of a Zinovievist organization in Leningrad.

Question: The investigation has established that the terrorist act against Comrade KIROV was committed by L. NIKOLAEV, a participant in the Zinovievist counterrevolutionary organization in Leningrad. Do you acknowledge that the center of the Zinovievist counterrevolutionary organization, and in particular you as a member of this center, bear responsibility for this murder?

Answer: Direct responsibility for the murder of Comrade KIROV rests first and foremost with the Leningrad underground counterrevolutionary organization of Zinovievists and the entire leadership of that organization.

As for our Moscow group of Zinovievists and myself personally, all of us must bear moral and political responsibility for this murder, since none of us told the young participants in the counterrevolutionary organization of the Zinovievist-Trotskyist bloc that we agreed entirely with the policy of the Party Central Committee; thus we educated these young people in a spirit of duplicity and hostility toward the Party leadership.

I acknowledge my guilt before the Party and Soviet power, deeply repent all the crimes I have committed, and ask to be given the opportunity to atone for my guilt at any work. —

A. KUKLIN.

Interrogated by: RUTKOVSKY.
