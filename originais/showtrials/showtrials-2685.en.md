INTERROGATION RECORD

OF NIKOLAEV, Leonid Vasilievich, dated 6/XII/34 [1].

Question: What part did the former oppositionists SHATSKY and KOTOLYNOV [2] play in preparing and carrying out the terrorist act against Comrade KIROV?

Answer: They are accomplices in the terrorist act. Each one's individual role was as follows:

SHATSKY — I first met him in 1933; he displayed great bitterness towards the Party leaders. Our next meeting was in the summer of 1934, when I encountered SHATSKY near KIROV's apartment at 28 Krasnykh Zor Street. SHATSKY was watching the apartment, establishing all KIROV's movements. He was doing this to prepare a terrorist act. On seeing CHUDOV, who drove past us in a car at that moment, SHATSKY indicated that CHUDOV was not a suitable target for a terrorist act, and said that a terrorist act should be committed against STALIN as leader of the Party. From this conversation with Shatsky I established that he was preparing an act against STALIN and that he was conducting this work independently of the preparations to murder KIROV.

During October 1934 I again encountered SHATSKY near KIROV's apartment, continuing his surveillance.

I did not speak to him, since I did not wish in any way to prevent him from carrying out the terrorist act against KIROV. As SHATSKY was watching KIROV's apartment, I turned my attention to Smolny and sought an opportunity to kill KIROV there, which I succeeded in doing on 1 December.

L. NIKOLAEV

INTERROGATED BY:

DEPUTY PEOPLE'S COMMISSAR OF INTERNAL AFFAIRS OF THE USSR — AGRANOV

HEAD OF THE EKO, GUGB, NKVD OF THE USSR — MIRONOV

ASSISTANT HEAD OF THE EKO, GUGB, NKVD — DMITRIEV.
