COPY

INTERROGATION RECORD

OF L. V. NIKOLAEV, DATED 9/XII/1934.

QUESTION: Repeat how you acquired the Nagant with which you shot Comrade KIROV.

ANSWER: I acquired it in 1918 from a worker who was a Red Guardsman and left for the front. He lived in our apartment. Before leaving, he took me to a shed in the courtyard and showed me where he had hidden the Nagant, saying he was giving it to me; the Nagant had no cartridges.

QUESTION: In 1918 you were only 14. How do you explain this gift of a weapon to a mere boy?

ANSWER: I explain it by the fact that adult workers did not see me as a boy, since I had helped the workers in the barricade fighting of 1917.

QUESTION: Who can now confirm that you actually acquired the Nagant in the way you testify?

ANSWER: My cousin Egor FOMIN, who lives at 20 Lesnoy Prospekt, apartment 10 or 12, can confirm this. In 1919 and 1920 and in later years I told him how I had acquired the Nagant.

QUESTION: Where did you keep the Nagant after acquiring it?

ANSWER: In 1919 and 1920 the revolver lay in the shed. In 1920 I took it out, put it in good condition and hid it again in the shed at 18 Lesnaya Street. From 1922 until the day of my arrest I generally kept the revolver in the apartment's writing desk, first in the middle drawer and then in a side drawer.

QUESTION: Did you have a permit to carry the revolver?

ANSWER: In 1924 I registered my revolver, and on 2/IX/1924 received a permit to carry and keep it. I renewed the permit in 1930, when it was issued to me on the petition of the responsible secretary of the “Karl Marx” factory cell and the secretary of the shop cell, valid until 21 April 1931. Since then I have kept the revolver without a permit.

QUESTION: Between 1924 and 1930 did you also keep the revolver without a permit?

ANSWER: Yes, I confirm this.

QUESTION: Why was the revolver taken from you in 1924?

ANSWER: Around January 1924 my sister's husband, Pavel SUTULA, shot himself with my revolver, having taken it from the writing desk. The criminal investigation police took the revolver as material evidence.

QUESTION: How did you get the revolver back, and who helped you?

ANSWER: After six or about eight months I approached GRIBOV, Head of the District Soviet's Administrative Department, asking for the return of the revolver, which was returned to me.

QUESTION: On what grounds was the revolver returned to you, if at the time it was taken you had no permit to keep it — which you only obtained later, in September 1924?

ANSWER: GRIBOV returned it because I presented a certificate from the VLKSM District Committee stating that the revolver belonged to me.

QUESTION: Do you consider that you had legal grounds for getting the revolver back?

ANSWER: Yes.

QUESTION: Who taught you to handle the Nagant?

ANSWER: Pavel SUTULA taught me; I went outside the city with him and shot there.

QUESTION: And in later years?

ANSWER: I taught myself.

QUESTION: The investigation demands a truthful answer: who taught you to shoot the Nagant before committing the terrorist act?

ANSWER: I have already testified that no one taught me.

QUESTION: In the interrogation record of 8/XII you testified that you obtained cartridges for the Nagant in 1932 at the “Dynamo” shop upon presenting a permit to carry the revolver. Yet with an expired permit issued to you in 1930 and valid through 1931, the “Dynamo” shop could not have sold you cartridges in 1932. Where exactly did you obtain them?

ANSWER: I was mistaken; I bought the cartridges not in 1932 but in 1930 and 1931.

QUESTION: Which of your relatives can confirm that the Nagant was kept at your home during recent years, right up to your arrest?

ANSWER: My wife Milda Petrovna and my sister Ekaterina ROGACHEVA can confirm this. I dismantled and cleaned the Nagant in their presence; before their eyes I took it out of the writing desk and took it with me on business trips. Until recently Pyotr DRAULE and my brother Pyotr also knew I had a revolver.

Correctly taken down from my words. I have read the record.

L. NIKOLAEV

Interrogated by:

Deputy People's Commissar of Internal Affairs

Head of the EKO, GUGB, NKVD of the USSR

Assistant Head of the EKO, GUGV, NKVD of the USSR — DMITRIEV
