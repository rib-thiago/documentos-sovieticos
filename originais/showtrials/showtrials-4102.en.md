INTERROGATION RECORD

Of FADEEV, Fyodor Fadeevich, dated January 5, 1935.

In addition to my testimony I consider it necessary to inform the investigation of the following:

During meetings at TOLMAZOV, A.’s apartment, in a conversation about the decisions of the 17th Party Congress, TOLMAZOV said that the functions of trade unions were now reduced to collecting membership dues; trade unions were bureaucratic organizations that gave the working class nothing. The conversation about the work of trade unions was counterrevolutionary in character.

The initiator of the “Combat Fellowship” attached to Istomol was KHANIK, Lev, a participant in the counterrevolutionary Zinovievist organization. Zinovievists were drawn into the “Combat Fellowship,” and we used this organization to recruit, to draw people into membership in the counterrevolutionary Zinovievist organization.

The “Combat Fellowship” was a legal cover for our counterrevolutionary Zinovievist organization.

Besides KHANIK and POPOV, the military man ZHESTYANNIKOV, a participant in the counterrevolutionary Zinovievist organization, also carried out active work in the “Combat Fellowship.”

Recorded correctly from my words and read aloud to me.

FADEEV.

INTERROGATED BY:

HEAD OF THE 1ST SECTION OF THE SPO OF THE GUGB OF THE NKVD OF THE USSR — PETROVSKY

OFFICER OF THE SPO OF THE GUGB — MAKAROV.

Certified correct: Churbanova
