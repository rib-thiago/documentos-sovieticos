INTERROGATION RECORD

Of Izolit Grigoryevich ZHESTYANNIKOV,

December 24, 1934.

I consider it necessary to inform the investigation of the following: David CHARNY, a participant in the Zinovievist opposition who later went over to the Trotskyists, is being held in the same cell as me. CHARNY told me that when they worked in Siberia, their organization included BRIKKE (or BITKER — I do not remember the surname exactly), who worked in the party's Territorial Committee at that time. BRIKKE, according to CHARNY, was a secret informant of the Zinovievist-Trotskyist organization, and his membership in the organization was kept secret. Later he knew that BRIKKE had been doing newspaper work somewhere in Moscow. From newspaper reports about the Seventeenth Congress of the All-Union Communist Party (Bolsheviks), CHARNY saw that BRIKKE was a member of the Party Control Commission.

Iz. ZHESTYANNIKOV

INTERROGATED BY: HEAD OF THE SIXTH SECTION OF THE SPO OF THE GUGB — KORKIN

Certified true: illegible
