TESTIMONY OF THE ACCUSED

ANISHEV, Anatoly Isaevich,

dated January 5, 1935.

About ABRAMSON I know that during the 14th Congress he headed the Organization Department of the Vasilyevsky Ostrov District Committee and took an active part in the opposition. Later, in 1927, I saw him in Saratov, where I had come to convey information from Moscow. There he also took an active part in factional work together with ROTENBERG [1].

In 1932, when he worked in the Housing Department, I intended to go to him for help in obtaining an apartment, counting on my contact with him through the opposition, but just then he was removed in connection with some affair, which I learned about, if I am not mistaken, from ROTENBERG’s wife (I do not remember her surname; she works in the personnel department of the Regional Committee in Leningrad). I know nothing about ABRAMSON after that.

ANISHEV

INTERROGATED BY:

HEAD OF THE 1ST SECTION OF THE SPO OF THE UGB — LULOV

Certified correct: A. Svetlova

RGASPI, fond 671, inventory 1, file 127, folio 19.

[1] Here and subsequently in the text: “Rottenberg”.
