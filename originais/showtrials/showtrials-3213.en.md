STENOGRAM

INTERROGATION RECORD

OF NIKOLAEV Leonid Vasilyevich — dated 23 December 1934.

QUESTION: Did KOTOLYNOV give you instructions on how to speak with the Latvian consul and what questions to put to him?

ANSWER: My entire conversation with the Latvian consul was prepared in advance by me with Kotolynov, since before going to the consulate I outlined the conversation with him.

Contact with the consul was to enable us to establish contact with TROTSKY and obtain money from the consul for the needs of our underground work; in exchange, we agreed in advance to give the consul materials of an anti-Soviet character about the Soviet Union's internal situation.

To make the consul actually believe me, we considered it necessary to reveal ourselves to him; accordingly, I told the consul that I belonged to an underground anti-Soviet organization and informed him of the nature of our organization.

I handed the money received from the consul to KOTOLYNOV in October, when I went to see him at the Institute.

KOTOLYNOV was pleased with the success of my contacts with the consul. Taking the money, he said: “Stingy devils — couldn't they have given more?” In general, it must be said that neither of us was particularly confident that the attempt to obtain money would succeed; we assumed the consul might refuse.

QUESTION: In what banknotes did you receive the 5,000 rubles from the consul?

ANSWER: The money was received in chervontsy, in denominations from one to five chervontsy. The entire sum formed a relatively small bundle, which I put in the briefcase I had with me.

QUESTION: Did you have KOTOLYNOV's consent to keep 500 rubles out of the 5,000 rubles received?

ANSWER: Yes, KOTOLYNOV gave me his consent to keep 500 rubles.

Taken down in shorthand correctly from my words and read by me.

L. NIKOLAEV

Interrogated by:

HEAD OF THE EKO OF THE GUGB OF THE NKVD OF THE USSR — MIRONOV

ASSISTANT TO THE HEAD OF THE EKO OF THE GUGB OF THE NKVD OF THE USSR — DMITRIEV

Certified true. — Potrokhova
