SUPPLEMENTARY TESTIMONY

OF MAGDARYEV, Nikolai Karpovich.

Dated January 12, 1935.

QUESTION: At the previous interrogation you testified about your meetings with KOTOLYNOV and RUMYANTSEV. Tell us under what circumstances you met them.

ANSWER: In 1932 or early 1933 I learned from the newspapers that an acquaintance of mine, the former oppositionist Zinovievist Vladimir RUMYANTSEV, was working in the Vyborg District Soviet. Needing living space, I visited RUMYANTSEV hoping to obtain an apartment with his help. In RUMYANTSEV's office I found the former oppositionist KOTOLYNOV, whom I knew from work in the opposition.

QUESTION: The investigation knows that during this meeting with KOTOLYNOV and RUMYANTSEV you spoke with them about the underground Zinovievist organization. Do you confirm this?

ANSWER: No, I deny it, since I never had conversations with KOTOLYNOV and RUMYANTSEV about the Zinovievist organization, including during this particular meeting.

QUESTION: The investigation knows that you maintained organizational ties with former oppositionists until the last days of your arrest. Do you confirm this?

ANSWER: I maintained only comradely ties with the former oppositionist KUZNETSOV Fyodor and occasionally met KHARITONOV Sergei; I worked with the latter in the same shop at the KIROV factory. I had no conversations with them about the Zinovievist organization with them [1].

QUESTION: KOTOLYNOV's testimony of December 13, 1934, as well as other testimony, incriminates you as a member of the underground Zinovievist organization. Do you continue to deny this?

ANSWER: I deny it, since I did not belong to the underground Zinovievist organization.

I have read the record; it was correctly recorded from my words.

N. MAGDARYEV.

INTERROGATED BY:

ASSISTANT HEAD OF THE 1ST SECTION OF THE OO — BRICCI.
