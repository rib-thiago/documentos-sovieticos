INTERROGATION RECORD

OF YUSKIN, Ignaty Grigoryevich — 18/XII-1934.

QUESTION: What do you know about ERMOLAEVA E. F. and her participation in the terrorist act against Comrade KIROV?

ANSWER: ERMOLAEVA Elizaveta Fedorovna is undoubtedly a person alien to the party. I have known her for two years, and I have always been struck by her astonishing knowledge of everything happening at the top.

For example, she did not hesitate to spread the most vile anti-Soviet jokes and rumors, weaving a tangle of lies around them, sometimes linking them with the names of the leaders of the Communist Party. All this is told “in secret” and, moreover, with the most intimate knowledge of the details.

I believe her connection with NIKOLAEV is not accidental. ERMOLAEVA has extensive connections among responsible officials, apparently in Moscow as well. I do not know for certain whether ERMOLAEVA knew of the terrorist act being prepared against Comrade KIROV, as NIKOLAEV did not tell me about this. Judging, however, by NIKOLAEV's telephone conversation with ERMOLAEVA, conducted in my apartment, they know each other well.

I have read the testimony; it was recorded correctly from my words.

YUSKIN.

INTERROGATED BY:

Operational officer of the SPO of the GUGB of the NKVD — FEDOROV.
