INTERROGATION RECORD

OF MYASNIKOV, Nikolai Petrovich —

dated December 23, 1934.

I consider it necessary to inform the investigation that, from what LEVIN V. and SOSITSKY L. told me, I know that a member of the Zinovievist organization named EZHOV, whose first name and place of employment I do not know, displayed intentions of violent action that were essentially terrorist in nature. I know that this EZHOV was arrested and exiled, and subsequently, from what LEVIN V. and SOSITSKY told me, I know that he appeared in Leningrad either at the end of 1933 or at the beginning of 1934.

My conversation with LEVIN and SOSITSKY about EZHOV's intentions of violent, terrorist action took place in 1931–1932.

MYASNIKOV.

INTERROGATED BY:

DEPUTY HEAD OF THE SPO OF THE GUGB OF THE NKVD OF THE USSR — LYUSHKOV.

CERTIFIED TRUE: M. Glazkov
