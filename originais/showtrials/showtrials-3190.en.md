INTERROGATION RECORD (supplementary)

OF LEVIN, Vladimir Solomonovich, dated 21/XII-34.

QUESTION: Describe the role played in the counterrevolutionary organization of Zinovievists in Leningrad by MYASNIKOV, Nikolai Petrovich, whom you previously named in your testimony.

ANSWER: MYASNIKOV, Nikolai Petrovich, was one of the principal active leaders of the counterrevolutionary Zinovievist organization in Leningrad. MYASNIKOV maintained contact with members of the organization in Leningrad and Moscow.

Recorded correctly from my words and read to me —

LEVIN.

INTERROGATED BY: HEAD OF THE 1ST SECTION OF THE SPO OF THE GUGB OF THE NKVD OF THE USSR — PETROVSKY.
