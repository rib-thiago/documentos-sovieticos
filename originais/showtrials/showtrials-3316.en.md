INTERROGATION RECORD

of POPOV, Georgy Fedorovich,

dated December 18, 1934.

Sincerely repenting of my crimes before Soviet power, I consider it necessary to report to the investigation:

1\. I plead guilty to the fact that, having returned from Kursk to Leningrad and restored old connections with participants in the Zinovievist-Trotskyist bloc who had entered the Party with double-dealing intentions, I effectively became a member of the Zinovievist counterrevolutionary organization.

2\. The Zinovievist counterrevolutionary organization struggled against the leaders of the VKP(b) and in this counterrevolutionary struggle actually degenerated into a fascist organization.

3\. I heard SHATSKY engage in counterrevolutionary talk against the Party leaders which, in its malice and hostility, bordered on the expression of terrorist sentiments. SHATSKY said that “the leadership oppresses the Party”, that the propositions set out in the theses for the Seventeenth VKP(b) Congress meant “the apparatus's victory over the Party”, and similar counterrevolutionary fabrications. Although at the time I argued with SHATSKY against his harsh counterrevolutionary judgments on the theses, having been drawn into the counterrevolutionary nest of Zinovievism, I did not struggle against this, of which I plead guilty.

I ask the organs of Soviet power to take my sincere repentance into account.

POPOV.

INTERROGATED BY: HEAD OF THE 6TH SECTION OF THE SPO OF THE GUGB — KORKIN.
