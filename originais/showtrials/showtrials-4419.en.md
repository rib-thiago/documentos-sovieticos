INTERROGATION RECORD

Of ROTSKAN Pyotr Eduardovich

dated January 11, 1935.

ROTSKAN Pyotr Eduardovich, b\<orn\> in 1894, mem\<ber\> of the VKP(b) since 1915; received a party disciplinary penalty in 1927 for participation in the Zinovievist opposition; in 1928 was expelled from the party for the same reason and reinstated. Head of the forestry administration of the N\<orth\> Caucasus Territorial Land Administration.

QUESTION: The investigation possesses information that you were a participant in the counterrevolutionary Zinovievist organization until the moment of your arrest.

Do you confirm this?

ANSWER: Yes, I acknowledge that until recently I was a participant in the counterrevolutionary Zinovievist organization.

I have been a participant in the organization since my return to the party, that is, since 1928.

In submitting my declaration for return to the party I acted as a double-dealer, concealing from the party that I retained the opposition's views and continued the struggle against it.

I acted thus to a considerable extent under the influence of ZINOVIEV's emissary who had come to Leningrad—I.P. BAKAEV—who proposed that opposition participants return to the party through formal submission to the decisions of the Fifteenth Congress of the VKP(b), in order to continue the struggle against the party.

BAKAEV pointed out that refusing to join the well-known declaration of the “23” would place oppositionists either in the position of non-party private citizens or in that of an anti-Soviet group, with all the consequences flowing from this.

Returning to the party, even on condition of formally renouncing opposition views, would enable the opposition to preserve itself as a tendency.

BAKAEV's propaganda had its effect, and I, like many of my comrades, returned to the party.

Recorded correctly from my words; the record has been read by me.

ROTSKAN.

INTERROGATED BY:

ASSISTANT HEAD OF THE EKO OF THE GUGB OF THE NKVD OF THE USSR — DMITRIEV.

Certified correct: A. Svetlova
