SUPPLEMENTARY TESTIMONY

OF MAGDARYEV, Nikolai Karpovich.

Dated January 13, 1935.

QUESTION: The investigation knows that in 1929 you approached RUMYANTSEV Vladimir for assistance in finding employment at the “Red Triangle” factory. Do you confirm this?

ANSWER: Yes, I confirm it. I did indeed meet RUMYANTSEV Vladimir during that period and asked him to find me work at the “Red Triangle” factory.

QUESTION: How do you explain your assertion at the previous interrogations that, after 1926, you had only one meeting with RUMYANTSEV, in 1932?

ANSWER: I had forgotten my meeting with RUMYANTSEV in 1929.

The record was correctly recorded from my words and read by me —

N. MAGDARYEV.

INTERROGATED BY —

ASSISTANT HEAD OF THE 1ST SECTION OF THE OO OF THE GUGB — BRICCI.

Certified correct:

RGASPI, F. 671, Op. 1, D. 134, fol. 125.
