INTERROGATION RECORD

OF ROGACHEVA, Ekaterina Vasilievna

dated 11 December 1934.

INTERROGATED BY: Deputy Head of the OO, GUGB, NKVD — SOSNOVSKY

QUESTION: State the financial circumstances in which your brother Leonid NIKOLAEV's family lived, generally and particularly during the last three or four months, when Leonid was not employed anywhere.

ANSWER: I can judge from my visits to NIKOLAEV's apartment and from what our mother, Maria Tikhonovna NIKOLAEVA, told me; she often visited Leonid's family. I can say that no privation was apparent in Leonid NIKOLAEV's family even recently. Leonid NIKOLAEV's wife never complained to me of financial difficulties. The apartment which Leonid NIKOLAEV obtained in the Housing Cooperative after paying his share consisted of three very good rooms and was kept perfectly clean until recently. NIKOLAEV furnished the apartment with decent furniture.

QUESTION: Why was Leonid NIKOLAEV not employed anywhere during the last few months?

ANSWER: No one in our entire family entertained the idea that Leonid could in any way be unable to find work for reasons beyond his control.

We were all convinced that Leonid NIKOLAEV himself did not want to work, although he could easily have obtained a job with a secure income.

I personally, as well as my and Leonid's mother, expressed our indignation to him and reproached him for not wanting to work. I sent Leonid several times to our district committee for a work referral, but he never listened to me.

Three or four weeks ago Leonid told me he had already received two summonses to attend the district committee to obtain work. I do not know whether he took advantage of this offer.

Correctly taken down from my words — E. ROGACHEVA.

INTERROGATED BY —

DEPUTY HEAD OF THE OO, GUGB, NKVD — SOSNOVSKY
