INTERROGATION RECORD

of NAUMOV, Ivan Kupriyanovich,

January 21, 1935.

QUESTION: Name the Zinovievists known to you.

ANSWER: 1) Moisei YAKOVLEV — in 1930, on meeting me in the street in Leningrad, developed the old Trotskyist-Zinovievist propositions on the party's economic policy; he said he was connected with the Zinovievists TATAROV, SEF [1], and GORBACHEV.

2) I. VARDIN — told me in 1932 that he had resumed meetings with ZINOVIEV. From our conversation I established that he sympathized politically with him; he expressed indignation at the severity of the repression applied to ZINOVIEV in connection with RYUTIN's counterrevolutionary document.

3) CHASOVSHCHIK — up to and including 1929, a “leftist”; I myself attended a meeting of my then supporters at his apartment on Tsvetnoi Boulevard in the summer of 1929; in 1931 he visited me and attempted to develop Trotskyist propositions on collectivization. In 1932 I met him at KUKLIN's apartment.

I consider CHASOVSHCHIK a Zinovievist.

4) Ilya IONOV — in 1930 he personally told me in his office in the State Publishing House of Fiction building that he had returned to ZINOVIEV; I had visited him about publishing my novel “The Political Department Worker.”

5) SEF — in 1929 in Tiflis and in 1930 in Moscow, while working at the Institute of History of the USSR Communist Academy, told me that he remained on his old positions, that is, the position of the Trotskyist-Zinovievist bloc, and was connected with the “leftists.”

6) Yakov ELKOVICH — in 1932 in Sverdlovsk, in his work office, personally told me that he was a Zinovievist.

7) KOSTINA, like KOZHURO — are politically dependent people and wholly in solidarity with their husbands, BAKAEV and GORSHENIN. They are Zinovievists.

8) BAKAEV, EVDOKIMOV, FEDOROV, SHAROV, GERTIK, GESSEN, Vladimir LEVIN, KUKLIN, and GORSHENIN — are completely organized associates and supporters of ZINOVIEV and KAMENEV and never disagreed with them on questions of anti-party and counterrevolutionary work.

9) Boris SAKHOV — a Zinovievist, continuously connected with EVDOKIMOV and KUKLIN since 1928.

10) Sergei ZORIN — in 1928 left ZINOVIEV for the “leftists”; unwilling to return to the party, he left us for the Trotskyists, where he remained until the end of 1930. Unless I am mistaken, until the last period he met both ZINOVIEV and KAMENEV and the Trotskyists VORONSKY and SEREBRYAKOV.

11) Nikolai DMITRIEV — in a conversation in 1932 revealed Zinovievist sentiments.

12) RUMYANTSEV, KOTOLYNOV, and MANDELSHTAM — in 1928 were “leftists.” In 1929 they began drawing closer to ZINOVIEV and, either in 1930 or in 1931, returned entirely to ZINOVIEV. They were the most extreme in their malicious attitude toward the party leadership.

13) ZINOVIEV and KAMENEV — during our meetings before 1930 did not conceal from me that during the Fifteenth Congress they had maneuvered to preserve their group, that the opposition's programmatic documents (“philosophy of the epoch,” the platform, and others) remained basically correct, including their assessment of the party leadership and Comrade STALIN personally.

In 1929–1930 they sought a bloc with other anti-party and counterrevolutionary groups to seize the party leadership. They distributed the principal party and government posts among themselves in advance. They counted on the growth of both internal and international difficulties; they counted on disagreements within the AUCP(b) Central Committee.

14) Boris BRAVO — after the Fifteenth Congress of the AUCP(b), until 1930, went over to TROTSKY, then returned to ZINOVIEV.

Besides the Zinovievists mentioned above, I know three Rightists who personally told me before 1930 that they were Rykovists. They are Boris KUSHNER, Zyama GOLDMAN, and G. KAMINSKY.

Recorded correctly from my words. I have read the record.

January 21, 1935.

NAUMOV.

Interrogated by:

Assistant head of the EKO, GUGB, NKVD of the USSR — DMITRIEV.

Certified true: V. A.

RGASPI, F. 17, Inv. 171., File 210, Folios 90–92.

[1] Here and subsequently in the text, incorrectly: “Sef” [spelled with э].
