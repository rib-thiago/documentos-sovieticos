INTERROGATION RECORD

OF G. F. POPOV, dated 15 December 1934.

(Supplementary)

QUESTION: What do you know of the membership of the counter-revolutionary Zinovievist organisation?

ANSWER: As I have testified, the organisation was built clandestinely on the chain principle, so I do not know its full membership. I know as members only persons with whom I was connected in some way and met periodically or constantly. As I indicated, these are V. RUMYANTSEV, SHATSKY, SEVEROV, ANTONOV, KHANIK, TSARKOV and VINOGRADOV.

The organisation arose from the remnants of the Zinovievist-Trotskyist bloc and was led, as in previous years, by ZINOVIEV and his closest assistants, such as EVDOKIMOV, BAKAEV and others.

Clarifying my testimony regarding the Leningrad organisation's leadership, I state that it includes Vladimir RUMYANTSEV and Ivan KOTOLYNOV. Members mainly gather around them, and they in turn maintain contact with the organisation's leaders.

I consider it necessary additionally to inform the investigation that the persons most aggressively opposed to Party policy and its leaders were SHATSKY, RUMYANTSEV and ANTONOV.

I also report that SEVEROV apparently maintained closer contact with RUMYANTSEV, since from time to time at our meetings he conveyed various political news to me, mainly concerning ZINOVIEV and his closest assistants. SEVEROV told me RUMYANTSEV maintained contact with “some important people”, from whom he received information. He did not name whom exactly RUMYANTSEV was connected with.

POPOV.

INTERROGATED BY

HEAD OF THE 6TH SECTION, SPO, GUGB, NKVD OF THE USSR — KORKIN
