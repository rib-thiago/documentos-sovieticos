INTERROGATION RECORD

conducted by the Head of Section 6 of the SPO of the GUGB of the NKVD of the USSR — KORKIN.

dated December 13, 1934.

KOTOLYNOV Ivan Ivanovich.

QUESTION: Who headed the Leningrad counterrevolutionary organization?

ANSWER: It developed historically that the organization's leaders are: RUMYANTSEV V., LEVIN V., MANDELSHTAM S., TOLMAZOV A., and myself — KOTOLYNOV I. I. —; the organization's members gathered around these people.

I. KOTOLYNOV.

Interrogated by: KORKIN.

Certified true:

RGASPI, fond 671, inventory 1, file 114, leaf 183.
