INTERROGATION RECORD

Of KHARITONOV Moisey (Mikhail) Markovich

December 26, 1934.

Question: The investigation has information about your participation in a meeting held in early 1929 at ZINOVIEV's apartment, at which a line of conduct in relations with the Right was worked out. What can you testify about this meeting?

Answer: I do not remember this meeting, but admit that such a meeting took place and that I might have participated in it. Personally, I was against negotiations with the Right, as I repeatedly told ZINOVIEV. Possibly this is the meeting about which I testified on December 24, when ZINOVIEV reported on KAMENEV's negotiations with BUKHARIN and TOMSKY.

Question: The investigation knows that you participated in a meeting at ZINOVIEV's apartment in February 1929 during discussion of the creation of the Leningrad center of the Zinovievist organization. What can you testify about this?

Such a meeting and my participation in it could quite possibly have taken place, but I do not now remember. I recall that someone reported that in Leningrad the “Leftists,” the so-called “leaderless,” had united with ZINOVIEV's supporters and were carrying out anti-Party work together. ZINOVIEV was pleased with this union; he linked it directly with the question of disagreements in the Central Committee and his calculations of returning to the Party leadership.

In addition to my previous testimony about meetings with ZINOVIEV, I testify:

In the summer of 1932, I was at ZINOVIEV's and KAMENEV's dacha in Ilyinskoye. GERTSBERG was there at the time. KAMENEV was in Moscow.

There were no political conversations between us at that time.

Whether GERTSBERG held political conversations with ZINOVIEV, I do not know, since for some time they walked alone together. Possibly at this meeting I spoke with ZINOVIEV about my impending appointment to work abroad. I related that I had been offered work in Italy but refused, and that I was now being offered work as deputy trade representative in London and was thinking of accepting this offer. ZINOVIEV approved these intentions of mine.

In the summer of 1933, I was at BAKAEV's and KOSTINA's apartment; there were no political conversations between us. —

KHARITONOV.

INTERROGATED BY THE HEAD OF THE 6TH SECTION OF THE SPO OF THE GUGB — KORKIN

Certified true: Churbanova
