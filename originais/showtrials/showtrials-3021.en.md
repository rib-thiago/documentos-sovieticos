STENOGRAPHIC TRANSCRIPT

INTERROGATION RECORD

OF NIKOLAEV Leonid Vasilyevich

14 December 1934.

QUESTION: In the interrogation records of 5 and 6 December you testified that you tried to establish contacts for counterrevolutionary work with consuls of the Latvian Consulate and subsequently the German and British consulates. Did you do this with the knowledge of the counterrevolutionary Zinovievist organization?

ANSWER: Yes, I approached the consulates to establish contacts with the knowledge of the counterrevolutionary Zinovievist organization, represented by SHATSKY and KOTOLYNOV. I discussed this with SHATSKY in August of this year and then with KOTOLYNOV in September.

SHATSKY indicated that it would be good to establish contact abroad through the Comintern apparatus; in response I put forward another proposal — the advisability of establishing contact with the consulates located in Leningrad. My proposal was supported by SHATSKY and KOTOLYNOV. We decided to interest the consulates by offering material on the Soviet Union's internal situation, expecting in return that the consulates would allow us to use their apparatus for contact abroad.

QUESTION: Did SHATSKY tell you for what purposes contacts abroad needed to be established?

ANSWER: SHATSKY did not develop this idea; I surmised that it concerned establishing contacts between organization members and certain elements abroad.

QUESTION: In your testimony you say that contacts with the consulates were not established. For what reasons was this contact not established?

ANSWER: I testified in detail in the interrogation records of 5 and 6 December about why I had not established contact with the consulates: with the Latvian Consulate — because I was frightened by the presence of a car at the consul's entrance which I believed belonged to the military authorities; with the German Consulate — because the consul with whom I spoke by telephone referred me to the Consulate-General in Moscow; with the British Consulate — because I failed to see the consul.

QUESTION: Did you discuss with SHATSKY and KOTOLYNOV exactly what material should be handed to the consulates in exchange for using their apparatus for contacts abroad?

ANSWER: Yes, we discussed it. We intended to hand the consulates material depicting the country's internal situation in an anti-Soviet spirit.

QUESTION: How did SHATSKY and KOTOLYNOV react to your report that you had failed to establish contacts with the consulates?

ANSWER: They thought that contact abroad would have to be established by some other means. Subsequently I did not return to this question in conversations with them.

Correctly dictated from my words and read by me.

L. NIKOLAEV.

INTERROGATED BY:

Deputy People's Commissar of Internal Affairs of the USSR (AGRANOV)

Head of the EKO, GUGB, NKVD of the USSR (MIRONOV)

ASSISTANT TO THE HEAD OF THE EKO, GUGB, NKVD OF THE USSR (DMITRIEV)
