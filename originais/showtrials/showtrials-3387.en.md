INTERROGATION RECORD

Of BAK, Pavel Aleksandrovich. —

December 22, 1934. —

BAK, Pavel Aleksandrovich, born in 1902, native of Leningrad; father of working-class social origin, died in July 1934, most recently headed the technical storeroom of the Vyborg District Soviet; mother, former worker at the Krasny Mayak factory, pensioner. Russian, citizen of the USSR. Leningrad, K. Marx Avenue, building 74, apartment 16; head of the organizational department of the LSPO. Unmarried. Close relatives: mother, Natalya Pavlovna BAK, aged 68, non-Party; brother Vasily, aged 42, member of the All-Union Communist Party (Bolsheviks) since 1917, works at the Lenemalier factory of the industrial cooperative system of Volodarsky District as head of the BRIZ. Without property. Completed parish school in 1913. Member of the All-Union Communist Party (Bolsheviks) since 1920, Party card no. 0159255. Adhered to the Zinovievist opposition in 1925. No Party disciplinary penalties. Joined the Komsomol in 1918. That same year was secretary of the Komsomol collective at the Nevka factory in Leningrad. From that time I successively held a number of responsible Komsomol and Party positions until 1926. From late 1926 until arrest, in responsible work and a member of Party collective bureaus. No convictions. Exempted from military service on grounds of illness. Did not serve with the Whites.

QUESTION: Name the Zinovievists with whom you maintain contact.

ANSWER: My connections with Zinovievists have mainly been preserved with Vladimir RUMYANTSEV, Andrei TOLMAZOV, Nikolai TSARKOV, Grigory POTASHNIKOV, Mikhail MURAVYOV and Nikolai ANTONOV.

QUESTION: Describe in detail the nature of the connections between the Zinovievists.

ANSWER: Contact between the Zinovievists did not cease until recently. Our meetings had the character of organizational connections, and the interest we showed in one another at meetings had the character of gathering the old Zinovievist cadres.

I know that former Zinovievists, still under the influence of their education in the Zinovievist underground, have preserved their sharply hostile attitude toward the present leadership of the All-Union Communist Party (Bolsheviks). The most irreconcilable among them are Nikolai TSARKOV, Grigory POTASHNIKOV and Vladimir RUMYANTSEV, who rallied the old Zinovievist cadres around himself and provided them with material support. In particular, such support was also given to me in July 1934.

QUESTION: The investigation has information that you are a member of the Zinovievist counterrevolutionary organization. Tell us about your activities in this organization.

ANSWER: Yes, I am a member of the Zinovievist counterrevolutionary organization. As I have already said above, organizational connections among us Zinovievists did not cease until recently. I repeat: I actively maintained these connections, actively participating in building the counterrevolutionary organization. —

Recorded from my words and read by me, in confirmation of which I sign. —

BAK. —

INTERROGATED BY: Operational Officer of the 4th Section of the SPO, M. KOGAN. —

CERTIFIED CORRECT: M. Glazkov
