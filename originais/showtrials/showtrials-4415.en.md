INTERROGATION RECORD

Of ANISHEV, Anatoly Isaevich, dated January 11, 1935.

QUESTION: In the record of your interrogation of January 7 of the curr\<ent\> y\<ear\>, you testified about gatherings of members of the organization at BAKAEV's apartment in Moscow in 1933. What other gatherings of organization members in Moscow did you attend?

ANSWER: Apart from the gathering of organization members at BAKAEV's apartment in 1933, I also attended gatherings in 1932. This gathering likewise took place at BAKAEV's apartment. Those present were organization members BAKAEV, GERTIK, KOSTINA, NATANSON, myself and ANISHEV. Aleksandra SIMASHKO \[1\] (the wife of the Trotskyist UFIMTSEV) was also present. I often met her at BAKAEV's. I do not know whether SIMASHKO is a member of the organization, but in any event conversations were held openly in her presence. We considered her one of our own. I do not now remember precisely which questions were discussed at this gathering, but the general tone of the conversations was counterrevolutionary in character, as always. Someone else among the organization's members also attended this gathering. But precisely who, I do not remember. Also in 1932 I attended a gathering of members of the Zinovievist organization at G. FEDOROV's apartment near Sukharevskaya Square. Organization members present at this gathering were FEDOROV G., BAKAEV, GERTIK, myself—ANISHEV—NATANSON and KOSTINA.

QUESTION: What can you add to your previous testimony about the Moscow center's connection with members of the Leningrad organization?

ANSWER: After returning to Leningrad I repeatedly informed members of the Leningrad opposition, in particular RUMYANTSEV and KOTOLYNOV, about Moscow “news,” attitudes and directives originating with members of the Moscow center: BAKAEV, GERTIK, KUKLIN. Overall, this information which I conveyed to the named members of the Leningrad organization was intended to sustain their counterrevolutionary attitudes and inflame hatred toward the party leaders.

QUESTION: In your previous testimony you spoke of GERTIK's and ELKOVICH's visits to Leningrad to maintain contact with the organization. Who else among the leading figures of the Moscow Zinovievist leadership came to Leningrad?

ANSWER: Besides GERTIK's and ELKOVICH's visits to Leningrad to maintain contact with members of the Leningrad counterrevolutionary Zinovievist organization, I know of G. FEDOROV's visit to Leningrad in 1932; he is one of the leading figures (and possibly also a center member) of the Zinovievist organization. During this visit by FEDOROV, NATANSON and I visited him. I do not know which other organization members FEDOROV met.

Recorded correctly from my words and read by me.

ANISHEV.

INTERROGATED BY:

Head of the 1st Section of the SPO — LULOV.

Certified correct: Khvatov

RGASPI F. 671, Op. 1, D. 134, fols. 57–58.

\[1\] Here and subsequently in the text, erroneously: “Semashko.”
