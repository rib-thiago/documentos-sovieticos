INTERROGATION RECORD

OF BOGOMOLNY Yakov Isaakovich, 31 December 1934.

On the investigation's question about VISNEVSKAYA I testify: I live in the same apartment with her, in separate rooms. I have had no contact with her for the past three years. I remember that in 1928, when I arrived in Leningrad from Ashkhabad, several months after the Fifteenth Party Congress, VISNEVSKAYA and KOSTRITSKY persuaded me one evening to subscribe to ZINOVIEV's statement and return to the party immediately at all costs. I objected, pointing out that I had not yet entirely freed myself of counterrevolutionary Trotskyist-Zinovievist views and could not deceive the party by submitting such statements. To characterize VISNEVSKAYA I also consider important the conversation, already reported to the investigation, with MANDELSHTAM after his release from arrest in late 1928, when he expressed dissatisfaction with the party leadership, pointing to what he called the harsh treatment of the opposition “leaders.” This counterrevolutionary remark by MANDELSHTAM, as far as I remember, met with no objection from VISNEVSKAYA, although she was a party member. In subsequent years, as far as I remember, KOSTRITSKY visited her. Which former oppositionists visited her besides KOSTRITSKY I do not know.

BOGOMOLNY.

INTERROGATED BY — KORKIN

Certified correct: A. Svetlova
