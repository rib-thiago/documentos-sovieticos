INTERROGATION RECORD

OF BAKAEV I. P., dated January 12, 1935.

QUESTION: Comrade KIROV's murder was committed by NIKOLAEV L. V., a former participant in the opposition and a participant in the Zinovievist organization that existed in Leningrad. What do you know about this matter?

ANSWER: I have no connection whatsoever with this vile act and knew nothing of its preparation. I did not know NIKOLAEV.

I consider that our entire organization bears grave moral and political responsibility for this act.

I consider this because our malicious attacks on the Party leadership and Comrade STALIN, which we cultivated over many years of struggle, because the currents of hostility and malice emanating from us, the individual members of the central group, and especially from ZINOVIEV, could not fail to be refracted in the hottest heads among our supporters as a call to extreme action; they could develop into terrorist sentiments.

Correctly recorded from my words. I have read the record.

I. BAKAEV.

INTERROGATED BY:

Assistant Head of the EKO of the GUGB of the NKVD of the USSR — DMITRIEV

Certified correct: Signature

Certified correct: Khvatov
