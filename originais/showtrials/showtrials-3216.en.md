INTERROGATION RECORD

OF MYASNIKOV, Nikolai Petrovich —

23 December 1934.

To clarify my testimony of 15/XII, I consider it necessary to inform the investigation that SOSITSKY, Lev Ilyich, was a member of the center of the organization of former participants in the Zinovievist-Trotskyist bloc in Leningrad. My testimony about SOSITSKY of 15/XII, in the formulation “SOSITSKY carried out leading work and undoubtedly also belonged to the center,” is explained by the fact that throughout the period of work in the organization until 1931 I had direct contact with SOSITSKY through work in the center of the organization of Zinovievists and personally knew him as a member of the center; during the period from 1931 until the most recent time, however, I did not have direct contact with him.

For these reasons I initially formulated my testimony about SOSITSKY's relationship to the center of the organization in that way.

During 1932–1934, while remaining a member of the center of our organization, SOSITSKY was directly connected with LEVIN V.

MYASNIKOV.

INTERROGATED BY:

DEPUTY HEAD OF THE SPO OF THE GUGB OF THE NKVD OF THE USSR — LYUSHKOV.

CERTIFIED TRUE: M. Glazkov
