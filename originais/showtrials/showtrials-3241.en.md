INTERROGATION RECORD

conducted by the Head of Section 1 of the SPO — LULOV, December 13, 1934.

VINOGRADOV, Vasily Alekseyevich (supplementary).

QUESTION: How was the counterrevolutionary organization of which you were a member structured?

ANSWER: In my previous testimony I described the composition of the Leningrad organization of former members of the Trotskyist-Zinovievist bloc. RUMYANTSEV, KOTOLYNOV, TOLMAZOV, and ZVEZDOV played the leading role in this organization. The Moscow center, which directed the Leningrad organization, included ZINOVIEV, BAKAEV, and EVDOKIMOV. Contact with the Moscow center was maintained chiefly through RUMYANTSEV. To consolidate the former oppositionists, we used the association of former Komsomol comrades-in-arms. This association provided legal cover for our organization. Of the members of our counterrevolutionary organization, the following belonged to the association: myself — VINOGRADOV, KHANIK, ZHESTYANNIKOV, FADEEV, SEREDOKHIN, and TOLMAZOV. This group constituted a branch of our organization within the association of former comrades-in-arms.

QUESTION: How did the members of your counterrevolutionary organization characterize the situation in the country and the Party?

ANSWER: In the course of our meetings and conversations, we assessed Party policy in a counterrevolutionary spirit in all decisive areas. We subjected the Party leadership and Comrade STALIN to particularly sharp attacks. All this brought about extremist sentiments against the Party leadership and, above all, against STALIN.

Recorded correctly from my words, read by me, and I sign in confirmation:

VINOGRADOV.

Interrogated by — LULOV.
