INTERROGATION RECORD

Of BASHKIROV, Alexander Fabianovich.

January 5, 1935.

(supplementary)

Question: The testimony of KUKLIN and MYASNIKOV establishes that in 1933 a meeting took place at LEVIN’s apartment, attended by LEVIN, V. SOSITSKY, MYASNIKOV, and you, BASHKIROV. At this meeting KUKLIN heard information from those present and instructed them to continue the work. What can you testify in this connection?

Answer: I have not seen KUKLIN for several years. I did not attend such a meeting and know nothing about it.

Question: GERTIK’s testimony establishes that in 1932 he visited your apartment. Inform the investigation about the character of this visit.

Answer: I confirm the fact of GERTIK’s visit to me in 1932. In addition, I saw GERTIK at LEVIN’s apartment at approximately the same time. I do not remember exactly the character of my conversation with GERTIK at my apartment. Perhaps I informed him about my position at the institute (I was then engaged in party work). I remember the conversation at LEVIN’s. The prospects of bringing ZINOVIEV into the party leadership were discussed. I have already testified about this.

Question: Sergei GESSEN’s testimony establishes that in November 1931, while in Leningrad, he visited LEVIN, Vladimir’s apartment, where you were also present. GESSEN testifies that questions of the country’s political situation were discussed there in a counterrevolutionary spirit. Do you confirm this?

Answer: In November 1931, as I have already testified, I did indeed attend S. GESSEN’s conversation at LEVIN’s apartment. The conversation took the form of a discussion of party policy in the spirit of Zinovievist positions. I cannot cite the facts precisely.

Recorded correctly from my words and read by me.

BASHKIROV

INTERROGATED BY:

HEAD OF THE 4TH SECTION OF THE SPO — KOGAN, L.

Certified correct: Churbanova
