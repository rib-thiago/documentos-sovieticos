TESTIMONY

Of the accused SHIRYAEV, Dmitry Nikitich.

December 27, 1934.

In addition to my previous testimony about the anti-Party and counterrevolutionary sentiments of G. E. EVDOKIMOV, with whom I was connected for more than seven years, I hereby report the following:

1\. Shortly after the Seventeenth Party Conference, I was present at EVDOKIMOV's apartment in Moscow during a conversation between EVDOKIMOV and GESSEN in which they touched on the Party conference resolutions concerning the tasks of the second five-year plan and criticized, in particular, the point in these resolutions dealing with the tasks of eliminating the remnants of capitalism in the economy and in people's consciousness and transforming all working people into conscious workers of a classless socialist society. Both EVDOKIMOV and GESSEN spoke of this task as unattainable within the second five-year plan, mocked this resolution, and, among other things, expressed themselves to the effect that it would be more appropriate to speak of eliminating consciousness in certain people; as far as I remember, this last expression belonged to GESSEN.

2\. In early 1934, I was present at EVDOKIMOV's apartment in Moscow during a conversation between EVDOKIMOV and GESSEN in which they discussed Comrade KAGANOVICH's speech concerning construction of the Moscow metro. Both were of the opinion that the metro was being built needlessly, that enormous sums of money and other resources were being wasted on it when the economy had more pressing needs.

Dm. SHIRYAEV.

INTERROGATED BY:

Authorized officer of the SPO of the GUGB of the USSR NKVD — ZAIKO.

Certified true: Polunina
