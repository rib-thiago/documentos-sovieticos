TESTIMONY

Of the accused VINOGRADOV, Vasily Alexeyevich.

January 5, 1935.

Question: Do you know KLADOVIKOV, and what do you know about him?

Answer: I remember that in 1924 KLADOVIKOV was engaged in active Komsomol work in the Vyborg district. Apparently he worked at the “Krasnaya Zarya” plant. In my opinion, the old Komsomol members of the Vyborg district should know him. I know nothing about KLADOVIKOV’s membership in the opposition.

Recorded correctly from my words. —

V. VINOGRADOV.

INTERROGATED BY — LULOV.

Certified correct: Churbanova

RGASPI, fond 671, inventory 1, file 127, folio 45.
