INTERROGATION RECORD

OF SOSITSKY, Lev Ilyich, dated 21/XII-34.

SOSITSKY, Lev Ilyich, born in 1899 (35 years old), from Leningrad, father a worker, Jewish, citizen of the USSR. Resides at 7th Krasnoarmeyskaya Street, house 9, room 6/9. Director of the Leningrad Soviet's Automobile Repair Plant. Family situation: Zoya Alexandrovna, 36, wife; Ilya Moiseevich, 62, father, worker, dependent on his son; Maria Lvovna, 60, mother, housewife; Valentina Ilyinichna VYDRINA, 35, sister, Klimov Lane, husband employed as a worker at a hat factory; Raisa Ilyinichna LEVINA, 31, sister, 7th Krasnoarmeyskaya, house 9, husband works in an artel; Elena Ilyinichna, 27, KOPILEVICH, 7th Krasnoarmeyskaya, housewife, husband works in an artel. All those listed are non-party; property status: had and have nothing. Elementary education; studies at the FOK attached to the Leningrad Institute of Municipal Construction Engineers. Member of the AUCP(b) since 1919; expelled from the party in 1927 for factional work, reinstated in mid-1928. From 1918 to 1919 in Soviet work; from 1919 to October 1926 in political work in the Red Army; from 1926 to 1927 in economic work; from 1927 to 1931 in production and economic work; since 1931 in economic work. No convictions. Military registration category in the reserve: KP-9. Did not serve with the Whites.

QUESTION: Which former participants in the so-called Zinovievist opposition do you know personally?

ANSWER: Among the former participants in the so-called Zinovievist opposition, I personally know LEVIN Vladimir, RUMYANTSEV V., MYASNIKOV N., SHLYAKOV Vasily, FEDOROV Georgy, KATAI Mikhail, TIMOFEEV Evgeny, ROMANOV Grigory, ALEXANDROV Alexander, POKHILAINEN Pavel, REM Mikhail. All live in Leningrad.

QUESTION: How often did you meet, and with whom among the former participants in the so-called Zinovievist opposition you have listed?

ANSWER: I frequently meet SHLYAKOV on official business; he is my immediate superior, deputy head of the Leningrad Soviet's Transport Administration; I see MYASNIKOV N. once every three or four months, encountering him on official business; I meet FEDOROV G. on official business, he is director of a bus depot; I meet LEVIN V. once every four or five months; before the October holidays in 1934 I saw him at the Smolny; in a domestic setting I met LEVIN once in September last year; KORSHUNOV Efim was also there, at LEVIN's apartment. LEVIN visited my apartment once in 1933 or early 1934. He came with RUMYANTSEV and MANDELSHTAM, since all four of us were returning from the funeral of SHURYGIN, a former participant in the Zinovievist opposition. They all stayed briefly, drank vodka, and left. I last saw RUMYANTSEV in February 1934 at a party activists' meeting, and from there went with him to my home for my birthday. MYASNIKOV came with us as well. That evening MININ, Ivan Vasilyevich, chairman of the Special Collegium of the Regional Court, and IVANOV, Georgy Ilyich, party organizer on the Finland railway section, were at my home. ROMANOV came to my home in March 1934 when I was ill. I have not seen REM for two or three years. KATAI works at my factory; POKHILAINEN works at my factory, dismissed in late November for absenteeism. I met TIMOFEEV in March 1934 at a party activists' meeting. I must add that I also personally know MANDELSHTAM S., but have not seen him for over three years.

I also state that MYASNIKOV, FEDOROV, and SHLYAKOV visited my apartment during the October holidays.

QUESTION: How would you characterize the political sentiments of V. LEVIN, MYASNIKOV, SHLYAKOV, RUMYANTSEV, FEDOROV, and MANDELSHTAM?

ANSWER: From conversations with these comrades and my personal impressions, I believe their political sentiments and all their work characterize them as steadfast members of the AUCP(b). I know FEDOROV little. Although I have not seen MANDELSHTAM for a long time, I think he works honestly, since even when I met him in 1929 he had completely overcome his anti-party views.

SOSITSKY.

INTERROGATED BY:

DEPUTY HEAD OF THE SPO OF THE GUGB — LYUSHKOV.
