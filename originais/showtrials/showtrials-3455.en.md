INTERROGATION RECORD

Of TARASOV Ivan Ivanovich — December 26, 1934.

I have recalled and consider it necessary to inform the investigation of the following fact: in 1927, while working in Samara, I had a conversation with a worker at some printing house, LIBIN, whose first name and patronymic I do not know, and while discussing opposition questions he said: “We must act with weapons in our hands.” I informed AZOVSKY, who was then the leader of the Samara group of oppositionists, about these sentiments of LIBIN.

Possibly I spoke about this to Sergey MANDELSHTAM, who was then working in Samara. I have not seen LIBIN since 1927 and do not know where he is. LIBIN was a member of the All-Union Communist Party (Bolsheviks) and was then between 25 and 37 years old.

I. TARASOV

INTERROGATED BY THE HEAD OF THE 6TH SECTION OF THE SPO OF THE GUGB — KORKIN

Certified true:

RGASPI, fond 671, inventory 1, file 121, leaf 227.
