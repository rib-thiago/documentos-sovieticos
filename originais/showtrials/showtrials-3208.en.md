INTERROGATION RECORD

OF RUMYANTSEV Vladimir Vasilyevich

22 December 1934.

QUESTION: Did you have a meeting with ANTONOV and FADEEV in August 1934? Where precisely, and what was its character?

ANSWER: I confirm that in August 1934, possibly at my apartment, possibly in my office at the Vyborg District Soviet, I met with ANTONOV and FADEEV. The three of us gathered on instructions from VRUBLEVSKY, secretary of the Vyborg District Committee of the Komsomol, to draw up lists of old Komsomol members.

QUESTION: ANTONOV's testimony establishes that, while recounting to him and FADEEV the contents of the book by AGABEKOV, who had fled the USSR, you touched upon the question of war against the USSR. All three of you, that is, you, ANTONOV, and FADEEV, concluded that, should war break out, the current leadership of the AUCP(b) would fail to cope with the tasks that arose, and that KAMENEV's and ZINOVIEV's arrival at the leadership of the country would be inevitable. The intention was to hasten war in order to use it to replace the leadership of the AUCP(b) and the Soviet government. Do you confirm this?

ANSWER: I allow that a conversation about AGABEKOV's book with ANTONOV and FADEEV may have taken place during this meeting. I deny that I spoke with them about war, the replacement of the leadership of the AUCP(b) and the Soviet government, and the return of ZINOVIEV and KAMENEV to the leadership.

V. RUMYANTSEV.

INTERROGATED BY:

DEPUTY HEAD OF THE SPO OF THE GUGB OF THE NKVD OF THE USSR — LYUSHKOV.

Certified true: A. Svetlova
