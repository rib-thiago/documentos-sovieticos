CONFRONTATION RECORD

Between the accused TARASOV, Ivan Ivanovich, and

RUMYANTSEV, Vladimir Vasilyevich. —

Conducted on December 22, 1934.

The accused TARASOV and RUMYANTSEV, summoned for confrontation, after confirming that they knew one another personally, testified:

QUESTION TO TARASOV: Describe your conversation with Vl. RUMYANTSEV when you met him in Leningrad in 1932.

ANSWER: In 1932 in Leningrad, at RUMYANTSEV's apartment, RUMYANTSEV told me and I. I. KOTOLYNOV: “The country is in a difficult situation. The Party leadership sees no way out of this situation. STALIN is leading the country toward becoming embroiled in war, proceeding from the position that it is better to perish in war with the bourgeoisie than as a result of the failure of domestic policy, which results from incorrect leadership.” The meaning of RUMYANTSEV's statement was that “STALIN is leading the proletarian revolution to ruin.”

QUESTION TO RUMYANTSEV: Do you confirm this testimony by TARASOV?

ANSWER BY RUMYANTSEV: The meeting did indeed take place at my apartment in the presence of I. I. KOTOLYNOV, Viktor USHAKOV, who works in the People's Commissariat of Heavy Industry, and my wife Aleksandra STEPANOVA. A conversation about economic difficulties, chiefly workers' supplies, did indeed take place in the spirit described by TARASOV. As for a conversation about unleashing a war that would lead to the destruction of the proletarian revolution, I deny it.

QUESTION TO TARASOV: Do you confirm that during your conversation with RUMYANTSEV, besides KOTOLYNOV, USHAKOV and STEPANOVA were present?

ANSWER BY TARASOV: I do not know USHAKOV and have absolutely no recollection of his being present during this conversation. STEPANOVA was at home but periodically left the room; therefore she evidently may not have heard the conversation with RUMYANTSEV about which I testified.

RUMYANTSEV.                                      TARASOV.

The confrontation was conducted by:

DEPUTY HEAD OF THE SPO OF THE GUGB OF THE NKVD OF THE USSR, LYUSHKOV.

HEAD OF THE 6TH SECTION OF THE SPO OF THE GUGB, KORKIN.
