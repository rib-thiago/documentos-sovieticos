INTERROGATION RECORD

Of EVDOKIMOV Grigory Yeremeevich (supplementary)

December 29, 1934.

Question: The investigation has established that you maintained contact with SAKHOV B. N. Describe SAKHOV's political sentiments and his conversations with you.

Answer: SAKHOV's political sentiments are the same as those of the other Zinovievists surrounding ZINOVIEV and KAMENEV, about whom I testified earlier.

He met with me, KUKLIN, NAUMOV, BRAVO, SHAROV, and BAKAEV. SAKHOV approached me several times with a request to ask ZINOVIEV whether the latter could receive him for “heart-to-heart” conversations.

I know that SAKHOV, without my assistance and on his own initiative, visited ZINOVIEV. As far as I remember, he called on ZINOVIEV in order to inform him about the Ryutin case. If SAKHOV did not inform ZINOVIEV about the Ryutin case, then in any event it was about a matter directly concerning ZINOVIEV that SAKHOV knew about through his work in the Procuracy.

Some Zinovievists, and especially ZINOVIEV himself, distrusted SAKHOV as a person unable to hold his tongue; ZINOVIEV also came to suspect that SAKHOV might be a GPU informer.

At the time before his departure for Arkhangelsk, SAKHOV informed me and other Zinovievists about the case of Iuda SHTERN \[1\], as far as I remember, to the effect that this act was individual and that attempts were being made to turn it into a case involving an organization.

In 1934, SAKHOV also spoke about the results of his official trips: about the degeneration of the leading Party elite in the East Siberian Territory and about the intolerable conditions created in the Northern Territory for prisoners serving terms of forced labor.

Concerning the Party bodies' proposal that he leave to work in the provinces, SAKHOV said that this was not dictated by necessity, since he was doing much useful work in Moscow, but resulted from his having been an oppositionist in the past.

EVDOKIMOV.

Interrogated by the ASSISTANT HEAD OF THE SPO OF THE GUGB OF THE NKVD — RUTKOVSKY.

Certified true: Biryukov

RGASPI, fond 671, inventory 1, file 126, leaves 131–132.

\[1\] Iuda Mironovich Shtern — a participant in the 1932 attempt on the life of von Twardowski, counselor at the German embassy in Moscow.
