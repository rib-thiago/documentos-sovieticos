INTERROGATION RECORD

Conducted on December 21, 1934, by L. Kogan, Head of the 4th Section of the SPO.

Of citizen TSARKOV, Nikolai Alekseevich.

QUESTION: What can you report to the investigation in addition to your testimony about instances of the organization's counterrevolutionary activities?

ANSWER: I have already testified that in 1930 I left Leningrad for the Volkhov aluminum combine and then the Tikhvin Aluminstroi. In 1933 I returned to Leningrad and remained here until February 1934; from February to November 1934 I again returned to Tikhvin. Thus for several years I was cut off from the organization, received no information and can report nothing to the investigation. It is true that in 1933 RUMYANTSEV invited me to call at his home for a conversation, but for reasons I do not remember I did not go. Also in 1933 I saw FADEEV, ANTONOV and VINOGRADOV relatively often. There were no Zinovievists at the Tikhvin combine. In October 1934 I met the secretary of the Svirstroi Party Committee, the Zinovievist IZRAILITIN, who told me in detail that ZINOVIEV had been removed from the editorial board of Bolshevik. IZRAILITIN was pleased about this circumstance.

I returned to Leningrad in November 1934. Before my arrest I managed to visit V. RUMYANTSEV several times. I was busy arranging my personal affairs, and RUMYANTSEV and KULESHEV, about whom I testified, helped me with this.

QUESTION: What do you know about your organization's terrorist activities?

ANSWER: I have already testified on this question too. I confirm that for a number of years hatred toward the Party leaders was cultivated within the organization, and the question of methods of removing STALIN from leadership was raised and discussed. On this soil grew the terrorist sentiments that led to the murder of one of the Party leaders, KIROV.

Recorded correctly from my words, in confirmation of which I sign.

TSARKOV.

INTERROGATED BY: KOGAN

Certified correct: A. Svetlova
