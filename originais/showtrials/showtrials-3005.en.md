INTERROGATION RECORD

OF NIKOLAEV, L. V. — dated December 13, 1934.

QUESTION: Which former Zinovievist oppositionists belonged to the counterrevolutionary organization of which you were a member?

ANSWER: Expanding on my previous testimony, I state that the organization included the following former oppositionists: KOTOLYNOV, RUMYANTSEV, SHATSKY, ZVEZDOV, ANTONOV, SOKOLOV, YUSKIN, KHANIK \[1\] Lev, TOLMAZOV, ALEKSANDROV, POPOV G., TSARKOV, and a number of other persons. There were two terrorist groups in the organization. One group was headed by KOTOLYNOV and included: myself — NIKOLAEV Leonid, ZVEZDOV Vasily, ANTONOV Nikolai, SOKOLOV Georgy, and YUSKIN. The other group was led by SHATSKY Nikolai and included RUMYANTSEV, POPOV Georgy, and TSARKOV N. \[2\]

QUESTION: What work did each of the terrorist groups carry out?

ANSWER: KOTOLYNOV's group was preparing a terrorist attack on KIROV, and its actual execution was entrusted to me personally, which I carried out on December 1, 1934.

I know from SHATSKY that the same assignment had been given to his group, which carried out this work independently of our preparations for the terrorist attack. I know from KOTOLYNOV that his group was preparing a terrorist attack on STALIN, using the connections it had in Moscow. As I stated in the interrogation record of December 6, 1934, SHATSKY, and apparently his group as well, was also preparing a terrorist attack on STALIN.

Thus each group had specific terrorist assignments, carrying them out independently of the other, although their immediate objectives often coincided. This can be seen from the preparations for KIROV's murder, which I prepared mainly in the Smolny area, while SHATSKY prepared it chiefly around KIROV's apartment.

QUESTION: What role in preparing and carrying out the terrorist attack on KIROV was played by the members you named of the terrorist group headed by KOTOLYNOV?

ANSWER: KOTOLYNOV — worked out the method of carrying out the attack directly with me, approved that method, and specifically ascertained how accurately I could shoot; he was my immediate leader in carrying out the attack.

SOKOLOV — assessed how suitable particular points along KIROV's usual route were, thereby facilitating my work. SOKOLOV ascertained KIROV's routes before the October celebrations of 1934.

YUSKIN — knew of the preparations for the attack on KIROV and worked out with me the option of an attack at Smolny. ZVEZDOV and ANTONOV — knew of the preparations for the attack; I can say nothing about their part in those preparations, since I had no contact with them in carrying them out; they were directly connected with KOTOLYNOV, from whom I know that they were used to gather information about Smolny.

QUESTION: What work did the terrorist groups carry out in preparing the terrorist attack on Comrade STALIN?

ANSWER: Both groups carried out this work, as stated above. SHATSKY worked using RUMYANTSEV's connections and position; KOTOLYNOV himself was to go to Moscow to prepare the attack, taking me with him; we intended to use YUSKIN, who had connections in Moscow, where he regularly traveled on business; I intended to use Pavel TITOV, who lives in Moscow. We intended to carry out the attack some time after KIROV's murder.

Recorded correctly from my words. The record has been read aloud to me —

L. NIKOLAEV

DEPUTY PEOPLE'S COMMISSAR FOR INTERNAL AFFAIRS OF THE USSR — AGRANOV
