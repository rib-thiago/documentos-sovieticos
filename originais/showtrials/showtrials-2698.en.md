INTERROGATION RECORD

OF NIKOLAEV, L. V.

dated 9/XII/1934.

QUESTION: Your testimony of 8/XII on preparing the terrorist act ended with the episode of 15/X, when you were detained. How did events develop afterwards?

ANSWER: SVESHNIKOV, KIROV's secretary, learned of my detention on 15 October. I sensed this from the wariness with which he received me. This is how I explain why, despite repeated attempts to obtain an audience with KIROV, he refused to receive me. On 4 and 5 November I saw KIROV in a car near his apartment; I still had not chosen the moment for the attempt. After those days I energetically sought KIROV, intending to attack.

On 14 November 1934 I was at Moscow Station, hoping to meet KIROV.

QUESTION: From whom did you learn that KIROV was to be at the station on 14 November?

ANSWER: I kept the apartment under observation; since KIROV did not appear and no cars came for him, I decided he had gone out of town. I decided he was in Moscow, since he had recently been on a business trip; I knew this from the newspapers. I began going to the station approximately every other day, hoping to meet him. Thus I came to be at the station on 14 November. I arrived when KIROV and those meeting him were walking from the carriage towards the cars waiting outside the station. I did not fire because I arrived in a rush and had no time to summon the determination to shoot.

After KIROV's arrival I watched his apartment for about two days, then had to stop because I needed to deal with various household affairs that took up much time.

QUESTION: When did you resume surveillance?

ANSWER: I resumed it on 29 November. I read in the newspapers that the VKP(b) Central Committee plenum had ended on 28 November and decided that KIROV should arrive the following day. I therefore went to Moscow Station on 29 November but did not meet KIROV; I thought either he had returned to Leningrad before the 29th or would return the next day.

30 November was a day off; I stayed home all day and went to the cinema in the evening.

QUESTION: How did you spend 1 December up to the moment of the murder?

ANSWER: That day a meeting of the active members was to discuss the results of the plenum. I twice telephoned my wife at work and asked her to get tickets for the meeting. By one in the afternoon I had established that she could not get a ticket, so after one I went to the Smolny District Party Committee on 25 October Prospekt, where I asked Committee employees GURYANOV and ORLOV for a ticket to the meeting. GURYANOV refused, while ORLOV promised one and suggested I collect it at the end of the day.

To be on the safe side, I decided to go to Smolny and try to get a ticket there through acquaintances on the City and Regional Committee staff. From 1.30 to 2.30 p.m. I was in the Smolny building. I had the Nagant with me. I met employees DENISOVA, SHITIK, SMIRNOV, LARIN and PETROSHEVICH and asked them all for a ticket. Only PETROSHEVICH promised a ticket, but only at the end of the day. While waiting, I decided to walk near Smolny; I thought I was most likely to obtain a ticket from PETROSHEVICH. After an hour I went back into Smolny, entered the lavatory and, on coming out, saw KIROV heading towards his office; this was on the building's third floor, at approximately 4.30 p.m.

There was no one within 40 paces behind KIROV, and I followed him. When he turned the corner in the corridor, I approached to within about five paces and shot him in the back of the head; the corridor was well lit by electric light, and the spot at which I aimed was clearly visible.

Correctly taken down from my words. I have read the record.

L. NIKOLAEV

INTERROGATED BY:

HEAD OF THE EKO, GUGB, NKVD — MIRONOV

ASSISTANT HEAD OF THE EKO, GUGB, NKVD OF THE USSR — DMITRIEV
