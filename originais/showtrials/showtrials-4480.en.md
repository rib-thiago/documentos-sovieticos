RECORD

OF THE ADDITIONAL INTERROGATION of the accused GORSHENIN, Ivan Stepanovich

14 January 1935.

In reply to the investigation's question concerning the role of L. B. KAMENEV in the Moscow center of the counterrevolutionary Zinovievist organization, I report:

I know definitely that L. B. KAMENEV, from the period following the Fifteenth Congress of the AUCP(b) until the day our organization was liquidated (December 1934), was a member of the Moscow Zinovievist center and the second leader of the entire organization after ZINOVIEV.

In developing and directing our organization's underground activities, KAMENEV, like ZINOVIEV, played a leading role.

Until late 1932, that is, until his exile from Moscow in connection with the Ryutin group case, KAMENEV's apartments in Karmanitsky and Manezhny lanes served as venues for underground gatherings and meetings of members of the Moscow center.

These gatherings, in which ZINOVIEV, he, KAMENEV, EVDOKIMOV, BAKAEV, SHAROV, GERTIK, and I, GORSHENIN, participated, were more or less frequent; for reasons of secrecy, they took place at different times and with varying participants.

At these gatherings, or rather conferences of members of the organization's center, current political events within the USSR and abroad and the principal decisions of the Central Committee of the AUCP(b) were discussed. The exchange of opinions on all these issues took place in an anti-party spirit, and in his speeches L. B. KAMENEV gave the harshest assessments of the Central Committee's policies and measures, particularly regarding the pace of collectivization and the direction of heavy industry's development.

KAMENEV frequently expressed, in one form or another, the idea that the Central Committee's policy under STALIN's leadership endangered the alliance between town and countryside and that this policy would result in the collapse of the present party leadership. All these statements of his were accompanied by constant malicious personal attacks on the party's leaders and principally Comrade STALIN.

KAMENEV concerned himself extensively with the country's economic situation, on which I, GORSHENIN, KAPITONOV, and LEIKIN kept him informed. We supplied him with the principal information, including secret information, concerning the country's economic life and the party's economic policy.

I remember that when I was intending to leave Gosplan, KAMENEV advised me against doing so, since, as he put it, from Gosplan “one can see the country's entire economy”.

In 1932, at the time of the country's greatest difficulties, when ZINOVIEV, undoubtedly with KAMENEV's knowledge, was negotiating with SAFAROV and STEN, and through them with RYUTIN's group and the Trotskyists, Kamenev, at another gathering at my apartment attended by BAKAEV, KAPITONOV, KAREV, LEIKIN, GERTIK, KOSTINA, and others, about 12 people in all, made sharp attacks on the party leadership, particularly emphasizing the stagnation allegedly prevailing in the country in the study of Marxism-Leninism and on the theoretical front generally.

According to his characterization, it appeared that the ideological and cultural front generally was, as a result of the current party leadership, in a state of “abomination and desolation”.

Incidentally, I organized this gathering at his, KAMENEV's, suggestion.

After returning from exile in 1933, KAMENEV exercised great caution and therefore, just like ZINOVIEV, did not attend conferences of members of the organization's center. From my conversations with ZINOVIEV, however, I know definitely that he, KAMENEV, remained closely connected with ZINOVIEV until very recently and that his role in the organization continued to be a leading one. I also know from conversations with ZINOVIEV that there was not a single fact of any political significance concerning the position of our organization on which they did not exchange opinions.

At the conference of the organization's center in August 1934, held in KAMENEV's study at his dacha (Ilyinskoye station, Nizhny Novgorod railway), attended by ZINOVIEV, KAMENEV, EVDOKIMOV, and myself, GORSHENIN, during discussion of the question of removing ZINOVIEV from the editorial board of the journal “BOLSHEVIK”, I personally became convinced once again that KAMENEV, as before, was hostile toward the party leadership.

Then, as before, KAMENEV spoke against STALIN with pronounced hostility.

The record has been written correctly from my words and read by me.

GORSHENIN.

INTERROGATED BY: DEPUTY HEAD OF THE SPO OF THE UGB — STROMIN.

Certified correct: Kazakova
