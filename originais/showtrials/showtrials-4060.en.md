INTERROGATION RECORD

Of YAKOVLEV, Moisei Nakhimovich, dated January 4, 1935.

Question: Tell the investigation what you know about the activities of the counterrevolutionary Zinovievist organization.

Answer: KIROV’s murder made clear even to me, sunk in the counterrevolutionary swamp, the old Leninist proposition that the logic of factional struggle leads to outright counterrevolution and fascism.

The murderers of S. M. KIROV, who emerged from the counterrevolutionary Zinovievist underground, were educated by us in a spirit of hostility and hatred toward the party leadership and the leader of the party, Comrade STALIN.

The entire counterrevolutionary Zinovievist organization bears political responsibility for the fascist murder, and I in particular, as one of the active participants in this organization.

After the 15th Congress I returned to the party for tactical maneuvering, without overcoming my disagreements with the party or abandoning counterrevolutionary struggle.

From the 15th Congress onward I headed the counterrevolutionary group of the Vasilyevsky Ostrov district, which existed as an active group until the beginning of 1929 and in a curtailed form until the end of 1934.

Over the past two years the group’s activities consisted of maintaining personal ties, spreading counterrevolutionary gossip against the party leadership, and gathering active members of this organization at SHEINBERG’s funeral.

The organization’s membership: FOKIN, SEMYONOV, S., KONDRATYEVA, Yevdokia, SEMYONOV, I., PANKO, SHEINBERG, and KASPERSKY.

The group’s membership changed little compared with 1929; only NOTMAN withdrew. The group had no contact with the Leningrad Zinovievist center, although it was Zinovievist in its basic positions. In addition, I met GORBACHEV, a longstanding active Zinovievist.

Written in my own hand.

M. YAKOVLEV.

INTERROGATED BY:

OPERATIONS OFFICER OF THE SPO OF THE GUGB — FEDOROV.

Certified correct: Churbanova
