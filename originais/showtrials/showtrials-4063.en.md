INTERROGATION RECORD

Conducted by the head of the 4th Section of the SPO, KOGAN, L., on January 4, 1935.

ALBANSKY, Dmitry Pavlovich, born in 1899, father a worker, now retired, native of Kazan, Russian, citizen of the USSR, residing in Leningrad, 27 Kirov Avenue, apartment 1, party organizer of the 9th carriage section of the Vitebsk passenger station of the October Railway; wife: Maria Tikhonovna ALBANSKAYA, non-party, housewife; daughter aged four; brothers: Sergei, aged 32, a worker in a confectionery factory in Leningrad, 7th Krasnoarmeiskaya, non-party; Pyotr, aged 35, Kokand, worker; sister: Yekaterina MAMUSHKINA, aged 22, does not work, 29 Kirov Avenue, apartment 4; parents live with me in the same apartment; without property; at one time his father had an iron trading business. Studied for two years at the Institute of the Civil Air Fleet (1931–33), member of the All-Union Communist Party (Bolsheviks) since 1919. In 1925, as a member of the Komsomol Provincial Committee in Leningrad, dissociated himself from the Provincial Committee’s anti-party line twenty days after the 14th Congress. No party penalties. From 1924 to 1925 a member of the Komsomol Provincial Committee in Leningrad; no criminal convictions; registered for military service at the Volodarsky District Military Commissariat, category KP-8; did not serve with the Whites.

Question: With which Zinovievists did you maintain contacts until the most recent period?

Answer: I maintained contacts with the following Zinovievists: RUMYANTSEV, Vladimir, TOLMAZOV, A., KHANIK, Lev, ZHESTYANNIKOV, I. G., BURMISTROV, P. P., SOROKIN, V. A., LUKIN, V., and VASILYEVA, Raisa. I visited the homes of some of those named, RUMYANTSEV, Vladimir, and LUKIN, V. TARASOV, Pyotr, who came to Leningrad, apparently from Kuznetskstroy, visited my home. All are former participants in the Trotskyist-Zinovievist bloc.

In 1933 RUMYANTSEV proposed that I transfer to work at the Vyborg District Soviet. I declined his proposal. I attended meetings of the “Combat Fellowship” attached to Istomol and was a member of that fellowship. I was invited by notice and, as a participant in the Civil War and an old Leningrad Komsomol member, was enrolled in the “Combat Fellowship.” I attended approximately five meetings. The last time was November 6, 1934. There I saw PANOV, ORLOV, Vasily, TOLMAZOV, Andrei, and others whom I do not remember.

Recorded correctly from my words and read by me.

ALBANSKY.

INTERROGATED BY — KOGAN, L.

Certified correct: Churbanova
