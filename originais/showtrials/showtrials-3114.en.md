SUPPLEMENTARY INTERROGATION RECORD

OF LEVIN, Vladimir Solomonovich,

Dated December 16, 1934.

QUESTION: Tell us who informed you that the GPU possessed material concerning your counterrevolutionary work.

ANSWER: In February or March 1933, Vladimir RUMYANTSEV informed me that a GPU information bulletin stated that Zinovievists were gathering around me and Vladimir RUMYANTSEV. RUMYANTSEV told me that he had learned this from GRANSKY, the former authorized officer for the Vyborg district. According to RUMYANTSEV, GRANSKY had been transferred to work in Moscow.

Recorded correctly from my words; read aloud to me —

LEVIN.

INTERROGATED BY:

HEAD OF SECTION 1 OF THE SPO OF THE GUGB — PETROVSKY. —

Certified true: illegible
