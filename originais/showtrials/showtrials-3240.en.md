INTERROGATION RECORD

December 12, 1934, Head of Section 1 of the SPO — LULOV.

VINOGRADOV Vasily Alekseyevich, born in 1904, a native of Nekouz district, Ivanovo Industrial Region, Russian, a USSR citizen; father — a shop assistant; resides at Kondratyevsky Avenue, block 9, building No. 40, apartment 35; shop head at Factory No. 4; married, with a wife and an eight-year-old son; education — a four-class urban school; a Party member since 1923, expelled from the Party in 1927 as an active member of the Trotskyist-Zinovievist counterrevolutionary bloc; in 1925–1926, head of the Organization Department of the Vyborg District Komsomol Committee; assigned to military training outside troop units on health grounds.

QUESTION: What do you know about the existence in Leningrad of a counterrevolutionary group of former members of the Trotskyist-Zinovievist bloc?

ANSWER: A counterrevolutionary group of former members of the Trotskyist-Zinovievist bloc has survived and continues to exist in Leningrad. This group includes the following persons: 1) myself — VINOGRADOV, 2) KHANIK L., 3) ANTONOV, 4) NIKANOROV, 5) ZVEZDOV, 6) KOTOLYNOV, 7) RUMYANTSEV, 8) TOLMAZOV, 9) SHATSKY, 10) ZEIT, 11) NADEL. SOKOLOV G. is closely associated with this group.

QUESTION: How was this group put together?

ANSWER: After our expulsion from the Party and our subsequent declared withdrawal, the group's members began concentrating in Leningrad again. On the basis of a directive from ZINOVIEV, personally conveyed to me by ELKOVICH Yakov before my departure for Leningrad, we began restoring old connections and rallying the forces of the former oppositionists, leading to the reestablishment of this group.

QUESTION: On what platform did the group's members unite?

ANSWER: The group united essentially on the platform of the Zinovievist-Trotskyist bloc.

QUESTION: Which members of the group maintained contact with the Moscow leaders of the counterrevolutionary Trotskyists and Zinovievists?

ANSWER: Contact with Moscow was maintained mainly through RUMYANTSEV, who received directives from ZINOVIEV and KAMENEV.

Recorded correctly from my words.

VINOGRADOV.

INTERROGATED BY: HEAD OF SECTION 2 OF THE SPO — LULOV
