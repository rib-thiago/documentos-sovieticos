INTERROGATION RECORD

dated January 12, 1935, conducted by the Head of the 1st Section of the SPO of the GUGB of the NVKD of the USSR — PETROVSKY.

MIRONOV Vladimir Davydovich, born in 1898, native of the city of Vinnitsa, Vinnitsa Region, Ukrainian SSR, residing in Moscow, N. Basmannaya Street, No. 14, apartment 40, Jewish, citizen of the USSR. Passport — Moscow militia administration; head of the political department of the Moscow-Kazan Railway; father a worker. Graduated from a commercial school. Until 1918, in the first year of the polytechnic institute in Odessa; father MAIDELMAN, David Meyerovich — pensioner; wife Zinaida Antonovna PRISHCHEPCHIK — secretary of the district Party committee of Egoryevsk District, Moscow Region; brothers: Leon Davidovich MAIDELMAN — works in Rostov at Tekstiltrest; El Davydovich MAIDELMAN — resides in Leningrad, works at the Comrade Kirov factory; sister — Roza Davydovna MIRONOVA, works in Moscow at the “Frezer” factory; secondary education, member of the All-Union Communist Party (Bolsheviks) since 1918. Order of the Labor Banner of the Uzbek Republic. Military registration category K-10; in the Red Army from 1919 to 1920. Engaged in Party work since 1921.

QUESTION: With which Zinovievists and Trotskyists did you maintain ties?

ANSWER: From late 1927 to early 1928 I met BARANOV Sergei, who was then working in the Central Committee of the All-Union Communist Party (Bolsheviks). BARANOV belonged to the Trotskyists. I have known the Trotskyist UFIMTSEV N. since 1922, from our work together in the Crimea. UFIMTSEV N. joined the Trotskyists in 1923. In 1928, after UFIMTSEV returned from abroad, I met his wife SIMASHKO, who also belonged to the Trotskyists. UFIMTSEV and SIMASHKO were exiled for active counterrevolutionary Trotskyist work. I began maintaining ties with UFIMTSEV and SIMASHKO upon my transfer to work in Moscow in 1931, and maintained these ties with them until UFIMTSEV's arrest, that is, until early 1933.

In 1932, at UFIMTSEV's apartment, I met the Zinovievists BAKAEV and KOSTINA.

Correctly recorded from my words; the record was read to me.

MIRONOV.

INTERROGATED BY:

HEAD OF THE 1ST SECTION OF THE SPO OF THE GUGB OF THE NKVD — PETROVSKY

Certified correct: A. Svetlova
