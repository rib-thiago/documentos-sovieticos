INTERROGATION RECORD

OF YUSKIN, Ignaty Grigoryevich

18 December 1934.

QUESTION: Do you confirm your previous testimony, and of what do you plead guilty?

ANSWER: Confirming all my previous testimony and deeply repenting before the Soviet authorities and the Communist Party for the crime I committed, I plead guilty to having known, from NIKOLAEV's words, of the terrorist act being prepared against Comrade KIROV and to having, by that very fact, been an accomplice in the murder of Comrade KIROV, since I knew about it.

YUSKIN.

INTERROGATED BY:

DEPUTY HEAD OF THE SPO OF THE GUGB — LYUSHKOV.

OPERATIONAL OFFICER OF THE SPO OF THE GUGB — FEDOROV.
