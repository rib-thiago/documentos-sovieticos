CONFRONTATION RECORD

Between the accused S. N. BOGRACHEV and the witness D. A. ANIKIN,

conducted on January 6, 1935.

The accused S. N. BOGRACHEV and the witness D. A. ANIKIN, summoned to the confrontation, after confirming that they knew each other personally, testified:

Question to ANIKIN: What do you know about BOGRACHEV's anti-Party, counterrevolutionary activity after the Fifteenth Party Congress?

Answer by ANIKIN: Through our joint work in the “Leningradodezhda” system, I observed that over a number of years, as director of this association, BOGRACHEV grouped participants in the former Trotskyist-Zinovievist bloc within “Leningradodezhda.” Thus he recruited the following active oppositionists to work in the “Leningradodezhda” system:

1\. In 1927 BOGRACHEV transferred the active Trotskyist MOMOT, who had previously worked at the Volodarsky garment factory, to “Leningradodezhda.”

2\. After the active Zinovievist KORSHUNOV returned to Leningrad from exile, BOGRACHEV hired him at “Leningradodezhda,” appointing him director of the “Krasny Parus” factory.

3\. In 1931 BOGRACHEV hired the active Trotskyist SHVARTS at “Leningradodezhda” and appointed him director of the Münzenberg factory.

Furthermore, BOGRACHEV promoted in every way the concealed participants in the former Trotskyist-Zinovievist opposition AGRANOVSKY and KUBLANOV, assisting the first's appointment as director of the “Leningradodezhda” association and the second's as director of the Volodarsky factory.

Question to BOGRACHEV: Do you confirm ANIKIN's testimony set out above?

Answer by BOGRACHEV: I confirm that I hired KORSHUNOV at “Leningradodezhda” after his return from exile and appointed him director of the “Krasny Parus” factory. I also confirm that in 1927 I transferred MOMOT, a former Trotskyist, from the Volodarsky factory to “Leningradodezhda.” As for SHVARTS's appointment as director of the Münzenberg factory, I confirm that on the eve of my departure from “Leningradodezhda” in 1932, I recommended to AGRANOVSKY, appointed in my place, that he promote SHVARTS to the post of factory director. I know nothing about SHVARTS's involvement in the Trotskyist opposition.

I also confirm that I appointed KUBLANOV director of the Volodarsky garment factory and recommended AGRANOVSKY as head of “Leningradodezhda.” Concerning AGRANOVSKY's and KUBLANOV's political views, I know from AGRANOVSKY himself that in 1927, during the anniversary session of the USSR CEC, both went to some clandestine Trotskyist meeting at which TROTSKY spoke. From this fact, and from other individual conversations with AGRANOVSKY and KUBLANOV, one could judge that during the Trotskyist-Zinovievist bloc's acute factional struggle against the Party, their political attitudes inclined toward the opposition.

Question to ANIKIN: State the facts you know concerning BOGRACHEV's anti-Party, counterrevolutionary statements.

Answer by ANIKIN: In late December 1930 or early January 1931, on one occasion I entered the office of the Volodarsky factory director and found BOGRACHEV, AGRANOVSKY, KUBLANOV, and SHEININ there.

During a conversation concerning the removal of KAMENEV and ZINOVIEV from leading Party bodies, BOGRACHEV declared that he considered it the greatest mistake to cut ZINOVIEV and KAMENEV off from the Party leadership, as “true Leninists and creators of the Party, tested in the struggle for the Party's victory.” According to BOGRACHEV, replacing KAMENEV and ZINOVIEV with other persons, particularly Comrades KALININ and VOROSHILOV, would have disastrous consequences for the Party leadership. At the same time BOGRACHEV spoke insultingly and slanderously about Comrades KALININ and VOROSHILOV.

Speaking of the Party's political line, BOGRACHEV stated that there were now no differences between the Party and the former opposition, since the Party was supposedly implementing the opposition's program by developing industrialization and crushing the kulaks. Thus his statement implied that the Party had fully adopted the opposition's platform in practice.

Question to BOGRACHEV: Do you confirm the circumstances and content of your counterrevolutionary, slanderous statement described in ANIKIN's testimony?

Answer by BOGRACHEV: I deny the facts set out in ANIKIN's testimony and declare that I made no anti-Party statements in the office of the director of the Volodarsky garment factory.

Question to BOGRACHEV: The investigation demands a truthful answer concerning your counterrevolutionary, anti-Party activity, established by concrete facts, including your own testimony.

Answer by BOGRACHEV: I declare once again that I consider none of my actions and statements either counterrevolutionary or anti-Party.

The answers to the questions have been recorded correctly from our words and read by us.

BOGRACHEV.                                      ANIKIN.

Confrontation conducted by

ASSISTANT HEAD, OO, GUGB, NKVD, GENDIN.

Certified correct: Churbanova
