INTERROGATION RECORD

conducted by the head of the 4th Section of the SPO — L. KOGAN

on December 18, 1934.

TSARKOV, Nikolai Alekseevich (supplementary)

Question: Inform the investigation about the practical activities of the organization to which you belonged.

Answer: I have already testified about the organization's structure insofar as it was known to me. I can add that a group of our organization existed until recently at the housing estate, to which ANTONOV, FADEEV and VINOGRADOV belonged. I too was connected with this group. This same group was under the immediate leadership of V. RUMYANTSEV, a member of the organization's Center.

The fundamental element binding us ideologically was hatred for the Party leadership and particularly STALIN, instilled in us since the time of the open counterrevolutionary struggle against the Party. Our immediate leaders, who headed our struggle against the Party — EVDOKIMOV, LEVIN, RUMYANTSEV and KOTOLYNOV — worked extensively and for a long time in this direction on our group of former active Komsomol workers. What did they teach us? We were told that STALIN was not leading the Party and country along the Leninist path. That STALIN had distorted the Leninist line. That STALIN's leadership endangered any possibility of the revolution's victory on a world scale. During the period of open struggle we were taught to fight against STALIN by winning a majority of the Party, through counterrevolutionary agitation and propaganda: distributing counterrevolutionary literature and so forth. When hopes of success in this endeavor collapsed, we were taught — and we did so — to appeal by the same methods to the non-Party masses, into whose consciousness we, myself included, hammered the same hatred for STALIN. We were taught to act in this direction by genuinely underground methods, including accompanying non-Party people to beer halls and visiting them at home. We also regarded KIROV with particular hostility, for he directly defeated our leaders in open battle before the broad masses. Our attitude toward KIROV was approximately the same as the Mensheviks' attitude toward the Bolsheviks when the latter, in 1917, wrested the masses from Menshevik influence.

Directives coming much later from the Center explained that the possibility of the Zinovievist organization's leaders — ZINOVIEV, KAMENEV and others — returning to the Party leadership depended on only two factors:

1\) An attack on the USSR by the imperialist countries. The resulting war would inevitably, we thought, bring our leaders into the Party leadership. In this case our positions coincided with the hopes and aspirations of all counterrevolutionary and fascist forces inside and outside the country. 2) STALIN's removal from the Party leadership. The first hope did not materialize: war did not come. Therefore, of the effective arguments in the arsenal of struggle against the Party, only one remained: remove STALIN. On this basis the most extreme extremist sentiments arose and grew among us young people. NIKOLAEV's shot at KIROV is thus a direct and immediate act arising from sentiments existing within the organization.

Question: What do you know about comrade KIROV's murderer, Leonid NIKOLAEV?

Answer: I know NIKOLAEV from working together in the Vyborg district. He is a Zinovievist. He was closest to KOTOLYNOV. I do not remember exactly whether he participated illegally in our struggle against the Party in the early years of the Trotskyist-Zinovievist bloc and signed the bloc's platform (all signatures to the platform passed through me and Mikhail MURAVYOV), or whether he belonged to the category who remained underground and did not openly act as members of the organization. Our counterrevolutionary organization, in whose ranks he developed, bears political responsibility for the terrorist act of its member L. NIKOLAEV.

Recorded correctly and read by me, in confirmation of which I sign:

TSARKOV.

Interrogated by: L. KOGAN.
