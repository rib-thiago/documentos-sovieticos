INTERROGATION RECORD

conducted by the operational officer of the 1st Section, SPO — MAKAROV,

dated 15 December 1934.

FADEEV, Fedor Fadeevich, born in 1899, native of Novgorod Province, Okulovka District, Perestovo village; resides at 40 Kondratievsky Prospekt, building 4, apartment 26; Chairman of the Railway Construction Committee of the Murmansk Railway. Unmarried; mother Ekaterina Egorovna lives in the village; brothers Vasily and Ivan work as firefighters at the Kulotino factory, bearing a different surname: KLIMOV. Without property. Elementary education; VKP(b) member since 1922. In 1927 received a severe reprimand for belonging to the opposition. In 192[…] secretary of the Vyborg District VLKSM Committee. No convictions. Military registration category: junior command and political personnel.

Question: With which Zinovievists have you remained connected to this day?

Answer: Among former Zinovievists I most often met TOLMAZOV, ANTONOV, RUMYANTSEV, KHANIK, SEVEROV, POPOV and, very rarely, VINOGRADOV. Except for the last three, I visited them all at their apartments; I went to RUMYANTSEV's with TOLMAZOV. At our meetings we generally touched on questions of Party policy.

Question: What tactical position did the Zinovievists known to you, including yourself, adopt?

Answer: I personally noticed no attempts by the persons listed to take organisational shape.

Correctly taken down from my words, in confirmation of which I sign:

FADEEV

Interrogated by: MAKAROV
