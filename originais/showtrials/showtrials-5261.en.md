INTERROGATION RECORD

Of SAFAROV, G. I.,

dated January 6, 1935.

QUESTION: Tell the investigation what you know about LURIE?

ANSWER: LURIE is an active, extremely insinuating Trotskyist with great abilities for chameleon-like adaptation. A Bundist until 1920 or even 1921, he retained, as I judge from conversations with him in November 1927, many of his Bundist-Menshevik acquaintances. In 1927 he was undoubtedly a direct agent of TROTSKY, since the Trotskyists sharply protested that I did not invite him to a number of important meetings of the counterrevolutionary bloc’s conference abroad. TROTSKY in Moscow in December 1927 also expressed indignation at this upon my arrival. He is connected, probably until the most recent period, with Ruth FISCHER, MASLOW, SCHOLEM [1], GRYLEWICZ, and the Wedding Trotskyist group, serving above all as their chief informant on the affairs of the All-Union Communist Party (Bolsheviks) and the USSR and using his position in the Communist Party of Germany, where he was in the Central Committee’s Agitation and Propaganda Department, for counterrevolutionary work. A man organically hostile to Bolsheviks, constantly extolling TROTSKY as the “only leader.” The GERTSBERGS [2] told of their conversations with him in 1932. He portrayed the situation of the Communist Party of Germany before Hitler’s accession to power as catastrophic, asserted that the party’s mass base was insignificantly weak, and that the leadership of its Central Committee headed by THÄLMANN was good for nothing. At the same time, upon arriving in the USSR, he tried in every way to ingratiate himself with HECKERT and other German comrades. He undoubtedly retained his old contacts with HEINZ NEUMANN, under whom he had worked in the Agitation and Propaganda Department of the Communist Party of Germany’s Central Committee. Already in 1934, after leaving the Communist International’s editorial and publishing department, where he worked, he obtained new work as a professor of ancient history with the help of the Trotskyist VAGANYAN [3], as I learned from meeting him in the Communist International library. At the same time, of course, he became acquainted with Vaganyan’s entire Trotskyist entourage. I consider it very likely that LURIE brought contacts and assignments from abroad from foreign Trotskyists, and possibly from TROTSKY himself, for Trotskyists in the USSR. Through GERTSBERG he had the opportunity to obtain information about the affairs of the counterrevolutionary Zinovievist organization and to be connected with it.

Written in my own hand — SAFAROV.

INTERROGATED BY:

HEAD OF THE 1ST SECTION OF THE SPO OF THE GUGB OF THE NKVD OF THE USSR — PETROVSKY

Certified correct: A. Svetlova

[Handwritten note on a separate sheet: Lurie, M., was not even interrogated on the basis of this record and remained at liberty until April 29, 1936.]

RGASPI, fond 17, inventory 171, file 253, folios 125–127.

[1] Werner Scholem, born December 29, 1895, into a Jewish family in Berlin. His father owned a printing house. Joined the Communist Party of Germany in 1920. From 1921 an editor of “Rote Fahne”; from 1924 to 1927 a Reichstag deputy. Headed the so-called “Fischer-Maslow” group, connected with the then chairman of the Comintern, G. E. Zinoviev, which formed the party’s “ultra-Left” leadership in 1923. After the removal of the Communist Party of Germany’s Left leadership, he sympathized with the Trotskyists and Left Opposition. Arrested by the Nazis in 1933. Placed in Buchenwald in 1938. Shot by the Nazis in 1940.

[2] A. V. Gertsberg and M. V. Pozdeeva.

[3] Here and subsequently in the text, erroneously: “Vaganian” [Ваганьян].
