INTERROGATION RECORD

OF KASPERSKY Ivan Feliksovich

(continued)

dated January 17, 1935.

QUESTION: Tell us when members of your organization last gathered, and at whose home the meeting took place.

ANSWER: Members of our organization last gathered on September 7, 1934, at PANKO's apartment. Present at the meeting were myself, KASPERSKY, KONDRATIEVA Evdokia, PANKO, FOKIN, YAKOVLEV Moisei, SEMENOV Semyon, SEMENOV Ivan (“Old Man”), and SKOROMNY.

This meeting was illegal in character and dedicated to the memory of the deceased former member of our organization SHEINBERG. Speeches of a political character were delivered at the meeting, and all of SHEINBERG's past opposition activities were recalled. The memorial gathering for SHEINBERG was essentially a review of the old opposition cadres. An active participant in organizing this illegal meeting were [1] KONDRATIEVA Evdokia, who first suggested the gathering and collected money for this purpose, myself, and all members of the organization.

Correctly recorded from my words and read by me, which I confirm by my signature.

KASPERSKY.

INTERROGATED BY:

OPERATIVE OFFICER OF THE 4TH SECTION OF THE SPO OF THE UGB — KOGAN M.

Certified correct: A. Svetlova

RGASPI, F. 671, Op. 1, D. 136, fol. 33.

[1] As in the text.
