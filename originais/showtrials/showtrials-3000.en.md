INTERROGATION RECORD

OF TOLMAZOV Andrey Ilyich

December 12, 1934.

QUESTION: What do you know about the counterrevolutionary activity of the organization of the former Trotskyist-Zinovievist bloc, which existed until recently?

ANSWER: Meeting regularly with RUMYANTSEV V., I know from him that, as I have already testified, in Moscow and Leningrad there are cohesive groups of former participants in the Trotskyist-Zinovievist bloc, united on a platform of disagreement with Party policy and hostile to the Party leadership — STALIN, KAGANOVICH, and others. On one of my visits to RUMYANTSEV V., I found at his apartment KOTOLYNOV, who had recently returned from Moscow and told us about his meetings there with FAIVILOVICH \[1\] Leonid and TARASOV Ivan. KOTOLYNOV said that in conversation with him FAIVILOVICH and TARASOV had bitterly and sharply criticized the Party leadership — STALIN and KAGANOVICH. KOTOLYNOV reported that in his conversation with FAIVILOVICH and TARASOV they had discussed possible candidates from the Trotskyist-Zinovievist bloc who could replace STALIN as Party leader. It emerged that the only acceptable candidate would be ZINOVIEV. BAKAEV, EVDOKIMOV, GERTIK, KHARITONOV, and others whose surnames I do not remember were named as other possible leading figures in the Party.

Recorded faithfully from my words, and I sign in confirmation.

A. TOLMAZOV.

INTERROGATED BY L. KOGAN.

Certified true:

RGASPI, fond 671, inventory 1, file 114, leaf 176.

\[1\] Here and elsewhere in the text, incorrectly written as “Raivilovich” (Райвиловичем).
