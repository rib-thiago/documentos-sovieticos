INTERROGATION RECORD

OF NIKOLAEV, L. V., dated 6 December 1934.

QUESTION: Whom did SHATSKY recruit to participate in preparing a terrorist act against Comrade STALIN?

ANSWER: In the conversation about which I have already testified, SHATSKY told me that he was recruiting V. RUMYANTSEV, a former oppositionist and secretary of the Vyborg District Soviet, who entertained terrorist sentiments.

L. NIKOLAEV

INTERROGATED BY:

DEPUTY PEOPLE'S COMMISSAR

OF INTERNAL AFFAIRS — AGRANOV

HEAD OF THE EKO, GUGB, NKVD OF THE USSR — MIRONOV

ASSISTANT HEAD OF THE EKO, GUGB, NKVD OF THE USSR — DMITRIEV.

Certified true
