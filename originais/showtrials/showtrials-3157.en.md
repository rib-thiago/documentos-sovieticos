INTERROGATION RECORD

OF RUMYANTSEV, VLADIMIR VASILYEVICH, DATED 18-XII-1934.

Fully confirming my testimony of 13 December of this year about my membership in the Zinovievist-Trotskyist counterrevolutionary organization and my counterrevolutionary activities within it until the most recent period, I wish to state the following to the investigation.

I deeply repent of my grave counterrevolutionary crimes before the party and the Soviet Government, and I acknowledge that the Zinovievist-Trotskyist counterrevolutionary organization, in its counterrevolutionary activities, took the path of fascism and of fascist methods of struggle against the party, such as terror.

I ask, through the investigative authorities, the party and the Soviet Government to give me the opportunity, in any sector of socialist construction, to give me the opportunity to atone for my grave guilt.

The record was taken down correctly from my words.

V. RUMYANTSEV.

INTERROGATED BY:

DEPUTY PEOPLE'S COMMISSAR FOR INTERNAL AFFAIRS — Ya. AGRANOV.

HEAD OF THE EKO OF THE GUGB — MIRONOV.

DEPUTY HEAD OF THE SPO OF THE GUGB — LYUSHKOV.

certified true: M. Koroleva
