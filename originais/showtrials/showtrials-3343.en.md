RECORD

Of the supplementary interrogation of EMELYANOV, Kondraty Nikolaevich

Dated December 19, 1934.

Question: The investigation has information that the archive of the Zinovievist counterrevolutionary organization was handed over to you for safekeeping by Vladimir LEVIN and Olga YURGENS. Do you confirm this?

Answer: LEVIN was not present when the counterrevolutionary organization's archive was handed over to me; I do not know the surname of the woman who handed over the archive. The description of “Marusya” or “Tonya”: tall, slim, blonde, Estonian by nationality.

Question: The investigation has information that SAFAROV came from Moscow to see you in Leningrad and assembled members of the Zinovievist counterrevolutionary organization concerning readmission to the Party for tactical purposes. Tell us what you know about this question.

Answer: In 1928, after submitting a statement to the Central Committee renouncing the opposition, SAFAROV came to Leningrad and assembled participants in the Zinovievist-Trotskyist organization. Up to 20 people attended the meeting; those at the meeting included myself, K. N. EMELYANOV, Pyotr GORODSKOI, Mikhail Ivanovich KASHCHEEV and Nikolai Aleksandrovich MAKSIMOV; I do not remember the others present. I do not remember where the meeting took place. At the meeting SAFAROV instructed us to obtain readmission to the Party in order to continue fighting the Party within its ranks.

SAFAROV's proposal to join for duplicitous tactical purposes was accepted, and most of our group submitted applications for readmission to the Party.

Recorded from my words and read to me: K. EMELYANOV.

Interrogated by: Head of the 1st Section of the SPO of the GUGB, PETROVSKY.
