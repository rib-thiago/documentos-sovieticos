INTERROGATION RECORD

OF YUSKIN Ignaty Grigoryevich, 15 December 1934.

QUESTION: In your testimony of 8 December you say that NIKOLAEV told you of his intention to commit a terrorist act against Comrade KIROV; the investigation has information that together with NIKOLAEV you worked through the variant of murdering Comrade KIROV at Smolny.

ANSWER: Yes, I knew of NIKOLAEV's intention to kill Comrade KIROV at Smolny.

I have read the testimony; it has been correctly recorded from my words.

YUSKIN.

Interrogated by:

HEAD OF THE 1ST SECTION OF THE SPO, GUGB, NKVD OF THE USSR — PETROVSKY.

HEAD OF THE 6TH SECTION OF THE SPO, GUGB, NKVD OF THE USSR — KORKIN

OPERATIONS OFFICER OF THE SPO, GUGB, NKVD OF THE USSR — FEDOROV

Certified correct:

RGASPI, fond 671, inventory 1, file 120, folio 15.
