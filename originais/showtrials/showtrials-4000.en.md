CONFRONTATION RECORD

between the accused FYODOROV Grigory Fyodorovich and KUKLIN Aleksandr Sergeyevich

held on January 2, 1935.

The accused FYODOROV G.F. and KUKLIN A.S., summoned to the confrontation, after mutually acknowledging that they knew each other personally, testified:

Question to KUKLIN A.S.: Do you confirm your testimony of December 27, 1934, that you did not want to accept ZINOVIEV's archive from FYODOROV for safekeeping?

Answer: I told FYODOROV that I would take the archive from him temporarily, since I had nowhere to keep it.

Question to FYODOROV G.F.: Do you confirm KUKLIN A.S.'s testimony?

Answer: I do not confirm the part of KUKLIN's testimony stating that this was ZINOVIEV's archive or materials. I told him that these materials of the former Zinovievist opposition belonged to me and that I wanted to destroy them. KUKLIN proposed that I not destroy the materials but hand them over to him, since, in his words, they could be useful for history.

After that, I handed the materials to KUKLIN A.S., and there was no conversation between us about the duration of safekeeping when I handed the documents to him.

Question to KUKLIN: Who was present during your conversation with FYODOROV about handing over the archive for safekeeping, and where did this conversation and the transfer of the archive take place?

Answer: The conversations between FYODOROV and me about the archive in the summer of 1934 took place in private at the Sovnarkom dining room, and FYODOROV handed me the materials immediately after the conversation.

Question to FYODOROV: Do you confirm KUKLIN's testimony?

Answer: Yes, I do.

ADDITION BY FYODOROV G.F.: I wish to clarify the time when the archive was handed to KUKLIN. I think it was at the end of 1933 or the beginning of 1934.

KUKLIN.                              FYODOROV.

Confrontation conducted by:

ASSISTANT HEAD OF THE SPO OF THE GUGB—RUTKOVSKY.

SECTION HEAD OF THE EKO OF THE GUGB—CHERTOK.

Certified correct: Biryukov
