INTERROGATION RECORD

OF NIKOLAEV, L. V.

6 December 1934.

QUESTION: What do you know about the former oppositionist YUSKIN [1]?

ANSWER: Although YUSKIN has been reinstated in the Party, he remains a concealed oppositionist. He expressed to me dissatisfaction with the Party's policy in the countryside; he is anti-Soviet in outlook and harbours bitterness towards the Party leaders.

I told YUSKIN of my destitute situation due to unemployment, saying that people claim there is no unemployment in the Union, whereas in reality this is not so: “there is no unemployment, but there are unemployed people.” YUSKIN answered this in sharply anti-Soviet terms.

I told YUSKIN that I was trying to obtain an audience with KIROV, but that KIROV kept refusing, and added: “It is as though he senses that I want to kill him.” I then told YUSKIN that I had reached my limit and wanted to murder KIROV.

He replied: “Why Kirov? — It is STALIN [who must be killed].”

This conversation with YUSKIN strengthened my terrorist intention. YUSKIN's address: 13/18 Lesnoy Prospekt, apartment 52.

Signed, L. NIKOLAEV.

INTERROGATED BY: —

Deputy People's Commissar of Internal Affairs — AGRANOV

Head of the EKO, GUGB, NKVD of the USSR — MIRONOV

Assistant Head of the EKO, GUGB, NKVD of the USSR — DMITRIEV.
