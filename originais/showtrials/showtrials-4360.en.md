TESTIMONY

Of EVDOKIMOV, Grigory Yeremeevich,

January 9, 1935.

To my previous testimony I can add:

1\. On contacts with the Trotskyists: I know that BAKAEV met with UFIMTSEV. But I do not know whether they had political conversations, and therefore I do not know the nature of the meetings themselves. BAKAEV told me nothing about this. I do not remember him telling me anything about UFIMTSEV's mood either, although the opinion in our milieu concerning the top Trotskyists was that most of them had not overcome their anti-Party sentiments.

2\. On my meetings with the Right: of these meetings, one meeting I had with N. KOMAROV has a certain significance. This was most probably in 1931. KOMAROV came to see me at the hotel. We had met before, and we met afterward as well, but we had no political conversations. As far as one could judge from his occasional, mostly ironic remarks on various economic questions, he was dissatisfied, but it was difficult to judge how deep and general this dissatisfaction was. On this occasion, during the visit I am speaking of, I do not remember in what connection, the conversation turned to shock work as an indicator of the workers' mood. He declared that all this was greatly exaggerated, that most shock workers were self-seekers and careerists, and that they were not genuine established workers. I replied to him (I quote almost verbatim): “Fedya \[2\], you people (I meant the Right) apparently have the same sentiments now that we had at first when we were removed from the leadership; just as we did then, you now think that everything is going badly without your participation in the leadership; in fact, this is not so, and you are mistaken about shock work.” He answered me angrily: “You remain an ‘idealist’ as before.” It was difficult to determine what meaning he attached this time to the little word “idealist.” He ended the conversation there. He was not alone. Someone else from Leningrad was with him; I do not remember who.

Written by myself — G. EVDOKIMOV.

INTERROGATED BY: RUTKOVSKY

Certified true: A. Svetlova

RGASPI, fond 671, inventory 1, file 134, leaves 44–45.

\[1\] N. Komarov's real name is Fedor Yevgenyevich Sobinov.
