INTERROGATION RECORD

OF KOTOLYNOV, Ivan Ivanovich. 13 December 1934.

QUESTION: Whom do you know among the members of the counterrevolutionary organization besides the persons named in your previous testimony?

ANSWER: SEVEROV Pyotr, SEREDOKHIN Aleksandr, MAGDARYEV, MIKHAILOV and TROFIMOV. I have not seen any of them except TROFIMOV for a long time and do not know where they now work. I met TROFIMOV (at the theater) in November 1934; during our brief conversation, TROFIMOV told me that SUROV's wife had gone to join him — SUROV — and expressed regret that SUROV had been exiled. TROFIMOV works as an instructor of the Vasileostrov District Committee of the All-Union Communist Party (Bolsheviks). During the period when I visited NATANSON, I met ANISHEV there.

QUESTION: In your previous testimony you described the sharply hostile attitude of your counterrevolutionary organization's members toward the leaders of the All-Union Communist Party (Bolsheviks). Explain how these counterrevolutionary sentiments developed in the organization.

ANSWER: At the time, the leaders of our organization — ZINOVIEV, KAMENEV, EVDOKIMOV, BAKAEV and KHARITONOV — systematically cultivated among the Zinovievist youth a sharply hostile attitude toward the leaders of the All-Union Communist Party (Bolsheviks), and especially toward Comrade STALIN. We were constantly told that STALIN was chiefly responsible for ZINOVIEV's defeat and removal from leading work in the party; various provocative fabrications were circulated about the supposedly personal motives for STALIN's removal of ZINOVIEV.

I personally heard ZINOVIEV, BAKAEV, KHARITONOV and EVDOKIMOV repeatedly say of Comrade STALIN: “It would be better if he were not there.” STALIN was called a Bonaparte relying on violence against the party.

Brought up in this way, the members of the counterrevolutionary organization not only retained this hostile attitude toward Comrade STALIN; when we met one another, we shared these counterrevolutionary sentiments and thus continued to cultivate and spread them. —

I. KOTOLYNOV.

Interrogated by:

Head of the 6th Section of the SPO, GUGB — KORKIN.
