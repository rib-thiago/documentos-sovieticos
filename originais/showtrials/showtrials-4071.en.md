CONFRONTATION RECORD

Between the accused SHAROV, Yakov Vasilyevich, and GORSHENIN, Ivan Stepanovich,

dated January 4, 1935.

After acknowledging that they knew one another, the accused testified:

Question to GORSHENIN: Do you know of SHAROV, Ya. V.’s membership in the Zinovievist organization?

GORSHENIN’s answer: Yes, I do. SHAROV was an active participant in the counterrevolutionary Zinovievist organization and, according to my information, a member of its Moscow center until the very latest period.

Question to SHAROV: Do you confirm GORSHENIN’s testimony?

SHAROV’s answer: No, I do not.

Question to GORSHENIN: What do you know about SHAROV’s activities in the organization?

GORSHENIN’s answer: SHAROV was an active participant in the organization’s gatherings, held until the liquidation of our organization, that is, until December 1934. These gatherings took place at the apartments of active participants in the counterrevolutionary Zinovievist organization, including SHAROV’s apartment.

Question to SHAROV: Do you confirm GORSHENIN’s testimony that you attended a number of meetings of the Zinovievist organization and that meetings also took place at your apartment?

SHAROV’s answer: No, I deny it.

Question to GORSHENIN: What additional testimony can you give about SHAROV?

GORSHENIN’s answer: SHAROV, as an active participant in our organization, was directly connected with ZINOVIEV. Being negatively disposed toward the principal decisions of the Central Committee of the All-Union Communist Party (Bolsheviks), SHAROV was the most consistent supporter of our organization’s struggle to return ZINOVIEV and KAMENEV to the leadership of the All-Union Communist Party (Bolsheviks). I know of these attitudes of SHAROV’s both from his statements at the organization’s gatherings and from personal dealings with him.

Question to SHAROV: Do you continue to deny your active participation in the Zinovievist organization?

Answer: Yes, I do.

GORSHENIN                                             SHAROV

The confrontation was conducted by:

DEPUTY HEAD OF THE SPO OF THE GUGB OF THE NKVD OF THE USSR — LYUSHKOV.

DEPUTY HEAD OF THE SPO OF THE GUGB OF THE NKVD FOR THE LENINGRAD REGION — STROMIN.

HEAD OF A SECTION OF THE EKO OF THE GUGB OF THE NKVD OF THE USSR — CHERTOK.

Certified correct: Biryukov
