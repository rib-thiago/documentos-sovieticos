INTERROGATION RECORD

OF ANTONOV, Nikolai Semenovich (supplementary).

22 December 1934.

QUESTION: In your testimony of 12 December 1934, you stated that NIKOLAEV L. belonged to the Zinovievist organization. State with whom NIKOLAEV L. maintained contact.

ANSWER: Yes, I confirm that NIKOLAEV L., a former employee of the Vyborg District Committee of the Komsomol, was a member of the counterrevolutionary Zinovievist organization. NIKOLAEV maintained contact with KOTOLYNOV Ivan.

QUESTION: How did you learn that NIKOLAEV Leonid was a member of the Zinovievist organization?

ANSWER: I learned from KOTOLYNOV Ivan that NIKOLAEV was a member of the counterrevolutionary Zinovievist organization.

The record was taken down from my words. It was read to me. —

N. ANTONOV

INTERROGATED BY:

HEAD OF THE 1ST SECTION OF THE SPO OF THE GUGB OF THE NKVD — PETROVSKY.

CERTIFIED TRUE: M. Glazkov
