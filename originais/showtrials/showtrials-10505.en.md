# M. I. Gai's Report on D. S. Taits's Connections

Top secret. –

REPORT

In the course of surveillance by agents of foreign nationals arriving in Moscow, connections have been established between several Polish citizens, strongly suspected of involvement with Polish intelligence, and the director of the Central Polyclinic of the Kremlin Medical Administration, Dr. David Semionovich TAITS, who has access to members of the Politburo and the Government.

1\. – In the final days of last year, 1934, and at the beginning of the current year, the Polish citizen VAGMAN, a correspondent for the newspaper “Nash Psheglend,” was in Moscow. According to available information, VAGMAN made a special trip to the town of Kolchugino to meet with the engineer KNAISTER, who is suspected of involvement in Polish espionage. From the USSR, VAGMAN sent correspondence of a tendentiously Zionist character to Poland. VAGMAN visited Dr. TAITS at his apartment.

2\. – At the same time, the Polish citizen Shaya SHPIGEL came to Moscow. Until recently SHPIGEL had been a contractor for “Intourist” in Warsaw and was removed from this work at the request of the Main Directorate of State Security (GUGB), on the basis of information about his connections with Polish intelligence. According to information from the Foreign Department of the GUGB (INO GUGB) dating to 1931, SHPIGEL, who was then living in Germany, was an agent of the German police. In addition, SHPIGEL has highly suspicious relations with Japanese nationals and rents out half of his Warsaw apartment to them. While in Moscow, SHPIGEL spoke of his close friendship with Dr. TAITS and of the personal services the latter rendered him.

3\. – In January of the c\<urrent\> y\<ear\>, a certain GRUFERMAN applied to the USSR plenipotentiary mission in Warsaw for permission to enter the USSR. He is directly connected with an office operating in Poland that searches for buried treasure on our territory and is linked with our “Kreditbiuro.” In 1920, GRUFERMAN was expelled from the RSFSR for criminal activities as a member of a Czech trade delegation. In view of his present work, GRUFERMAN is undoubtedly linked to Polish counterintelligence. When filling out a visa application at the plenipotentiary mission, GRUFERMAN named Dr. TAITS in the field asking for a person who knew him well.

4\. – At present, two Polish citizens are living in Dr. TAITS's apartment: KLEIN Rakhil Moiseevna and KLEIN Raaika Isaakovna.

5\. – In addition, Dr. TAITS has relatives abroad—in Poland, America, and Lithuania.

Regarding the foregoing as deliberate actions by Polish intelligence, which has established close contact with Dr. TAITS, the NKVD considers it necessary to remove Dr. TAITS immediately from his position in the Kremlin.

HEAD OF THE SPECIAL DEPARTMENT

OF THE GUGB, NKVD OF THE USSR –                       M. Gai                                      (GAI)

February 19, 1935.
