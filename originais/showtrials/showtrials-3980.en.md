INTERROGATION RECORD

OF PANOV, Aleksandr Nikitich

January 2, 1935.

(supplementary).

QUESTION: Describe your participation in the Zinovievist opposition.

ANSWER: I never joined the Zinovievist opposition or shared its orientations. On the contrary, I fought the Zinovievists, and at a meeting of Leningrad Komsomol activists I put forward a proposal to recognize the decisions of the Fourteenth Party Congress, which was rejected by the majority, consisting of Zinovievist Komsomol activists. After the opposition leadership was removed, in 1926 I joined the Provincial Committee of the VLKSM and was subsequently secretary of the Smolny VKP(b) collective.

QUESTION: What do you know about SOSITSKY's relations with the Zinovievists FYODOROV and SHLYAKOV, and what was the nature of your connection with them?

ANSWER: The Zinovievists SOSITSKY, SHLYAKOV and FYODOROV, who worked within the Leningrad Soviet's transport administration system, were in close contact through their main work. In particular, SHLYAKOV was well disposed toward SOSITSKY and FYODOROV, especially SOSITSKY, whom he supported in every way. I personally had contact with these persons only on official business, from September 1934 onward. Before that, I did not personally know either SOSITSKY or FYODOROV.

QUESTION: What was the nature of your connection with the Zinovievists KHANIK, SOROKIN and ZHESTYANNIKOV?

ANSWER: I had no connections with these persons, and if I met KHANIK and SOROKIN, these were chance meetings at the “Combat Comrades' Association.” The founders of this organization were the Zinovievists KHANIK, SOROKIN and ZHESTYANNIKOV.

Recorded correctly, which I confirm by signing.

A. PANOV.

INTERROGATED BY:

OPERATIONS OFFICER OF THE 1ST SECTION OF THE SPO—MAKAROV.

Certified correct: A. Svetlova
