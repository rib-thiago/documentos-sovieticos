INTERROGATION RECORD

OF YAKOVLEV, Moisei Nakhimovich

January 1, 1935.

YAKOVLEV, Moisei Nakhimovich (Naumovich), \<born\> in 1897, native of the town of Mstislav\<l\>, Byelorussian SSR; father a merchant; Jewish; USSR citizen. Res\<ides\> in Leningrad, V\<asilievsky\> I\<sland\>, Sredny Avenue, building No. 48, apartment 40. USSR Academy of Sciences—deputy \<chairman\> of the Pacific Committee and the Mongolian Committee, and professor at the ENUKIDZE Oriental Institute. Wife—KONDRATYEVA, Vera Nikolayevna, Leningrad, postgraduate student at the Academy of Sciences; brother YAKOVLEV, Zakhar Naumovich, design engineer, Leningrad; brother—YAKOVLEV, Solomon Naumovich, employee of Kraiplan, Simferopol; sister—YAKOVLEVA, Anna Naumovna, physician, Leningrad. Father owned a small house and a shop. Higher education. Graduated from Leningr\<ad\> State University in 1923 and completed postgraduate studies at the Academy of Sciences in 1931. Member of the VKP(b) since 1916. In 1915–1916, lead\<er\> of underground circles. In 1918–1919, chairman of the Vasil\<e\>ostr\<ovsky\> District Soviet of Workers', Peasants' and Soldiers' Deputies \[1\]. In 1920–1921, head of the Political Department of the city of Leningrad and deputy \<head\> of PUOKR \[2\]. In 1926, head of the political economy chair of the Naval Academy. No convictions. Military registration cat\<egory\>—reserve—K.-P. 10. Did not serve with the Whites.

QUESTION: Which former participants in the Zinovievist-Trotskyist bloc have you met in recent years?

ANSWER: After the Fifteenth Party Congress, following some hesitation, I broke both with SAFAROV's group and with the Zinovievist group, submitted a statement breaking with the opposition, and requested permission to remain in the party.

Before the Fifteenth Party Congress, I was a member of the Leningrad center of the Zinovievist-Trotskyist bloc, which at that time included, besides myself: NAUMOV, LEVIN V., GESSEN, ROTSKAN, KASPERSKY, TIMOFEYEV E., and, from the Trotskyists, BRONSHTEIN A.L., KARPOV and a third person I do not remember.

There were also district groups of three and five. Almost throughout this time, I was the representative for the Vasileostrovsky district.

I was not expelled from the party by the Fifteenth Congress, I believe for accidental reasons; however, this gave rise to widespread rumors among members of the former Zinovievist-Trotskyist bloc that I had supported the party line all along, which led to my almost complete isolation from former comrades in the opposition.

I was convinced of the correctness of the Fifteenth Party Congress's decisions, considered the accusation of Thermidor against the party slanderous, and understood that continuing factional work led to counterrevolution; at the same time, not having fully overcome my old oppositional views, after the Fifteenth Congress until the end of 1928, I nevertheless allowed myself a whole series of organizational meetings with former members of the United Opposition and conversations on anti-party subjects.

I regularly met remnants of the former opposition group, mainly from the KAZITSKY factory—SEMYONOV Semyon, his wife KONDRATYEVA Evdokiya, FOKIN Aleksei and SHEINBERG Davyd. With this group, we visited former oppositionists, mainly those who had supported SAFAROV's and TROTSKY's group, and persuaded them to return to the party.

In the summer of 1928, REM and SHURYGIN visited me and asked and urged me to stop visiting former oppositionists.

In the summer of that same year, 1928, I went to Kaluga to see ZINOVIEV G.E.

QUESTION: On behalf of which group or center did REM and SHURYGIN conduct talks with you?

ANSWER: REM and SHURYGIN spoke to me personally on their own behalf.

QUESTION: The investigation has information that REM and SHURYGIN conducted talks with you on behalf of the Leningrad center, and that you knew this. Do you confirm it?

ANSWER: I did not know that REM and SHURYGIN were conducting talks with me on behalf of the center.

QUESTION: For what purpose did you go to Kaluga to see Zinoviev G.E.? Inform the investigation of the content of your conversations.

ANSWER: Upon arriving in Kaluga, I told ZINOVIEV that rumors about me were circulating widely to the effect that my current position and my remaining in the party were explained by my having worked in the opposition on the party's instructions; that this was, of course, untrue, that I had received no party instructions, and that while in the opposition I had been sincerely mistaken.

ZINOVIEV replied that there were always many rumors in political struggle.

QUESTION: Did you inform the party's control bodies about your talks with ZINOVIEV?

ANSWER: No, I did not.

QUESTION: The investigation has information that you met EVDOKIMOV and KUKLIN.

ANSWER: I met KUKLIN A.S. about twice in Kislovodsk, in the presence of LAZURKINA, a former member of the Reg\<ional\> Control Commission, and Comrade OLBERT, in the summer of 1929.

I had no political conversations with KUKLIN.

I met EVDOKIMOV once in Sochi. I had no conversations with him on political subjects. The meeting took place in the presence of several persons staying at the sanatorium.

QUESTION: The investigation has information that you constantly met the Zinovievist GORBACHEV, had anti-party conversations with him and instructed him to hold talks with KAMENEV about counterrevolutionary work.

ANSWER: I did not meet GORBACHEV G.E. systematically, but at times frequently. He visited my apartment, and I visited his. Our conversations chiefly concerned literature, but also politics, and each time we noted how profoundly wrong we had been in our struggle against the party and how correct the party line was.

GORBACHEV did not engage in anti-party talk in my presence.

I did not instruct GORBACHEV to hold talks with KAMENEV. GORBACHEV himself intended (approximately seven or eight months ago) to see KAMENEV about an article on LERMONTOV.

In conversation with GORBACHEV, I told him that since L.B. KAMENEV had begun working at the Academy of Sciences, it would be interesting to see how he now appeared in his new role.

QUESTION: Tell us who attended SHEINBERG's funeral \[3\] and what anti-party conversations took place there.

ANSWER: Some Zinovievist workers from the KAZITSKY fac\<tory\>, where SHEINBERG had previously been secretary, attended the funeral of SHEINBERG D. Those present were: myself—YAKOVLEV, KASPERSKY I.F., FOKIN, SEMYONOV S., KONDRATYEVA Evdokiya and SKOROMNY.

Ordinary conversations took place at the memorial gathering, during which SKOROMNY dropped a remark approximately as follows: “Do you remember how SHEINBERG fought in the opposition?”—and wanted to continue saying something in the same spirit. I cut him short and added that SKOROMNY had been expelled from the party with good reason; the others supported me and did not let him speak.

I have read the testimony; it has been recorded correctly from my words—YAKOVLEV.

INTERROGATED BY:

OPERATIONS OFFICER OF THE SPO OF THE GUGB OF THE NKVD—FYODOROV.

Certified correct: Kazakova
