STENOGRAPHIC TRANSCRIPT

INTERROGATION RECORD

OF NIKOLAEV, Leonid Vasilyevich

14 December 1934.

Question: What motives guided the counterrevolutionary organization, represented by KOTOLYNOV and SHATSKY, in planning the terrorist act against Comrade KIROV?

Answer: The motives were as follows: KIROV headed the Leningrad party organization's struggle against the Trotskyist-Zinovievist bloc. His speeches against the bloc earned him great bitterness on the oppositionists' part. Carrying out the terrorist act against KIROV meant eliminating one of the former opposition's very dangerous political adversaries and weakening the existing leadership of the All-Union Communist Party (Bolsheviks).

As I have already testified, our plans went further still: a terrorist act against STALIN was being prepared. All this together was intended to facilitate the former oppositionists' return to the leadership of the party and the country.

In our discussions of this subject — those of KOTOLYNOV, SHATSKY and YUSKIN — we preferred ZINOVIEV to TROTSKY. We believed that ZINOVIEV–KAMENEV and their group's arrival in the leadership would have to be accompanied by bringing the most prominent figures of the Right deviation, BUKHARIN and RYKOV, into leading work. We regard their present membership of the Party Central Committee as an opportunity to use their influence and connections to achieve the stated objectives.

The record was correctly dictated from my words and read by me.

L. NIKOLAEV.

INTERROGATED BY:

Deputy People's Commissar of Internal Affairs of the USSR — AGRANOV

Head of the EKO, GUGB, NKVD of the USSR — MIRONOV

Assistant to the Head of the EKO, GUGB, NKVD of the USSR — DMITRIEV
