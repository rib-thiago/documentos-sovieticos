INTERROGATION RECORD

Of NALIVAIKO Foma Georgiyevich, December 27, 1934.

Question: The investigation has information that in the autumn of 1928 you participated in a clandestine meeting of the anti-Soviet Zinovievist group. Do you confirm this?

Answer: I do not confirm it.

Question: The investigation has information that in 1929 you participated in a gathering of Zinovievists organized by KAMENEV at the European Hotel. Do you confirm this? Who was present?

Answer: I did indeed visit KAMENEV at the European Hotel. RUMYANTSEV and SAFRONOV Pyotr were there; I do not remember who else was there. This was, I believe, in 1928.

Question: According to the investigation's information, at this meeting those present declared that they remained on their former positions, expressing a hostile attitude toward Comrade STALIN. Do you confirm this?

Answer: I do not remember what conversations took place there.

Question: Was MOROZOV Aleksandr present?

Answer: Yes, he was present.

Taken down correctly from my words.

NALIVAIKO.

INTERROGATED BY — LULOV.

Certified true: Churbanova
