INTERROGATION RECORD

OF NIKOLAEV L. V., dated December 5, 1934.

Question: What do you know about the form\<er\> oppositionist Nikolai BARDIN?

Answer: Nikolai BARDIN undoubtedly has terrorist inclinations; he held the same views as I did, and I even consider BARDIN’s convictions to be much stronger than mine. He formerly possessed a weapon and was arrested twice by the OGPU for underground opposition activity. In 1932 he invited me to his apartment, saying that we needed to have a serious conversation. I understood this as an attempt by BARDIN to draw me into underground activity, but I did not go to see him.

Knowing BARDIN, I am sure that he regrets not having been recruited by me to carry out the terrorist attack against KIROV.

If, for one reason or another, I had been delayed in carrying out the murder of KIROV, I would have begun forming a group to accomplish it and would have recruited first of all BARDIN, KOTOLYNOV, and SHATSKY, none of whom would have rejected my proposal. –

L. NIKOLAEV.

INTERROGATED BY:

DEPUTY PEOPLE’S COMMISSAR FOR INTERNAL AFFAIRS, IA. AGRANOV

ASSISTANT HEAD OF THE ECONOMIC DEPARTMENT, MAIN DIRECTORATE OF STATE SECURITY (EKO GUGB), DMITRIEV
