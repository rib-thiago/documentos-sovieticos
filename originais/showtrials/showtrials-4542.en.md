RECORD

of the supplementary interrogation of the accused GERTIK, Artem Moiseyevich,

January 21, 1935.

In answer to the investigation's question concerning the role and activity of Nikolai GORDON, a member of our organization, I can report the following:

Nikolai GORDON joined the Zinovievist opposition from the Fourteenth Congress and maintained contact with us throughout; in particular, he was also connected with me.

On almost all his visits to Moscow he called on me, and sometimes visited ZINOVIEV and KUKLIN.

I last saw him in December 1933 in Rostov-on-Don.

At our meetings I informed GORDON of all rumors and news in an anti-party spirit and of the state of our Zinovievist organization; he, in turn, provided information about the situation in the North Caucasus, chiefly that of the peasantry (I believe he then worked in the agricultural department of the regional committee).

In particular, I remember that he gave detailed information about the events in the North Caucasus in November 1932 and brought a collection of local newspapers to Moscow. His information was of a decidedly counterrevolutionary nature. He spoke indignantly about mass shootings of Communists from among the workers for trivial mistakes, the shooting of more than a thousand collective farmers, the deportation of almost entire districts, and so on.

At our last meeting in Rostov in 1933, I visited him with PERIMOV.

I know that the Rostov counterrevolutionary Zinovievist group, which he headed, included, besides himself, ROTSKAN and Timofei DMITRIEV. The former Zinovievist Pyotr SOLOVYOV lived in Rostov for a time, but whether GORDON met him I do not know.

The collection of Rostov newspapers brought by GORDON, as well as his information, was passed on to ZINOVIEV. Initially he wanted to visit ZINOVIEV himself, but later said that for some reason he had been unable to do so.

GORDON remained on his old counterrevolutionary positions until the last period.

GERTIK.

INTERROGATED BY:

ASSISTANT HEAD OF THE SPO, GUGB, NKVD OF THE USSR: (RUTKOVSKY)

DEPUTY HEAD OF THE SPO, UGB FOR LENINGRAD REGION: (STROMIN)
