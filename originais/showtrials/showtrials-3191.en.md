INTERROGATION RECORD

OF KOTOLYNOV Ivan Ivanovich

21 December 1934.

QUESTION: During your and TARASOV's meetings with RUMYANTSEV V., were there any counterrevolutionary statements about the situation in the country and the party leadership?

ANSWER: At one of our meetings in his apartment in 1932, RUMYANTSEV said approximately the following to me and I. TARASOV: “The country is in an extremely difficult situation. The party leadership sees no way out of this difficult situation. STALIN is leading the country into involvement in a war, proceeding from the premise that it is better to perish in a war with the world bourgeoisie than as a consequence of the failure of domestic policy resulting from incorrect leadership.” I personally and I. TARASOV listened to these statements by RUMYANTSEV but said nothing ourselves.

I. KOTOLYNOV.

INTERROGATED BY: KORKIN.

Certified true: A. Svetlova
