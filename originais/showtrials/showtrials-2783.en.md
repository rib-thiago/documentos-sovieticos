RECORD

OF SUPPLEMENTARY INTERROGATION

OF ANTONOV, Nikolai Semionovich

12 December 1934.

QUESTION: Relate what you know, and from whom, of the ties between your counter-revolutionary organisation and [1] the Trotskyists.

ANSWER: I know that the bloc between Zinovievists and Trotskyists which existed before the Fifteenth Party Congress, temporarily disrupted by the break between the “leaders” Trotsky and Zinoviev–Kamenev, has recently been restored; I know this from Ivan KOTOLYNOV's words.

QUESTION: Relate what you know about the membership of the Moscow Centre of the counter-revolutionary Zinovievist-Trotskyist organisation.

ANSWER: I know from Ivan KOTOLYNOV's words that the Moscow Centre of the counter-revolutionary Zinovievist-Trotskyist organisation included ZINOVIEV, EVDOKIMOV, GERTIK, BAKAEV and KHARITONOV.

QUESTION: Which members of your counter-revolutionary organisation did you tell about the membership of the Moscow Centre of the counter-revolutionary Zinovievist-Trotskyist organisation?

ANSWER: I spoke about the membership of the Moscow Centre of the counter-revolutionary Zinovievist-Trotskyist organisation to Vasily ZVEZDOV.

QUESTION: Relate who belonged to the Leningrad Centre of the counter-revolutionary Trotskyist organisation.

ANSWER: The Leningrad Centre of the counter-revolutionary Zinovievist-Trotskyist organisation included Ivan KOTOLYNOV, Vladimir RUMYANTSEV and Nikolai TSARKOV. Ivan KOTOLYNOV told me about the membership of the counter-revolutionary organisation's Leningrad Centre.

Correctly taken down from my words and read to me.

(N. ANTONOV)

Interrogated by:

Head of the 1st Section, SPO, GUGB (Petrovsky)

RGASPI, fond 671, inventory 1, file 114, fols. 163–164.

[1] The text erroneously gives “с” (“with”).
