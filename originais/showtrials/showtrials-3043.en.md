INTERROGATION RECORD

OF TOLMAZOV, Andrei Ilyich — 15 December 1934.

QUESTION: Your testimony of 13 December 1931 establishes that RUMYANTSEV was the most consistent advocate of terrorist methods of action by the organization. Tell the investigation in what concrete form he formulated his view of terror.

ANSWER: To clarify my testimony of 13 December 1931, I state: in repeated conversations with RUMYANTSEV in which we discussed party policy in a counterrevolutionary spirit, he always brought the conversation around to the assertion that STALIN was responsible for all misfortunes. According to him, there was no hope of our organization winning by gaining a majority in the party. Nor was there hope of an “internal palace coup,” as he put it.

RUMYANTSEV concluded that only STALIN's removal could bring about ZINOVIEV's return to the party leadership. By the expression “remove” he meant forcible removal, and I understood him in that sense. Such conversations took place when I visited him at his apartment. —

Correctly recorded from my words, in confirmation of which I sign.

A. TOLMAZOV

INTERROGATED BY — KOGAN
