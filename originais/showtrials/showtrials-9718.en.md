INTERROGATION RECORD

OF MANDELSHTAM Sergei Osipovich — 17 December 1934.

MANDELSHTAM Sergei Osipovich, born in 1896, native of Riga, son of a physician, Jewish, citizen of the USSR, residing in Leningrad, Skorokhodov Street, No. 21/9, apartment 32; head of the Organizational Design Sector of Giprovod; wife: ANTIPOVA Alexandra Alexandrovna, member of the AUCP(b), secretary of the Party Committee of the Dzerzhinsky factory; son aged 16; correspondence student at the Leningrad Correspondence Metallurgical Institute; member of the AUCP(b) since 1917, expelled in January 1928 by the Arkhangelsk Provincial Control Commission for factional activity, reinstated in 1929 by the Leningrad Regional Control Commission. Arrested in 1928 by the OGPU Plenipotentiary Representation in Leningrad and released. Private in the reserve.

QUESTION: What do you know about the counterrevolutionary Zinovievist organization existing in Leningrad?

ANSWER: A counterrevolutionary Zinovievist organization has survived in Leningrad and still exists today. This organization survived and took shape on the basis of the practical work we carried out to rally and consolidate the former Zinovievist cadres.

QUESTION: Name the members of this organization.

ANSWER: Among the members of the counterrevolutionary Zinovievist organization I can name: myself, MANDELSHTAM, RUMYANTSEV Vl., LEVIN Vladimir, MYASNIKOV, SOSITSKY, KOTOLYNOV. I will give supplementary testimony about the organization's other members.

QUESTION: What do you know about the leadership of the center of your counterrevolutionary Zinovievist organization?

ANSWER: Our organization's activities were directed by the Moscow center. Among the members of the Moscow center known to me I can name: ZINOVIEV, KAMENEV, EVDOKIMOV, BAKAEV, FEDOROV Grigory, GERTIK Artyom. Contact with the Moscow center was maintained partly through me. I will speak later about how our counterrevolutionary organization took shape, the tasks it set itself, its political directives, its political directives \[1\], and its practical work.

Recorded correctly from my words, read by me, which I confirm by my signature.

S. MANDELSHTAM.

INTERROGATED BY:

HEAD OF THE 1ST SECTION OF THE SPO OF THE UGB OF THE NKVD OF LENINGRAD REGION — LULOV.
