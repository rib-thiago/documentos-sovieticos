INTERROGATION RECORD

OF REM Mikhail Semyonovich, January 2, 1935.

QUESTION: What statement do you wish to make to the investigation about SHMIDT Yakov?

ANSWER: SHMIDT Yakov works as a personnel inspector attached to the head of the October Railway; I have known him for about two years through our work together. During the June 1934 plenum, SHMIDT visited me with another October Railway employee, VKP(b) member VLASOV. In a conversation about the plenum of the CC of the VKP(b), SHMIDT said in VLASOV's presence: “They say there are disagreements in the party again.” He connected this with the CC plenum, supposing that this would emerge and make itself felt during the plenum's work. I told SHMIDT that there could be no disagreements at present. VLASOV remained silent during this conversation. That is all I wished to report to the investigation.

REM.

INTERROGATED BY: KORKIN.

Certified correct: Kazakova
