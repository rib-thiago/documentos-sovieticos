INTERROGATION RECORD

Of GERTIK, Artem Moiseyevich,

dated January 5, 1935.

QUESTION: The investigation has established that you maintained contact with members of the political center of the Zinovievist organization until the most recent period. Do you confirm this?

ANSWER: I previously testified about my contacts with ZINOVIEV, EVDOKIMOV, BAKAEV, and others. I confirm all the testimony I previously gave on this question.

QUESTION: What do you know about the existence of the political center of the Zinovievist organization?

ANSWER: I know that the political center of the Zinovievist organization disintegrated after the 15th Party Congress, but contacts between the members of this center and its leaders were preserved chiefly on the basis of anti-party views that had not been overcome.

QUESTION: Do you admit your guilt in having acted in the name of the leading Moscow center of the Zinovievist organization rather than on your own behalf when informing Zinovievists living in Leningrad in a spirit hostile to the party?

ANSWER: I received no special assignments for the Leningrad Zinovievists from either members of the center or ZINOVIEV. When meeting Zinovievists in Leningrad up to and including 1932, I conveyed everything I knew from our Zinovievist milieu. The Leningrad Zinovievists may have considered me a representative of the center, but I never discussed this question with any of them.

QUESTION: With which other Zinovievists, apart from those living in Moscow and Leningrad, were you in contact?

ANSWER: In 1932 GORDON, DMITRIEV, Nikolai, and GESSEN, who came to Moscow on business, visited my apartment. In November or December 1933, while in Rostov, I called on GORDON, who arranged a hotel room for me. I had no political conversations with him. I also happened to meet PERIMOV, who had come from Ordzhonikidze. I went to a restaurant with him. The setting in which I was with PERIMOV ruled out the possibility of political conversations.

A. GERTIK

INTERROGATED BY:

ASSISTANT HEAD OF THE SPO OF THE GUGB OF THE NKVD — RUTKOVSKY

Certified correct: A. Svetlova
