INTERROGATION RECORD

OF L. V. NIKOLAEV

dated 8 December 1934.

QUESTION: From which employees of the VKP(b) Regional Committee did you obtain information about KIROV needed to keep his movements under observation?

ANSWER: Having decided to commit a terrorist act against KIROV, I frequently visited the VKP(b) Regional Committee to establish KIROV's habits, the places he visited, the time he arrived at work, and so on.

For this purpose I approached individual employees of the city Regional Party Committee whom I had known since my own time working in the Regional Committee. Without revealing my intentions, I sought answers to the questions that interested me.

From VLADIMIROV, an employee of the city Regional Committee who is a referent and close to KIROV's secretariat, I learned KIROV's habits, that KIROV was moving to another office, at what times his meals were served, and so forth. I received most of my information about KIROV from him.

Regional Committee employee PETRASHEVICH told me in which rooms plenary meetings were held and when KIROV would attend them.

In conversation, KARMANOVA, an employee of the city Regional Committee, informed me of changes in the Regional Committee staff; from her I learned the locations of the departments, and so on.

All these people knew that I had left the Regional Committee two years earlier and currently had no connection with it; they knew I had been dismissed and had left dissatisfied and aggrieved.

I forgot to mention one other employee: this was in the summer of this year, and I wanted to know when KIROV would return from holiday and be at work.

I put this question to the employee who distributes newspapers and mail and received information from her that KIROV would arrive in two weeks; I took this into account. I cannot remember the employee's surname. Her description: a woman of a national minority, thin, of medium height, about 40.

Correctly taken down from my words. I have read the record.

L. NIKOLAEV.

INTERROGATED BY:

HEAD OF THE EKO, GUGB, NKVD OF THE USSR — MIRONOV.

ASSISTANT HEAD OF THE EKO, GUGB, NKVD OF THE USSR — DMITRIEV.
