CONFRONTATION RECORD

between the accused BAKAEV Ivan Petrovich and GORSHENIN Ivan Stepanovich

10/1-1935.

The accused BAKAEV I. P. and GORSHENIN I. S., after confirming that they knew each other, testified:

QUESTION TO BAKAEV: Do you know of GORSHENIN I. S.'s membership in the Moscow center of the counterrevolutionary Zinovievist organization?

ANSWER: Yes, I know that GORSHENIN I. S. belonged to the Moscow center of the counterrevolutionary Zinovievist organization.

QUESTION TO GORSHENIN: Do you confirm this testimony by BAKAEV I. P.?

ANSWER: Yes, I confirm that I belonged to the Moscow center of the counterrevolutionary Zinovievist organization.

QUESTION TO BAKAEV: Name the entire membership of the Moscow center of the Zinovievist organization.

ANSWER: The center included ZINOVIEV, KAMENEV, EVDOKIMOV, FEDOROV, KUKLIN, SHAROV, GERTIK, myself, BAKAEV, GORSHENIN, and, until 1932, KHARITONOV.

QUESTION TO GORSHENIN: Do you confirm BAKAEV's testimony about the Moscow center's membership?

ANSWER: Yes, I confirm that the Moscow center of the Zinovievist organization indeed included ZINOVIEV, KAMENEV, EVDOKIMOV, FEDOROV, KUKLIN, SHAROV, GERTIK, BAKAEV, myself, GORSHENIN, and, until 1932, KHARITONOV.

QUESTION TO BAKAEV: What do you know about GORSHENIN's role and activities in the center of your organization?

ANSWER: GORSHENIN participated in discussions of political questions. These discussions also took place at his apartment; he supplied ZINOVIEV with various materials on economic questions.

QUESTION TO GORSHENIN: Do you confirm this testimony by BAKAEV?

ANSWER: Yes, I confirm BAKAEV's testimony.

CONFRONTATION CONDUCTED BY:

ASSISTANT TO THE HEAD OF THE EKO OF THE GUGB OF THE NKVD OF THE USSR — DMITRIEV.

DEPUTY HEAD OF THE SPO OF THE NKVD DIRECTORATE FOR LENINGRAD REGION — STROMIN.

Certified true: A. Svetlova
